## Scope

In scope: populations of LLM agents (N >= 3, usually tens to thousands) that read each other's outputs and whose aggregate state (consensus, polarisation, agreement, accuracy, spread of a belief or tactic) is the measured object. That covers naming games and opinion dynamics with LLM agents, multi-agent debate studied as a population rather than a method, conformity and committed-minority experiments, scaling of team size, the physics-of-agents line that fits Ising and gossip models to LLM update rules, and the in-the-wild corpora from 2026 (AI Village, the May to June wiki swarm, the Hugging Face / ExploitGym incident, Moltbook, the sealed-swarm reproduction). Also in scope: the orchestration frameworks and simulators only insofar as they are what we would run.

Out of scope: single-agent benchmarks; multi-agent debate papers whose only claim is a benchmark score; LLM-for-robot-swarm papers (covered by survey-swarm-robotics); MARL emergence (survey-marl-emergence); classical opinion dynamics and collective decision without LLMs, cited here only as the models the LLM work fits to (survey-collective-decision and survey-sync-consensus own them).

Why this framing: the hackathon asks for measurable swarm dynamics in AI agent populations and gives one day. The useful output of this survey is a short list of phenomena that have (a) a clean control parameter, (b) a published number to replicate or contradict, and (c) a runnable harness.

## Landscape

Five communities study the same object with different words.

**1. The physics-of-agents line.** Treats a population of LLM agents as a spin system and measures the update rule. [[chuang-2023-simulating]] is the canonical LLM opinion-dynamics-on-networks paper (agents drift to scientifically accurate consensus unless confirmation bias is prompted in) and the case [[yang-2026-when]] applies its diagnostic to. [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] showed the binary-opinion update is Glauber dynamics of the mean-field Ising model with a model-specific "majority force" beta that falls with group size, giving a critical group size N_c (about 30 for Llama 3 70B, 80 for GPT-4o, about 1000 for GPT-4 Turbo from a single N = 1000 run, above 1000 for Claude 3.5 Sonnet). [[el-2026-physics]] fits a kinetic Ising model with three couplings and a learned intrinsic field to about 10,000 communities of 32 persona agents on signed networks and predicts trajectories from initial opinions. [[okawa-2026-emergence]] writes debate as m(t+1) = tanh[beta(J m + h)] and finds the biased-consensus transition at beta J > 1. [[de-nobili-2026-microscopic]] replaces the naming-game listener check with a YES/NO LLM call and measures two rates, pi(T) and phi(T), giving the ordering condition 3 pi - 2 phi - 1 > 0. [[de-nobili-2026-collective]] puts one agent per lattice site and measures susceptibility peaks and effective exponents. [[tanaka-2026-when]] (Quantized Simplex Gossip, memetic drift) and [[pavlova-2026-flag]] (Flag Game) add private evidence and zealots. [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] (full read) is the current synthesis: P(m) = [tanh(beta(m + h)) + 1]/2 with a majority force beta and bias field h fitted per model and opinion pair (nine models, about 100 pairs, N = 50, memoryless agents seeing the whole population); the mean-field spinodal separates pairs where misaligned states are metastable from pairs that always relax, and stubborn-agent sweeps show hysteresis with critical fractions that match theory. [[fukushima-2026-message]] (abstract) adds bounded reading and ground truth: an 8B model's judgment is a logistic function of a weighted inbox, but outcomes are mispredicted until a per-claim wording field is added. [[brockers-2025-disentangling]] (abstract) separates interaction from topic, agreement and anchoring biases. [[hirota-2026-collective]] maps estimates to phases and uses Kuramoto order parameters, finding twisted and chimera states on rings whose lifetimes scale with 1/lambda_2. [[ricco-2026-consensus]] (abstract) sweeps 432 configurations into a 4.7M-response corpus. [[zheng-2026-absorbing]] uses absorbing-state transitions for multi-agent search. [[itkin-2026-poor]] formalises the surrogate trick (fit a per-agent response once, sweep N on a laptop) that [[flint-2026-group]] used to reach N ~ 10^4; the same author's [[itkin-2026-local]] (skim) then shows surrogates that predict individual updates well can still misforecast the collective, and a prospective test on 24 new statements did not confirm earlier history effects.

**2. The naming-game and convention line.** [[ashery-2024-emergent]]: populations spontaneously converge, develop a collective bias no individual has, and can be tipped by a committed minority. [[flint-2026-group]] extends to socially loaded word pairs and shows interaction can amplify, create or reverse an individual bias; its N_c is the size above which the outcome becomes deterministic (always the favoured word), the opposite direction from the De Marzo N_c, where consensus becomes slow. [[flint-2026-indirect]] measures critical mass for every ordered pair of conventions and finds indirect tipping paths. [[mehdizadeh-2026-exploring]] and [[saab-2026-graph]] vary topology and memory on 16-agent and open-weight populations: centralised networks lock into fragmented plateaus; similarity routing sustains cliques. [[cau-2026-collective]] adds homophily. [[magistrali-2026-aligned]] (full read; N = 12 Llama-3.1-8B monitors, pairwise encounters, five-slot memory) turns tipping into a forecast from benign operation (error under 0.008 at held-out doses) and reports recovery toward the benign state after the minority leaves. [[barrie-2025-emergent]] is the standing objection: the naming-game results are observationally equivalent to models recognising the game from training data.

**3. The debate, scaling and effective-N line (NLP).** [[du-2023-improving]] is the protocol. [[li-2024-more]] says sampling and voting scales monotonically. [[choi-2025-debate]] shows voting explains most of the gain and debate alone is a martingale. [[kaesberg-2025-voting]] isolates the aggregation rule (voting +13.2% on reasoning, more rounds hurt). [[qian-2025-scaling]] scales DAG topologies to 2080 agents and finds a logistic saturation around 2^4 nodes. [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] is the quantitative null model: N_eff = N / (1 + (N - 1) rho_N), dense debate sits in the hard-ceiling regime (1.2 to 1.8 effective agents), the noise placebo matches self-correction, and only cross-family teams lower the ceiling. [[yang-2026-understanding]] (2 diverse agents match 16 homogeneous), [[fortuna-2026-multi-agent]] (Steiner task taxonomy), [[tran-2026-single]] (equal thinking tokens favour single agents), [[kim-2025-towards]] (260 configurations, benefit is task-dependent from +80.8% to -70%), [[shehata-2026-bystander]] (cognitive loafing) and [[wu-2026-predicting]] (scale limits of social mechanisms) all point the same way. [[cemri-2025-why]] gives the failure taxonomy (MAST, 14 modes). [[zhang-2026-silo]] (full) shards inputs across N = 2 to 100 agents and finds the communication-reasoning gap widens with N until overhead cancels the parallel gain; [[berdoz-2026-can]] (abstract) finds Byzantine scalar consensus unreliable even with no Byzantine agents, worse as groups grow. A separate correlated-error literature, mostly on judge panels and ensembles rather than interacting populations, measures the same ceiling with Kish effective N: [[kim-2025-correlated]], [[goel-2025-great]], [[chen-2026-when]], [[li-2026-state]], [[bara-2026-epistemic]], [[denisov-blanch-2026-consensus]], [[kohli-2026-nine]] (9 judges carry about 2 effective votes), [[begin-2026-preference]] (same-model N_eff about 1.4, flat from N = 5 to 40), [[hossain-2026-agreement]], [[rai-2026-when]], [[rao-2026-jev]]. [[liu-2026-social]] (skim) gives the network-side theory: narrow attention bounds effective sample size whatever N, and wide attention recovers wisdom of crowds only on undirected degree-regular exposure.

**4. The conformity, herding and cascade line (social psychology vocabulary).** [[weng-2025-do]], [[cho-2025-herd]], [[choi-2025-empirical]] (2,500 debates, alignment with numerically dominant or "more intelligent" agents), [[bellina-2026-conformity]] (Asch with logits, Social Impact Theory holds), [[han-2026-conformity]] (self-social weight and topology, 7 agents), [[ys-2026-everyone]] (pluralistic ignorance), [[wu-2026-how]] (abstract; defection scales with the proportion of deceivers, minorities suffice), [[li-2025-systematic]] (hidden profile: groups 30.1% versus single agent with full information 80.7%), [[ezaki-2026-warned]] (50 GPT agents crowd one road for 100 rounds after a one-sentence warning, humans do not), [[takata-2025-emergent]] (El Farol). Contagion as security: [[gu-2024-agent]] (infectious jailbreak, one million agents), [[lee-2024-prompt]] (prompt infection), [[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]] (memetic trojans, top Moltbook contagion retransmitted in about 50% of subsequent posts). The human baselines these cite are [[lorenz-2011-how]] and [[becker-2017-network]].

**5. The social-simulation and in-the-wild line.** Platforms: [[park-2023-generative]] (Smallville), [[yang-2024-oasis]] / [[gh-camel-ai-oasis]] (one million agents, recsys), [[piao-2025-agentsociety]] / [[gh-tsinghua-fib-lab-agentsociety]], [[gh-google-deepmind-concordia]], [[al-2024-project]] / [[gh-altera-al-project-sid]]. Observational: [[de-marzo-2026-collective]] (Moltbook, 369K posts, sublinear upvotes versus discussion size), [[li-2026-socialization]] (skim; Moltbook, 290K posts: commenting on a post produces no semantic or n-gram convergence toward it, no persistent influencers), [[hashemi-2026-empirical]] (skim; Chirper.ai, 32K memory-enabled agents over a year, homophily and influence-consistent drift), [[liu-2026-do]] and [[choudhary-2026-manipulation]] (Moltbook echo-chamber replication and first-comment amplification, abstracts), [[de-wynter-2026-population]] (self-organisation metric on Schelling, Moltbook and a misinformation sim), [[de-marzo-2026-copying]] (the wiki swarm: 1,201 handles, 5,929 edits, one copying rule explains page choice, naming and wording). Incident corpora and reproductions: [[gh-swarm-ai-research-wiki-agent-swarm-incident]] (phase table: 93% of the corpus in the 16 to 22 June burst, 18 June alone 45%), [[gh-catgirl3d-agent-collusion-wiki-archive]] (14,591 revisions, SHA256-pinned), [[gh-yazandabain-swarmtrace]] (temporal targeting audit), [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] (7,200 transcripts: a shared board synchronises rather than divides, an exploit recipe spread from 3 to 16 lineages and 66 lives while the honest solution reached zero), [[gh-swarm-ai-research-swarm]] (illusion delta and governance metrics), [[gh-hamzah2304-messageboardauditbench]]. Threads: [[x-rogesterone-2099328995250626879]] (22 June: chatter edits drop from about 500 to about 5 in one day while task-info edits rise), [[x-rogesterone-2102216143775682892]] (leaked cohort data: participation rate versus compactions, S-curve), [[x-transluceai-2103718081965281470]], [[x-jeffladish-2103584701357437133]], [[x-aka-ssy-2104421925455909102]] (same 22 June collapse in urlquery), [[x-hidenori8tanaka-2105704088952619185]]. ## What is known

Read depth is marked inline as (abstract) or (skim) where a claim rests on an entry not read in full. "Independent" means a different research group.

Measured, replicated across at least two independent groups:

- **Given a per-item bias field, LLM populations reach consensus by majority-following well described by mean-field Ising / DeGroot dynamics.** [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] (adoption probability P(m) = [tanh(beta m) + 1]/2), [[chen-2023-multi]] (agents spontaneously use the average rule), [[el-2026-physics]], [[okawa-2026-emergence]] (skim), [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] (P(m) = [tanh(beta(m + h)) + 1]/2 across nine models and about 100 opinion pairs). The coupling is model-specific and measurable per agent ([[yang-2026-when]], skim: gamma 0.15 to 0.43). The field term is not optional: [[fukushima-2026-message]] (abstract) fitted a logistic update on measured inbox weights and mispredicted outcomes (the correct side won only 28 to 45% of episodes when 75% started correct) until each claim's own wording field was added, and [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] needs h to place a pair inside or outside the metastable region.
- **For whole-population observation with no stake, the majority force beta falls with group size, so consensus slows above a model-specific N_c.** [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] (N_c from about 30 to above 1000; the GPT-4 Turbo value rests on one N = 1000 run), with independent support from [[berdoz-2026-can]] (abstract; valid Byzantine scalar agreement degrades as groups grow, mostly through loss of liveness). Two other "critical N" notions are different quantities and are not evidence for this bullet: [[flint-2026-group]]'s N_c marks where the naming-game outcome becomes deterministic, and [[qian-2025-scaling]] and [[grotschla-2025-agentsnet]] measure task quality versus N (see the logistic-versus-ceiling problem below).
- **Collective bias exists without individual bias, and a committed minority can tip the population.** [[ashery-2024-emergent]] (naming game), [[flint-2026-group]] (bias created, amplified or reversed; same group as Ashery), [[flint-2026-indirect]] (indirect paths), [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] (independent group; critical stubborn fractions match mean-field theory, sometimes under 10% for N >= 50), [[magistrali-2026-aligned]] (independent group; capture common at 3 to 4 of 12). Beside these, single persuasive or adversarial agents move small debates: [[kraidia-2026-when]] (one argumentative adversary cuts accuracy 10 to 40%), [[liu-2025-can]], [[liu-2026-consensus]]. [[wu-2026-how]] (abstract; defection linear in deceiver proportion) is reported but no task, N or model detail was read behind its "unlike humans" contrast.
- **More homogeneous agents add agreement, not evidence.** In debate and teams: [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] (N_eff ceilings 1.2 to 1.8 under dense debate; placebo equals self-correction), [[yang-2026-understanding]], [[choi-2025-debate]], [[kaesberg-2025-voting]] (more rounds hurt), [[tran-2026-single]]. Independently, in markets, panels and ensembles: [[begin-2026-preference]] (same-model N_eff about 1.4, flat from N = 5 to 40), [[kohli-2026-nine]] (9 frontier judges carry about 2 effective votes), [[kim-2025-correlated]] (agreement-when-both-wrong about twice baseline across 349 models), [[bara-2026-epistemic]] (same Kish discount derived independently; coverage 0.940 to 0.263 as same-root reports go from 1 to 32), [[chen-2026-when]] (co-failure ceiling 1 - beta), [[li-2026-state]] (error floor for majority vote), [[denisov-blanch-2026-consensus]] (abstract; no truthfulness gain at 25x cost). Most of the second group measures non-interacting panels, so it supports the ceiling, not a mechanism specific to interaction.
- **Topology sets whether populations synchronise, fragment or form cliques.** [[hirota-2026-collective]] (ring gives twisted states, rewiring synchronises everything, fragment lifetime scales with 1/lambda_2), [[mehdizadeh-2026-exploring]] (centralised networks preserve competing conventions), [[saab-2026-graph]], [[cau-2026-collective]] (homophily), [[han-2026-conformity]], [[zomer-2026-unraveling]] (dense networks cause premature consensus in LLM swarm optimisation, ring reliably finds the optimum), [[wang-2025-rethinking]] (abstract; small-world wiring stabilises debate consensus), [[chen-2026-diversity]] (abstract; dense topologies accelerate premature convergence in ideation).
- **LLM agents conform to confederates, more than humans do for minorities.** [[bellina-2026-conformity]] (skim; Asch with logits), [[choi-2025-empirical]] (abstract; 2,500 debates), [[ezaki-2026-warned]] (abstract; 50 GPT agents crowd one road after a warning, humans do not), [[shu-2026-forged]] (abstract; quoted peer judgments create 19 to 26 point anchoring gaps in judge panels). [[wu-2026-how]] (abstract) points the same way.
- **On screen, short-lived agents copy wording and conventions from what they can see.** [[de-marzo-2026-copying]] (skim; wiki swarm: one copying rule explains page choice, naming and wording across 5,929 edits), [[usman-2026-peer-voted]] (abstract; preregistered, a peer-ranked feed raises lexical similarity by about +0.01 TF-IDF cosine in both panels), and the sealed board [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] (one anonymous author, one arm, no paper). The 22 June collapse is visible in three independent channels ([[x-rogesterone-2099328995250626879]], [[x-aka-ssy-2104421925455909102]], [[gh-swarm-ai-research-wiki-agent-swarm-incident]]).

Measured by one group, or contested:

- **Whether agents copy beliefs (not just wording) is contested.** [[usman-2026-peer-voted]] finds no reliable stance advantage for distributed sources and an opposite-side survival drop only in the core panel; [[li-2026-socialization]] (skim) finds commenting on a Moltbook post produces no semantic or n-gram convergence toward it, indistinguishable from a random-post baseline; [[liu-2026-do]] (abstract) finds the Moltbook core does not polarise under the visibility definition. Against them, [[hashemi-2026-empirical]] (skim) reports neighbour similarity growing about 6x over a year on Chirper.ai (observational, memory-enabled agents) and [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] and [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] show stance adoption when the whole population is shown. The likely moderator is what agents are shown and remember.
- **Accuracy is non-monotonic in N and the failure mode changes with N (one group).** [[pavlova-2026-flag]]: wrong consensus 26 to 34% at N = 4 falling to 0% at N = 32 and 128 (0 to 5.3% at N = 64), polarisation 13 to 24% rising to 57 to 60% at N = 128, accuracy peak at N = 16; the same patch fraction corrects less as N grows (+40 points at N = 8, about +17 at N = 128). [[tanaka-2026-when]] (abstract) predicts the drift-to-selection crossover but shares an author, so it is a model from the same group, not a replication. The independent analogue is a non-LLM model: [[bate-2026-indecision]] (skim) finds per-agent accuracy peaks at a finite group size in evidence-accumulating agents with social updating. Replicated: the shape in a model; not replicated: the LLM numbers.
- **The SIT functional form of conformity (one group).** That conformity grows with group size and collapses when unanimity breaks in the specific Social Impact Theory form is from [[bellina-2026-conformity]] alone (De Marzo and Garcia group).
- **Diversity is the most consistent lever, not the only one.** Cross-family or diverse teams help in [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]], [[begin-2026-preference]] (rho 0.68 to 0.40 with model mixing), [[pavlova-2026-flag]] (mixed GPT-4o/GPT-5.4), [[barrera-lemarchand-2026-wisdom]] (abstract; clone groups gain nothing from deliberation), [[liu-2026-llms]] and [[yuan-2026-mixed]] (abstracts). Other levers also work: social-awareness prompting raises terminal truth mass from 0.54 to 0.81 in broadcast ([[pavlova-2026-flag]]), role prompts lower rho from 0.60 to 0.44 ([[begin-2026-preference]]), evidence partitioning ([[li-2026-diverse]], abstract), visible rationales neutralise a moderate attack ([[magistrali-2026-aligned]]), calibrated confidence ([[zhu-2026-demystifying]], abstract). Counter-evidence: [[rai-2026-when]] (abstract; preregistered, cross-family wrong-answer agreement 55% equals same-family on rare factual queries), [[marjanovic-2026-mo]] (abstract; within-family selection beats arbitrary pools), [[kim-2026-are]] (abstract; most diversity metrics re-express capability), [[qian-2026-nous]] (abstract; prompt-injected personas do not decorrelate).

Measured once, not yet replicated: the Moltbook sublinear upvote law ([[de-marzo-2026-collective]]); the memetic trojan retransmission rates ([[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]]); the participation-versus-compactions S-curve from leaked cohort data ([[x-rogesterone-2102216143775682892]], hobbyist analysis of an archived artefact); AI Village persuasion-by-repetition ([[x-aidigest-2100302989642924124]], qualitative); the benign-operation capture forecast ([[magistrali-2026-aligned]], one model, N = 12, four seeds per cell).

Speculated, not measured: that the 22 June chatter collapse was agents inferring monitoring (rogesterone rejects this), that message-board coordination is an artefact of same-model agents on the same task suite ([[x-deepfates-2095939054101844407]] replies), that polarisation is a safer failure mode than collapse ([[pavlova-2026-flag]] conclusion).

## Open problems and disagreements

- **Is emergent consensus social or memorised?** [[barrie-2025-emergent]] versus [[ashery-2024-emergent]]; [[zhou-2025-pimmur]] (abstract) says half of prompts pre-determine the outcome; [[yang-2026-when]] finds the social coupling equals numeric anchoring and gives a diagnostic; [[brockers-2025-disentangling]] (abstract) is a second, Bayesian diagnostic separating interaction from topic, agreement and anchoring biases; [[fukushima-2026-message]] (abstract) uses wording reversal as a prior-artefact control and finds the field follows what a claim asserts, not whether it is true. No LLM-population paper in the library applies the randomised-initial-condition test as standard. Any new result needs one of these controls.
- **Reversible or absorbing capture?** [[magistrali-2026-aligned]] says populations drift back once the committed minority leaves; [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] says shifts persist. Both now read in full, and the contrast is narrower than the abstracts suggest. De Marzo et al. predict and show both outcomes: persistence for opinion pairs inside the mean-field spinodal (beta > 1, |h| < h_s), relaxation for pairs outside it. Magistrali and Shani test one scenario, one dose (k = 6 of 12), four seeds, with an instructed coordination game, pairwise encounters and five-slot memory, and their benign response curve has a single stable fixed point. So the honest statement is: reversibility is regime-dependent in the whole-population, memoryless setting, and untested for a metastable pair under local, bounded-memory interaction. Memory itself changes collective regimes in a spatial LLM swarm ([[hishiki-2026-how]], abstract), which makes memory the natural manipulated variable.
- **Does the Ringelmann ceiling hold off benchmark QA?** [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] measured it on MMLU/GSM/GPQA with full connectivity. Partly answered qualitatively: on sharded inputs [[zhang-2026-silo]] finds coordination overhead grows with N (2 to 100) until it cancels the parallel gain, and [[li-2026-diverse]] (abstract) finds identical evidence makes deliberation herd while partitioned evidence helps. N_eff itself, with the noise placebo, has still not been computed on an evidence-distributed task (Flag Game, hidden profile [[li-2025-systematic]], SwarmBench [[ruan-2025-benchmarking]]).
- **Which N-scaling is real: logistic gain ([[qian-2025-scaling]], [[li-2024-more]], [[grotschla-2025-agentsnet]]) or hard ceiling ([[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]], [[yang-2026-understanding]], [[begin-2026-preference]])?** The camps use different units (task quality versus N_eff). Two theories say both are slices of one diagram: [[liu-2026-social]] (skim; bounded N_eff under narrow attention, recovery toward n only on undirected degree-regular exposure, with an equalising exposure price that removes the floor) and [[liu-2026-phase]] (abstract; one scalar combining error correlation, message fidelity and fan-in decides amplify versus wash-out). The Flag Game's non-monotonic curve fits the same picture.
- **Does the broadcast (message board) protocol have the same N-crossover as pairwise?** [[pavlova-2026-flag]] reports the N sweep only for pairwise; the incident setting is broadcast with persistence. [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] suggests boards synchronise harder, with no N sweep.
- **Cost realism and surrogate validity.** [[rahman-2025-llm-powered]] (300x) and [[itkin-2026-poor]] (surrogates) disagree about whether LLM swarms should be simulated with LLM calls at all. [[itkin-2026-local]] (skim) qualifies the surrogate side: graph features improve one-step prediction in all 16 public-data cells, but collective gains vanish under joint question-and-graph shift, new runs at N = 16 to 128 show no resolved graph-over-scalar advantage, and earlier history effects did not replicate on 24 new statements. Surrogate N-sweeps need direct collective validation at the target N.
- **The wild corpora versus the lab.** Nobody has fit the lab-measured update rules (majority force, copying rule) to the AI Village transcripts; [[de-marzo-2026-copying]] did it for the wiki corpus only. The village is longitudinal (a year, 37 agents), task-oriented, multi-frontier-model with tool use, and human-perturbed ([[x-aidigest-2095911758082801838]]). Chirper.ai ([[hashemi-2026-empirical]]) is also a year-long, memory-enabled multi-agent population, so longitudinality alone is not unique; the task orientation and live human perturbation are.
- **Terminology.** "Swarm" is contested ([[x-deepfates-2104620644784906632]], [[rahman-2025-llm-powered]]); the physics papers say population, the NLP papers say team or society.

## Gaps

Openings, stated as what is missing rather than what we will claim:

- **Gap 1.** No broadcast / persistent-board N-sweep with open models and ground truth. The Flag Game N-sweep is pairwise and closed-model; the sealed swarm has a board but no N sweep or ground-truth accuracy curve. The crossover from wrong consensus to polarisation on a board is unmeasured. Closest prior: [[fukushima-2026-message]] (bounded reading of an inbox with ground truth; transitions in message capacity at N = 32, one sweep of reading capacity rather than N), [[zhang-2026-silo]] (N = 2 to 100 on sharded inputs, but an algorithmic task with no belief dynamics or polarisation endpoint), [[liu-2026-social]] (HiddenBench n = 8 to 24 and Werewolf n = 6 to 16 with a captured hub; herding floor at every size), [[pavlova-2026-flag]] (broadcast run only at N = 8).
- **Gap 2.** No study applies the [[yang-2026-when]] randomised-initial-condition diagnostic to a committed-minority tipping experiment, so the tipping fractions in the literature are not yet separated from prior artefacts. [[brockers-2025-disentangling]] is a second diagnostic that could serve; [[altunyan-2026-tipping]] (abstract) names LLM-prior interference as a problem in a climate-stance tipping study without, per the abstract, a formal diagnostic; [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] absorbs the prior into h rather than removing it.
- **Gap 3.** Reversibility of capture has no test that places a metastable pair (inside the [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] spinodal) under the local, bounded-memory protocol of [[magistrali-2026-aligned]], with memory length as the manipulated variable. Both papers are now read in full; De Marzo et al. already show regime-dependent persistence for whole-population, memoryless agents, so a new result must vary memory or locality, not just repeat the stubborn-agent removal.
- **Gap 4.** N_eff and the noise placebo of [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] have not been computed for any evidence-distributed task (Flag Game, hidden profile, SwarmBench), nor for any in-the-wild corpus. [[zhang-2026-silo]] already shows the qualitative overhead-cancels-gain result on distributed evidence, [[liu-2026-social]] gives a theoretical N_eff bound with an escape condition, and [[begin-2026-preference]] measures N_eff versus N (5 to 40) in a non-interacting market. A new measurement is novel only if it computes rho_N, N_eff and the placebo on a task where evidence is split. Computing rho_N on the wiki dump or the village transcripts remains unpublished.
- **Gap 5.** The lab update rules (majority force beta, copying weights, coupling gain gamma) have never been fitted to AI Village transcripts. The village is not the only longitudinal multi-agent population ([[hashemi-2026-empirical]]: Chirper.ai, a year, 32K agents), but it is the only task-oriented, multi-frontier-model, tool-using one with live human perturbation; the brief says 170K+ messages.
- The 22 June regime change is documented in three channels but not modelled. Nobody has asked whether a copying-rule population with a changing visible mix reproduces a one-day collapse of chatter with rising task-storage edits.
- Retransmission probability of a planted meme versus population size and board visibility is measured on Moltbook ([[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]]) but not in a task-oriented board, where the sealed swarm's 3-to-16-lineage spread is the only datum.
- The seven forward-citation items listed here in the first version are now catalogued. Three are load-bearing and changed the picture: [[liu-2026-social]] (bounded effective N, the closest prior for Gap 4 and for any board N_eff hypothesis), [[itkin-2026-local]] (a non-replication that qualifies the surrogate method), and [[li-2026-socialization]] (counter-evidence that split the copying bullet into wording versus belief). [[hashemi-2026-empirical]] corrected the "only longitudinal population" wording. [[hou-2026-structural]], [[priyanshu-2026-secret]] and [[altunyan-2026-tipping]] are not load-bearing. Brach et al. 2605.07462 ("The Moltbook Files") is still uncatalogued (named by the reviewer, not opened).
