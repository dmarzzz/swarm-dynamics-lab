# Surveys

A survey is a prior-art scan of one topic or question, and it is the gate in front of every hypothesis.
`python3 scripts/lab.py gate <survey-id>` lists what a survey still lacks. The rules are in
[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#the-prior-art-gate).

Reviews of surveys, hypotheses and experiments are in [`reviews/`](reviews/README.md).
