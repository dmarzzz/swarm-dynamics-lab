## Scope

In scope: any setting where many agents (people, nodes, robots, software agents, LLM agents, block builders or searchers) interact and one principal can present several identities. The survey follows one question through every setting: what does an identity cost, and does a defence bound the number of identities a principal can hold or the influence each identity has? The settings are peer-to-peer overlays and social graphs, robot swarms and networked control, LLM agent collectives, mechanism design (false-name-proofness), cryptographic credentials and rate limiting, and MEV infrastructure built or studied by Flashbots.

Collusion among genuinely distinct agents is in scope only where the sources contrast it with Sybil attacks, because the two need different defences [[glynn-2026-wash]]. General multi-agent safety, jailbreaks and prompt injection are out of scope unless the source models an adversary that controls several identities. Classical Byzantine fault tolerance with a fixed, known membership is background: it assumes the Sybil problem is solved [[lamport-1982-byzantine]].

## Landscape

### P2P overlays and social graphs

The founding result is Douceur's: without a logically central certifier, distinct identities can be guaranteed only if all entities have equal resources, all identities are validated at the same time, and vouching thresholds exceed the faulty count [[douceur-2002-sybil]]. His asynchronous-onboarding lemma is the attack that applies to any swarm that admits members one at a time, and Ford restates it for humans as cumulable asynchronous verifications [[ford-2020-identity]]. Three families respond.

Resource testing prices identities. Aspnes et al. show that if identities are priced by puzzle solutions, Byzantine agreement survives while the adversary holds under 1/3 or 1/2 of compute [[aspnes-2005-exposing]]. Gupta et al. generalise this to resource burning, with the goal that honest agents spend asymptotically less than the attacker [[gupta-2020-resource]] [[gupta-2018-proof]], and extend it to churn: Ergo keeps bad identities under 1/6 with honest cost O(sqrt(TJ)+J) [[gupta-2021-bankrupting]], optimal for purge-based schemes [[gupta-2019-resource]]. Structured overlays price or constrain identities directly: certified ids and constrained routing tables [[castro-2002-secure]], puzzle-bound keys and disjoint lookup paths [[baumgart-2007-skademlia]], geometric distinctness certificates [[bazzi-2005-establishment]]. Stake is the deployed form: Algorand sortition is binomial over stake units, so splitting stake across identities gains nothing [[gilad-2017-algorand]], and Casper weights votes by slashable deposit [[buterin-2017-casper]].

Bounding influence rather than identities is the second family. Singh et al. show eclipse works even when Sybils are prevented, and bound it with degree limits enforced by anonymous audit [[singh-2006-eclipse]]. Deployed eclipse attacks on bitcoind [[heilman-2015-eclipse]] and geth [[marcus-2018-low-resource]] are the practical form of a Sybil attack on one agent's view; fixes include test-before-evict, outbound quotas and /24 limits, and [[gh-ethereum-devp2p]] notes that a global per-IP cap is itself an attack vector. Peer scoring in GossipSub is the main deployed reputation-as-influence bound [[vyzovitis-2020-gossipsub]] [[gh-libp2p-specs]].

Social-graph defences are the third family. They bound Sybil influence by the number of attack edges: O(sqrt(n) log n) accepted Sybils per attack edge in SybilGuard [[yu-2006-sybilguard]], O(log n) in SybilLimit [[yu-2008-sybillimit]], bogus votes capped by attack edges in SumUp [[tran-2009-sybil-resilient]], Bayesian inference in SybilInfer [[danezis-2009-sybilinfer]], and SybilRank in production [[cao-2012-aiding]]. Viswanath et al. show these schemes reduce to community detection [[viswanath-2010-analysis]], and Alvisi et al. respond with local white-listing [[alvisi-2013-sok]]. DSybil bounds recommendation loss using track records alone [[yu-2009-dsybil]]. Surveys of the period are [[levine-2006-survey]], [[yu-2011-sybil]] and [[mohaisen-2013-sybil]]. Proof of personhood ties one identity to one body through synchronous pseudonym parties [[ford-2008-offline]] [[borge-2017-proof-of-personhood]]; subjective variants are reviewed in [[siddarth-2020-who]], and the Ghost Trilemma argues that sentience, location and uniqueness cannot all be verified in a decentralised system [[mukherjee-2023-sok]].

### Robot swarms and networked control

Robotics resists Sybils in three ways. Physical identity: Gil et al. give each identity a confidence weight from its Wi-Fi multipath fingerprint and prove bounded influence for spoofed identities on coverage control [[gil-2015-guaranteeing]], extended to consensus [[gil-2018-resilient]] [[renganathan-2017-spoof]], to backscatter tags [[huang-2019-lightweight]] and to wireless channels generally [[xiao-2009-channel]], and reviewed in [[gil-2023-physicality]]. Newsome et al. had already framed Sybil attacks in sensor networks with radio resource testing [[newsome-2004-sybil]]. Trust side channels: with stochastic trust observations, consensus converges almost surely even when malicious agents exceed half the connectivity [[yemini-2021-characterizing]] [[yemini-2022-resilient]], tested in hardware under a Sybil majority [[cavorsi-2024-exploiting]]; sharing neighbours' opinions cuts detection to a constant number of rounds [[mallmann-trenn-2021-crowd]] [[cavorsi-2023-dynamic]]. Economic scarcity: blockchain deposits in robot swarms [[strobel-2018-managing]] [[strobel-2020-blockchain]] [[strobel-2023-robot]] [[castello-ferrer-2021-following]], reviewed in [[dorigo-2024-blockchain]] and [[pena-queralta-2023-blockchain]].

Beneath these sits resilient control that assumes bounded identities: W-MSR and (r,s)-robustness [[leblanc-2013-resilient]], resilient consensus and flocking [[saldana-2017-resilient]] [[saulnier-2017-resilient]], adaptive trust for coverage [[pierson-2016-adaptive]], and signed accusations with maximum matching [[wardega-2023-byzantine]], which assumes a central identity issuer. Taxonomies and surveys are [[prorok-2021-beyond]], [[liao-2024-survey]] and [[wang-2023-resilient]]. Physics-based trust in traffic networks [[shoukry-2018-sybil]] and multi-robot exploration [[sagwal-2026-detecting]] are newer applications. [[yan-2025-reliability]] is the only review found that bridges swarm robots and AI agents.

### LLM agent collectives

The strongest source here redefines the problem. An extra report is a Sybil if it adds no conditional information about the true state, however well authenticated its agent is [[bara-2026-epistemic]]. Byzantine-robust LLM consensus papers bound the adversary by identity count or graph robustness without admission control, so they assume Sybil resistance rather than provide it [[jo-2025-byzantine]] [[chen-2024-blockagents]] [[luo-2025-weighted]] [[lee-2026-robust]] [[el-mir-2026-byzantine]]; fault and adversary studies with a fixed adversary count are [[amayuelas-2024-multiagent]] and [[huang-2024-resilience]]. Reputation laundering in agent routing costs one fabricated episode [[xia-2026-when]]. Marketplace testbeds vary the number of Sybil sellers [[karten-2026-agent]] or support agentic markets generally [[bansal-2025-magentic]].

The identity side is mostly proposals: instance IDs [[chan-2024-ids]], visibility and infrastructure for agents [[chan-2024-visibility]] [[chan-2025-infrastructure]], authenticated delegation [[south-2025-authenticated]], personhood credentials [[adler-2024-personhood]], protocol trust models for ERC-8004-style registries [[hu-2025-inter-agent]], identity-bound human challenges [[maleki-2026-human]], Sybil-resistant service discovery [[shi-2025-sybil]], and the individuation question of how to count AIs [[arbel-2026-how]]. Gossip-based agent networks bring the P2P line into this setting [[laws-2026-panda]] [[zhang-2026-distributed]]. In the wild, social bots and LLM botnets [[ferrara-2016-rise]] [[yang-2023-anatomy]] [[feng-2024-what]] and CAPTCHA breaking [[plesner-2024-breaking]] show that content checks fail. The converse case, distinct identities acting as one bloc, is collusion [[motwani-2024-secret]] [[nakamura-2026-colosseum]] [[fish-2024-algorithmic]] [[tailor-2025-audit]]. Reviews: [[de-witt-2025-open]], [[hammond-2025-multi]], [[yang-2026-sok]], [[yu-2025-survey]], [[zhu-2026-blockchain]].

### Mechanism design

False-name-proofness starts with Yokoo et al.: no efficient false-name-proof combinatorial auction exists [[yokoo-2004-effect]]. Conitzer and Yokoo list four escapes: costly identities, verifying some identities, social-graph cuts, and anonymity-proof solution concepts [[conitzer-2010-using]] [[ohta-2008-anonymity]]. The line covers voting [[waggoner-2012-evaluating]] [[aziz-2011-false]], matching [[todo-2013-false]], reputation [[cheng-2005-sybilproof]], referral and query networks [[babaioff-2012-bitcoin]] [[drucker-2012-simpler]] [[chen-2013-sybil]] [[zhang-2023-collusion]] [[chen-2022-sybil]], restaking [[chitra-2025-sybil]] and public goods [[buterin-2019-flexible]] [[ethresearch-2023-collusion]] [[mazorra-2023-optimality]]. The two results closest to agents come from authors affiliated with Flashbots: the collapse of Sybil-proof allocation to the second price auction [[pan-2024-sybil]], and the price of identity and the power of credible commitments [[mazorra-2023-cost]]. Attribution among sources and agents [[patel-2025-maxshapley]], airdrop farming [[messias-2023-airdrops]] [[yaish-2024-tierdrop]] [[liu-2022-fighting]] and Sybil-proofness of decentralisation metrics [[yaish-2026-inequality]] are applied variants.

### Cryptographic credentials

The literature splits Sybil defence with anonymity into an admission cost and a rate bound per identity. RLN states the split explicitly [[barrywhitehat-2019-semaphore]] [[taheri-boshrooyeh-2022-privacy]]. Clone-resistant and k-times credentials [[camenisch-2006-how]], decentralised credentials [[garman-2013-decentralized]], blacklisting [[tsang-2011-nymble]] [[henry-2011-formalizing]] and zk-creds [[rosenberg-2023-zk-creds]] give per-context pseudonyms and per-epoch limits. Privacy Pass [[davidson-2018-privacy]] [[davidson-2024-privacy]] and its descendants [[kreuter-2020-anonymous]] [[chu-2023-security]] [[durak-2024-non]] [[karantaidou-2024-blind]] [[chairattana-apirom-2025-everlasting]] [[chase-2014-algebraic]] [[yun-2026-anonymous]] address token pooling and quotas. Tor uses proof of work and anonymous tokens for onion-service DoS [[torproject-2020-first]] [[torproject-2021-res]]. Credentials can also bound where Sybils act, as in mixnet routing [[kleinstein-2025-sybil]]. For agents, the designs are ZK API usage credits [[crapis-2026-zk]], anonymous credentials for ERC-8004 agents [[ethresearch-2026-anonymous]], and attributable operator signatures with no quota [[cloudflare-2025-forget]] [[cloudflare-2025-age]]. Pay-per-request without real cost fails: [[ling-2026-how]] and [[li-2026-five]] measure and attack x402.

### MEV and Flashbots

Flashbots mostly prices actions rather than identifying actors: per-bundle payments, explicit auctions and upfront fees [[collective-2024-dealing]] [[flashbots-2022-order]] [[resnick-2023-contingent]], with measurement showing spam bots used over half of gas while paying under 10% of fees [[flashbots-2025-mev]], and a model where equilibrium spam is independent of identities [[mazorra-2026-timing]]. The most concrete production Sybil defence is BuilderNet's refund identity constraint [[buildernet-2025-refunds]] over the marginal-contribution refund rule [[collective-2024-refund]]. The relay design debates name the attacks: newcomer-queue flooding [[flashbots-2021-proposal]], stake splitting [[flashbots-2022-relay]] [[collective-2022-decentralized]], and the relay code attaches demotion to an operator rather than a key [[gh-flashbots-mev-boost-relay]]. Private order flow uses subsampling for unidentifiable users [[passerat-palmbach-2025-differentially]] [[collective-2023-mev-share]].

TEE attestation is the newer identity anchor and the weakest one. Attestation does not separate operators or instances [[collective-2024-portrait]] [[zhou-2025-dstack]]; TEE.fail and WireTap extract attestation keys with under $1,000 of hardware [[chuang-2025-teefail]] [[seto-2025-wiretap]] [[ethresearch-2026-physical]]; BuilderNet responded with allowlists [[collective-2025-why]] [[gh-flashbots-builder-hub]] [[eigenphi-2025-buildernet]]; Proof of Cloud binds identity to a vetted chassis [[rezabek-2025-proof]]. Key encumbrance makes identity rental undetectable [[austgen-2023-complete]] [[austgen-2024-liquefaction]] [[buterin-2023-what]], countered by Complete Knowledge proofs [[kelkar-2024-complete]], while an agent that proves its own autonomy is encumbered by design [[malhotra-2024-setting]] [[sun-2024-tee]]. SUAVE design threads trade stake and whitelists for attestation [[quintus-2023-problems]] [[charbonneau-2023-suave]] [[miller-2024-sirrah]] [[gh-flashbots-flashtestations]] [[flashbots-2023-block]] [[collective-2024-tee]].

From the wider Ethereum research forum: Dave lets one honest party with a laptop beat any number of Sybil claims with logarithmic delay [[coutinho-de-paula-2025-dave]] [[coutinho-de-paula-2024-dave]]; anti-correlation penalties price hidden common control [[buterin-2024-supporting]] [[obol-2026-deployment]]; linear rewards lose to identity splitting even with slashing [[bahrani-2026-capacity]]; AUCIL is individually incentive-compatible but not Sybil-proof [[nag-2026-sybil]] [[neuder-2024-block]]; open gossip with free identities can only resist spam by toll, bond or trust [[ankushin-2026-public]]; stake-backed anonymous credentials give one unlinkable identity per validator [[kadianakis-2023-proof]] [[alpturer-2026-aetherweave]].

## What is known

Measured:

- Social-graph detectors work when attack edges are scarce. SybilRank reached about 90% precision in production at Tuenti [[cao-2012-aiding]]. SybilSCAR and SybilBelief reached AUC 1 on the bundled Facebook example with a copied Sybil region and 1,000 attack edges, run in this lab [[gh-binghuiwang-sybildetection]]; this is a best case.
- Eclipse is cheap where identities are free: 4,600 bots or 32 /24 blocks eclipse bitcoind with at least 85% probability [[heilman-2015-eclipse]], and two IPs eclipse geth [[marcus-2018-low-resource]].
- Physical identity works for one radio per attacker: 96% detection and 3 to 6% false positives on hardware, with positions within centimetres of optimal under heavy spoofing [[gil-2015-guaranteeing]]. Under a Sybil majority with trust observations, detection error is about 29 to 30% [[cavorsi-2024-exploiting]].
- Deposits defeat Sybil spam in a simulated robot swarm that breaks both LCP and W-MSR with a single attacker; the attacker's wealth, not its identity count, decides success [[strobel-2020-blockchain]].
- In LLM agent experiments, naive coverage fell from 0.940 to 0.263 as reports from one evidence source rose from 1 to 32, and same-model agents had correlated errors (gamma 0.719) [[bara-2026-epistemic]]. Reputation laundering raised routing regret from 0 to 0.94 on 14 AppWorld agents [[xia-2026-when]]. Sybil revenue share rose from under 5% at K = 3 to 10 to 17% at K = 9 in a car market, and an RL-trained buyer reached 92% detection [[karten-2026-agent]]. MG-EigenTrust cut cross-topic disguise-collusion success to 0.0291 from 0.3759 [[zhang-2026-distributed]].
- In the wild: a 1,140-account ChatGPT botnet [[yang-2023-anatomy]], 100% reCAPTCHAv2 solving [[plesner-2024-breaking]], 21.2% of 136.7M x402 settlements on Base fictitious [[ling-2026-how]], two entities behind more than 80% of Base spam [[flashbots-2025-mev]], airdrop farming selling up to 66% of tokens quickly [[messias-2023-airdrops]], and a Hop label set of 14,195 eliminated addresses [[data-hop-sybil-2022]].
- TEE attestation keys extracted in about 45 minutes (SGX) and about 15 minutes (TDX) with under $1,000 of hardware [[seto-2025-wiretap]] [[chuang-2025-teefail]].

Proved:

- Douceur's impossibility [[douceur-2002-sybil]]; no efficient false-name-proof combinatorial auction [[yokoo-2004-effect]]; a single extra identity collapses every symmetric, non-wasteful, incentive-compatible allocation to the second price auction [[pan-2024-sybil]]; equal-split rewards lose welfare of order n under an identity cost [[mazorra-2023-cost]]; referral rewards are Sybil-proof only in equilibrium, not in dominant strategies [[babaioff-2012-bitcoin]]; social-graph acceptance bounds per attack edge [[yu-2006-sybilguard]] [[yu-2008-sybillimit]]; Ergo's churn bound [[gupta-2021-bankrupting]]; Eth2.0 peer scores let peers withhold messages in chosen topics while keeping positive scores [[kumar-2024-formal]].

Inferred by the sources or by the lanes, not measured: that Gil's weight-by-unforgeable-signal design is the best template for LLM swarms (foundations lane); that proving autonomy and proving non-rental conflict (gap-fill inference from [[malhotra-2024-setting]] and [[kelkar-2024-complete]]); that BuilderNet's identity constraint carries over to credit assignment among agents (Flashbots lane).

## Open problems and disagreements

- Count identities or price actions? The P2P and personhood lines try to bound identities [[douceur-2002-sybil]] [[borge-2017-proof-of-personhood]]. The Flashbots corpus, Dave and ZK API credits price actions or tolerate Sybils instead [[collective-2024-dealing]] [[coutinho-de-paula-2025-dave]] [[crapis-2026-zk]]. For agents that copy, split and merge [[arbel-2026-how]], the second view looks more natural, but no source compares the two on the same swarm.
- Is a Sybil an identity or an information source? [[bara-2026-epistemic]] says distinct, authenticated agents can still be Sybils; the credential and personhood lines treat identity as the unit. Content-based dedup tracked wording rather than ancestry in that paper, so the information view lacks a working detector.
- Social-graph defences assume homophily and fail on modular graphs [[viswanath-2010-analysis]]; team-structured agent swarms are modular by design, and agents can form edges with honest agents cheaply. No source tests the graph detectors on agent interaction graphs.
- TEE attestation as identity: Flashbots and dstack rely on it while TEE.fail and WireTap break it under physical custody [[collective-2025-why]] [[chuang-2025-teefail]]. Proof of Cloud narrows the binding to vetted data centres [[rezabek-2025-proof]], which re-centralises admission.
- Identity rental: encumbrance makes renting real identities undetectable [[austgen-2024-liquefaction]], which undercuts personhood credentials for agents [[adler-2024-personhood]] [[buterin-2023-what]]. Complete Knowledge proofs counter it at about 1.5M to 7.6M gas [[kelkar-2024-complete]].
- Sybil versus collusion: Sybil defences do not catch wash-collusion among distinct principals [[glynn-2026-wash]], and Sybil-proofness and collusion-proofness conflict in query networks [[zhang-2023-collusion]].
- What nobody has done (per the coverage notes): vary the number of identities an adversary holds in LLM debate or voting; test the robotics mechanisms on software or LLM agents; measure eclipse or Sybil attacks on a live agent message bus (A2A, ERC-8004 registries, MCP gateways); measure how many agent identities one operator can register under any attestation scheme; measure how often Sybil splitting happens in MEV.

## Gaps

- No experiment varies how many identities an adversary holds in LLM multi-agent debate, voting or consensus, as Douceur's model asks and as [[karten-2026-agent]] does only for a market.
- No source tests physical-identity, trust-side-channel or deposit mechanisms from robotics on software or LLM agent swarms, though [[strobel-2020-blockchain]] and [[yemini-2021-characterizing]] state their guarantees in terms that do not depend on robots.
- No measurement of Sybil or eclipse attacks on a live agent message layer, though GossipSub-based agent swarms exist [[laws-2026-panda]].
- No detector separates replication from independent corroboration in agent reports; [[bara-2026-epistemic]] shows embedding dedup does not.
- No study counts how many agent identities one operator can register under TEE attestation registries [[gh-flashbots-flashtestations]] or agent signature registries [[gh-cloudflare-web-bot-auth]].
- No evaluation of rate-limited, clone-resistant credentials against one principal running many agents [[rosenberg-2023-zk-creds]] [[crapis-2026-zk]].
- The MEV setting has design-level Sybil defences [[buildernet-2025-refunds]] but no public data on how often identity splitting happens.
