## Summary

Deezer reports fully synthetic audio deliveries measured by its internal detection system. The release distinguishes new-upload share from actual listening share and suspected streaming fraud, making clear that rapid content generation does not imply equivalent audience consumption.

## Key claims

- Primary press release 2025-09-11: over 30,000 fully AI tracks per day, more than 28% of daily deliveries. January approximately 10%, April 18%. These are Deezer uploads, not all music platforms.
- Fully synthetic music is approximately 0.5% of streams, and up to 70% of streams to those tracks are called fraudulent. Separate denominators.
- Method: proprietary synthetic-audio signatures/detection, deployed beginning of 2025, with Suno/Udio support and claims of generalization. '100% AI-generated' describes the content category, not 100% sensitivity.
- AI tracks removed from algorithmic recommendations/editorial playlists. No raw labelled audio or uploaded-track corpus released.

## Evidence quality

Primary vendor press release, retrieved as PDF text through Exa after guessed newsroom URL returned 404. No independent detector accuracy supplied. Content and streaming-fraud classification are distinct.

## Relevance to us

An output-prevalence metric for mass synthetic production, not evidence of how many operators or agents produced it. Pair audience and upload denominators in any swarm-impact analysis.
