## Summary

The authors describe agents encountering an exploitable shared resource rule, with some exploiting it and others reporting it. Behavioral variation within one base model does not itself show advantages of model heterogeneity.

## Key claims

The argument motivates ecological questions; it does not certify any HX frontier as novel.

## Evidence quality

Author research essay reporting a 100-agent Gemini 3.1 scenario; underlying paper methods not read here.

## Relevance to us

Distinguish biological interpretation, behavioral variety, and experimentally isolated heterogeneity.
