## Summary

Continuously updated tracker page (last updated 23 June 2026 at access) from NewsGuard, a commercial news-rating company. It reports 3,749 "AI Content Farm" news and information sites identified across 16 languages. A site is listed only if all four criteria hold: clear evidence that a substantial share of content is AI-produced; strong evidence of publication without significant human oversight (for example articles containing chatbot error messages); presentation that a reader could take as human-written news; and no disclosure of AI authorship. Such sites typically have generic names, publish dozens of articles a day, and are funded by programmatic advertising. The page also indexes NewsGuard's monthly audits of leading chatbots' propensity to repeat false claims.

## Key claims

- The count of AI content farm sites keeps rising (the page title updates the number).
- Programmatic advertising gives these farms an economic incentive at scale.

## Evidence quality

Vendor tracker; the full domain list is available only on request, so the count is not independently checkable. The detection criteria rely heavily on leaked chatbot text, which undercounts farms that filter their output. Skimmed: read the header, counts and criteria; did not open the linked audits.

## Relevance to us

A long-running, dated base-rate series for one form of unattended AI agent output on the web (automated news sites), with explicit inclusion criteria. Its dependence on leaked error text is the same weakness noted for social bots ([[yang-2023-anatomy]], [[nbcnews-2024-ai]]).
