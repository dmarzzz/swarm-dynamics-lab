## Summary

Originality's monthly tracker reports a classifier-defined likelihood of partial AI assistance in selected written posts. It does not establish what fraction of all X tweets is synthetic, and its sampling-length filters mean the measurements cannot be substituted for short-tweet prevalence.

## Key claims

- Published 2026-09-30, August 2026 posts except Reddit July due to delay. X: 99/174 randomly selected English posts >=100 words, 56.9% Likely AI. Reddit 173/564 July posts >=100 words, 30.7%.
- LinkedIn August 4-31: 90/119, 75.6%. Facebook 98/166, 59%. Threads 107/162 of 50-99 words, 66%.
- Method: vendor detector's confidence >=50% that a post is at least 15% AI-written. Thus '57% X Likely AI' is neither 57% fully synthetic nor 57% of all X posts.
- Report calls X decrease from July 63% to August 57% '6%'; interpreted as six percentage points, not a six-percent relative decrease.
- Bluesky absent from the report; no dedicated primary Bluesky AI-prevalence estimate was confirmed in targeted searches during this audit. No cross-platform extrapolation.

## Evidence quality

Primary commercial-detector sample with explicit thresholds/counts. Small length-filtered samples and classifier domain calibration constrain generalization. Linked causal attribution to platform policy is speculative.

## Relevance to us

A source of measured sampled proportions, not autonomous-agent or coordination ground truth. Keep selected longform X posts separate from [[yelp-2026-trust]] and ordinary short-form tweets.
