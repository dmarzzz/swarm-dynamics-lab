## Summary

Akamai summarizes its fraud and abuse report using network observations of AI bots, grouping training, assistant and search identities. Commerce dominates measured volumes and most healthcare AI bot traffic is scraping, but the report introduction does not establish a global malicious-agent prevalence.

## Key claims

- Published 2025-11-04. Reports AI-powered bot traffic increased 300% over the preceding year; exact start/end dates of that comparison are not specified on this page.
- Commerce received more than 25 billion AI bot requests during a two-month observation period; the geographic comparison explicitly covers July to August 2025. North America accounts for 54.9%, EMEA 23.6%, APAC 20.2%, LATAM 1.3% of observed AI bot activity.
- More than 90% of healthcare AI-bot traffic is attributed to scraping.
- Method: Akamai customer-network telemetry assigned to training, assistant/agent and search bot categories. Sample size and classifier validation are not provided in this summary.

## Evidence quality

Primary vendor report summary, not an independent census. The increase and regional shares refer to different temporal comparisons. No public per-request ground truth supplied.
