## Summary

OSoMe's archive provides historical platform-verified information-operation campaigns plus matched organic controls. Its record is a practical, documented access path for Twitter-era takedown ground truth rather than assuming the retired X information-operations page is still an open download service.

## Access

Zenodo primary metadata opened 2026-10-03. Record published 2024-11-19, access policy updated 2026-09-09: faculty, research staff or graduate students at recognized institutions only, institutional email required, restrictions on military/Foreign Entity of Concern affiliation. One file per researcher per day. No access request submitted and no restricted files downloaded.

CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0 is explicitly stated. Noncommercial and no-derivatives restrictions apply to redistribution; do not assume arbitrary derivative-corpus publishing or commercial reuse is permitted. Metadata access_right=restricted and files=[] mean not immediately runnable anonymously.

26 campaign count is from primary description. Fields include post_text, post_time, accountid, is_repost, URLs, mentions and is_control. Controls discuss similar topics in the same periods, supporting leakage-conscious splits.

## Relevance to us

Stronger historical operator/campaign labels than vendor traffic aggregates, but not LLM-agent ground truth. X archive URL failed initial retrieval; this institutional archive offers a reachable, honest alternative with explicit restrictions.
