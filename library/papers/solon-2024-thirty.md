## Summary

A short popular review marking roughly thirty years since the Vicsek model. Starting from starling murmurations, it explains how the question of how flocks order led to rich physics: long-range order in 2D, the discontinuous (banded) nature of the flocking transition, and giant number fluctuations, from the perspective of a researcher who has worked on these problems.
