## Summary

Diverse models exchange answers, explanations and confidence over multiple rounds before confidence-weighted voting. The authors report reasoning improvements on seven benchmarks and analyze contributions from model diversity and discussion components.

## Limitations and open questions

Confidence weighting and mixed-model debate are established. Benefits need task-specific replication and equal-budget alternatives.
