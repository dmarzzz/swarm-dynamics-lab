## Summary

Review of the theory of active matter (biofilaments and motors, motile microorganisms, animal flocks,
synthetic imitations) in a unified framework: dry systems (no momentum conservation) with polar versus
nematic order, then active suspensions, large-scale instabilities, and semi-microscopic derivations of the
continuum equations. Read at abstract level from arXiv 1207.2929 (preprint title "Soft Active Matter"; arxiv
field null because the title differs).

## Contribution

The most cited active-matter review; places flocking theory ([[toner-1998-flocks]],
[[toner-2005-hydrodynamics]]) inside the broader active-matter programme.
