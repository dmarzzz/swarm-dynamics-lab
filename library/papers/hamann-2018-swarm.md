## Summary

A textbook on swarm robotics written from the modelling side. The publisher page I read describes it only
briefly (swarm robotics as a key technology for robotics, transport and medicine, with real-world examples);
the book is known for treating swarms with formal tools such as rate equations, random walks, Fokker-Planck
descriptions and collective decision models. I have not read its contents, so details beyond the publisher
description are not checked.

## Contribution

The main single-author textbook for the field and a common entry point for students; widely cited by later
work, including [[ben-zion-2023-morphological]].
