## Summary

A position paper proposing that multi-agent systems driven by competition and cooperation generate their own curriculum (an autocurriculum): solving one social task creates new ones, continually producing novel challenges and promoting innovation, sometimes with increasing complexity that requires agents to accumulate innovations. The argument draws on evolution as a multi-scale mosaic of interacting adaptive units.

## Contribution

Conceptual framing behind [[baker-2020-emergent]] and the Melting Pot programme ([[leibo-2021-scalable]]).
