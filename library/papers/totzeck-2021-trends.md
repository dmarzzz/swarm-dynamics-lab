## Summary

Review chapter on consensus-based optimisation: recalls the original model and its analysis
([[pinnau-2017-consensus]], [[carrillo-2018-analytical]]), then covers variants with component-wise independent or
common noise, mini-batch versions for machine learning with dimension-independent analytical estimates, the
relationship between CBO and PSO, a CBO variant for optimisation constrained to hypersurfaces, and applications,
preprints and open problems.

## Contribution

The CBO-specific review, by a co-author of the founding paper; the entry point to the fast-growing CBO literature
(115 citing works with DOIs found by forward-citation chase of the founding paper).

## Key results

- Review; abstract notes analytical estimates are dimension independent for the component-wise noise variants.
