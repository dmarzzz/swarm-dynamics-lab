## Summary

Extends ACO to continuous domains (ACO_R) without major conceptual change to its structure, presenting the general
idea, implementation and results. Compares with other ant-related continuous methods
and with metaheuristics adapted from combinatorial to continuous optimisation, and analyses efficiency and
robustness. (Abstract read via CORE.)

## Contribution

Standard continuous ACO variant, widely used as a baseline in continuous swarm-optimiser comparisons.
