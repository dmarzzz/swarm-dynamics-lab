## Summary

Textbook on the principal-agent model, in which one party delegates a task to another through a contract. The publisher description names three information problems the book treats: adverse selection, moral hazard and non-verifiability, with applications in industrial organisation, labour and behavioural economics. I read only the Princeton University Press page.

## Contribution

Standard graduate reference for incentive and contract design under hidden information and hidden action.
