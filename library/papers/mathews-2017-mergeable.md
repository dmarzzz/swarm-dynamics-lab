## Summary

Modular robots usually rely on purely distributed control and show only a limited, hard-wired range of
behaviours. Here robots physically connect and merge both their bodies and their control systems into a new
robot with a single centralised controller that retains full sensorimotor control; they can split again into
independent robots and self-heal by removing or replacing malfunctioning parts.

## Contribution

Introduces mergeable nervous systems, the physically connected precursor of the self-organised hierarchy in
[[zhu-2024-self]] and an instance of the hierarchy agenda in [[dorigo-2020-reflections]].

## Key results

- Demonstrated merge, split and self-healing with full sensorimotor control (from abstract; numbers not checked).

## Methods and models

Self-assembling modular robots whose control systems merge into one controller and split again. Abstract
read; platform and protocol not checked.

## Limitations and open questions

Small numbers of units; physical connection required.

## Relevance to us
