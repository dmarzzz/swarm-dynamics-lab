## Summary

A survey of multi-agent communication organised by the Five Ws: who communicates with whom, what, when and why. It traces three paradigms: MARL, moving from hand-designed or implicit protocols to end-to-end learned communication; emergent language, which seeks more structured and symbolic protocols but struggles with grounding, generalisation and scalability; and LLM-based agents that bring natural-language priors. It distils design patterns, trade-offs and open problems across the three.

## Contribution

The most recent communication survey and the only one in this set spanning MARL, emergent language and LLM agents, connecting [[zhu-2024-survey]] and [[lazaridou-2020-emergent]] to the LLM-swarm topic.
