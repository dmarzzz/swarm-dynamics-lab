## Summary

Solon, Kafri, Tailleur and coauthors show that the mechanical pressure exerted by a wide class of active fluids on a
container depends on the details of particle–wall interactions, so active fluids generally have no equation of state.
Pressure is a state function only in specific models (e.g. torque-free spherical active Brownian particles) that
implicitly restrict particle–wall or particle–particle interactions. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

A key no-go result for thermodynamic reasoning in active matter, cited in [[shaebani-2020-computational]] (read in
full) and central to [[obyrne-2022-time]].

## Key results

- No equation of state in general; wall-dependent pressure (abstract, theory and simulation).

## Methods and models

Theory with simulations of active particles with wall potentials and torques. arXiv:1412.3952; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Leaves open which coarse quantities are universal.

## Relevance to us

For swarms pushing against boundaries (herding, containment, robot arenas) the force on walls depends on how agents
turn at walls, not only on density and speed. Related: [[deblais-2018-boundaries]], [[cates-2015-motility]].
