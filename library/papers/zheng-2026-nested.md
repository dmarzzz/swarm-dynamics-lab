## Summary

Finds that the interaction networks of rummy-nose tetra schools during manoeuvres are highly nested
(NODF metric). A contagion model shows perfectly nested interaction networks maximise information transfer
efficiency, and adding them to a self-propelled system improves responsiveness and robustness in semi-physical
simulations and experiments with 50 robots.

## Contribution

Shifts responsiveness from per-agent rules to interaction-network structure, with robot
validation.

## Key results

- High nestedness of interaction networks during manoeuvres in fish (measured).
- Perfectly nested networks give optimal information transfer efficiency in a contagion model.
- Improved collective response in 50-robot experiments.

## Methods and models

Trajectory analysis of Hemigrammus rhodostomus; NODF nestedness; contagion model; self-propelled
robot swarm.
