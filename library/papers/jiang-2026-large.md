## Summary

A Physics Reports review of how LLMs are being used in the study of spreading processes on networks, framed from complex-systems physics and network science. It covers two domains: digital epidemics (misinformation, rumours) and biological epidemics (infectious disease outbreaks). After reviewing classical epidemic modelling foundations and how LLM approaches relate to them, it organises the literature into epidemic modelling (including LLM agents embedded in propagation systems that alter pathways and feedback), detection and surveillance, and prediction and management, and closes with open challenges.

## Contribution

The first physics-venue review linking LLM agents to spreading dynamics; useful for the contagion side of LLM swarms (e.g. infectious jailbreaks in [[gu-2024-agent]], prompt infection in [[lee-2024-prompt]]).
