## Summary

A survey of multi-agent deep RL applied to multi-robot systems, classified primarily by robotic application (for example coverage, navigation, formation, pursuit). The authors note the previous survey of learning in multi-robot systems dated to 2004, discuss open challenges, and list future multi-robot applications that could benefit from MADRL.

## Contribution

Robotics-side map of MADRL, complementary to ML-side surveys ([[gronauer-2022-multi]]) and physics-side reviews ([[cai-2025-reinforcement]]).
