## Summary

This longitudinal measurement tests more than 640,600 free proxies from eleven providers over thirty months. The abstract reports low availability, thousands of exposed vulnerabilities, and 16,923 proxies manipulating content, warning that proxy use creates privacy and security risks rather than guaranteeing anonymity.

## Contribution

Daily proxy testing, Shodan vulnerability checks, and content-manipulation measurement.

## Key results

- 34.5% active at least once; 4,452 distinct vulnerabilities; 16,923 proxies manipulate content.

## Methods and models

Daily proxy testing, Shodan vulnerability checks, and content-manipulation measurement.

## Limitations and open questions

The population covers collected free proxies, not all proxy infrastructure; a proxy endpoint does not identify its upstream user or operator.

## Relevance to us

A comparison source for coordinated automation and security measurement. Full-text methods and replication have not been checked.
