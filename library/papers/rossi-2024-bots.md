## Summary

Rossi's dissertation brings together five publications tracing the shift from machine-learning social-bot detection to generative bots and human recognition experiments. The early studies address automated accounts and political or conspiratorial activity; later work asks whether people identify modern bots and how foundation models can support experiments. The abstract also flags reduced access to Twitter as a research constraint.

## Contribution

A dissertation linking older automated bot classifiers, modern human-recognition tests, and generative experimental methods.

## Key results

- The thesis comprises five publications and totals 143 pages in the publisher record.
- The third and fourth publications focus on human detection of generative-AI bots rather than machine classifiers.
- The fifth generalizes foundation-model use in experiments; exact performance results were not available in the abstract read.
