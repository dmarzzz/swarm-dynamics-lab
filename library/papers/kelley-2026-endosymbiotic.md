## Summary

Digital host-symbiont evolution shows that mutualism can constrain host diversity and complexity. Some symbionts lose independent reproduction, while experimentally removing mutualists can release the host from evolutionary constraint.

## Contribution

Digital organelle formation and mutualistic lock-in already have a direct predecessor.

## Key results

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

## Methods and models

Symbulation with evolving instruction genomes, trait matching and controlled resource exchange.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract, introduction and selected methods read, not full. Trait matching and synergy are explicitly parameterized.
