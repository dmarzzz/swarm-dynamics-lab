## Summary

Hosain, Shuvo, Ogbe, Mazumder, Rahman, Hakim and Pandey survey AI-enhanced defences deployed at CDN and edge points: behaviour-based WAFs within web application and API protection, adaptive DDoS mitigation, bot management that resists human mimicry, and API discovery and encrypted-traffic anomaly analysis. They give a threat taxonomy mapped to edge-observable signals, evaluation metrics, deployment playbooks and a research agenda. They report that edge AI improves time to detect and mitigate but adds risks of model abuse and poisoning.

## Contribution

Short survey (7 pages) of edge bot management; the only review article I found that is specific to web bot defence in the LLM era.
