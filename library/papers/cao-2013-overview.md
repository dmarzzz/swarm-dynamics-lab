## Summary

Reviews results on distributed coordination of multiple vehicles (UAVs, UGVs, UUVs) published in major control
and robotics journals since 2006, grouped into consensus, formation control, distributed optimisation and
distributed estimation, and closes with open problems and promising directions.

## Contribution

The standard post-2006 survey of the control-side multi-agent literature, updating [[ren-2007-information]] and
[[olfati-saber-2007-consensus]]; it is also one of the most cited forward citations of [[cucker-2007-emergent]].

## Key results

- Abstract-level: taxonomy (consensus, formation, optimisation, estimation) and open problems.

## Methods and models

Survey. Abstract from OpenAlex.

## Limitations and open questions

Covers to about 2012; learning-based and event-triggered work after that is missing.
