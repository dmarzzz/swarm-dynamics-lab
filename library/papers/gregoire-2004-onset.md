## Summary

Studies the onset of collective motion in noisy self-propelled particle models with local interactions, with
and without cohesion (adding attraction/repulsion to Vicsek alignment). In 2D the transition is always
discontinuous, including for the original Vicsek model, overturning the earlier claim of a continuous
critical point. Cohesion is always lost near onset because of the interplay of density, velocity and shape
fluctuations. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

Established that the Vicsek transition is first order (via large simulations and the vectorial or
"extrinsic" noise variant), and introduced the cohesive Grégoire-Chaté model used by
[[ballerini-2008-interaction]]. Debated at the time (Nagy, Daruka, Vicsek 2007) and later settled in favour of
discontinuity ([[chate-2008-collective]], [[chate-2020-dry]]).

## Key results

- Claimed: discontinuous onset in 2D for all variants studied.
- Claimed: cohesion lost near onset.

## Methods and models

Vicsek-type SPP models, 2D, with an added attraction-repulsion term for cohesive groups; finite-size
analysis.
