## Summary

Natural insect (midge) swarms show power laws, scale-free correlations and imperfect dynamic scaling, with exponents unlike known equilibrium or nonequilibrium classes. Studying the harmonically confined Vicsek model, the authors find that its scale-free-chaos transition has, for finite N, an extended critical region with several critical lines. As noise vanishes, power laws link critical confinement and noise and allow static exponents to be computed; mixing data across critical lines and N (mimicking observations made under different conditions) reproduces the measured static and dynamic exponents. Preprint arXiv:2309.05064 has a slightly different title ("...are fingerprints of...").

## Contribution

A candidate explanation for the anomalous critical exponents of wild midge swarms.

## Key results

- Simulation (abstract): extended critical region with multiple critical lines in the confined Vicsek model; exponents agree with midge observations.
- Claimed (abstract): unmeasurable confinement strength proportional to measured perception range.

## Methods and models

Harmonically confined 3D Vicsek model simulations, finite-size scaling.
