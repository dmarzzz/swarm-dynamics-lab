## Summary

A structured survey of communication in multi-agent deep RL (Comm-MADRL). It proposes nine dimensions along which communication methods can be analysed and compared, such as who communicates with whom, what is sent, when, and under which constraints, projects existing work into this space to reveal trends, and suggests new designs from unexplored combinations of dimensions.

## Contribution

The most systematic taxonomy of learned communication in MARL, updating [[lazaridou-2020-emergent]] for the MARL (not language) side.

## Key results

- Review with a 9-dimension classification (per abstract).

## Methods and models

Literature survey. Abstract-level read (Crossref record).

## Limitations and open questions

Communication-centric; physical-interaction coordination (as in flocking) is outside scope.

## Relevance to us

Use its dimensions to design communication ablations in a swarm experiment. Related: [[foerster-2016-learning]], [[sukhbaatar-2016-learning]].
