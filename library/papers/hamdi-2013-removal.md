## Summary

The authors study Bayesian agents communicating finite actions on a time-dependent network. Repeated use of the same underlying information creates dependence. They give graph conditions and an algorithm for removing this effect, illustrated numerically.

## Contribution

Provides an explicit prior-art baseline for duplicate evidence in collective learning.

## Key results

Graph conditions and a removal procedure are reported; this scan does not independently verify the proofs.

## Methods and models

Bayesian private observations and communicated actions on a time-indexed graph.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract and introduction inspected; no full-methods review or replication. Transfer to opaque Jev decisions is unestablished.
