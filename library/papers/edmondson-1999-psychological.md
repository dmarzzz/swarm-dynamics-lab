## Summary

A multimethod field study of 51 manufacturing teams relates psychological safety to learning behavior. The paper reports an association after accounting for team efficacy and describes learning behavior as mediating the relationship with performance.

## Contribution

Observational team study combining measures of antecedents, processes and outcomes.

## Key results

51 teams; psychological safety is associated with learning in the reported field study.

## Methods and models

Assessment based on the source sections specified below; no implementation was run.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract and opening page only. Association is not randomized causation; human beliefs must not be attributed to software agents.
