## Summary

Opinion piece proposing optimal foraging theory, specifically the marginal value theorem, as a way to predict individual preferences against which group behaviour can be compared under different models of social influence, enabling field tests of how groups make collective decisions.

## Contribution

Methodological bridge between behavioural ecology and collective decision research; complements field tracking studies such as [[strandburg-peshkin-2015-shared]].

## Key results

- Framework proposal; no new data.
- Abstract-level reading only; numbers beyond the abstract were not checked.

## Methods and models

Conceptual; marginal value theorem predictions for patch-leaving.

## Limitations and open questions

Untested framework at the time of writing.

## Relevance to us

Useful if the hackathon builds foraging swarms and wants a normative baseline for leave-or-stay decisions.
