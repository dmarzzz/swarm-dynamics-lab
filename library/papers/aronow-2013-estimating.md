## Summary

Aronow and Samii develop inference for randomized treatments whose effects spill across units. Assignment probabilities, an exposure mapping and an estimand are separate components. The framework accommodates network dependence instead of treating every observation as independent.

## Contribution

Randomization-based estimation under specified interference.

## Key results

Uses inverse-probability weighting and dependence-aware variance estimation.

## Methods and models

Skimmed introduction, exposure mapping, misspecification, application and conclusion in arXiv v4.

## Limitations and open questions

Exposure mappings require substantive justification and support. The reduced-form effects do not automatically identify a mediation mechanism.
