## Summary

A PRISMA-style systematic review of 40 peer-reviewed journal studies on swarm-robotics technology from 2014 to 2024. It focuses on how hardware (sensors, actuation, communication devices, energy) and software (meta-heuristics, middleware such as ROS) co-evolved. It concludes that hardware and software are tightly interdependent, that uniform design standards are lacking, and that practical limits hinder scalability and deployment. It recommends interoperable, energy-efficient systems with machine learning.

## Contribution

A recent engineering-side review with a replicable protocol. It is narrower and more hardware-focused than [[dorigo-2020-reflections]] or [[kegeleirs-2025-towards]].

## Key results

- 40 studies analysed (from the abstract). Qualitative trends in sensing, actuation, communication and energy.
