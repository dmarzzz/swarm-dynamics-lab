## Summary

Four open-weight families (Llama 3.1, Qwen 2.5, Gemma 2, Yi 1.5) judge each other in a fully crossed pairwise design with 9,312 judgments. After correcting a per-family statistic that correlates with candidate quality at r = 0.95, all four families show same-family preference of 3.4 to 8.4 points; 55.4% of AB/BA pairs reverse; panel composition changes 18.5% of pairwise outcomes relative to a family-balanced panel.

## Contribution

Family-conditioned preference in judge panels, with a quality-controlled estimator.
