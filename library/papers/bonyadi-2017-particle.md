## Summary

Review of recent high-impact studies that analyse or modify PSO for single-objective continuous problems, with
suggested areas for future work. Its reference list (132 works, scanned via OpenCitations) centres on theory:
order-1 and order-2 stability, stagnation assumptions, rotation and transformation invariance, local convergence,
bound handling and topology.

## Contribution

The most complete pre-mean-field review of PSO theory; documents that classical PSO is not rotation invariant and
that convergence results depend on stagnation assumptions, which motivates the SDE/mean-field route.

## Key results

- Review; abstract states focus on analyses and modifications of PSO.
- From the reference list (titles as listed by OpenCitations): "Analysis of stability, local convergence, and
  transformation sensitivity of a variant of the particle swarm optimization algorithm" (IEEE TEVC 2016),
  "Order-2 stability analysis of particle swarm optimization" (Evolutionary Computation 2015), "Particle swarm
  variants: standardized convergence analysis" (Swarm Intelligence 2015), "Why standard particle swarm optimisers
  elude a theoretical runtime analysis" (FOGA 2009), and invariance studies comparing CMA-ES and PSO.
