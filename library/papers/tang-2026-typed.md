## Summary

This early review organizes typed-decision evaluations around older classification, constrained-output, calibration and routing ideas. It argues that interface guarantees and model accuracy must be assessed separately and that initial evidence remains narrow.

## Contribution

Supports treating Jev specificity as an interface and operating-regime question, rather than declaring every classifier experiment a new model class.

## Key results

The abstract reports a corpus of 28 early papers and a 14-item evaluation checklist.
