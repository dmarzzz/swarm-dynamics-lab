## Summary

Digregorio and colleagues map the complete phase diagram of 2D self-propelled hard disks from the equation of state
and local order parameter statistics. At low activity the equilibrium melting scenario survives (liquid–hexatic
coexistence, hexatic, solid); increasing activity shifts hexatic and solid order to higher density; above a critical
activity MIPS appears and the dense phase can be liquid, hexatic or solid depending on activity. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Reconciled 2D melting and MIPS in one diagram for active Brownian disks, completing [[redner-2013-structure]] and
[[fily-2012-athermal]]. 358 OpenAlex citations.
