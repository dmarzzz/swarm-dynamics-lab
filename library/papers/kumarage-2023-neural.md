## Summary

Studies neural authorship attribution, tracing AI-generated text to its originating LLM, with stylometric features (lexical, syntactic, structural) added to pretrained-LM classifiers. It analyses writing signatures of proprietary versus open-source models and variation within each group, aiming at interpretable attribution for misinformation forensics.

## Contribution

Early systematic stylometric analysis of LLM writing signatures for attribution.

## Key results

- Stylometric features yield interpretable signals and can augment PLM-based attribution classifiers (abstract; no numbers).

## Methods and models

Stylometric feature extraction plus PLM classifiers over texts from several 2023-era LLMs.

2023 model set; abstract-only reading.
