## Summary

Lecture notes (Microswimmers Summer School 2015, Jülich) on the Vicsek model: its algorithmic
implementation, the basic properties of its universality class, numerical results, and the role of
symmetries and conservation laws, with simplified analytical arguments and pointers to the advanced
literature. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

The most practical pedagogical reference for implementing and interpreting the Vicsek model correctly (noise
type, update order, banding, finite-size effects).

## Key results

- Abstract only; covers the discontinuous transition with bands ([[gregoire-2004-onset]],
