## Summary

The authors propose iterative network-based detection of wash trading based on nearly closed clusters of counterparties. The retrieved abstract estimates Polymarket activity consistent with wash trading peaking near sixty percent of volume in December 2024 and rising again to roughly twenty percent in early October 2025.

## Contribution

Detects wash trading by iteratively finding nearly closed counterparty clusters, applied to Polymarket.

## Key results

- Abstract estimates nearly 60% of volume at the December 2024 peak and about 20% in early October 2025.

## Methods and models

Iterative counterparty-network scoring of potentially colluding trading clusters.

## Limitations and open questions

Patterns indicative of wash trading are not legal adjudication or verified shared ownership; legitimate automated market making may resemble some patterns.

## Relevance to us

A concrete Sybil-cluster detector on a live prediction market (about 60% of volume at the December 2024 peak, about 20% in October 2025); the closed-cluster idea transfers to agent wallets.
