## Summary

Applies the Condorcet jury theorem to majority-vote ensembles of LLMs and simpler NLP models for sentiment analysis. Adding larger models, including GPT-4, gives only marginal improvements, which the authors read as a lack of independence among the models.

## Contribution

An early empirical note that LLM ensembles violate Condorcet independence.
