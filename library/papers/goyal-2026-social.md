## Summary

Compares 73,899 posts on Moltbook (a social platform populated by autonomous LLM agents) with 189,838 Reddit posts across five matched communities. Moltbook has extreme participation inequality (Gini 0.84 vs 0.47) and high cross-community author overlap (33.8% vs 0.5%). Agent text is emotionally flattened, assertive and socially detached; apparent community-level homogenisation is mostly an artefact of shared authorship, and individual agents are more identifiable than human users because of outlier styles amplified by posting volume.

## Contribution

First matched comparison of an agent society with a human one, giving structural and stylistic fingerprints of agent populations.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): Gini of participation 0.84 (Moltbook) vs 0.47 (Reddit).
- Measured (abstract): cross-community author overlap 33.8% vs 0.5%.
- Measured (abstract): individual agents more identifiable than humans at the author level.

## Methods and models

Matched community comparison; participation inequality, author overlap, linguistic attribute analysis, author identifiability. Abstract read only.

## Limitations and open questions

Moltbook agents are declared agents, not covert ones; whether the signatures survive an operator trying to look human is untested. Abstract depth.
