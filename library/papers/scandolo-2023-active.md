## Summary

Scandolo, Pausch and Cates use Doi–Peliti field theory to derive hydrodynamics of several active Ising models (flocking
with discrete symmetry). Most share a first-order isotropic-to-flocked transition for nonzero self-propulsion, except a
variant with purely pairwise local alignment, which fails to flock at all. At zero propulsion the models stay out of
equilibrium and, they argue, lie outside the equilibrium Model C universality class. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Systematic field-theory derivation (including noise) for the active Ising class used in [[solon-2015-phase]].

- First-order transition generic; pairwise-only alignment gives no flocking (abstract).
