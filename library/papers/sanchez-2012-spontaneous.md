## Summary

Sanchez, Dogic and coworkers assemble extensile microtubule bundles driven by kinesin motors into active gels, liquid
crystals and emulsions. Percolating networks show internally driven chaotic flows, instabilities, enhanced transport and
mixing; confined to droplet surfaces they form 2D active nematics with streaming flows, defect unbinding and
annihilation, and the droplets become motile. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Created the microtubule–kinesin active nematic, the workhorse experimental system of active nematics
([[doostmohammadi-2018-active]]) and active turbulence ([[alert-2022-active]]). 1468 OpenAlex citations.

## Key results

- Chaotic flows, enhanced transport and mixing in active networks (abstract).
- Motile ±1/2 disclination dynamics and autonomous droplet motility (abstract).

Experiment: microtubules, kinesin clusters, PEG depletant, ATP. arXiv:1301.1122; full text not read.
