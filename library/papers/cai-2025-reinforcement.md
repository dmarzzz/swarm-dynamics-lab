## Summary

A review of reinforcement learning applied to active matter, organised in two parts: optimal motion strategies for single active particles (navigation, foraging, locomotion) and regulation of collective dynamics in active swarms, including RL-driven self-organisation and goal-directed control. It argues RL can advance understanding and control of active systems with applications in biology, robotics and medicine.

## Contribution

The most recent review bridging RL and active-matter physics; updates [[cichos-2020-machine]] with a dedicated collective-dynamics section, and cites [[durve-2020-learning]] and [[brambati-2025-learning]].
