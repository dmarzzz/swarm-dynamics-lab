## Summary

A review of reinforcement learning applied to active matter, organised in two parts: optimal motion strategies for single active particles (navigation, foraging, locomotion) and regulation of collective dynamics in active swarms, where RL is used both to make self-organisation emerge and to steer swarms toward goals. It closes with prospects for biology, robotics and medicine.

## Contribution

The main 2025 review bridging RL and active-matter collective motion, relevant for learned models of flocking.
