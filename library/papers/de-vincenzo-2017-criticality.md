## Summary

A spin-like model of human group decision-making with social interaction strength, individual
confidence and expertise as control parameters. Phase diagrams show a critical front where fitness and consensus
change sharply together; at this front information leakage from the fitness landscape to agents is maximised,
triggering collective intelligence and better decisions. Larger groups can help or hurt depending on parameters.

## Contribution

Links criticality to collective intelligence in a decision-making (rather than motion) model.

## Key results

- Critical transition front where fitness and consensus jump together (model).
- Information leakage from landscape to agents maximal at the front (model).
- Group size effect depends on control parameters.

## Methods and models

Continuous-time Markov chain spin model with social energy and local fitness (NK-landscape-like).
