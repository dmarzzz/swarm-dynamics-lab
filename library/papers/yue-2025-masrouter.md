## Summary

A cascaded controller chooses collaboration mode, roles and models jointly. Reported reasoning and coding evaluations improve cost-performance, making generic heterogeneous routing and automatic role assignment direct prior art for this project.

## Limitations and open questions

Compare a new adaptive allocator to learned routing and simple deterministic assignment; training cost matters.
