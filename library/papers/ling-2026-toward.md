## Summary

This review synthesizes 247 papers using a lifecycle-oriented account of information flow, delegated authority and persistent state. It identifies persistent corruption and multi-agent propagation as emerging concerns alongside prompt injection and tool-mediated control-flow hijacking. The authors argue that existing defences compose poorly and that benchmarks underrepresent long-horizon, stateful and deployment-sensitive risks.
