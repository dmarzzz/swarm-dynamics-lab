## Summary

This paper tests whether existing AI-text detectors can identify LLM-written peer reviews. The abstract reports difficulty detecting GPT-4o reviews without excessive false positives and proposes a new approach with improved detection at low false-positive levels, emphasizing individual-review detection limitations.
