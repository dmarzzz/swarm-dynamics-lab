## Summary

Repeated sampling from the strongest proposer often beats mixed proposers. The authors examine output quality and diversity jointly, including specialized tasks; cross-model mixtures sometimes help but diversity alone does not justify weaker proposals.

## Limitations and open questions

Include a best-model repeated-sampling baseline; results do not establish that all heterogeneous stateful teams lose.
