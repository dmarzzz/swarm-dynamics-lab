## Summary

A review of bot types, impacts and detection methods, using Botometer as a case study of how the public uses AI countermeasures. A user survey suggests bot checking became routine for many users, but users often misread the scores; the authors describe algorithmic and interpretability changes to bot scores, and argue that the arms race forces constant retraining.

## Contribution

Review plus user-experience evidence from the most-used public bot detector.

## Key results

- User survey: bot detection is part of many users' social media practice, but score interpretation causes misunderstandings (abstract).
- Arms race requires continual updates of training data and features.

## Methods and models

Literature review; Botometer user survey; description of score calibration changes. Abstract-level read.
