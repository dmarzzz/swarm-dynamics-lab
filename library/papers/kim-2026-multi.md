## Summary

The authors study a producer-consumer market for agent trajectories, allowing agents to reuse external experience without joint training. They report better task performance and fewer steps on ALFWorld and WebArena.

## Contribution

Trajectory reuse is already an implemented agent-memory mechanism.

## Key results

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

## Methods and models

Trajectory producers, consumers, and a mediating platform.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract read; does not establish resource-mediated coexistence or reciprocal habitat selection.
