## Summary

Extends the Condorcet jury theorem to heterogeneous agents that first calibrate beliefs about their own fixed competence and then vote or abstain through a confidence gate. Derives a non-asymptotic lower bound on group success and shows selective participation generalises the asymptotic guarantee; validated by Monte Carlo simulation, with a discussion of collective LLM hallucination.
