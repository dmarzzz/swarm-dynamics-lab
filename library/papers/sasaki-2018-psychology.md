## Summary

Annual Review of Entomology article on studying insect colony decisions with methods from psychology: neural models of speed-accuracy trade-offs, tests of collective rationality, cognitive capacity and perceptual discrimination at individual and colony levels, and the nascent field of collective learning. Colonies often outperform individuals, but reliance on positive feedback can amplify errors.

## Contribution

Recent review of the 'superorganism psychology' programme; collects [[sasaki-2013-ant]], [[marshall-2009-optimal]] and related experiments.

## Key results

- Synthesis: improved colony-level function emerges from interactions of less capable individuals, with error amplification as a cost.
- Abstract-level reading only; numbers beyond the abstract were not checked.
