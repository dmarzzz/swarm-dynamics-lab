## Summary

Majority voting ignores heterogeneity and correlation across models. The authors propose Optimal Weight and Inverse Surprising Popularity aggregators that use first- and second-order information, prove they mitigate majority-voting limits under mild assumptions, and report gains on synthetic data, UltraFeedback, MMLU and a real healthcare setting (ARMMAN).
