## Summary

Compares single-LLM, single-vendor and mixed-vendor multi-agent conversations with three doctor agents (o4-mini, Gemini-2.5-Pro, Claude-4.5-Sonnet) on RareBench and DiagnosisArena. Mixed-vendor teams consistently beat single-vendor teams in recall and accuracy; overlap analysis attributes the gain to complementary inductive biases surfacing diagnoses that homogeneous teams miss.

## Contribution

An applied replication of the cross-vendor diversity gain in a deliberating team.
