## Summary

Adapts classical mathematical models of disease spread, information and competing beliefs to honeybee nest-site selection, and shows that a swarm can select one of several nest sites without any individual bee comparing sites. The collective comparison emerges from competing recruitment dynamics.

## Contribution

Early compartmental (epidemic-style) model of swarm choice; a precursor of the ODE models in [[marshall-2009-optimal]] and [[pais-2013-mechanism]].

## Key results

- Consensus on one site arises without individual comparison (model).
- Abstract-level reading only; numbers beyond the abstract were not checked.

## Methods and models

Compartmental ODE models of scout states (uncommitted, committed and dancing for each site).

## Limitations and open questions

Very short abstract; deterministic model without cross-inhibition.

## Relevance to us

Shows epidemic-style models are a natural language for swarm decisions; related to opinion dynamics in [[reina-2024-speed]].
