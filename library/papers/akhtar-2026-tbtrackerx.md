## Summary

TBTrackerX studies keyword-triggered bots on X using a honeytrap account and analysis of replies and campaigns. The abstract reports 4,452 replies from 2,647 distinct agents and interactions with over 84,000 users, alongside shared controllers, fake followers, short activity bursts, and dormant periods.

## Contribution

Keyword-triggered collection connects individual social bots to coordinated campaign infrastructure.

## Key results

- The abstract reports 4,452 replies, 2,647 agents, more than 84,000 users, reply similarity up to 0.97, and bursts lasting 15 seconds to five minutes.

## Methods and models

Honeytrap collection and data-driven detection and characterization of trigger-bot behavior on X.

## Limitations and open questions

These are script-controlled trigger bots; the abstract does not prove they are LLM agents. Campaign scale and evasion observations are author-reported, not independently reproduced.

## Relevance to us

This provides a prior-art comparison for detecting coordinated automation, not evidence that any observed population is an LLM swarm.
