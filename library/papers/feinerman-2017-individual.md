## Summary

Review contrasting two sources of colony-level cognition in social insects: amplification of capable individual cognition versus emergent group-level computation from connectivity. Unlike neurons, individual insects can process colony-relevant information themselves, and insect communication lacks a fixed network, so the brain analogy is imperfect.

## Contribution

Corrective to the neuron analogy in [[couzin-2009-collective]] and [[marshall-2009-optimal]]; written by a biophysicist and a distributed-computing theorist.

- Examples span the spectrum from individual amplification to emergent computation (conceptual).
