## Summary

Multi-author roadmap of motile active matter: short perspectives on propulsion mechanisms, synthetic micro- and
nanomachines, motility-induced collective behaviour, and agents from microswimmers to fish, birds and people. Stresses
that persistent energy consumption invalidates equilibrium concepts (free energy, detailed balance, time-reversal
symmetry) and calls for interdisciplinary work. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
