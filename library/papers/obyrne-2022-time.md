## Summary

Review of time irreversibility in active matter: separates the roles of the arrow of time (entropy production) and of
non-Boltzmann steady-state statistics in single- and many-body active systems; shows that effective time-reversible
descriptions can exist at all scales, and discusses how interactions with other constituents or external operators
reveal the nonequilibrium source of energy, with implications for designing active materials. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
