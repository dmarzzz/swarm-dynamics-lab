## Summary

Historical review of heuristic and metaheuristic optimisation over 50 years, mainly from a European operations
research perspective: from philosophical roots through the main milestones to recent hybrids of metaheuristics and
machine learning, with attention to theories that changed thinking about problem solving and to the role of EJOR.
Identifies potential future research lines.

## Contribution

Up-to-date (2025) review placing swarm methods within the broader metaheuristics history, co-authored by
[[sorensen-2015-metaheuristics]]'s author.
