## Summary

Survey of UAV and flying ad hoc network security combining attack-surface analysis with threat identification, and grouping countermeasures into prevention and detection. Emphasises insider threats in decentralised, dynamic FANETs and adds simulations of four routing attacks with realistic parameters.

## Contribution

Review article for the UAV swarm side of the lane; Sybil is treated as one insider threat among routing attacks.

## Key results

- Simulation analysis of four routing attacks on FANETs (abstract).

## Methods and models

Survey plus simulation. Abstract read only; details beyond the abstract not checked.

## Limitations and open questions

Networking focus; little on swarm-level decision manipulation.

## Relevance to us

Background citation for UAV swarms; confirms that the networking community treats Sybil as a routing and authentication problem rather than a collective-decision problem.
