## Summary

An 86-page survey that traces the concept of agents from philosophy to AI, proposes a brain-perception-action framework for LLM agents, and reviews single-agent, multi-agent and human-agent applications. A dedicated part covers "agent societies": personality and behaviour of LLM agents, social phenomena that emerge in agent societies, and what they reveal about human society.

## Contribution

Broad early survey whose agent-society section is one of the first systematic treatments of emergent social phenomena among LLM agents.
