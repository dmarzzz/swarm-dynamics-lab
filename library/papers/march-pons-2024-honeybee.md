## Summary

Programs a kilobot swarm to follow a honeybee nest-site selection model with self-discovery and imitation (independence and interdependence) and shows the swarm reaches decentralised consensus. Consensus strength depends not only on the independence-interdependence balance but also on swarm density and robot motion, which together control whether a percolated communication network forms.

## Contribution

Recent physical test of a bee-inspired decision model highlighting embodiment factors (crowding, mobility, percolation) that mean-field models omit; complements [[valentini-2016-collective]] and [[talamali-2021-when]].

## Key results

- Kilobots reach consensus decisions like honeybees (experiment).
- Density and motion determine a percolated communication network that governs consensus strength (experiment plus model).
