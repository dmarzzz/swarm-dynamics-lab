## Summary

Cyborg insects, live insects with miniature electronic backpacks that steer them by stimulation, adapt to rough terrain and are energy-efficient, but multi-cyborg control has been lacking because individual insects respond variably to control input. The authors propose a swarm navigation algorithm for cyborg insects that copes with this individual variability and verify it experimentally in unknown, obstructed soft terrain.

## Contribution

An early multi-agent control result for biohybrid swarms, where heterogeneity is intrinsic and not an engineering defect.
