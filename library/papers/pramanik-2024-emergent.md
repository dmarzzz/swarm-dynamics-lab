## Summary

Fully coupled fluid-structure simulations of helical, magnetically actuated soft micro-swimmers in a viscous fluid show emergent 3-D spatiotemporal patterns from non-reciprocal hydrodynamic interactions. Swimmers offset along the swimming direction attract, laterally offset swimmers repel, and the group converges to a single lateral plane drifting radially outward.

## Contribution

It extends robotic-swarm hydrodynamics from rigid 2-D swimmers to soft 3-D swimmers.

## Key results

- Axial offsets give attraction, lateral offsets give repulsion; convergence to a common plane with radial drift (simulation, per the abstract).

## Methods and models

Two-way coupled fluid-structure interaction simulations of helical magnetic soft swimmers.

Abstract-depth entry. Simulation only; microrobot regime far from macroscopic robot swarms.
