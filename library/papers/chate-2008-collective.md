## Summary

Comprehensive numerical study of Vicsek-style models in 2D and 3D with only local alignment. The onset of
collective motion is discontinuous (first-order like). In a large region of parameter space including the
transition, high-density, high-order propagating solitary bands dominate; far from the transition a
statistically homogeneous ordered phase appears, with anomalously strong density fluctuations,
superdiffusion and strong intermittency. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

The detailed companion to [[gregoire-2004-onset]]; the reference for travelling bands and giant number
fluctuations in Vicsek models.

## Key results

- Claimed: first-order onset in 2D and 3D; bands near threshold; giant density fluctuations, superdiffusion
  and intermittency in the ordered phase.

## Methods and models

Large-scale simulations of Vicsek-style models, 2D and 3D.
