## Summary

The authors model a team learning while its expertise and interaction network change together. They distinguish stubborn and nonstubborn participants, connecting collective learning outcomes to network properties and the evolving distribution of expertise.

## Contribution

Mathematical model of cooperative learning and transactive memory.

## Key results

The abstract reports convergence to the best expert for the nonstubborn model and graph-dependent behavior with stubborn participants.
