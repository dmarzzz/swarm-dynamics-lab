## Summary

The study changes the mapping between option names and rubrics while holding the underlying question and evidence fixed. Semantic label polarity can alter the decision ranking even though every response still conforms to the schema.

## Contribution

Option-name attacks alone are replication work. New work must isolate changing menus, semantic equivalence and downstream collective allocation.

## Key results

The hosted Jev comparison reports AUC changing from .8146 to .5806 under the tested swap. Neutral names reduce the effect.
