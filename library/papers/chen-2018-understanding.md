## Summary

This paper characterizes Ethereum through three transaction-derived graphs covering transfers, contract creation, and contract invocation. The retrieved abstract describes systematic collection, cross-graph observations, and approaches to two security issues, but does not provide enough detail to label all observed patterns as bots or common operators.

## Contribution

Collection of Ethereum transactions and construction of three activity graphs.
