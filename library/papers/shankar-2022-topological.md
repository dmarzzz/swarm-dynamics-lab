## Summary

Introductory review of topology in active matter: self-propelled and proliferating topological defects in active
nematics (controllable by geometry and patterning), and topologically protected edge modes of waves in active fluids and
solids, traced to topological invariants. Discusses active microfluidics, tissues, robotic metamaterials and active
granular and colloidal systems. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution
