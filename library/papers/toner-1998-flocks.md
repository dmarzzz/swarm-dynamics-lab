## Summary

Full presentation of the continuum theory of flocking. The ordered phase is a spontaneously broken
rotational symmetry whose Goldstone modes (fluctuations of the direction of motion) mix with the conserved
density to give propagating sound modes and enormous density fluctuations. The equations resemble
compressible Navier-Stokes and a relaxational time-dependent Ginzburg-Landau theory for an n = d component
ferromagnet. Linear theory is exact for d > 4; for d < 4 nonlinear fluctuations change the long-distance
behaviour, and in d = 2 a true long-range-ordered phase exists. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

The long-form Toner-Tu paper, standard reference for the hydrodynamic description of polar flocks and for
predictions (anomalous density fluctuations, sound modes) testable in simulations and imaging of real
flocks.

## Key results

- Claimed: propagating sound modes, giant density fluctuations, long-range order in 2D.
- Claimed exact 2D exponents, later revised ([[mahault-2019-quantitative]], [[chate-2024-dynamic]]).

## Methods and models

Symmetry-based hydrodynamic equations for velocity and density; dynamic RG.
