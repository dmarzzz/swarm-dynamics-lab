## Summary

Models synchronised emergence of periodical cicadas as a consensus decision under spatially correlated thermal noise. Short-range communication among underground nymphs gives an Ising model with a quenched, spatially correlated random field and annealed site dilution, which produces the successive emergence swarms seen in nature.

## Contribution

Statistical-physics treatment of a natural collective timing decision; one of few random-field Ising applications to animal decisions.

## Key results

- Ising consensus model with correlated random fields reproduces swarm-like emergence waves (model).
- Abstract-level reading only; numbers beyond the abstract were not checked.

## Methods and models

Ising model on a lattice with quenched correlated random field and site dilution; simulation.

## Limitations and open questions

Communication mechanism among nymphs is hypothetical; authors call for fieldwork.

## Relevance to us

Random-field Ising framing is a handy null model for swarm consensus under heterogeneous local information. Related: [[hartnett-2016-heterogeneous]].
