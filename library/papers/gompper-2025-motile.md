## Summary

A multi-author roadmap (32 authors) on motile active matter, from nanomotors and cells to insects, fish, birds and people. It frames the core challenge as non-equilibrium physics (no free energy, detailed balance or time-reversal symmetry) with non-additive and non-reciprocal interactions, and stresses agents that sense, process and adapt. Highlighted directions include motion in complex environments, chirality, micro-robots, emergent collective behaviour and swarming of intelligent self-propelled particles, and microbial systems. The author list includes Cavagna and Giardina (natural flocks and swarms) and Korman and Feinerman (collective behaviour). The arXiv version (2411.19783) is titled "The 2024 Motile Active Matter Roadmap"; the journal version is "The 2025 motile active matter roadmap".
