## Summary

Studies active systems in which particles switch their self-propulsion on and off. Even when only
off-to-on transitions are allowed, a 2-state active system behaves as an effective 3-state system with a sharp
transition in 1D and critical behaviour in 2D, with scale-invariant activity avalanches.

## Contribution

Shows criticality can arise generically from coupling between internal state and activity, a
mechanism relevant to the onset of collective motion (e.g. sheep).

## Key results

- Sharp phase transition in 1D and criticality with scale-invariant activity avalanches in 2D (theory/simulation).

## Methods and models

Two-state active particle models with activation fronts. arXiv 2311.06208.
