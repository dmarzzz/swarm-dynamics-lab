## Summary

Annual Review that set out active matter as a field: collections of particles that take in and dissipate energy to
move, treated with nonequilibrium statistical mechanics and hydrodynamics with minimal microscopic detail. It presents a
unified view of living and inanimate active systems and discusses theory and experiment together. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Early unifying review by one of the founders of active hydrodynamics ([[simha-2002-hydrodynamic]]); precursor to
[[marchetti-2013-hydrodynamics]]. 2044 OpenAlex citations.

## Key results

- Defines active particles by internal energy uptake and systematic movement (abstract).
- Presents a unified theory spanning organisms, motors and inanimate analogues (abstract).
