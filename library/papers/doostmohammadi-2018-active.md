## Summary

Review of active nematics, systems of elongated self-driven units with head-tail symmetric order, focusing on
microtubule–kinesin mixtures and their hydrodynamic theories. Central themes are active turbulence and self-propelled
topological defects, ways to control active turbulence, and the appearance of active nematic physics and defects in
cells and tissues. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Main review of wet active nematics, following the experimental system of [[sanchez-2012-spontaneous]].

## Key results

- Active turbulence and motile defects are the defining phenomena (abstract).
- Control of active turbulence discussed as a prerequisite for harvesting energy (abstract).

Review (Nature Communications, open access). Full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Wet, nematic systems; limited direct transfer to polar robot swarms.
