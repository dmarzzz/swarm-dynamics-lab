## Summary

Develops a model of social information use by fully rational agents and shows that the apparently stochastic social decision rules seen in animal and human collectives can arise from information asymmetries between individuals and between decision-makers and the observer, without ad hoc departures from rationality.

## Contribution

Rational-choice foundation for the empirical decision rules modelled in [[perez-escudero-2011-collective]] and [[arganda-2012-common]].

## Key results

- Rational agents with private information generate observed-looking stochastic rules (model).

Model of fully rational agents with private information observing earlier decisions; analytical and numerical.
