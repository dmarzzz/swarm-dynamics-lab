## Summary

A review that maps animal collective behaviours (fish schools, wolf packs, bird flocks and others) to the
design of unmanned swarm systems. It introduces the origin of swarm intelligence, gives application cases, and
surveys aerial, ground and marine robot swarms, emphasising the mapping from biological cooperative mechanisms
to unmanned cluster systems, and closes with key technologies for human-machine cooperative systems.

## Contribution

A recent, broad bio-inspiration review from a large Chinese aerial-swarm group, useful for the 2018-2023
literature on UAV swarms that Western reviews cover less.

## Key results

- Review claims: self-organisation, robustness, adaptability and expansibility are the transferable properties.
