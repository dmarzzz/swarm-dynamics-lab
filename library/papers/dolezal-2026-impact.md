## Summary

Builds a representative sample of websites published 2022-2025 from the Internet Archive and runs a state-of-the-art AI text detector over them: by mid-2025 roughly 35% of newly published websites are classified as AI-generated or AI-assisted, up from zero before ChatGPT. Rising AI share correlates with lower semantic diversity and more positive sentiment, but not with lower factual accuracy or stylistic diversity, against majority public belief measured in a US user study.

## Contribution

A representative-sample web prevalence estimate with tests of "dead internet" hypotheses.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): about 35% of newly published websites AI-generated or AI-assisted by mid-2025.
- Measured (abstract): significant negative correlation with semantic diversity and positive correlation with positive sentiment; no significant effect on factual accuracy or stylistic diversity.
- Measured (abstract): most surveyed US adults believe all four negative hypotheses; infrequent AI users believe them more.

## Methods and models
