## Summary

Ziepke, Maryshev, Aranson and Frey study self-propelled agents that locally process information carried by chemical
signals (emit and respond). This communication capacity greatly expands the structures they form: the agents
self-organize through a series of collective dynamical states at several hierarchical levels. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
