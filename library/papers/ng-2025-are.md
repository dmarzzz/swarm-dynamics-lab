## Summary

Generates LLM-powered bot networks (personas, tweets, interactions) with a mix of manual effort, network science and LLMs, then compares their network and linguistic properties with empirical data on wild bots and humans. Both network and linguistic properties of the LLM-powered bots differ from wild bots and humans.

## Contribution

A check on the realism of simulated LLM botnets used to train detectors; the answer is that they are distinguishable.

## Key results

- Network and linguistic properties of LLM-powered bots differ from wild bots and humans (abstract; which metrics and by how much not given there).

## Methods and models

Synthetic persona generation, simulated interaction networks, comparison with empirical bot and human datasets.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; workshop-length paper.

A caution for training swarm detectors on simulated swarms such as [[qiao-2025-botsim]]: the simulation may be easier to detect than the real thing, or simply different.
