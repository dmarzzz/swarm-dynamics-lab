## Summary

A broad review article on security and privacy threats to LLM agents. It introduces the basics of LLM agents, categorises threats, discusses their impact on humans, the environment and other agents, reviews defences, and explores future trends, with case studies throughout. It is 35 pages and was accepted to ACM Computing Surveys according to the arXiv comments.

## Contribution

An early general taxonomy of agent threats that includes effects on other agents.

## Key results

Review article. No new measurements.

## Methods and models

Literature synthesis with case studies.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only. It predates most of the memory-poisoning literature catalogued here (2025 to 2026).
