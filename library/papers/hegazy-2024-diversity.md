## Summary

Extends the Du et al. debate protocol across model scales. After 4 rounds, a diverse set of medium models (Gemini-Pro, Mixtral 8x7B, PaLM 2-M) reaches 91% on GSM-8K, above GPT-4, versus 82% for 3 instances of Gemini-Pro; the diverse set also reaches 94% on ASDiv.

## Contribution

An early demonstration that model diversity beats homogeneous copies in debate.
