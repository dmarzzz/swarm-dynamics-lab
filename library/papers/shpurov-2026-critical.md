## Summary

A two-dimensional cellular automaton of bee movement in the hive reproduces key features of the data
in [[shpurov-2024-beehive]]: divergence of correlation length with density as control parameter and a scale-free
distribution of jammed clusters, supporting a critical (second-order) transition interpretation.

## Contribution

Minimal model for jamming-type criticality in social insect movement.

## Key results

- Correlation length diverges as a function of density in both data and model.
- Scale-free distribution of jammed cluster sizes in the model.

## Methods and models

2D cellular automaton with density as control parameter.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read.

## Relevance to us

Simple CA we could reuse to test criticality measurement pipelines on jamming-like swarms.
