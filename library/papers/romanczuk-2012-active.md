## Summary

Review of stochastic models of active Brownian particles: individual motility described by Langevin equations with
velocity-dependent friction (energy depots, Rayleigh-Helmholtz), the effect of different noise types, stationary
velocity distributions and diffusion coefficients, confinement, and interacting particles with resulting collective
dynamics and pattern formation. Examples range from catalytic nanorods to cells and animals. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Bridges the statistical-physics view of active particles with collective-motion models used for animals; the
reference for the "active Brownian particle" name in the Schweitzer–Ebeling–Schimansky-Geier tradition.

## Key results

- Stationary velocity distributions and diffusion coefficients for several friction functions (abstract).
- Overview of collective dynamics and pattern formation of interacting active particles (abstract).
