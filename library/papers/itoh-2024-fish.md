## Summary

Describes a multi-object tracking competition (https://ftc-2024.github.io/) built on the SweetFish dataset, in which participants track 10 schooling sweetfish simultaneously from video with bounding-box annotations. The paper presents background, the dataset, a baseline and the approaches of the top three teams.

## Contribution

A public benchmark for tracking schooling fish, a prerequisite for data-driven collective motion work.

## Key results

- Dataset/benchmark (abstract): 10-fish schooling videos with bounding boxes; winners' methods summarised.

## Methods and models

MOT benchmark, detection plus association pipelines.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; 2D, 10 animals.
