## Summary

A perspective on "robophysics", the study of the physical principles governing living-like robots in complex
environments. It argues that robots still face efficiency limits, at the individual level in locomotion and at
the collective level in achieving shared purpose, coordination, communication and cost efficiency, and reviews
recent work that brings active-matter physics and biology into the modelling and design of robot swarms.
