## Summary

Ferrara synthesizes risks from generative models, including synthetic identities, AI-powered social botnets, targeted misinformation, scams, and malware. The abstract combines a review of existing research with prospective scenarios and preparation strategies. It is a threat-taxonomy source rather than a new detector, controlled influence experiment, or measurement of coordinated LLM-agent deployments.

## Contribution

Places social automation within a broader generative-AI misuse landscape and connects fabricated identities to scalable content campaigns.
