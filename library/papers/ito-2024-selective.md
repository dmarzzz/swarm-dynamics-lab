## Summary

An agent-based model in which a fish's direction of attention moves, guided by visual stimuli from images of nearby fish, so that only part of the visual information is used. It reproduces the branching (bifurcation) where a fish selectively follows one individual as the distance between two or three neighbours grows, and produces vortex, polarised school, swarm and turning patterns. The authors discuss the topological nature of visual interaction and maps of pairwise and three-body interactions, comparing with experiments.

## Contribution

A mechanistic attention model for selective following, consistent with decision bifurcations reported in fish and insects.

## Key results

- Simulation (abstract): branching selective following; standard collective phases; induced pairwise and three-body interaction maps.

## Methods and models

Visual-image-based agent model with dynamic attention direction.
