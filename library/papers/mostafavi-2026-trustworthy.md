## Summary

This systems-security survey covers 206 foundational studies and regulatory standards, using a stateful agent model and a six-dimensional trustworthiness taxonomy. It reviews risks in reasoning loops, memory, tool execution and multi-agent interaction, and proposes a layered zero-trust architecture. Its proposed controls include separate trusted and untrusted reasoning, capability-based permissions and sandboxed runtimes.

## Contribution

Review and taxonomy of agent security risks.

## Key results

Review of 206 studies and standards, reported in the abstract. Proposed architecture is not evidence of universal prevention.
