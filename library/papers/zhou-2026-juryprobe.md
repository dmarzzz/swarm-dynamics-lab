## Summary

Reference-free factuality judge panels on audited FEVER corruptions show correlated false negatives (false-negative-only correlations 0.402 and 0.368; false-consensus lifts 3.13x and 18.13x). A calibration probe flags high-risk panels and routes majority accepts to grounded verification; a fixed rule flags 8 to 10 of 10 splits and 0 of 10 on a negative control.

## Contribution
