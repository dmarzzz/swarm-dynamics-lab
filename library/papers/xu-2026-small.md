## Summary

A roadmap from the 1st Workshop on Small-Scale Testbeds for Connected and Automated Vehicles and Robot Swarms (IEEE IV 2024, Jeju). It has three parts: enhancing accessibility and diversity, sharing best practices for building and maintaining testbeds, and connecting testbeds through an abstraction layer for collaboration. A companion survey (arXiv:2408.14199) keeps an online comparative table of more than 25 testbeds at https://bassamlab.github.io/testbeds-survey.

## Contribution

A meta-level map of physical testbeds for multi-robot and swarm experiments, useful for choosing hardware.

## Key results

- A qualitative roadmap with three action areas; the companion survey compares more than 25 testbeds.
