## Summary

Self-propelled particles interacting through pairwise attractive and repulsive (Morse-type) potentials. The
authors predict the stability and morphology of the resulting collective patterns (flocks, mills/vortices,
clumps, collapse) from the shape of the two-body interaction, using H-stability ideas from statistical
mechanics, giving a theory for all possible phases of collective motion in this model class. Read at
abstract level via Europe PMC.

## Contribution

The standard attraction-repulsion (no explicit alignment) model of swarming with a principled criterion
(H-stability) separating catastrophic collapse from well-spaced patterns; widely used in robotics and
applied mathematics.

## Key results

- Claimed: phase diagram (and collapse vs stable patterns) determined by the two-body potential shape.

## Methods and models

Second-order self-propelled particles with Rayleigh-type self-propulsion/friction and Morse pairwise
potentials; simulations plus statistical-mechanics stability analysis.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read. No noise, all-to-all potentials.

## Relevance to us

A milling/flocking model without alignment, useful as a contrast to Vicsek in our experiments.
