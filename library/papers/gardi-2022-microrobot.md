## Summary

A magnetic microrobot collective at the air-water interface that reconfigures on demand between modes, from
isotropic to anisotropic behaviours, and transitions between a globally driven mode and a self-propelling mode.
Experiments and simulations show the transitions and demonstrate navigation, exploration and interaction with
the environment using reconfigurability.

## Contribution

Shows multi-mode, reconfigurable collectives at the microscale with robust transitions, extending
[[xie-2019-reconfigurable]] to self-propelled modes.

## Limitations and open questions
