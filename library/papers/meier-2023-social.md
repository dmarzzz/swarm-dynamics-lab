## Summary

A short review of cyber-enabled social influence operations that rely on sock-puppet accounts, and of how LLMs that write targeted, persuasive, near-human text could make such puppets more deceptive. It summarises recent developments at the intersection of LLMs and influence operations and lists near-term mitigation measures.
