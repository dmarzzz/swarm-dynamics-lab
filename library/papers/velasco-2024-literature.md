## Summary

Analyses 111 recent papers proposing new, hybrid or improved optimisation algorithms. Only 43% mention the no free
lunch theorem; 65% present improved versions of established algorithms rather than new analogies. Compiles results on
standard engineering design problems, and deconstructs Black Widow Optimization and Coral Reef Optimization, finding
them to be deficient mixtures of existing evolutionary operators with no innovation.

## Contribution

Quantitative snapshot of current publishing practice in metaheuristics, adding two more deconstructions to the
