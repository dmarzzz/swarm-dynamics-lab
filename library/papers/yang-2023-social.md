## Summary

A handbook chapter that reviews evidence of social bots in online discussions, the challenges facing detection methods, recent Botometer developments as a case study, and closes with a practical guide for handling bots in social media research.

## Contribution

Compact practitioner review from the Botometer group, including guidance on using detectors in research.

## Key results

- Covers presence and actions of bots, detection challenges, Botometer case study and practical handling advice (abstract).

## Methods and models

Review chapter. Abstract-level read.
