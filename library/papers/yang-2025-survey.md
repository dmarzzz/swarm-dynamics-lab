## Summary

Surveys communication protocols for LLM agents, motivated by the lack of a standard way for agents to talk to tools, data sources and each other. It proposes a two-dimensional classification, context-oriented versus inter-agent protocols and general-purpose versus domain-specific protocols, compares existing protocols on security, scalability and latency, and outlines what next-generation protocols need: adaptability, privacy preservation, group-based interaction, layered architectures and collective-intelligence infrastructure.

## Contribution

A map of the engineering protocol layer (tool-context protocols and agent-to-agent protocols) on which large deployed agent collectives run. Background for coordination-protocol questions raised in the task brief.

## Key results

- Taxonomy: context-oriented vs inter-agent; general-purpose vs domain-specific.
- Comparative qualitative analysis on security, scalability, latency.
