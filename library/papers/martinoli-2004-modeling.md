## Summary

Presents a time-discrete, incremental methodology for modelling the dynamics of distributed swarm robotic
systems at microscopic (individual robot) and macroscopic (population) levels, applied as a case study to
collaborative distributed manipulation. Per the abstract, the models are built incrementally and compared with
simulated and real robot experiments; the macroscopic level tracks expected numbers of robots in each
behavioural state.

## Contribution

A classic micro-macro modelling paper that established rate-equation (probabilistic) models for swarm robotics,
precursor to [[berman-2009-optimized]] and surveyed by [[elamvazhuthi-2019-mean]].
