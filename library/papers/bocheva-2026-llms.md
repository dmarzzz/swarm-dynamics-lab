## Summary

A scoping review of 200 academic, policy and industry sources on whether LLM-powered social bots persuade, how well, and through what mechanisms. Evidence suggests LLM content can match or beat human persuasion and is hard to tell apart from human writing; effects depend on personalisation and adaptive interaction and may be amplified by coordinated activity, but evidence is mixed and long-term behavioural effects are poorly understood.

## Contribution

The most recent review of the LLM social-bot literature, from the persuasion side rather than detection.

## Key results

- 200 sources reviewed (abstract).
- LLM content can match or exceed human persuasiveness and is hard to distinguish from human writing; evidence mixed and context-dependent.
