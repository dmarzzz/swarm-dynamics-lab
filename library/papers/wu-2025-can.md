## Summary

Uses Knight–Knave–Spy logic tasks to examine six debate factors, including team composition, confidence visibility and depth. The abstract attributes success mainly to reasoning strength and diversity, while identifying majority pressure as an obstacle to independent correction.

## Contribution

Separates structural debate choices from reasoning capability using verifiable logic tasks.

## Key results

Reported claims are summarized above; no local experimental result.

## Methods and models

Controlled logical reasoning; six factors and process-level analyses, as stated in the abstract.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract read only; no extracted effect size or replication. Diversity claim should be compared with Ferreira rather than assumed universal.
