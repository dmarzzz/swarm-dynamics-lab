## Summary

Reviews cooperative transport by ants as a problem in statistical physics. Ants appear to communicate
through forces on the load, so collective transport maps to an Ising model with ant decisions as spin flips.
The group is poised near the transition between uncoordinated and coordinated motion, gaining both coordination
and maximal responsiveness to temporarily informed leaders. The authors call for a formalism combining
statistical physics with individual cognition.

## Contribution

Review consolidating the ant-transport criticality story ([[gelblum-2015-ant]]) and mapping it to
Ising physics.

## Key results

- Cooperative transport mapped to an Ising model; group poised near coordination transition (review of prior experiments).

## Methods and models

Review of experiments with Paratrechina longicornis and theory.
