## Summary

Overview of the criticisms of evolutionary and bio-inspired computation: inadequate benchmarking, problem-specific
overfitting, insufficient theory, and proposals justified only by metaphor. Analyses the positions in the literature
and summarises guidelines for designing optimisers, running experimental comparisons and deriving genuinely novel
proposals, with a note on automating algorithm creation.
