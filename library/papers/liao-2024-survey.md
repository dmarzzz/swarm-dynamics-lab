## Summary

Survey of resilient coordination in cyber-physical systems focused on node injection attacks. Proposes a taxonomy by the multi-layer CPS framework and reviews resilient consensus from three perspectives: physical structure, communication mechanism and network topology. Extends to multi-robot systems and smart grids, resilient containment and resilient distributed optimisation, and lists future directions.

## Contribution

A recent control-theory review that explicitly organises the literature around injected (fake) nodes, the control community's name for Sybil-like attacks.
