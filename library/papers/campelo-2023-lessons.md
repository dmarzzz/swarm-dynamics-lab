## Summary

Discusses the explosion of metaphor-centred optimisation methods inspired by birds, mammals, fish, invertebrates,
sports, reincarnation, zombies and gods, arguing that hundreds of barely distinguishable variants under different
names have been counterproductive: they neither improve understanding or simulation of biological systems nor
contribute generalisable design principles. Examines causes, consequences and efforts to rebalance inspiration and
scientific soundness.

## Contribution

Written by the maintainers of the Evolutionary Computation Bestiary (github.com/fcampelo/EC-Bestiary, which per
[[kudela-2023-evolutionary]] lists over 250 metaphor methods); the artificial-life community's view of the problem.
