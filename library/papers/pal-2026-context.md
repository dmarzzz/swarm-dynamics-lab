## Summary

The paper combines a novelty-based cache, recurrent summary and recency window according to the information a task needs. Small experiments distinguish tasks requiring exact recall from those benefiting mainly from compressed summaries or nearby context.

## Contribution

Novelty-gated memory; character control and synthetic Medicare event-stream tasks.

## Key results

The abstract reports full-attention performance with attention to roughly half the tokens on a matched character-level control; benefits vary by task.
