## Summary

Review in Trends in Ecology and Evolution that proposes a general definition of swarm intelligence, as group living enabling solutions to cognitive problems beyond single individuals, to unite work on animal groups with human applications such as prediction markets, elections and product development. It sets out criteria for swarm intelligence to operate and areas for progress, and warns that the topic attracts wild speculation.

## Contribution

Bridges animal collective decision research ([[couzin-2009-collective]], [[conradt-2005-consensus]]) and human crowd-wisdom applications ([[dyer-2009-leadership]]).
