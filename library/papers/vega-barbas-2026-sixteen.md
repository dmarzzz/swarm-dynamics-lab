## Summary

Sixteen LLMs from ten families formulate psychotherapeutic cases; their Vendi-score semantic diversity averages 1.69 distinct formulations against 1.43 from one model repeated, over 7,082 formulations of 15 vignettes. A preregistered per-model dissent contribution shows model identity structures dissent, but scale and family explain it only partly and the most divergent voice changes with panel composition.

## Contribution

An effective-number measure of ensemble diversity on a task with no single correct answer.
