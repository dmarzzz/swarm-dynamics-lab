## Summary

Review of programmable active matter, systems that combine information processing (spins, molecular sequences, ion
concentrations, shapes) with energy transduction (chemical, magnetic, electrical to mechanical), across scales from
supramolecular systems to colloids, microorganisms, droplets and macroscopic robot swarms; focuses on how programmable
local interactions produce global functions and speculates on a soft-matter brain. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
