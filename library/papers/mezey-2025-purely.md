## Summary

A decentralised swarm of terrestrial robots that achieves polarised, collision-free collective motion using only minimal visual interactions computed on board from each robot's own camera stream, with no central processing or communication. Agent-based simulations show ordered motion can emerge even with a strictly limited field of view and in confined spaces, and highlight limitations.

## Contribution
A physical demonstration of vision-only collective motion with onboard processing, a step beyond motion-capture-driven robot experiments such as [[zheng-2024-body]].

## Key results

- Robots (abstract): polarised motion and collision avoidance from onboard vision alone.
- Simulation (abstract): order persists with limited field of view and confinement.

## Methods and models

Minimal visual interaction model computed from each robot's camera stream (model equations and hardware not read).
