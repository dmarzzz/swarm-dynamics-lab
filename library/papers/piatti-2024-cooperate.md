## Summary

GovSim is a generative simulation of a common-pool resource (for example a fishery) in which a society of LLM agents must balance exploitation against sustainability through negotiation. All but the most powerful LLMs fail to reach a sustainable equilibrium; the best survival rate is below 54%. Communication between agents is critical for cooperation, failures stem from inability to reason about long-term group-level effects of actions, and "universalization" moral reasoning prompts substantially improve sustainability.

## Contribution

A tragedy-of-the-commons benchmark for LLM societies, showing collapse is the default outcome.

## Key results

- Highest survival rate below 54% across tested LLMs (abstract).
- Removing communication harms cooperation; universalization reasoning helps (abstract).

## Methods and models

Multi-round harvesting with negotiation phases; open and closed LLMs. Code released (not opened this session).

## Limitations and open questions

Small groups (a handful of agents); single resource dynamics. Abstract-level read.
