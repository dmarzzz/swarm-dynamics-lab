## Summary

Redner, Hagan and Baskaran simulate self-propelled Brownian hard spheres (disks) interacting only by excluded volume.
Without any alignment the system shows anomalous number fluctuations and phase separation; they quantify the phase
diagram and separation kinetics with simulations and an analytic model. The dense phase is an "active hexatic": crystal-
like structure near the hexatic transition but fluid-like (viscoelastic) rheology and transport. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

With [[fily-2012-athermal]], the canonical simulation evidence for MIPS of repulsive active Brownian particles;
the structural characterization was later completed by [[digregorio-2018-full]].
