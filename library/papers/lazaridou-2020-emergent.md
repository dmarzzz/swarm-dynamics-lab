## Summary

A survey of language-emergence studies among deep agents from two angles: the scientific one, where emergent communication in communities of networks can shed light on human language evolution, and the applied one, where agents that communicate with each other and with humans should be more flexible and useful.

## Contribution

The standard review of emergent communication; frames the referential-game and grounded-communication literature descending from [[foerster-2016-learning]] and [[mordatch-2018-emergence]].
