## Summary

A survey of approaches to analysing and controlling systems with very many agents, from epidemics to robot swarms to finance, with MARL as the model-free route. Because scalability makes standard MARL intractable for large populations, it brings in adjacent fields that model large populations, namely mean-field games and control, collective intelligence and complex network theory, as sources of tractable formulations, and it surveys applications for large-scale control.

## Contribution

The only review in this set organised around the many-agent scaling problem itself, bridging mean-field theory ([[lauriere-2022-learning]]), swarm robotics and MARL; from the Koeppl group, which also produced the swarm MDP ([[sosic-2017-inverse]]).
