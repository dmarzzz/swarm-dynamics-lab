## Summary

Combines video of wild starling flocks pursued by a robotic falcon (RobotFalcon) with StarEscape, a data-driven 3D agent-based model. In the field, flock members often differed in evasive manoeuvres and several collective escape patterns arose simultaneously in different parts of the flock. In the model, individual-level rules reproduce starling-like escape; patterns depend on how fast escape information propagates, escapers' positions relative to the predator, and the flock's previous state (hysteresis). Preprint at bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.10.27.620514.

## Contribution

A rare field-plus-model study of murmuration escape dynamics with a controllable predator.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): heterogeneous evasive manoeuvres; co-occurring patterns within one flock.
- Model (abstract): propagation speed, predator-relative position and hysteresis shape escape patterns.
