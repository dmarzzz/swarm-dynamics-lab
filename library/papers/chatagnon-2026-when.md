## Summary

Proposes a minimal two-level pedestrian model that couples upper-body and leg dynamics, so that individuals can switch between balanced and unbalanced states. Unlike existing two-dimensional contact-based dense crowd models, this coupling produces emergent collective behaviours seen empirically in ultra-dense crowds, including self-organised waves and large-scale rotational motion.

## Contribution

Brings biomechanical balance into dense crowd models and claims to reproduce rotational collective motion, a phenomenon also reported in [[gu-2025-emergence]] from a mean-field active-matter view.

## Key results

- Simulated (abstract): self-organised waves and large-scale rotations emerge only with body–leg coupling.

## Methods and models

Two-level agent model (upper body plus legs). Preprint, January 2026.

## Limitations and open questions

Preprint, not peer reviewed; abstract only. Quantitative comparison with data not checked.

Alternative microscopic mechanism for crowd rotations; a test case for whether internal degrees of freedom of agents change swarm phases. See [[gu-2025-emergence]], [[ma-2025-unraveling]].
