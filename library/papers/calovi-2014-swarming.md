## Summary

Maps the phase diagram of the data-driven fish school model of Gautrais et al. (2012, fitted to barred
flagtails, Kuhlia mugil) beyond the measured parameters, plus a variant with frontal preference. For groups
of order 100, it studies the speed-induced transition to schooling and the conditions for milling. Finds a
transition region with multistability and intermittency between schooling and milling for identical
individual parameters, and that milling does not occur for arbitrarily large groups because of
distance-dependent interactions and information delays. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

Connects a model fitted to real fish data to the classical state diagram, confirming multistability as a
generic feature ([[tunstrom-2013-collective]], [[couzin-2002-collective]]).

## Key results

- Simulated: swarming, schooling and milling phases; multistable/intermittent region; no milling for very
  large groups.

## Methods and models

Persistent-turning-walker style model with attraction and alignment fitted from data; group sizes ~100.
