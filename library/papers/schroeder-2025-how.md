## Summary

A Policy Forum-style piece arguing that swarms of collaborative, malicious LLM agents can coordinate autonomously, infiltrate online communities and fabricate consensus at scale, mimicking human social dynamics and threatening democratic discourse. Because the harms stem from design, commercial incentives and governance, the authors prioritise interventions at multiple leverage points with pragmatic mechanisms over voluntary compliance.

## Contribution

High-profile statement (Science, 22 authors spanning computational social science and AI) that "AI swarms" are a societal risk category, framing fabricated consensus as a collective-dynamics attack.
