## Summary

Proposes dynamical equations for strongly polarized groups that can perform correlated collective turns using
only social forces. Exploiting rotational symmetry and conservation laws, the authors formulate the theory in
generalized coordinates for velocity directions (akin to a Hamiltonian treatment of rotations) and derive the
corresponding individual-level model: the "inertial spin model". In the overdamped limit it reduces to the
Vicsek model, which dissipates rotational information and cannot produce polarized turns; at large scales it
reduces to Toner-Tu hydrodynamics. Read at abstract level from arXiv. Crossref dates the issue to 2014
(online); the volume is 2015.

## Contribution

The model behind the linear, undamped information propagation seen in turning starling flocks
([[attanasi-2014-information]]): adds a spin (behavioural inertia) variable conjugate to heading.

## Key results

- Claimed: Vicsek is the overdamped limit; new model supports polarized collective turns and second-sound-like
  propagation.

## Methods and models

Inertial spin model: each agent has velocity and an internal spin generating rotations; social alignment
torque, friction and noise.
