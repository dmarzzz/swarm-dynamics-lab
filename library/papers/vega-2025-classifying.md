## Summary

A conceptual note proposing a framework that separates externally observable states from latent, unobservable ones, so that existing definitions of 'swarm' and 'emergence' can be compared on common ground. The authors argue both concepts are ultimately observer-dependent and that a swarm is defined by the process generating the group behaviour, not the behaviour alone. They draw a line between multi-robot systems and 'true swarms'.

## Contribution

A careful definitional piece that complements quantitative emergence measures (information-theoretic, order parameters) with an observer-centred view.

## Key results

- A conceptual framework, no experiments.

## Methods and models

Formal separation of observable and latent states, applied to existing definitions.
