## Summary

Applies a coordination-detection framework to May 2024 US election conversation on X and finds a network of inauthentic accounts with similar link-sharing that pushes users to other platforms and suspicious low-quality sites, which in turn promote the same X and YouTube accounts. Members also shared AI-generated images. X had suspended part of the network, but more than 75% remained active.

## Contribution

An in-the-wild IO case from 2024 that included generative-AI content, with a measured platform-enforcement gap.

## Key results

- More than 75% of the coordinated network still active after X suspended a subset (abstract).
- Network shared deceptive AI-generated images alongside attack content.

## Methods and models

Co-URL similarity networks and coordination detection; content analysis. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Single month of data; attribution of the operator unknown.
