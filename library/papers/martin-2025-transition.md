## Summary

Introduces a minimal two-species agent-based model with motility and nonreciprocal interactions that can be coarse-grained exactly into fluctuating hydrodynamics, valid in the nonlinear regime. Without motility it finds two transitions to oscillatory phases (Hopf and saddle-node-on-invariant-circle bifurcations). With motility, weak nonreciprocity accelerates flocking and synchronises bands; strong nonreciprocity replaces flocking with a "chase and rest" phase that linear phenomenological models miss. Finite-size fluctuations can reveal microscopic nonreciprocity from macroscopic time correlations.

## Contribution

Exact micro-to-macro route for nonreciprocal flocking; a method to detect nonreciprocity from data.

## Key results

- Claimed (abstract): chase-and-rest phase at strong nonreciprocity; weak nonreciprocity speeds up flocking.
- Claimed (abstract): nonreciprocity measurable from macroscopic time-correlation functions in finite systems.

## Methods and models

Lattice/agent model, exact coarse-graining, dynamical systems analysis, simulations.
