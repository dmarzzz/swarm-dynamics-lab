## Summary

A longitudinal map of text-repetition coordination in X's Japanese-classified stream: 734,173 Chinese-language coordinated accounts and about 495 million coordinated posts from May 2024 to March 2026. Most of these accounts are crude and show classic automation marks; the same criterion yields a six times lower share among Japanese-language accounts. Content is mostly non-political, and most reactions in large cascades come from the coordinated accounts themselves.

## Contribution

Recent large-scale evidence that much coordinated automation on X in 2024-2026 is still crude spam rather than sophisticated LLM agents.

## Key results

- 734,173 coordinated accounts, about 495M coordinated posts (May 2024 to March 2026).
- Coordinated share among Chinese-language accounts six times higher than among Japanese-language accounts under the same criterion.
- Within large cascades, most reactions come from coordinated accounts themselves; potentially harmful or illegal posts draw a higher share of outside reactions.

## Methods and models

Text-repetition-based coordination criterion over a stream sample; cascade analysis. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Detects only crude repetition, so sophisticated LLM coordination would be missed by design. Preprint posted days before this scan.

## Relevance to us

A recent negative-space data point: the bulk of detectable coordinated automation is crude, which says little about how much LLM-driven coordination exists below the repetition threshold. Compare [[yang-2023-anatomy]] and [[ricker-2024-ai]].
