## Summary

Robots in a single-file loop, interacting via position sensors, reproduce the traffic fundamental diagram: free flow turns into congested flow with stop-and-go waves as density rises. Long, repeatable runs show that above a density threshold the mean jam duration and the number of robots per jam diverge. This reveals a second transition from intermittent congested flow to a fully congested state.

## Contribution

It shows robot swarms are a controllable, repeatable experimental model for traffic and many-particle jamming physics.

## Key results

- Fundamental diagram with a free-flow to congestion transition and stop-and-go waves (measured).
- Divergence of mean jam duration and jam size above a critical density, a second transition to total congestion (measured).

## Methods and models

Physical robots in single-file circulation with sensor-based following; statistics over extended runs.
