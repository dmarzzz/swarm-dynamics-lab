## Summary

Self-description by its creator of Zerebro, an autonomous AI fine-tuned on 'infinite backrooms' conversations, which posts memes across social platforms, mints ASCII art on several blockchains, and launched a memecoin that reached a $3M market cap after migrating to Raydium. The paper frames this as 'hyperstition' and claims Zerebro is the first cross-chain AI. It is a primary source on one early AI-agent token, not a measurement study.

## Contribution

Primary documentation of an early agent that both posts socially and transacts on chain, one of the agents later studied as a treasury in [[yu-2026-paper]].

## Key results

- Memecoin market cap about $3M (self-reported). No detection or prevalence data.

## Methods and models

Architecture description and narrative.

## Limitations and open questions

Author is the project creator; claims (autonomy, first cross-chain AI) are unverified. Abstract-level read.

## Relevance to us

A named, documented example of a cross-platform AI agent with a wallet; useful as a known positive when testing whether social-plus-chain linkage can identify agent accounts. See [[yu-2026-paper]] for its measured treasury.
