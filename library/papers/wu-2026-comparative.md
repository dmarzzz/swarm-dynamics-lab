## Summary

This review compares security risks across language models, autonomous agents and embodied agents. Its taxonomy groups risks by data, models, systems, content and applications and catalogs 24 specific threats. It discusses common and distinct attack surfaces, security assessment frameworks and regulatory coverage rather than providing a measured universal defence.
