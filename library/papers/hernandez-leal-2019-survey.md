## Summary

An overview of multiagent deep RL that also takes a critical stance. It revisits components from classical multiagent learning and RL and shows how they were adapted to deep settings, gives practical guidelines and lessons for newcomers, points to benchmarks, and raises practical problems such as implementation difficulty and computational demands. Its categories include analysis of emergent behaviours, learning communication, learning cooperation and agents modelling agents.

## Contribution

The deep-MARL survey most explicit about "emergent behaviour" as its own research category, and a counterweight to hype through its critique section. Complements [[busoniu-2008-comprehensive]] and [[gronauer-2022-multi]].
