## Summary

Replaces the "bird's-eye" social forces of classical models with a model in which each agent responds only
to its visual field (the projection of others on its retina). Shows that such purely vision-based
interactions are sufficient to generate organized collective behaviour without explicit spatial
representation and without collisions, and provides a mathematical framework for perception-based
interactions. Read at abstract level via Europe PMC.

## Contribution

Leading example of the move from position-based to perception-based interaction models, echoed by
[[pearce-2014-role]], [[strandburg-peshkin-2013-visual]], [[lavergne-2019-group]] and the vision-based robot
swarms of the 2020s.

## Key results

- Claimed: vision alone (no spatial representation) suffices for organized collective motion and collision
  avoidance.

## Methods and models

Agents with a 1D visual projection field; movement response is a functional of the visual field and its
derivatives.
