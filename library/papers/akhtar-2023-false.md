## Summary

A systematisation of social media manipulation that ties together false information, bots and malicious campaigns, including user psychology and bot prevalence and tactics, which the authors say earlier surveys treated separately. It identifies common threads and gaps and calls for interdisciplinary work.

## Contribution

SoK-style overview linking bots to campaigns and false information.
