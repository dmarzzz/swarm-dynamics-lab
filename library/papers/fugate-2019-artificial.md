## Summary

A position and overview article by authors at SPAWAR Systems Center Pacific and the US Department of Defense. It starts from the asymmetry that defenders must cover every vulnerability while attackers need one, and argues for cyber deception that changes the attacker's perception of where systems and data are. It proposes combining game-theoretic models of defender strategy with AI methods that estimate attacker behaviour, and argues that adaptive cyber deception reduces defender risk while raising attacker cost and detection likelihood.

## Contribution

A programmatic case, from a defence-research perspective, for adaptive, AI-driven deception as a standard layer of network defence.
