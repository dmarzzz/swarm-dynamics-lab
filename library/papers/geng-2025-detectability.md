## Summary

Position paper arguing there is no consistent definition of "LLM-generated text": detection targets usually cover only part of what LLMs can produce, and human edits plus LLM influence on human writing blur the line. Existing benchmarks do not match real deployment conditions, so detector numbers are often misread; results should be treated as references, not decisive indicators.

## Contribution

Conceptual critique of the detection target itself.

## Key results

- Argued (abstract): benchmark scores do not transfer to real-world conditions; detector outputs are references, not verdicts.
