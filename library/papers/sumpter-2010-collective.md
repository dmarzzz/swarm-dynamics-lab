## Summary

Monograph that synthesises mathematical modelling, theoretical biology and experiments on how animals move
and arrive together, transfer information, make decisions, synchronise and build collective structures,
combining behavioural ecology with self-organization and complex-systems ideas from physics. Only the
publisher's description was read (312 pages, published 17 October 2010).

## Contribution

The standard textbook on collective animal behaviour from the biology/modelling side.
