## Summary

Builds on the result that standard multi-agent debate cannot beat majority voting because correlated errors pull agents toward a wrong consensus. AceMAD has agents predict their peers belief distributions; truth-holders anticipate the crowd misconception, which under a proper scoring rule and nonlinear aggregation gives positive drift toward truth. Reported gains on hard subsets of six benchmarks.

## Contribution

A peer-prediction mechanism to escape the debate martingale.
