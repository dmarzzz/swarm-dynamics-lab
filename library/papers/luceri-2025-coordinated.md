## Summary

Adapts coordination networks to TikTok using synchronised posting, similar captions, reused media and hashtag-sequence overlap, with graph pruning, on 793K videos about the 2024 US election. Finds synchronised amplification and semi-automated content replication using AI-generated voiceovers and split-screen formats. Classic indicators transfer, but transcript similarity and Duet/Stitch interactions do not work as signals.

## Contribution

First empirical test of CIB detection on a video-first platform, including AI-voiceover content farms.

## Key results

- 793K TikTok videos analysed.
- Traditional coordination indicators generalise to TikTok; transcript-text similarity and Duet/Stitch signals proved ineffective (abstract).
- Observed semi-automated replication using AI-generated voiceovers.

## Methods and models

User similarity networks over several behavioural traces, graph pruning. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

No ground truth of operators; detected groups are 'likely coordinated'.

## Relevance to us

Evidence that coordination methods port across platforms while content signals do not. Method lineage: [[pacheco-2020-uncovering]]; survey: [[mannocci-2024-detection]].
