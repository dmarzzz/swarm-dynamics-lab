## Summary

Review of AI-generated text forensic systems organised into three pillars: detection (is the text AI-generated), attribution (which model produced it) and characterisation (what intent lies behind it, for example propaganda). It catalogues resources and discusses challenges for forensic systems facing LLM-scale misinformation.

## Contribution

Adds characterisation of intent to the detection and attribution pair, closer to what influence-operation analysts need.
