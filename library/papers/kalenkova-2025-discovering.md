## Summary

Argues that because LLM-generated text is hard to detect, coordinated AI-driven campaigns must be found from metadata such as post timings. Applies process-mining methods, which discover process models with routing constructs, to Twitter/X event data and shows that structural and behavioural properties of the discovered process models can reveal coordinated AI and human behaviour.

## Contribution

Brings process mining from business-process analysis into coordination detection, framed explicitly around AI-driven campaigns.

## Key results

- Discovered process models' structure distinguishes coordinated from organic behaviour on real X data (abstract; qualitative).

## Methods and models

Event logs of posts and interactions, process discovery algorithms, comparison of model properties.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; no quantitative detection results in the abstract.
