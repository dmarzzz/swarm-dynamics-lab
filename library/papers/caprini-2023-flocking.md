## Summary

Caprini and Löwen show in simulations that active Brownian particles with attractive but non-aligning interactions can
flock: a first-order transition from a disordered phase of small clusters to a single flocking cluster, with scale-free
velocity correlations in the flocking state and exponential decay otherwise. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Part of the "flocking without alignment" line with [[das-2024-flocking]] and [[baconnier-2025-self]].

## Key results

- First-order transition to flocking from attraction alone (abstract).
- Scale-free connected velocity correlations in flocks (abstract).

## Methods and models

ABP simulations with attractive pair potential. arXiv:2303.07746; full text not read.

Simulation; inertia and parameter dependence not recorded here.

## Relevance to us

Cohesion rules alone may create flocking in a swarm, and scale-free correlations (as in starlings) are not proof of
explicit alignment. Related: [[das-2024-flocking]], [[mahault-2019-quantitative]].
