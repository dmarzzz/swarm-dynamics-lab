## Summary

Speck, Bialké, Menzel and Löwen coarse-grain repulsive active Brownian particles systematically into an effective
Cahn–Hilliard equation, showing phase separation maps onto that of passive particles at large scales. They find a lower
density threshold for clustering, with hysteretic nucleation first and a spinodal-like instability above a higher
threshold, in agreement with particle simulations. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
