## Summary

Physics Reports review of vehicular traffic as a system of interacting particles driven far from equilibrium, explaining the principles behind all main modelling approaches and concentrating on particle-hopping (cellular automaton) models such as Nagel–Schreckenberg and its variants. Phenomena covered include dynamical phase transitions, criticality and self-organised criticality, metastability and hysteresis, and phase segregation.

## Contribution

The definitive review of the cellular-automaton and driven-diffusive-system line of traffic modelling (TASEP-like exclusion processes), complementary to the continuum-and-force view of [[helbing-2001-traffic]].

## Key results

- Review claims (abstract): traffic CA exhibit transitions between dynamical phases, metastability, hysteresis and phase segregation, analysable by exact and mean-field methods of non-equilibrium statistical physics.
