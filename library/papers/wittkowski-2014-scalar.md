## Summary

Wittkowski and colleagues introduce Active Model B, a scalar φ⁴ field theory that breaks detailed balance minimally
through a square-gradient term λ(∇φ)² in the chemical potential. The term only modestly changes coarsening but shifts
the coexisting densities by creating a pressure jump across flat interfaces, surprising because interfaces do not affect
phase equilibria in equilibrium. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

The minimal continuum model of MIPS (summarized in [[cates-2015-motility]], read in full, Eq. 52: "uncommon tangent"
construction), later extended to Active Model B+ and Active Model H ([[shaebani-2020-computational]]).

## Key results

- Modest effect on coarsening; altered binodals via interfacial pressure jump (abstract).

## Methods and models

Field theory and numerical integration. arXiv:1311.1256; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Does not explain arrested cluster phases (noted in [[cates-2015-motility]]).

## Relevance to us
