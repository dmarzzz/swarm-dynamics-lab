## Summary

A benchmark and open-source framework for two-player, sequential, language-based economic games in three base families with consistent parameterisation (family names not checked), with measures of self-gain, efficiency and fairness. The authors collect LLM-vs-LLM and human-vs-LLM interaction datasets and find that market parameters and model choice have complex, interdependent effects on outcomes.

## Contribution

Standardised parameterisation of language-mediated two-player economic games with human comparison data.

## Key results

- Reported (abstract): outcome depends jointly on market parameters and LLM choice; no single headline number in the abstract.
