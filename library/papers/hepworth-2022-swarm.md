## Summary

Organises swarm indicators into an ontology of "information markers" that an external recognition
agent can use to characterise a swarm, its members and its collective dynamics, founding a field the authors
call swarm analytics, with a focus on shepherding and on heterogeneous and cognitive swarms.

## Contribution

A measurement taxonomy for swarms from an observer's perspective, from the robotics and
shepherding community rather than physics.

## Key results

- Framework of information markers for detecting, recognising and tracking swarm properties (conceptual).

Conceptual framework and ontology. arXiv 2208.12386.
