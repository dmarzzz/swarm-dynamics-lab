## Summary

A tutorial overview of information consensus for multivehicle cooperative control. It summarises theoretical
results on consensus seeking under time-invariant and dynamically changing communication topologies and
describes several applications of consensus algorithms to coordinating multiple vehicles.

## Contribution

The magazine-length tutorial companion to [[olfati-saber-2007-consensus]], from the group that proved the
spanning-tree condition ([[ren-2005-consensus]]); an extended conference version, "A survey of consensus problems
in multi-agent coordination" (Ren, Beard and Atkins, ACC 2005), was found in a web search but not catalogued.

## Key results

- Abstract-level: tutorial; consensus under fixed and switching topologies; vehicle-coordination applications.
