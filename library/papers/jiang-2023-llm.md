## Summary

The authors combine a learned pairwise ranker with a generative fuser to exploit differences among candidate answers. Their experiments show improvements over individual models and comparison ensembles on their evaluation suite.

## Limitations and open questions

Learned selection and fusion are established; ranking quality and evaluator bias require separate controls.
