## Summary

Instead of assuming metric, topological or Voronoi neighbourhoods, reconstructs each golden shiner's visual
field during leadership (information propagation) experiments and builds visual interaction networks.
Visual networks predict the propagation of behavioural change better, and their structure differs markedly
from metric and topological networks. Read at abstract level via Europe PMC (a short Correspondence).

## Contribution

Introduced sensory (visual) networks as the interaction structure; a direct alternative to the
metric/topological debate opened by [[ballerini-2008-interaction]]; developed further in
[[rosenthal-2015-revealing]].

## Key results

- Measured: visual networks outperform metric/topological/Voronoi in predicting information spread; structural
  differences between network types.

## Methods and models

Raycast reconstruction of visual fields from tracked positions and body postures of golden shiners.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read; short format.
