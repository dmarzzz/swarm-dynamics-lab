## Summary

Book chapter reviewing paradigms and models for pedestrian dynamics, emphasising the shift from classical approaches to models built for dense crowds, where physical contact forces shape the dynamics. It argues that new experiments and new modelling paradigms are needed to capture high-density behaviour.

## Contribution

A recent review specifically of dense-crowd modelling, complementary to the broader [[corbetta-2023-physics]].

## Key results

- Review claims (abstract): classical models are poorly suited to contact-dominated dense crowds; new experimental and modelling paradigms are needed.
