## Summary

A graduate monograph by a founder of the hydrodynamic (Toner-Tu) theory of flocking. It introduces dry active matter and explains, with hydrodynamic theory, the gradient expansion and the renormalisation group, why long-range polar order is possible in 2D for moving agents ("why a crowd of people can all walk, but not point, in the same direction"). Assumes undergraduate statistical mechanics.

## Contribution

The standard reference text for the continuum theory of polar flocks as of 2024.

## Key results

- Textbook; no new results claimed in the publisher description.

## Methods and models

Toner-Tu hydrodynamics, symmetry-based gradient expansion, dynamical renormalisation group.

## Limitations and open questions

Read the publisher description only. Biological realism (cognition, heterogeneity) outside scope.
