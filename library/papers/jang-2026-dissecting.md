## Summary

This study measures browser-extension bandwidth sharing through Mellowtel using store measurements, controlled regional experiments, and a student deployment. The abstract finds only sixteen of 116 equipped extensions actively injecting crawl iframes, geographic task disparities, and runtime code-loading and potentially unsafe crawl targets.

## Contribution

Chrome Web Store measurement, six AWS regions, and 34 students across 25 countries.

## Key results

- 16 of 116 equipped extensions actively crawl; United States nodes receive up to 8.7 times more tasks.
