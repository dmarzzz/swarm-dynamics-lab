## Summary

The authors report cases where strong-weak pairings underperform weak-weak pairings. Adaptive guidance intensity and retrieved experiences improve their evaluated reasoning, coding and routing tasks, motivating explicit tests of interface mismatch.

## Limitations and open questions

Entropy-based understanding is a proxy; distinguish better communication from extra context or inference.
