## Summary

Proposes a minority-triggered reorientation rule: when local order is high, agents sometimes follow
a strongly deviating neighbour instead of the majority. This produces heavy-tailed cascades of reorientations
over broad parameter ranges, preserves cohesion and amplifies group-level responses to localised cues, giving
critical-like fluctuations without fine tuning.

## Contribution

A parsimonious mechanism for critical-like responsiveness across parameter ranges, an alternative
to tuning near a transition.

## Key results

- Heavy-tailed reorientation cascades over broad parameter ranges (simulation).
- Enhanced collective responsiveness to localised directional cues with preserved cohesion.

## Methods and models

Agent-based flocking model with a minority-following rule. arXiv preprint 2603.07254.

## Limitations and open questions

Preprint; abstract-level read.

## Relevance to us

A rule we could implement in robot swarms to get responsiveness without precise tuning; test against
[[klamser-2021-collective]]-style controls.
