## Summary

Dmitriev and colleagues present Swarmodroid 1.0, an open-source bristle-bot platform with reconfigurable 3D-printed
bodies and electronics allowing external control of speed and basic trajectory adjustment, characterize single-robot
kinematics, and release AMPy, a Python package using OpenCV to extract swarm kinematics and compute physical quantities
of collective dynamics. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Open hardware plus analysis software for robotic active matter, filling a gap noted by the authors (many platforms are
closed or single-purpose).
