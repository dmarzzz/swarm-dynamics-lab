## Summary

Short review of ant colony optimisation (ACO), written as a follow-up to one of the most-cited articles of the first
20 years of Physics of Life Reviews (an earlier ACO article in that journal). It gives a chronology of algorithmic developments (not
applications) and then a bibliometric analysis of the ACO literature: fields of publication, geographic origin,
publication types and changes in research focus over time.

## Contribution

The most recent map of the ACO field by one of its core researchers; it fills the gap the scan noted (the 2024
Physics of Life Reviews ACO review was found but not reached). Complements the theory survey
[[dorigo-2005-ant]] and the tutorial [[dorigo-2006-ant]].

## Key results

- Claimed (abstract and highlights): journal publications on ACO rose over time while conference publications fell;
  trends in geographic origin and research focus are shown in graphs (numbers not read).

## Methods and models

Chronology of algorithm variants plus a bibliometric study of the ACO literature (database and query not read).
Elsevier full text and the CSIC repository copy (hdl 10261/377981) were both behind bot walls during this audit.

## Limitations and open questions

Bibliometric counts measure activity, not merit; given [[sorensen-2015-metaheuristics]] and
[[camacho-villalon-2023-exposing]], growth in applications papers says little about algorithmic progress. Unread
beyond the abstract.
