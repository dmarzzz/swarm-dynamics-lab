## Summary

Davis, Proesmans and Fodor derive a framework for thermodynamically optimal control of active matter, combining
stochastic thermodynamics and nonequilibrium response theory to find protocols that switch continuous- or discrete-state
active systems between states at minimum dissipation, arbitrarily far from equilibrium. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
