## Summary

The experiment varies whether sender types are fixed or reassigned across periods. Feedback depends on the treatment. The authors report more Out choices under fixed types, consistent with a self-confirming learning prediction.

## Contribution

Shows why opportunities to learn about alternatives matter for persistent decisions.

## Key results

The reported direction agrees with the treatment-specific theoretical prediction. No effect size is extracted here.

## Methods and models

Human signaling-game experiment with two types and In/Out actions.

## Limitations and open questions

Publisher abstract read only. Neither spatial mapping nor Jev was studied.
