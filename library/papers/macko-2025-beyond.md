## Summary

Reports what the authors call the first empirical evidence of LLM-generated text in recent real-world, multilingual disinformation datasets, documenting an increase in machine-generated content after ChatGPT's release and patterns that differ across languages, platforms and time periods. Positions the result between claims that disinformation fears are overblown and claims that long-tail contexts carry overlooked risk.

## Contribution

Brings prevalence measurement to multilingual disinformation corpora rather than general platforms.
