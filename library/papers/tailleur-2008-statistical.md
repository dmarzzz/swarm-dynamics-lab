## Summary

Tailleur and Cates treat interacting run-and-tumble particles (E. coli-like) in 1D, adding interactions and noise to a
drift-diffusion description, which allows domain formation by self-trapping and other collective effects to be
discussed; in some cases the dynamics maps onto a detailed-balance (equilibrium) system. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Origin of the motility-induced phase separation theory reviewed in [[cates-2015-motility]] (read in full there: this is
ref. [37] of that review, the source of the P ∝ 1/v result for interacting particles and the free-energy mapping).

## Key results

- Self-trapping and domain formation for density-dependent motility (abstract).
- Mapping to detailed-balance dynamics in certain cases (abstract).

## Methods and models

Fluctuating hydrodynamics of run-and-tumble particles. arXiv:0803.1069; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions
