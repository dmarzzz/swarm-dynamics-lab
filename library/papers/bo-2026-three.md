## Summary

Bo and colleagues build a tunable collective of magnetomechanically augmented spinning robots (MASBots) with
particle-level control of chirality, activity and pairwise forces. Increasing repulsion relative to attraction and
transverse forces, they experimentally map transitions from an odd elastic crystal to an odd viscous liquid to a chiral
active gas; the gas is non-space-filling and stabilized by long-range hydrodynamic attraction, with statistics like a 2D
self-gravitating point-vortex gas. Spinning frequency and patterned activity tune odd mechanical responses; breaking
polar and rotational symmetry gives phase separation and collective translation. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

A single robotic platform spanning odd elasticity and odd viscosity, proposed as a testbed for non-reciprocal active
matter and a blueprint for treating robot swarms as programmable matter.
