## Summary

A 98-page review of Kuramoto oscillators on complex networks: how network patterns shape local and global
synchronisation, the most used analytical approaches and numerical results, and recent variants including noise and inertia (second-order
Kuramoto, relevant to power grids). Applications in engineering, neuroscience, physics and Earth science are
discussed, along with open problems.

## Contribution

The most complete Kuramoto-on-networks reference after [[arenas-2008-synchronization]]; includes the inertia
(second-order) variant that [[dorfler-2013-synchronization]] treats from the control side.

- Abstract-level: review; covers network effects, noise and inertia variants, and open problems.
