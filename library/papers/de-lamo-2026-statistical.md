## Summary

Review of recent theory and experiment recasting fish schooling in statistical physics, centred on heterogeneous and time-dependent interactions. It covers Vicsek-type models on complex and weighted social networks (empirical link weights typically reduce global alignment), trajectory inference showing selective nonreciprocal responses (attention to faster neighbours, transient speed-induced leadership), and mesoscale avalanche-like turning cascades with scale-free size and duration statistics, aftershocks and Omori-type decay with short memory. It argues that order and near-critical variability coexist.
