## Summary

A deep-learning method that estimates probability currents directly from stochastic trajectories of high-dimensional systems, making entropy production rate (EPR) estimation feasible. The authors derive a link between the probability current and two local EPR definitions for inertial systems and apply it to a canonical flocking model, finding that entropy is produced and consumed on the spatial interface of a flock as alignment and fluctuations create and destroy order.

## Contribution

A tool to map where and when a flock is out of equilibrium.

## Key results

- Claimed (abstract): EPR localised at flock interfaces in a canonical flocking model.

## Methods and models

Score/current learning with neural networks on simulated trajectories.
