## Summary

Decomposes belief entrenchment in multi-agent debate into a biased static initial belief and homogenised debate dynamics that amplify the majority regardless of correctness. DReaMAD elicits prior knowledge to fix the initial belief and enforces perspective diversity; on a new MetaNIM Arena benchmark it gains 9.5% accuracy over ReAct and a 19.0% higher win rate than standard debate.

## Contribution

A two-cause account of why homogeneous debate reinforces shared errors.
