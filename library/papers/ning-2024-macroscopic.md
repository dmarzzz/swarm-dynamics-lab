## Summary

Review of macroscopic artificial active matter (vibrated particles, robots, camphor boats): motion models and
mechanisms of single active particles in free and complex media, collective behaviour of dry active matter in
experiments and theory, the effect of hydrodynamic interactions, and future challenges. Stresses the advantages of
macroscopic systems for studying confinement and crowding. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution
