## Summary

Review. A greenbeard gene was proposed as a thought experiment: a gene that produces a visible tag, recognises the tag in others, and directs help to tag carriers, showing that altruism can be selfish at the gene level. It was thought unlikely in nature because it needs an improbable set of properties, yet claims of real greenbeard genes have multiplied. The authors bring theory and experiments together and argue for dropping the cartoon version to refocus empirical work on the underlying concept. Abstract only.
