## Summary

Perspective arguing that animal aggregations are integrated wholes with emergent properties (pattern,
information storage, collective decisions), and asking whether all emergent properties are functional or
some are just pattern. Calls for linking theory and models to empirical work on individual choices and
trajectories within groups. Read at abstract level via Europe PMC.

## Contribution

Widely cited framing of the "is the pattern adaptive or epiphenomenal?" question that still runs through the
field ([[sumpter-2006-principles]], [[romanczuk-2022-phase]]).
