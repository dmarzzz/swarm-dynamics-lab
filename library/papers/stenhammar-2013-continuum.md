## Summary

Stenhammar, Tiribocchi, Allen, Marenduzzo and Cates derive a continuum theory for phase-separating active Brownian
particles by direct coarse-graining, with an effective bulk free energy plus density-gradient terms that break detailed
balance. Coarsening still closely resembles equilibrium phase separation, and the theory matches large-scale ABP
simulations for domain growth, topology and coexistence densities. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

The continuum model discussed in Sec. 7.2 of [[cates-2015-motility]] (read in full: coarsening exponent ≈ 0.28 in
2D), precursor of Active Model B ([[wittkowski-2014-scalar]]).

## Key results

- Continuum theory reproduces ABP domain growth and coexistence densities (abstract).

## Methods and models

Coarse-grained PDE versus Brownian dynamics. arXiv:1307.4373; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions
