## Summary

Review of the empirical data on animal groups (laboratory and field), how it has been analysed, and how
physicists have synthesised, modelled and interpreted it at individual and group scales, with a focus on
the analogies drawn between animal groups and traditional areas of physics. Read at abstract level via
Europe PMC.

## Contribution

A recent (2022) map from an experimentalist who works on insect swarms; useful as a check on which physics
analogies (active matter, thermodynamics, criticality) have held up against data.
