## Summary

A survey of LLM-based multi-agent systems organised around questions: which domains and environments they simulate, how agents are profiled, how they communicate (paradigms, structures), what mechanisms grow their capabilities (memory, self-evolution, feedback), and which datasets and benchmarks exist. Divides the field into problem-solving and world-simulation uses, and maintains an open GitHub paper list.

## Contribution

The most-cited dedicated MAS survey; a map of the early (2023-early 2024) literature and a dense reference list for backward chasing.

## Key results

- Taxonomy of communication structures (layered, decentralised, centralised, shared message pool) and paradigms (cooperative, debate, competitive). Review, no new data.

## Methods and models

Literature review. Paper list maintained on GitHub by the authors (repository not opened).

## Limitations and open questions

Pre-dates most collective-dynamics and failure studies (2024-2026); little statistical-physics perspective.
