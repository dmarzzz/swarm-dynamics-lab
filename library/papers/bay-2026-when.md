## Summary

Repeated sampling raises coverage (some sample is correct) while selection of one answer by vote stalls within a few dozen draws (the modal ceiling), and benchmark scoring saturates sooner (the correlation ceiling); past that, extra samples add cost and can entrench a confident mistake. The paper summarises the cutoff as an effective number of samples readable from any sampling run.
