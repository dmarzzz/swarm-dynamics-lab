## Summary

The author models unreliable capability advertisements as a market-for-lemons problem and proposes probabilistic capability descriptions, screening and reputation. Reliability composition and incentive conditions are central to the argument.

## Limitations and open questions

Theory and proposed architecture, not independent proof that the trust layer works for Jev/Qwen teams.
