## Summary

An invited review of evolutionary game dynamics in which individuals learn by RL (trial and error, introspective strategy refinement) instead of the usual imitation paradigm of copying successful neighbours by fixed rules. It introduces evolutionary game theory and both learning paradigms, then synthesises RL-based results on the emergence of cooperation, trust, fairness, optimal resource coordination and ecological dynamics, arguing RL offers a unified framework.
