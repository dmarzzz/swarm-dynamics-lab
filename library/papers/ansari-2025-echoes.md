## Summary

Applies three detectors (Binoculars, Fast-DetectGPT, GPTZero) to over 40,000 news articles from major, local and college outlets and finds substantial growth in generative-AI use in recent years, especially in local and college news. Sentence-level analysis shows LLMs are used mostly in introductions while conclusions stay human; AI use raises word richness and readability, lowers formality and makes local media more uniform.

## Contribution

Detector-ensemble prevalence with sentence-position and homogenisation analysis for news.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): substantial increase in GenAI use, strongest in local and college news.
- Measured (abstract): LLM text concentrated in article introductions; conclusions mostly human-written.
- Measured (abstract): more uniform writing styles, particularly in local media.
