## Summary

Compares two sources of diversity for majority-vote ensembles on binary questions (BoolQ, StrategyQA, PubMedQA): several models answering the same question versus one model answering differently framed versions of it. Question-interpretation diversity consistently gives better ensemble accuracy; model diversity typically lands between the best and worst member with no clear improvement (GPT and LLaMA analysis).

## Contribution

Evidence that input-side diversity can beat model diversity for voting ensembles.
