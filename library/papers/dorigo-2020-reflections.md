## Summary

A short Science Robotics perspective by three founders of the field (robotics, animal behaviour, collective
intelligence). Its one-line thesis: swarm robotics will move to real-world applications by leveraging
automatic design of controllers, heterogeneity among robots, and hierarchical self-organisation rather than
the flat, homogeneous swarms of most past work. Only the abstract/summary was accessible to me.

## Contribution

A agenda-setting viewpoint that motivated later work on self-organised hierarchy ([[zhu-2024-self]],
[[mathews-2017-mergeable]]) and heterogeneous swarms; companion to the longer Proceedings of the IEEE point of
view by the same authors (2021), which I could not access.

## Key results

- Claim (opinion): flat homogeneous swarms with purely local interaction are insufficient for applications;
  automatic design, heterogeneity and hierarchical self-organisation are the needed ingredients.

## Methods and models

Perspective article; no new data.

## Limitations and open questions

Position piece; the specific recommendations need to be read in the full text, which I did not see.

## Relevance to us

Frames what the field's leaders think is missing; useful for positioning a hackathon project (for example,
hierarchy or heterogeneity in an otherwise standard swarm model). See [[brambilla-2013-swarm]],
[[kegeleirs-2025-towards]], [[garzon-ramos-2024-designing]].
