## Summary

Applies nonperturbative functional renormalization group methods, numerically and analytically, to a
simplified Toner-Tu model and finds a new universality class whose scaling exponents agree well with the
large Vicsek-model simulations of [[mahault-2019-quantitative]] in both 2D and 3D; the authors argue this class
describes Vicsek's ordered flocking phase. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

Alternative 2024 theoretical resolution of the ordered-phase exponents, contemporaneous with
[[chate-2024-dynamic]]; the two approaches (FRG on a simplified model vs exact scaling relations from a
Goldstone-mode equation) should be compared before citing either as settled.

## Key results

- Claimed: novel universality class matching Mahault et al. exponents in d = 2 and 3.

## Methods and models

Functional RG on simplified Toner-Tu equations.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read; simplified model.
