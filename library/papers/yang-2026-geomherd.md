## Summary

Quantifies herding from agent-interaction graphs rather than price correlations, using Ollivier-Ricci curvature on action graphs from a persona-conditioned LLM trader simulator and the Cividino-Sornette continuous-spin substrate. A mean-field bridge links the curvature metric to the CSAD herding statistic. The detector fires a median 272 steps before order-parameter onset, a contagion detector recalls 65% of critical trajectories 318 steps early, the effective action vocabulary contracts during cascades, and the signature transfers to the Vicsek model.

## Contribution

An early-warning herding metric on interaction graphs, tested on LLM and spin substrates.
