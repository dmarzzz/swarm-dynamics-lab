## Summary

Systematic review of particle swarm optimisation covering PSO methods and applications published between 2017 and
2019. Organises the literature into a taxonomy of hybridisations, improvements and variants, and classifies
applications into health care, environmental, industrial, commercial, smart-city and general domains, with notes on
accuracy, evaluation environments and case studies, and a list of open issues.

## Contribution

The most-cited recent PSO review (1882 citations in OpenAlex), so it is the review that most applied papers cite. It
covers a narrow window (2017-2019) and is application-oriented; for theory and the canonical algorithm, see
[[poli-2007-particle]] and [[bonyadi-2017-particle]].

## Key results

- Claimed (abstract): a systematic-review-process taxonomy of 2017-2019 PSO methods (hybrid, improved, variant) and
  applications; strengths, drawbacks and open issues for each study class. No experiments.

## Methods and models

Systematic Review (SR) protocol over 2017-2019 publications. Springer full text was behind a login redirect during
this audit, so only the abstract was read; selection criteria and paper counts not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Application-catalogue reviews of this kind rarely test claims; it does not engage with the benchmarking and
novelty critiques ([[sorensen-2015-metaheuristics]], [[kudela-2022-critical]], [[vermetten-2024-large]]). Its
citation count reflects use as a generic PSO reference, not depth.

## Relevance to us

Background citation for "PSO is widely used"; not a source for load-bearing claims. Prefer [[bonyadi-2017-particle]]
for the algorithmic map.
