## Summary

The authors measure decentralization loss on 32 cooperative tasks using 11 models. Their account identifies failures of grounding, asking, integrating and re-deriving, and reports capability-dependent behavior in heterogeneous pairs.

## Limitations and open questions

Consulted v2 abstract; reported conference status was not independently verified. Two-agent findings do not directly establish large-swarm behavior.
