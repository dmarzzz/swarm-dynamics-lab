## Summary

Seven heterogeneous language models interact in controlled discussions spanning 208 runs. The author reports differentiation without assigned roles, while model names and prompt scaffolding materially influence the behavioral profiles observed.

## Contribution

Removing scaffolding reduces differentiation; names change behavior.

## Key results

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

## Methods and models

Group composition, naming and prompt interventions with behavioral coding.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract read; measured discussion behavior is not demonstrated functional specialization.
