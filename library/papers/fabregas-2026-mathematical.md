## Summary

Formalises "behavioural swarms": Cucker-Smale-type models in which each particle also carries an internal activity variable that co-evolves with position and velocity through nonlocal interactions, to capture adaptive, heterogeneous behaviour of living agents. Applications to behavioural economics and crowd dynamics; positions the framework against kinetic theory of active particles and agent-based models, and adds a section on scientific machine learning (PINNs, SINDy). arXiv title differs slightly (2508.12183, "Behavioural Swarms: Towards...").

## Contribution

A mathematical review and framework from the kinetic-theory community.

## Key results

- Review/framework; claims predictive capacity for macroscopic patterns from internal states (abstract).

## Methods and models

Cucker-Smale extensions, kinetic theory, PINNs and SINDy discussion.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; little contact with animal trajectory data.
