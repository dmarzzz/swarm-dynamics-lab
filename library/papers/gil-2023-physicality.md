## Summary

Survey arguing that the physical layer of multi-agent cyberphysical systems (wireless channels, sensing, context) can be used to infer and measure inter-agent trust. Three themes: threats specific to multi-agent CPS where agents may not trust each other (including Sybil), sensing and authentication that yield quantifiable inter-agent trust, and using those trust quantities for resilient coordination that keeps working under attack.
