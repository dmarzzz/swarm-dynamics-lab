## Summary

Survey of network attacks on multi-agent consensus covering three classes: denial-of-service, spoofing and Byzantine attacks. For each it introduces attack mechanisms, attack models and resilient consensus control structures, with tutorial-style examples, and closes with open issues.

## Contribution

Comparative review placing spoofing (identity) attacks alongside DoS and Byzantine attacks in the consensus-control literature.
