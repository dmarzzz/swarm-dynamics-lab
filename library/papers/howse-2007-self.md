## Summary

Howse, Golestanian and colleagues fully characterize the motion of a catalytic Janus microswimmer: at short times it
has a directed component whose speed depends on fuel (H₂O₂) concentration; at long times it becomes a random walk with a
much enhanced diffusion coefficient, i.e. a persistent random walk. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Canonical experimental demonstration of active Brownian motion of synthetic swimmers, the single-particle basis of the
ABP model used in [[cates-2015-motility]] and [[buttinoni-2013-dynamical]] (which uses the same MSD form).

## Key results

- Measured: fuel-dependent propulsion speed; crossover from ballistic to enhanced diffusive MSD (abstract).

## Methods and models

Experiment on catalytic Janus colloids in fuel solution (PRL 99, 048102). arXiv:0706.4406; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Single particles only.

## Relevance to us

The MSD formula ⟨Δr²⟩ = 4D₀t + (v²/2D_r²)(2D_r t + e^(−2D_r t) − 1) (form as used in [[buttinoni-2013-dynamical]]) is
how we would calibrate any agent's activity from tracking data. Related: [[bechinger-2016-active]].
