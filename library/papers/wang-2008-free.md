## Summary

This paper proposes a free-roaming mobile-agent protocol combining anonymous onion routing with a chain relation of two forward hops and k anonymous backward hops. The retrieved abstract argues resistance to colluding truncation attacks while protecting previously visited servers and limiting communication overhead.

## Contribution
