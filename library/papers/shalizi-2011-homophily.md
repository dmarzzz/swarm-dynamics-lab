## Summary

Shalizi and Thomas show how latent homophily, contagion and individual traits can generate confounded observational network patterns. Similar behavior across linked participants therefore need not identify a causal influence effect without additional assumptions.

## Contribution

Formal counterexamples constrain social-contagion inference.

## Key results

Asymmetric regression coefficients do not by themselves identify contagion.

## Methods and models

Skimmed introduction, causal examples, figures, constructive responses and conclusion; proofs not fully checked.

## Limitations and open questions

The result concerns identification under latent confounding, not impossibility under randomized interventions.
