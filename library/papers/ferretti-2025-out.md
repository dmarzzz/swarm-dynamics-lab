## Summary

Studies the response of self-propelled particle flocks to perturbations and shows that equilibrium
fluctuation-dissipation relations between response and correlation are violated both locally and globally.
Violation is strongest at the ordering transition, mirroring the entropy production rate. Entropy is produced
locally and linked to local violations via Harada-Sasa relations, but near the transition interactions spread
non-equilibrium effects to the whole system.

## Contribution

Shows that inferring response (susceptibility) from correlations, as done in many criticality
studies of flocks, is not exact in active systems and is least reliable near the transition.

## Key results

- FDR violations peak at the ordering transition, as does entropy production (simulation/theory).
- Local entropy production relates to local FDR violation via Harada-Sasa; near criticality effects become system-wide.

## Methods and models

Vicsek-type self-propelled particle models; linear response and correlation functions; Harada-Sasa
relation. arXiv 2405.12874.

## Relevance to us
