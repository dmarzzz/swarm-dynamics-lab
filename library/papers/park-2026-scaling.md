## Summary

Communicating teams solve research-style tasks more effectively than independent samples in the authors’ evaluations. The abstract also reports failures when compute is limited or useful intermediate progress is difficult to recognize.

## Contribution

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

Communicating versus independent teams on ARC-AGI-3 and discovery tasks.
