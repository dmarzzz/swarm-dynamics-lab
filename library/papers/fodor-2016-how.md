## Summary

Fodor and coauthors study interacting particles driven by persistent (Ornstein–Uhlenbeck) noise and show that for
small but finite persistence time the dynamics still satisfies time-reversal symmetry: the entropy production rate
vanishes perturbatively, giving an effective fluctuation–dissipation theorem; they also reinterpret such systems as in
equilibrium with a viscoelastic bath but driven by non-conservative forces. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Quantified "how far from equilibrium" active matter is and introduced the active Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle (AOUP)
perturbative framework; part of the irreversibility literature reviewed in [[obyrne-2022-time]]. 663 OpenAlex
citations.

## Key results

- Entropy production vanishes to leading order at small persistence time (abstract).
- Effective FDT in that regime (abstract).

## Methods and models

Perturbative steady-state measure for AOUPs. arXiv:1604.00953; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Small-persistence expansion; MIPS and flocking regimes are far beyond it.
