## Summary

Kuramoto's monograph on reduction methods for nonlinear oscillators and reaction-diffusion systems: phase
reduction of weakly coupled limit-cycle oscillators, the mean-field model of mutual entrainment now called the
Kuramoto model and its self-consistent solution, chemical waves and phase turbulence (Kuramoto-Sivashinsky
dynamics). It is the book-length source cited for the model by [[strogatz-2000-kuramoto]],
[[acebron-2005-kuramoto]] and the swarmalator papers.

## Contribution

Seminal monograph that introduced phase reduction and the Kuramoto self-consistency analysis to a wide audience
(the model's first statement was a 1975 conference proceedings paper).

## Key results

- Book-level: phase reduction; mutual entrainment of oscillators with distributed frequencies above a critical
  coupling; phase waves and chemical turbulence. Not read; chapter records "Chemical Waves" and "Chemical
  Turbulence" appear in Crossref under this book's DOI.
