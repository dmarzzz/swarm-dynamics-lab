## Summary

Applies integrated information (Phi) to groups of people and computers: four-person work groups with
higher Phi score higher on collective intelligence tasks, Wikipedia editor groups with higher Phi produce higher
quality articles, and the Phi of internet communication rose over six years.

## Contribution

Shows Phi can serve as a group-interaction metric correlated with performance in human and
human-computer collectives.

## Key results

- Higher Phi correlates with higher collective intelligence in 4-person groups and with Wikipedia article quality (measured correlations).

## Methods and models

Phi computed on time series of group communication activity. arXiv preprint 1702.02462 (longer
title).

## Limitations and open questions

Correlational; small groups; Phi approximation choices. Abstract-level read.

## Relevance to us

Relevant if the hackathon measures coordination in LLM agent collectives: a precedent for
integration metrics predicting group performance. See [[niizato-2020-finding]].
