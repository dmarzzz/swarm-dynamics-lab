## Summary

Builds a fully 3D, data-driven model of pairwise attraction and alignment interactions in rummy-nose tetra (Hemigrammus rhodostomus) from pairs swimming freely in a hemispherical bowl; simulations reproduce empirical distributions of speed, distance and relative orientation. The model is then run in closed-loop virtual reality so a real fish interacts in real time with a virtual conspecific driven by the same rules, validating that the model captures the key social interactions.

## Contribution

Extends the Theraulaz-Sire 2D interaction-reconstruction method to 3D and adds a closed-loop biohybrid validation step.

## Key results

- Model vs data (abstract): quantitative agreement for speed, distance, orientation distributions in 3D.
- Closed-loop VR (abstract): real fish coordinate with the model-driven virtual fish.

## Methods and models

3D tracking in a bowl, reconstruction of interaction functions, closed-loop VR (details not read).
