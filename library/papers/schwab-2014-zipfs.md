## Summary

Shows analytically and numerically that Zipf's law, taken by [[mora-2011-biological]] as a signature
of criticality, arises generically in latent-variable (mixture) models in which an unobserved variable such as
a stimulus modulates many observed units. No fine tuning of parameters is required.

## Contribution

Seminal critique of Zipf-based criticality claims.

## Key results

- Zipf-like rank-frequency distributions arise naturally from unobserved fluctuating variables, without fine tuning (analytic and numerical).

## Methods and models

Latent variable models of binary units; large-N analysis of entropy-energy relation. arXiv
1310.0448.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read.
