## Summary

'Analytical Swarm Chemistry' treats controller parameters of minimally capable robots (binary line-of-sight sensing) like thermodynamic variables. It defines macrostates and draws phase diagrams that show which parameter regions reliably produce behaviours such as milling and diffusion. The authors identify sufficient conditions for those behaviours and report preliminary real-robot validation that the predicted regions match observed behaviours.

## Contribution

It brings phase-diagram thinking from statistical physics to the design of computation-free swarms, connecting behaviour discovery ([[mattson-2025-discovery]]) with predictable deployment.

## Key results

- Parameter-space regions that reliably produce milling and diffusion, with sufficient conditions (claimed in abstract).
- Preliminary real-robot validation (claimed in abstract).

## Methods and models

Macrostate definitions plus phase-diagram sweeps over controller parameters for line-of-sight unicycle agents.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-depth entry. Preliminary hardware validation. Minimal capability model only.
