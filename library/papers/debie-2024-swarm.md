## Summary

A survey of why robot swarms are rarely used for multi-tasking. It reviews multi-tasking swarm studies by
platform, scenario, sub-task allocation method and performance metric, discusses neighbouring disciplines that
affect multi-tasking, and proposes two taxonomies: one by the characteristics of the scenario and one by the
swarming strategy used to get multi-tasking capability. It ends with limitations and recommendations for
real-world multi-tasking.

## Contribution

Extends the task-allocation strand of [[brambilla-2013-swarm]] to concurrent and sequential multi-task
settings.

## Key results

- Taxonomies of scenarios and strategies (organisational, not empirical).
