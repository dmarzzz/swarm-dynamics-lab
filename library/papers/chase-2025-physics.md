## Summary

Review of temporal and spatiotemporal dynamics in group sensing and decision-making, emphasising links between models and experiments. It covers temporal models (consensus, excitation-inhibition networks), spatiotemporal dynamics in sparse groups (information propagation and movement synchronisation with SPP models parameterised by length and time scales), and crowding in dense groups using soft condensed matter (interactions as physical potentials and forces). Focus on invertebrates (e.g. fireflies, bees), with broader applicability.

## Contribution

The most recent Annual Review touching collective motion from a physics angle; spans sparse flocks to dense aggregates.
