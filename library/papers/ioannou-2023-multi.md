## Summary

Prologue review of a Philosophical Transactions B theme issue arguing that collective behaviour research, including group decision-making, has mostly studied snapshots of seconds to hours, and that understanding requires longer timescales: how individuals and their interactions change over development (ontogeny) and across generations (evolution).

## Contribution

Recent agenda-setting review (2023) that frames decision-making as one property among information transfer and group size that depends on timescale; complements the evolutionary modelling in [[hein-2015-evolution]].

## Key results

- Identifies developmental and evolutionary timescales as under-studied in collective behaviour (conceptual).
- Abstract-level reading only; numbers beyond the abstract were not checked.

Review.

Broad scope; not decision-specific.
