## Summary

E. coli populations partition a costly peroxide-detoxification function. Helper and dependent cells coexist for at least 1,200 generations in three replicate populations under peroxide exposure, while dependence on helpers persists.

## Contribution

Loss of a costly, leaky function can maintain interdependence.

## Key results

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

## Methods and models

Experimental bacterial evolution with plasmid-encoded detoxification.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract read. Tool-policy pruning is an analogy, not a claim of changing foundation-model weights.
