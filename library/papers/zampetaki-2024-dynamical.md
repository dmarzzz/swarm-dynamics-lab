## Summary

Treats zebrafish groups as interacting active particles and studies how two-body and many-body spatial correlations and dynamical order parameters depend on group size. Three fish is the smallest group that shows the multistate behaviour of schooling, milling and swarming, and states of groups of three resemble those of much larger groups. Subgroups of three sampled from larger groups behave much like isolated triplets, suggesting fish interact mainly with nearest neighbours and treat the rest as a fluctuating background.

## Contribution

Quantifies the minimum group size for multistate collective behaviour and supports nearest-neighbour dominated interactions.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): multistate behaviour from N = 3; little change in state occupancy or correlations for triplets embedded in larger groups.

## Methods and models

3D tracking of zebrafish (likely, not checked), correlation functions and order parameters from soft-matter physics.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; lab tank conditions.
