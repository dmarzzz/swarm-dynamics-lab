## Summary

Perspective (review) from the IRIDIA group on how blockchains can enhance mobile multi-robot systems: making autonomous robots identifiable and accountable, ensuring adherence to applicable laws, identifying and neutralising malfunctioning robots, and letting robots take part in economic transactions. Surveys first applications, open challenges and research directions.

## Contribution

The current reference review for blockchain-coordinated robot swarms, summarising [[strobel-2018-managing]], [[strobel-2020-blockchain]], [[strobel-2023-robot]] and related work.

- Review; no new measurements.
