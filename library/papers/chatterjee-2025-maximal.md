## Summary

Directly tests maximal susceptibility at criticality in cooperative transport by Paratrechina
longicornis ants. A robotic system that mimics a transient leader applies ant-scale tactile forces to a
food-carrying group; the group's response, measured in sub-, near- and supercritical regimes set by group size,
is maximally amplified at the critical group size, consistent with theory and simulations.

## Contribution

Claimed as the first direct experimental demonstration of peak collective response at
criticality, using a controlled perturbation rather than inferred correlations.

## Key results

- Collective response to an external force peaks at the critical group size (measured with robotic perturbation).
- Agreement with theory and simulations of the cooperative transport model.

## Methods and models

Robotic force application to ant groups carrying loads; group size as control parameter; Ising-like
cooperative transport model.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read; one species and task.
