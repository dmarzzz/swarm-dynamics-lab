## Summary

Review of distributed ledger use cases in multi-robot systems. Finds that despite scalability concerns, new designs tolerate intermittent connectivity and permissioned networks scale; argues that most work on open, permissionless blockchains only fits a subset of robotics use cases, and that novel DLT architectures and permissioned ledgers drive adoption in industrial settings.

## Contribution

Counterweight to [[dorigo-2024-blockchain]]: argues permissioned ledgers, which settle Sybil resistance by an admission authority, are what actually gets deployed.
