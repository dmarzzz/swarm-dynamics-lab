## Summary

Review of information cascades in non-human animals (ants, fish, social mammals) proposing a classification adapted from behavioural economics: cascades classified by fitness outcome (adaptive, maladaptive, neutral) and by transmission mechanism (signals or cues), plus four mechanisms animals use to limit maladaptive cascades.

## Contribution

Recent terminology-cleaning review; useful for connecting quorum and positive-feedback errors ([[sumpter-2009-quorum]], [[sasaki-2013-ant]]) to the economics cascade literature.

## Key results

- Framework of three outcome types times two transmission types (conceptual).
