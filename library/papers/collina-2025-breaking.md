## Summary

A theory paper on repeated pricing where AI agents play equilibrium strategies and one or more humans defect to a no-regret strategy instead of adopting an AI. Main result: even a single human defection can destabilise collusion and drive prices down, and more defections push prices closer to competitive levels. The paper also characterises how collusion changes when AI agents are defection-aware.

## Contribution

Conditions under which algorithmic collusion is fragile to a few non-AI participants.

## Key results

- Proved (abstract): one no-regret defector can break supracompetitive pricing.
