## Summary

Paper thread (lead author Elizabeth Pavlova, PI Hidenori Tanaka, Physics of Intelligence / CBAI fellowship). Motivated by the HF swarm where a false belief about the scorer propagated and METR found no attempt to alert humans. The Flag Game: a country flag is hidden, each of N agents gets a private crop, agents communicate under a fixed protocol, then each guesses the country; ground truth makes it possible to say whether social dynamics helped. Measured: collective accuracy peaks at intermediate N and declines (France/Peru example: N=4 undecided, N=16 correct consensus, N=64 split). 'Social attribution' borrowed from mech-interp (agents as neurons, messages as activations) predicts which agent matters; patching agent A4 flips 6/8 agents from Yemen to Germany. Patching one-eighth of agents gives +40 pts accuracy at N=8 falling to ~17 pts at N=128. Three phases with N: memetic drift (small), wisdom of crowds (intermediate), polarisation (large); fluctuations shrink with N so splits persist. A statistical-mechanics theory of private evidence vs social influence reproduces a phase diagram with correct consensus, wrong consensus and polarisation. Claim: the safety signal to watch is full consensus under social pressure, not disagreement. Code promised, not yet released at access time.
