## Relevance to us

Population-level detection: tracking marker-word frequencies across a corpus estimates aggregate AI use without classifying single documents, the same move a swarm detector needs. The coevolution framing also warns that markers fade as humans and models adapt. See [[geng-2025-human]] and the related corpus-level estimator described in [[x-mahler83-1769526799510520239]].
