## Summary

Author post by James Zou, senior author of the GPT-detector bias paper. Claims reported as the paper's findings: detectors classify more than 50% of real text by non-native English speakers as AI-generated, while most GPT-polished essays evade detection. The second tweet adds that detectors tend to rate more literary language as more human, which produces the bias against non-native speakers, and that prompt design easily fools them. The advice to be cautious is the author's recommendation.
