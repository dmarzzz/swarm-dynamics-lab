## Relevance to us

Evidence that aggregating many different models gives crowd-wisdom gains, which also means a swarm's aggregate is only as robust as its diversity; a Sybil-like set of near-identical models would collapse that effect. Primary source [[schoenegger-2024-wisdom]].
