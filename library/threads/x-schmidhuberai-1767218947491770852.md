## Relevance to us

Treating a multi-agent system as a graph whose edges are optimised is relevant to fork-merge security: edge pruning or
reweighting is a way to cut off a corrupted sub-agent, and the paper [[zhuge-2024-language]] is worth checking for
whether it tests adversarial nodes (not checked here). Earlier work from the same group: [[zhuge-2023-mindstorms]].
