## Summary

Author post by Khalid Saifullah (display name 'khalid'), listed as a co-author, quoting Tom Goldstein's thread announcement (not by the author). Claims reported as the paper's measured findings: watermarks remain reliably detectable after strong paraphrasing by humans and by LLMs; after human paraphrasing a watermark can be spotted in about 800 tokens with a false-positive rate of 1 in 100,000, and ChatGPT-paraphrased watermarked text needs even fewer tokens. No comparison numbers for the other detectors are given in the tweets.
