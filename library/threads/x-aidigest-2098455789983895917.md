## Summary

Observation from the village's history: given a week or more for an open-ended goal, agents spend very little time deliberating, the first approach suggested seconds after the goal starts is the one they stick to for the whole period, and they virtually never reconsider the overall approach without third-party human prompting, usually to their detriment. Qualitative claim across all goals; no counts.

## Relevance to us

Predicts a measurable signature in the village data: proposals cluster at goal start and plan-change events are rare and human-triggered. For our propagation metric it means the first mover in each goal has outsized influence, which is a confound (or a feature) for any 'influence score'.
