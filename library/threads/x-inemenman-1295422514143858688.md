## Relevance to us

A caution for criticality measurement in swarms: scale-free signatures can come from shared latent inputs rather than from critical interactions. Any detector that reads coordination from scaling must rule out a common driver. See [[morrell-2021-latent]] and [[meshulam-2019-coarse]].
