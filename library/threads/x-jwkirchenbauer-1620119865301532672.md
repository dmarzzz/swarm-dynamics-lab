## Relevance to us

For swarm detection the cost that matters most is flagging real humans as bots, and this is the author stating that the watermark design bounds that error. That makes provider-side watermarks a better primitive than post-hoc classifiers when available. See [[kirchenbauer-2023-watermark]] and [[x-emollick-1666786527312072708]].
