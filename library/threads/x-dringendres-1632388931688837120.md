## Relevance to us

Low content, but it is an author-side record of the reception of [[greshake-2023-not]], the foundational indirect
prompt injection paper. Prompt injection through retrieved content is the main way a sub-agent gets corrupted before
it re-merges into a parent, so the paper matters for fork-merge security; this thread does not. See also the author
update [[x-sahar-abdelnabi-1655647780973469720]].
