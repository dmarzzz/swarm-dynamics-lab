Author post (Zhiwei He is in the author list). Motivation, as he states it: the autoregressive nature of LLMs makes
self-correction during generation hard, so the work asks what happens when several LLMs debate. The quoted

## Relevance to us

In debate, sub-agents' outputs are reconciled by a judge, which is another merge step where one persuasive bad
agent could dominate. The judge-fairness analysis in [[liang-2023-encouraging]] is the relevant part.
