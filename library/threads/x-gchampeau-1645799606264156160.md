## Summary

Third-party commentary in French, not by an author of [[park-2023-generative]]. It restates the paper: Stanford researchers simulated human social behaviour with 25 artificial agents that talk to each other and act autonomously in a Sims-like world; humans can intervene; in the showcase example, telling one agent that another wanted to organise a Valentine's Day party led the agents to plan it, spread the word, invite dates and show up. The last tweet is the poster's speculation: agents like these could populate social networks soon or already, with fake appearances from generative image, video and voice models, making it more important to tell bots from humans.
