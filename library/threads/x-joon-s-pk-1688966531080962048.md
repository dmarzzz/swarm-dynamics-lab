## Summary

Author thread (Joon Sung Park is the first author of [[park-2023-generative]]) marking the UIST 2023 presentation and the open-source repo. It does not restate the technical contribution; instead it gives three reflections. 1: the repo shows how the original generative agents were implemented in a full simulation game, alongside other open-source implementations such as LangChain's. 2: applications beyond games, such as immersive environments, rehearsal spaces for communication and simulations for testing social theories. 3: realising human-like agents will need design patterns and architectures beyond first-order templates such as few-shot or chain-of-thought prompts, by analogy with object-oriented programming, MVC and operating systems. All of this is opinion and outlook; no results. The thread is numbered to 14 but tweet 11/14 is missing from the bundle, so read_depth is skim.

3. What will future systems look like with LLMs? We are at an interesting point in computing history. LLMs and other foundation models have become powerful enough to give us a chance to realize our long-standing goals, such as fully general human-like agents. (8/14)
