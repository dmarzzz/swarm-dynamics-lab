## Summary

Opinion and commentary by Ethan Mollick, not an author. He calls it a destructive irony that Mechanical Turk is now just AI, and restates the paper's estimate as 33-46% of Turkers using LLMs to do tasks; the paper's own thread limits that estimate to one text-production task (summarising medical abstracts). A self-reply points to the authors' thread, and another says MTurk had been of declining research value for a while. No new data; the framing is broader than the measurement.
