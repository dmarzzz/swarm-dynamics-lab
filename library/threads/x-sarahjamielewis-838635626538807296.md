## Summary

Commentary, not an author thread: the bundle flags a match on the name Jamie (Jamie Hayes is a co-author of [[piotrowska-2017-loopix]]), but the poster is Sarah Jamie Lewis, who is not on the author list. Her opinion: Loopix's "3rd Party Anonymity" is an interesting construction, but the comparison table feels disingenuous because anonymity networks serve very different threat models and applications; it is fair to say Tor does not protect against a global passive adversary, but a system that is not a Tor replacement is not comparable. She still encourages reading the paper. No measurements.
