## Summary

Short, very widely engaged post from Nikita Bier (X) predicting that bot detection and human verification will be among the most urgent business needs in the next few years, because agent swarms will overwhelm websites and forms, with small companies and government sites most exposed. He says that when X surveyed the market for its own use, no vendor combined the latest techniques, so X built its detection and verification stack in-house. No details of that stack are given. The quote post by @tr4777 asks how to defend public MCP endpoints against "agentic swarm exfiltration" and lists rate limits, $1 sign-up fees (as early WhatsApp did) and human-adjacent bot identity, concluding a Sybil-resistant portable public-key identity plus reputation primitive seems inevitable.
