## Summary

Commentary in Chinese by a third party, not an author. It quips that AI annotators have started using AI themselves, then restates the paper: the authors re-ran a summarisation task on Amazon Mechanical Turk and, combining keystroke detection with a synthetic-text classifier, estimated that 33-46% of crowd workers used LLMs. A reply gives the opinion that human preferences cannot be labelled this way. The numbers are the paper's estimates as relayed.
