## Relevance to us

Conformity to peers is the lever a Sybil cluster pulls in a multi-agent system: if agents defer to apparent
majorities when unsure, a few coordinated fake peers can swing the group, and a corrupted sub-agent presenting its
output confidently could carry a parent. [[cho-2025-herd]] is worth reading for the confidence and presentation-format
effects. Related: majority aggregation in [[li-2024-more]].
