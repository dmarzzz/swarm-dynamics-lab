## Relevance to us

[[arora-2024-here]] is the defensive mirror of our fork-and-merge threat: merging several sibling models dilutes a backdoor in one of them. It suggests a concrete defence to test, merging a returning sub-agent with honest siblings rather than accepting it alone, and the open question of how many corrupted siblings defeat it.
