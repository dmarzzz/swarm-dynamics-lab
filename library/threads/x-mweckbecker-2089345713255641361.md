## Summary

Author thread quoting a new paper on a "mind virus", a self-propagating idea or persona that spreads between agents in a multi-agent system, and contrasting it with his own "Thought Virus" paper [[weckbecker-2026-thought]]. The one contrast archived is detectability: Thought Virus hides the bias in meaningless "subliminal tokens" entangled with the target concept, so the author says it stays undetected by an automatic monitor, while mind viruses persuade in plain English and are therefore detectable. These are the author's characterisations, not measurements shown in the tweets.
