## Relevance to us

Shows how enterprise defenders operationalised "detect agent swarms" the week after a real incident: attribute by declared operator IP ranges and audit what those IPs did. It is the simplest possible attribution method and it only catches OpenAI's own hosted agents, which is exactly the limitation worth naming in a swarm-detection survey (third-party operators of OpenAI models egress elsewhere).
