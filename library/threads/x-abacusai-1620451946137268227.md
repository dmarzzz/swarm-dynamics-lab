## Relevance to us

A pointer to [[mitchell-2023-detectgpt]], a per-text zero-shot detector that could serve as one weak signal in
a swarm detector. On its own it says nothing about accuracy; [[x-furongh-1645780628724502528]] argues that
detection improves when many samples from one source are pooled, which is the setting for account-level swarm
detection. [[x-svpino-1624030448799350784]] makes almost the same post.
