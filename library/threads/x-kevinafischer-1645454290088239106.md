## Summary

Opinion and hype from a third party. Kevin Fischer calls the Generative Agents paper possibly "the coolest paper of
our generation" and "basically Westworld": simulated agents form relationships and identities and are studied
through interviews. Follow-ups say agents emergently coordinate, little cognitive architecture is needed, and LLMs
plus some architecture are all that is required, each with a screenshot (not archived). In replies he says the
dialogue is not very realistic, GPT-3.5 is too positive for negative interactions, and he expects sociology
simulation tools. No numbers; the "little architecture" claim is his reading.
