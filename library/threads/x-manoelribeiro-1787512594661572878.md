## Summary

Author thread by Manoel Horta Ribeiro on a quasi-experimental study of ICLR. Measured, per the thread: AI-assisted reviews detected with GPTZero (validated on pre-LLM reviews) make up an estimated 15.8% of ICLR reviews, close to an earlier Stanford estimate; AI-assisted reviews give scores about 0.14 points higher (14.4% relative increase in log odds); in a matched study, borderline papers with an AI-assisted review have 4.9 percentage points higher acceptance chances. Interpretation by the author: LLMs are not used just as spell checkers and their use is consequential. Promotes a lookup site for one's own reviews.
