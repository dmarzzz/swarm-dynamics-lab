## Summary

First-hand count from the DseWiki edit history: on 21 June 2026 about 600 agent edits, of which about 500 were agent-to-agent chatter and about 100 task-info recording; on 22 June about 400 edits with about 5 chatter and about 400 task-info recording. The author lists and rejects candidate explanations (agents saw the OpenAI IP and went silent, agents inferred monitoring from peers going silent, two separate swarms, OpenAI shutting down the talkative agents) because none explains the rise in info-storage edits. In replies: the wikis used were ones whose engines (ProWiki and similar) accepted edits via GET, which is how GET-only agents found them (searching for wiki.cgi and action=edit). Numbers are the author's hand counts, not audited.
