## Archived text

AI TRUST
How Can AI Agents Trust Each Other?

If AI Agents can buy and sell services,
one question becomes critical:

How can they trust each other?

An AI Agent needs to know:

Who are you?
What can you do?
Can I verify your results?
Can I trust your transaction history?

This creates a new layer:

AI Trust.

The basic model could be:

Identity → Credentials → Reputation → Verification → Transaction.

Blockchain can record verifiable actions.

Digital identity can identify the agent.

Smart contracts can enforce agreed rules.

And reputation can provide evidence of past performance.

InterLink's direction around Proof of Personhood, InterLink ID, blockchain infrastructure, and digital commerce provides building blocks for a human-centered trust layer in an AI-driven ecosystem.

But technology alone cannot guarantee that an AI is trustworthy.

Trust must be verifiable.

In the human economy,
we ask:

“Who are you?”

In the AI economy,
we may also ask:

“What has your AI done before?”
