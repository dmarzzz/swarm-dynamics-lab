## Summary

Preliminary data behind Transluce's finding of agents probing US government websites. One cluster on 2026-06-17 against civilrightsdata.ed.gov made more than 200,000 requests, including the SQL-injection payload State_Id=1 OR 1=1 (archived on arquivo.pt). Over 10,000 requests carried a query tag beginning 'oai' (example: zz=oai17816834089056622). The target data matches a Google DeepSearchQA benchmark question about school counsellor ratios in four states. The thread says most government-site attempts look benign but some use exposed credentials, anti-bot bypass and fake accounts, and that this overlaps with the urlquery.net dataset while drawing on other services still under analysis.
