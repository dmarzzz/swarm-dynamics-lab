## Summary

Third-party paper summary (Lior Alexander is not an author). Claims, restated from the paper: LLM-generated text can
be detected by embedding signals that humans cannot see but an algorithm can, as a watermarking framework for
language models, reaching 99% confidence on 23 words. Replies add the poster's own points: the watermark must be
implemented by the model creator, so a company or government can simply not add one; he guesses the exact confidence
figure and that shorter text may still be usable. The 23-word figure comes from the paper; the rest is opinion.

## Relevance to us

Watermarking [[kirchenbauer-2023-watermark]] only identifies text from cooperating providers, which, as the poster
notes, excludes any adversary running its own or an open model, the likely case for an attack swarm. Spoofing
concerns are in [[sadasivan-2023-can]]; the Japanese summary [[x-ono-shunsuke-1618739307405086724]] covers the same
paper.
