## Summary

Author thread (Satpreet Singh is co-first author). The bundle holds posts 1-3, 7-10 and 13-15; posts 4-6 and
11-12 are missing, so read_depth is skim. Described: agents model the weakly electric fish G. petersii, which uses
electric organ discharges (EODs) to sense and communicate, in a custom physics engine that simulates the
electric landscape. Reported results, without numbers in the thread: electric-field sensing gives curved homing
paths that qualitatively resemble real fish, while a ray-casting baseline goes nearly straight; agent EOD
statistics resemble biology (heavy-tailed inter-discharge intervals, faster EOD rates while feeding, suppression
near larger fish); aggression emerges without explicit social rules beyond a higher penalty for being bitten by
larger fish; larger fish win more confrontations, and size inequality correlates with inequality in food
intake. Falsifiable predictions for experiments are listed. Code is on GitHub.
