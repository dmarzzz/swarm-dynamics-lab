## Summary

Author thread (Fernando Rosas is the first author of [[rosas-2020-reconciling]]). Claims about the paper: it uses Integrated Information Decomposition to quantify emergence from multivariate data, focused on how macro features can have causal or predictive power beyond that of the parts. Two theoretical contributions are named: a quantitative definition of downward causation, and "causal decoupling" as a complementary mode of emergence. It derives practical measures that avoid the curse of dimensionality, and with these the authors report confirming emergence in Conway's Game of Life and in certain flocking patterns, plus a short application to ECoG data on motor representations in the monkey brain. No numbers are given in the thread. The 2021 reply says he knows of no social application yet, and offers money as an emergent property with downward causation on agents, which is his speculation.

## Relevance to us
