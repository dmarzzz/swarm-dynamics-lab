## Summary

Opinion post by a third party, not an author. Mollick credits the paper for taking false positives and bias against ESL writers seriously and for working well in a lab, but says its detection rate is 90% and that in real use small, easy tweaks break detectors, which he says the paper does not test. He remains skeptical. The 90% figure is his summary of the paper; the robustness criticism is his opinion, not a measurement.
