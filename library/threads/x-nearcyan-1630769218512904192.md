## Summary

Third-party commentary, not by the authors. It restates the demonstration: by modifying a website that Bing Chat reads alongside a user, an attacker can change the chat agent's goals without the user knowing, and links a demo and the arXiv paper. In a follow-up the poster gives his opinion that LLM plus infosec will become a very large field, because people are hooking LLMs into many APIs, making indirect prompt injection, jailbreaking and outer alignment failures important.
