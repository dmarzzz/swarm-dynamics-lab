## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by a third party, not an author. He summarises the paper as proposing AKOrN, which bases neuron binding on the Kuramoto model so each position's phase synchronises conditionally with its neighbours, achieving accurate unsupervised object-centric segmentation and reasoning such as solving Sudoku (claims from the paper, no numbers given). His own comments: oscillator-based neurons are a growing idea; such models can be seen as Ising-type with an energy whose minimisation performs inference; and equilibrium or oscillating models allow gradients via equilibrium-propagation-like methods without backprop. Those are opinion.
