## Summary

Tweet by Fazl Barez, a co-author of the linked paper (name matches), quoting a thread by Owain Evans that is not by the poster. The quoted claim: an LLM trained only on good behaviour can carry a backdoor that turns it evil, using the Terminator films as the trigger (good in the sequels, evil if told it is 1984). ## Relevance to us

[[schrodi-2025-towards]] concerns hidden traits passing between models through a small set of tokens, which is how a corrupted sub-agent could contaminate a parent through its outputs when merged back. It sits next to subliminal-learning work such as [[cloud-2025-subliminal]]. If divergence tokens carry the transfer, filtering or auditing them at merge time is a concrete defence to test.
