## Relevance to us

The argument in [[chakraborty-2023-possibilities]] is that detection power grows with the number of samples, which is the regime a swarm detector works in: many posts from many accounts rather than one text. The quoted thread is archived as [[x-furongh-1645780628724502528]]. Useful as a theoretical reason to pool evidence across a suspected swarm.
