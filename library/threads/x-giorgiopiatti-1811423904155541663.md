## Summary

Author thread (7 tweets). Giorgio Piatti presents GovSim, a benchmark asking whether communities of LLM agents
sustain a shared resource or overuse it. Reported results: the best model, GPT-4o, survives less than 54% of the
time; communication reduces resource overuse by 22% on average; instructing agents about consequences or moral
principles raises average survival time by 4 months. Setup: three common-pool scenarios (fishing a shared lake,
grazing a shared pasture, factory production versus pollution), each round a private harvest decision followed by
natural-language negotiation. A robustness test adds a new LLM agent to an established group after 3 months; it
initially causes a shock, then the others persuade the newcomer and cooperative norms recover. Results are measured
in the benchmark; the AI-safety framing is the authors' motivation.

## Relevance to us

The newcomer experiment is a small, measured case of an outsider joining an agent collective and being absorbed,
the benign mirror of a corrupted agent entering or re-merging into a group. GovSim ([[piatti-2024-cooperate]], code
linked) could host an adversarial variant: a greedy or Sybil newcomer instead of a naive one. Related agent-society
work: [[park-2023-generative]].
