## Summary

Author thread, verified (Kalpesh Krishna is first author). Measured: an 11B paraphraser (DIPPER, trained on aligned translations of novels, Par3) defeats DetectGPT, watermarks and GPTZero across 3 LMs including GPT3.5-003 and two datasets; DetectGPT accuracy falls from 70.3% to 4.6% while meaning and quality are kept, and rates drop below 10% overall. Proposed defence: API providers keep a database of their outputs and retrieve against it; over 15M generations it detects 80-97% of high-diversity paraphrases while flagging 1% of human text. He disputes the concurrent claim that detection is unsolvable and lists ways his study goes further. The paper discusses the defence's limitations.

**3. @kalpeshk2011, 2023-03-24 14:33 UTC** (status 1639274169001992192)

To stress test detectors, we build a 11B size paraphraser (DIPPER) which can 1) paraphrase long text; 2) use context outside inputs like user prompts; 3) control output diversity.

We train DIPPER on pairs of aligned English translations of non-English novels (the Par3 dataset). https://t.co/NngNMtQjAS

**4. @kalpeshk2011, 2023-03-24 14:34 UTC** (status 1639274377639329795)

Across 3 large LMs (including GPT3.5-003) and two datasets, we find that current detectors are surprisingly brittle to DIPPER paraphrases.

Paraphrasing drops the accuracy of DetectGPT from 70.3% to just 4.6%, while preserving the semantics and quality of the input LM generation. https://t.co/smgatGIprC

**5. @kalpeshk2011, 2023-03-24 14:34 UTC** (status 1639274496174465025)

How can we defend against paraphrase attacks?

We propose that language model API providers store a database of their API outputs.

Given a candidate text, API providers retrieve over this database to detect whether it was API-generated, akin to traditional plagiarism detectors. https://t.co/jki8ZNWcwS

**6. @kalpeshk2011, 2023-03-24 14:35 UTC** (status 1639274640177524740)

## Relevance to us

The retrieval defence is a provider-side detection model: whoever served the generations can recognise them later, even paraphrased, which is relevant to attributing swarm content to a model provider. It needs provider cooperation and output logging. Paper: [[krishna-2023-paraphrasing]]; the opposing view is [[x-feizisoheil-1638198588634275841]].
