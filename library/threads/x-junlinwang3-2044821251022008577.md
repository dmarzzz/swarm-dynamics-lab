## Relevance to us

Majority vote versus learned aggregation is the merge step of fork-and-merge. An aggregator that reads and
synthesises sub-agent outputs ([[wang-2024-mixture]]) is more capable than voting but may also be easier for one
corrupted branch to steer than a vote count; that trade-off is our inference, not the thread's. Compare
[[li-2024-more]].
