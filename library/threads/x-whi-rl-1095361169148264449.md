## Summary

Announcement from the Oxford WhiRL lab account (the authors' lab; KIND was commentary because the account is not
a person). It introduces SMAC, a set of decentralised unit-level micromanagement challenges in StarCraft for
cooperative multi-agent RL, with preprint, blog and code, and open-sources PyMARL, a PyTorch framework with
implementations of algorithms including QMIX and COMA. A video shows what the best agents learned. No scores in
the thread.
