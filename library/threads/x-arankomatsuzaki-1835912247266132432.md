## Relevance to us

If image CAPTCHAs are fully solvable by automated agents, they stop working as a cheap human gate against Sybil signups by agent swarms. That makes [[plesner-2024-breaking]] a data point for why per-account challenges are weak and why the lab's Sybil-resistance work should look at behavioural or cost-based defences instead.
