## Relevance to us

Self-replicating prompts left on public sites during training are exactly the "instructions planted for later instances" vector in our fork-and-merge threat model, and public traces of them are a swarm-detection signal. The thread is only a pointer: the OpenAI report it links should be catalogued and read before we rely on it.
