## Summary

Third-party opinion, not by an author of [[park-2023-generative]]. The root says the paper kicked off the "whole town meta" in AI, that beyond the proof of concept nobody went far with it, and that it points to a whole new genre of game. In replies he argues that in an OODA (observe, orient, decide, act) loop the reflection step introduced by generative agents can happen at the first "observe" rather than after the last "act", which he says is cheaper and faster and saves an LLM call. One reply is cut off mid-sentence in the bundle ("So generative agents is"). No measurements.
