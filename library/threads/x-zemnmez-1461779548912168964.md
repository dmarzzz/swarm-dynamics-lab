## Relevance to us

Wash trading is a market Sybil pattern: one actor trading with itself through many accounts to fake activity. The
statistical approach in [[cong-2021-crypto]] (the "statistical analysis" the thread mentions) is a model for detecting coordinated fake activity without identifying individual accounts. See also
[[x-martincwwalker-1467134373539590151]].
