## Summary

Commentary (not by an author) recommending the paper "Deanonymizing Ethereum Validators: The P2P Network Has a Privacy Issue". Restated claim: running an Ethereum validator node lets any minimally sophisticated party link the node's IP address to its ETH addresses. In replies to others he gives opinions: he is surprised no major government has yet attacked Bitcoin or Ethereum P2P networks and expects it soon; he thinks Bitcoin is more robust to partitioning and deanonymisation than Ethereum; and he argues validators' strict latency requirements make p2p-level privacy tech essentially impossible to adopt. The last three points are his speculation.

## Relevance to us

Network-layer linkage of many validator identities to one operator is exactly the kind of operator attribution a Sybil detector wants, and the same leak works against honest operators. See [[heimbach-2024-deanonymizing]].
