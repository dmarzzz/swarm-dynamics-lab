## Summary

Ryder claims that failed arbitrage bots account for over half of Solana transactions and that Jito has distributed more than 4.3 million SOL in MEV rewards. The post argues that cheap retries can be rational and promotes direct execution bidding through Raiku JIT instead. These numbers are claims in the post, not independently checked measurements.

## Archived text

Over 50% of transactions on Solana today are failed arbitrage bots spamming the network for a chance at MEV.

They're not "wrong" to do it  with fees this low, spamming positive-EV trades pays off over time, even with a majority failure rate. But it means half of Solana's throughput is bots retrying, not humans transacting.

Jito alone has distributed over 4.3M SOL in MEV rewards since launch, proof of how much value is being fought over in that spam.

Raiku's JIT lets searchers bid for bundle execution directly instead of flooding the mempool with retries  MEV capture without the network-wide spam tax.
