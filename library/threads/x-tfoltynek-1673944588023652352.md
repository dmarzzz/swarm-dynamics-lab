## Summary

Author announcement. Tomas Foltynek, a co-author, announces the preprint "Test of Detection Tools for AI-Generated
Text": the team tested 14 tools on a set of 54 documents. In a reply he states the conclusion: an easy solution for
detecting AI-generated text does not exist, and educators should focus on prevention and on assessing the process of
skill development rather than the final product. The counts are from the study; the teaching advice is opinion.
