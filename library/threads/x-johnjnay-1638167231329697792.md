## Summary

Third-party paper summary (John Nay is not an author). It restates the claims of arXiv 2303.11156: attackers can use
a paraphraser to remove LLM signatures, and can query a watermarked LLM to learn the scheme and spoof it; even the
best possible detector performs only marginally better than a random classifier. The last point is the paper's
theoretical bound as the poster renders it, not a measurement. An attached figure is not archived.
