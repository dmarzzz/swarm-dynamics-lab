## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by Kyosuke Nishida, not an author. Summarises Social GAN (Stanford): it predicts the trajectories of several people at once and avoids predictions that are physically plausible but socially wrong, such as collisions. The generator is an encoder, pooling module and decoder; the pooling module models interactions using each person's encoded state and relative positions to others. Description only, no reported numbers.
