## Summary

Commentary (not by an author) quoting a thread on the paper, then a first-hand anecdote from 2010 Facebook fake-account work. Only tweets 1, 3 and 5-10 of the 10-tweet thread are in the bundle (2 and 4 missing), so this is a skim. Reported by him: computing the local clustering coefficient for a large sample of accounts surfaced a cluster of outliers with zero edge density; the "SI team" then found the accounts shared simple account-creation signals (device etc.) from an endpoint that skipped normal checks; it was 1-2 million accounts while Facebook added over 1 million per day organically, so a growth forecast moved only about a day. His later attempts with graph-theoretic measures added nothing over simple existing signals; his favourite was the interquantile range of friends' ages, which humans have naturally and spammers find hard to fake. Lesson stated: keep detection simple and close to context.
