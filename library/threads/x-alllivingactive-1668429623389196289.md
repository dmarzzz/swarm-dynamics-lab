## Summary

Author thread by Nitin Kumar, last author of the linked paper (name matches the author list), described as the first work from his lab. Claims: self-propelled differential-wheeled robots can be programmed to show active Brownian, run-and-tumble and Brownian motion over a wide range of tunable parameters. The robots are 7.5 cm in diameter, carry an Arduino and onboard IR and light-intensity sensors, and respond to light, which gives spatiotemporal control over their dynamics. The third tweet credits his PhD student. No quantitative results are given in the thread.
