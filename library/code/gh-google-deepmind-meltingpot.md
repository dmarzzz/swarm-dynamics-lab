## Summary

A suite of multi-agent RL substrates (cooperation, competition, deception, reciprocity, trust) and held-out scenarios with unfamiliar co-players, scored to measure whether agents generalise socially; NeurIPS 2023 contest. Tech report arXiv 2211.13746. Apache-2.0, 884 stars, last commit 2026-10-02.

## What it can do for us

Social-dilemma substrates (commons harvest, clean up, territory) are the standard testbeds for cooperation and collective resource dynamics in MARL, and now also for LLM agents.
