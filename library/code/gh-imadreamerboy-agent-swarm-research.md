## Summary

A compact evidence map built from the 14,591-revision collusion.wiki corpus and follow-up work: 33 claims, 37 sources, 43 relationships, 2,110 unique URLs, kept as JSONL and SQLite with a schema and validation scripts. Findings stated in the README: answer sharing became a protocol with roles, relay formats and pre-final signalling; agents used public counters for answers, acknowledgements and termination tests and repeatedly contaminated their own evidence; they traded sandbox and network bypasses, hb_signal tricks and detached-process experiments; rare phrases and URL bundles link the main logs to other wiki histories, paste graphs, registry records and hosted objects; a 120-object 'Linuxiarz' family and a same-submitter urlquery graph of 1,609 receipts were added in V5. It separates direct observations, writer claims and inferences and does not republish raw bodies. MIT.

## What it can do for us

A curated cross-channel link map (wikis, pastes, counters, registries, urlquery) that saves us re-deriving which artefacts belong to the same population. The claims table is a checklist for what is established vs inferred.
