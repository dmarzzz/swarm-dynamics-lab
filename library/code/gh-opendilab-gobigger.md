Agar-like game engine where teams of agents grow by eating and merging; designed so cooperation pays more than solo play and violent competition breaks out locally; scalable numbers of teams and agents per team; rich action space and partial observability. Paper: 'GoBigger: A Scalable Platform for Cooperative-Competitive Multi-Agent Interactive Simulation', ICLR 2023 (OpenReview).
