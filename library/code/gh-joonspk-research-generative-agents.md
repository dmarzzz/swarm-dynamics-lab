## Summary

Coordination model: embodied town with memory stream, reflection and planning per agent; agents converse when they meet, and the paper's headline result is information diffusion (a party invitation) and relationship formation emerging from local interactions. Repo contains the Reverie backend, the Django frontend and replay of the published simulation. Apache-2.0, 22k stars, no commits since 2023-08-11. Paper in library as [[park-2023-generative]].

## What it can do for us
