## Summary

Marchetti separates energy-consuming local drive from ordinary thermal fluctuations. At 12:00-32:00, active Brownian particles with only steric repulsion can phase-separate: persistent collisions slow propulsion, density rises, and the slowdown reinforces accumulation. This motility-induced phase separation cannot generally be represented by heating passive colloids; effective temperature fails when interactions make it state-dependent. At 34:00-49:00, coupled director and flow equations describe active nematics. Comet-shaped +1/2 defects self-propel, while -1/2 defects lack net propulsion by symmetry. Activity can drive oppositely charged defects apart despite elastic attraction. At 50:00-54:00, four +1/2 defects on a sphere oscillate between tetrahedral and planar arrangements, with periods depending on activity and size. At 54:00-59:00, flocking on a sphere produces equatorial bands and a theoretical unidirectional, topologically protected sound mode. Questions through 66:00 clarify incompressibility and the limitations of deriving continuum parameters from low-density microscopic approximations.
