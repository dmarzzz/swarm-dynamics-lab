## Summary

The first part describes TERMES robots with mechanical stair-climbing, simple manipulation, corrective sensing and compiler-generated growth rules. A robot reobserves the structure on return; direct robot communication is chiefly collision avoidance. The second part contrasts three construction robots with thousand-Kilobot shape formation. A roughly twelve-hour collective run exposes traffic jams and rare hardware or message failures hidden by small experiments. Q&A acknowledges that stuck construction robots are not yet removed automatically, and identical-program robots focus on one task rather than demonstrating rich caste allocation.

## Relevance to us

Concrete reliability boundary: being able to repair placement errors is different from removing a permanently stalled agent or orchestrating diverse roles. Complements the separate 2015 lecture [[nagpal-2015-taming]] without claiming they are the same recording.
