## Summary

At 3:13-5:28 Werfel challenges the classic cement-pheromone explanation of termite construction, reporting evidence for recruitment by active diggers and responses to local ground shape instead. The shared environment closes a feedback loop between behavior and geometry. At 9:15-11:37 he translates this to robot construction rules that preserve access and avoid unfinished walls while supporting unpredictable arrival orders and numbers of builders. He explicitly distinguishes animal variability from robot-rule models and presents lunar construction as a future application, not a completed deployment.

## Relevance to us

Stigmergy offers task-specific coordination without centralized assignments, but guarantees require the stated common-rule and reachable-structure assumptions. The biological mechanism correction cautions against recycling convenient ant/termite stories as settled science.
