## Summary

Costanzo asks whether alignment alone can generate milling without attraction, repulsion or confining walls. At 03:30-07:30, the Vicsek model is modified with a rear blind angle and a maximum angular turning rate. Random initial conditions in a periodic square produce multiple and counter-rotating mills. At 07:30-11:00, milling is identified jointly by low polarization and high normalized angular momentum, since angular momentum alone can misclassify bands. The reported protocol uses 100 runs, 2000 time steps, a box of side 20 interaction ranges, and averages over the last 500 steps. Milling appears around a 200-degree field of view and a speed-to-turn-rate ratio near one, with sufficiently low noise and high density. At 11:00-15:00, mixed populations either induce rotating motion in otherwise non-milling particles or destroy mills when speeds differ too much.

## Relevance to us

A minimal and implementable alternative to multi-zone flocking models. Useful order-parameter warning: rotation alone does not establish a vortex. Heterogeneous-population experiments could test entrainment versus disruption rather than assuming additional agents preserve a collective state.
