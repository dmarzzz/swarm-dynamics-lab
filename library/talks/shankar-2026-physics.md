## Summary

Shankar gives a public introduction to energy-consuming collective materials, with live demonstrations. At 16:00-24:00, minimal flocking rules and synthetic electrically activated beads motivate emergent collective motion. At 27:00-30:00, evaporation-driven propylene-glycol and water droplets demonstrate chase-like behaviour without life, showing why behaviour alone does not establish a biological mechanism. At 34:00-55:00, bacterial swarms, aligned rods and motor-driven filaments introduce nematic order and motile half-charge defects. Defect locations are linked to cell accumulation, bacterial layers and hydra morphogenesis, as possible organizing principles rather than a sole explanation of development. At 55:00-61:00, light-patterned activity manipulates defects, while actin-myosin sheets illustrate how similar stresses can cause fracture in solids instead of flow. Questions through 94:00 distinguish statistical time-reversal breaking from ordinary dissipative relaxation, identify biological error correction as an energy-consuming process, and leave a universal definition of life unresolved.
