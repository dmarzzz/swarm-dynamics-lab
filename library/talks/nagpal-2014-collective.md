## Summary

Nagpal distinguishes collective decision-making, where everyone may agree, from construction, where different local decisions must fit one global structure. An abstract tile compiler combines stored local state with contiguous-growth invariants and has guarantees for restricted shapes without holes. Stigmergy transfers information through locations and modifications rather than direct conversations. The physical TERMES implementation co-designs staircase locomotion, grippers and construction material; robots reobserve current work rather than rely on stale global memories. The talk explicitly leaves reliable compilation against arbitrary errors unresolved.

## Relevance to us

A constructive global-to-local methodology with a clearly limited admissible shape class. Links abstract proofs to the separate difficulties of sensing, manipulating and correcting embodied systems.
