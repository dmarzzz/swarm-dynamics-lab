## Summary

Salles distinguishes theoretical extractable value from observed realized extraction at 4:37-6:02. At 6:05-9:54 he decomposes searcher and producer flows, opportunity cost and unsuccessful competitive attempts, arguing that extraction-related network cost deserves separate accounting. At 10:00-16:54 he enumerates ambiguities: signer versus protocol participant versus network operator, permissioned gains such as airdrops, reverted transactions, state-transition scope, cross-domain units and single-block versus multiblock rights. At 17:00-21:08 he sketches a deliberately provisional definition conditional on ordering privileges and capital; taking a minimum over actors to represent permissionlessness can collapse to zero for censored actors, an acknowledged defect.

## Relevance to us

Essential measurement discipline for distinguishing addresses, operators and strategic players. Do not equate detected arbitrage totals with all available MEV or interpret a conceptual proposal as a proved canonical definition. Related empirical coverage limits: [[raj-2022-case]].
