## Summary

The talk abstract presents social reinforcement learning as a way to improve communication, coordination, and generalization. It describes causal social influence for coordination and the PAIRED method, where an adversary creates environments maximizing regret between two learners. The abstract claims more than 20-fold improvement on unknown test environments, without supplying the baseline or full protocol. Human affective cues and personalized affect detection are also discussed.
