## Summary

Marchetti distinguishes scalar, polar and nematic active systems. At 17:00-27:00, persistence and steric collisions generate motility-induced phase separation without attraction. At 28:00-33:00, active-passive mixtures exhibit nonreciprocal cross-diffusion and traveling density patterns, linking activity to time-dependent phases rather than only aggregation. At 35:00-54:00, coupled nematic order and flow are reduced to interacting defects: +1/2 defects self-propel, while -1/2 defects are passive to leading order. A one-dimensional escape picture must be corrected for orientational persistence to predict a finite unbinding threshold. At 56:00-60:00, patterned light-controlled motor activity is proposed as a topological tweezer for moving defects and programming flows. Questions through 71:00 address chirality, hydrodynamics and uncertain activity calibration. The sign of activity can often be inferred from defect motion, but a convincing absolute measurement is not asserted.
