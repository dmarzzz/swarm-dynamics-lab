## Summary

Transcript coverage: 0:00-34:52, including closing discussion when present. Panelists from zkPass, Polygon ID and Manta explain proving credential predicates while hiding underlying attributes, proving possession of a key and nonrevocation, and using application-specific identifiers to avoid cross-service tracking. They stress that reusing an Ethereum address can defeat this separation. Discussion contrasts centralized KYC followed by private credential presentation with proving locally that a digitally signed identity document is valid. Improved languages, cryptographic tooling and proving efficiency are credited for deployability, while application adoption and business incentives remain obstacles. Audience Q&A emphasizes trade-offs between proof-generation latency and onchain verification cost rather than declaring one proof system uniformly best.

## Relevance to us

ZK limits disclosed information, but uniqueness still depends on credential issuance and the application-scoped duplicate-prevention mechanism. Do not infer Sybil resistance from proof validity alone. Related privacy and cost-of-forgery caveats: [[reclaim-2024-frontiers]].
