## Summary

The published abstract describes geometrically polar granular rods in a vibrated two-dimensional layer, with propulsion direction set by rod polarity. Adding spherical beads produces large negative velocity fluctuations, motivating large-deviation and fluctuation-relation measurements. The advertised collective result is flocking mediated by a sufficiently concentrated non-motile bead medium even at low active-rod coverage. Activity-based trapping and sorting are the final announced topic. This is an abstract-level summary, not a reconstruction of the experimental plots. The description cites Flocking at a distance in active granular matter, Nature Communications 5:4688, DOI 10.1038/ncomms5688, and sorting preprint arXiv:1603.08535.

## Relevance to us

Useful pointer to medium-mediated alignment, where passive particles transmit interactions rather than merely add noise. Primary papers are needed before implementing a bead-mediated flocking model or claiming its measured thresholds.
