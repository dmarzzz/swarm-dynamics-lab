## Summary

Kumar demonstrates GPS-free mapping and small collision-tolerant aerial robots. At 7:23-9:33 he gives three swarm principles: local neighbor awareness, anonymity to neighbor identity and time-varying mathematical target shapes. Four-robot following and circular formation adapt to membership changes without every member knowing a global trajectory. Agricultural mapping is presented alongside projected rather than measured yield/input improvements. At 15:59-16:43 a two-robot mapping experiment takes two and a half hours; reducing this to emergency-response times with a much larger swarm remains the motivating vision.

## Relevance to us

Concrete contrast between observed local-control demos and hoped-for population-scale speedup. Anonymous neighbor rules are not Sybil-resistant membership, and a human-led follower formation is not evidence that leaders are unnecessary in every task.
