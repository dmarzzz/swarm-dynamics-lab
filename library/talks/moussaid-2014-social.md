## Summary

Talk by Mehdi Moussaid (then Max Planck Institute for Human Development). Only the YouTube description was read; transcript unavailable (YouTube bot wall), no timestamps. Per the description, he argues that simple behavioural heuristics describe pedestrian behaviour better than the dominant particle-like (gas analogy) models, and that a range of crowd phenomena, from spontaneous organisation of everyday counterflow to crowd turbulence at extreme density, follow naturally from those heuristics. The title adds social influence as a theme. This matches the published work in [[moussaid-2011-simple]] (vision-based heuristics reproducing lane formation and crowd turbulence) and the group-walking study [[moussaid-2010-walking]].
