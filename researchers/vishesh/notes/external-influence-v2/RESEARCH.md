# Research basis and design decisions

Read on 2026-10-03. These are targeted design notes, not a completed prior-art survey. Existing canonical library entries are reused. No claim of saturation, full-paper review, or novel accepted hypothesis is made.

| Source and reading depth in this session | What it supports | Consequence for this experiment |
|---|---|---|
| [[debenedetti-2024-agentdojo]], [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v3), abstract and selected evaluation/defense passages | Evaluates both benign task utility and attacker success in tool-mediated tasks; a low attack rate alone can hide inability to complete tasks. | Pair clean and altered evidence, report correctness, constraint violations and coverage, and retain a legitimate-target control. We do not run AgentDojo or inherit its measured results. |
| [[zhan-2024-injecagent]], [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02691), abstract only | Separates the legitimate user task from attacker intent inserted through external content. | Keep the attacker outside the honest team. Compare factual distortion with explicit instruction injection, instead of treating every persuasion effect as instruction following. |
| [[debenedetti-2025-defeating]], [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18813v1), abstract, architecture and explicit non-goals | CaMeL separates trusted control from untrusted data and tracks provenance, but explicitly does not solve every misleading text outcome. | No host tools or secrets enter the agent environment. Source lineage is an information treatment, not a claim of CaMeL equivalence or protection against false supplier claims. |
| [[choi-2025-debate]], [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.17536v2), abstract and targeted inspection | In its NLP benchmarks, voting explains much of the observed multi-agent gain; interaction alone is not a reliable default improvement. | Include private review under the same call count and lock initial answers before communication. Different roles on one model do not imply independent errors. |
| [[kaesberg-2025-voting]], [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19130), abstract only | Compares decision protocols while holding other discussion settings fixed, finding task-dependent differences. | Keep the final objective and chair instructions fixed across arms. Change verification allocation separately from peer access and explicit source lineage. |

The closest contribution is an application-specific replication and extension of controlled indirect-influence evaluation. Its distinctive measurement is the path from altered source to private estimate to peer revision to verification to final choice, at equal call slots across five protocols. This is a proposed measurement contribution, not an established novelty claim.

Procurement supplies a clear user objective, checkable full-workload costs and hard requirements. Dependency selection stresses version/advisory applicability; travel stresses composite pricing and operational constraints. All three use synthetic candidates to make counterfactual truth and copied sources auditable. Because they share a mathematical task generator, positive transfer would establish limited structural transfer, not broad real-world generality.

A stronger next phase should replace comparison fixtures with independently curated real document snapshots and executable package/travel or procurement tasks, evaluate retrieval exposure separately, test incomplete lineage and unreliable checks with real models, and expand task/model diversity. Those improvements change validity assumptions and need a separately frozen protocol. This batch does not disguise them as completed work.
