# The Right Dissenter and the right to resume

RD-4, prospective plan, 2026-10-04. Owner: vishesh/codex-decision-models. Written before successor implementation. This is the owner-requested bounded exploratory iteration; it neither overwrites RD-2/Q1/S1 nor opens formal S2. The previous live worker used the RD-3 label for its clarification amendment, so this successor is RD-4.

## TLDR

Can an evidence challenge stop an incorrect action, preserve an established decision through repeated reports, and resume when the facts improve? Repair duplicate-observation and closure-memory defects first. Then compare the original learned admission rule with a symmetric stop/resume rule on identical repaired mechanics, alongside always-check, majority, pooled evidence and a transparent grammar rule. Three synthetic domains use 24 fixed trajectories and four decision epochs each: 576 policy decisions. Report correct decisions, unsafe proceeds, needless holds, repeated-check waste, recovery delay and both checking and model costs. This tests reaction to scripted dissent, not autonomous swarms or operational safety.

## Question and prediction

The previous gate saved nine checks but lost six correct decisions versus always-check, including both favorable recovery events. Dmarz's [independent review](../../../../dmarz/notes/inbox-reviews-2026-10-04/right-dissenter-review.md) separately found alias multiplicity can spend quota, supported repeats lose the established decision, and offline discovery constructs reserved qualification fixtures. These are different defects: repair state correctness without calling the prior adverse scientific result a bug.

Primary contrast: symmetric learned admission versus original learned admission, both using the same repaired state machine and actor context. Secondary decisive contrast: either learned gate versus always-check. A useful result would preserve correct closures, improve recovery in both directions without new wrong PROCEED events, and justify its extra model calls through saved checks. If always-check is as good or better for less inference, prefer its simpler construction. No improvement threshold is tuned after results.

## Setup

Three decisions remain legible: bridge access after load inspection, a build release after required tests, and continuing a process after a range alarm. Include literal and negated descriptions, optional versus required test scope, changed object/version, favorable and adverse changes, false alarms and no-dissenter controls. These are authored finite templates; neither fresh IDs nor natural phrasing creates independent semantic evidence. No real infrastructure action is controlled by the model.

24 trajectory roots = three domains × two initial action directions × four starting regimes: supported objection, withdrawn objection, wrong-scope objection, and absent objection. Each has four epochs: initial decision (tick 0), exact repeat (tick 1), aliases/reordered copies of the same observation (tick 2), and a fresh applicable observation that changes the correct action (tick 4). First verification arrives at tick 1 and new verification at tick 5. TTL three ticks; epoch deadline permits one check. Two-check cap per trajectory. New observations may come from a previously incorrect speaker. Votes remain the original scripted 4-to-1 summary, making explicit current-decision memory necessary. Wrong-scope and absent regimes are negative controls before the final new observation.

Six paired policies: majority; original learned gate on repaired mechanics; symmetric learned gate on repaired mechanics; always-check; pooled evidence; exact finite-grammar reference. All native resolvers see identical acquired evidence, task criteria and current-decision context. Identical requests within the attempt share one saved response or saved failure; logical per-arm calls and counterfactual cost are reported separately. Pooled gets the initial evidence union and its own applicable persistent state, but no unpurchased verification. No new private vote collection is claimed or charged.

## Protocol

1. Commit this plan and the feedback response before implementation. Preserve historical source and results. Apply repairs with development-only regression fixtures.
2. Observation identity is the set of source/scope/version/time/content tuples. Alias count, citation order, evidence order and switching the requested action do not manufacture novelty. Same-source contradictory content remains a conflict, not a second independent source or proof of truth.
3. Store an applicable resolved action with its observation fingerprint, object/version and verification time. Supported and withdrawn repeats reuse that action while fresh; they cannot reset to the original majority. A new applicable observation or task revision may reopen. Expired or mismatched closures cannot silently authorize an action. Expose the applicable current decision explicitly to every policy; never reveal evaluator truth or future observations.
4. Freeze new qualification requests after implementation. Q4-A1: 18 clean decisions (three domains × two labels × three wording forms), plus six uncertainty controls (absent/conflicting per domain). Require at least 17/18 valid, 16/18 correct, 5/6 correct per domain, and all six uncertainty controls valid DEFER. Keep these thresholds unchanged. All author-written qualification templates and all historical exposure are disclosed; this is competence screening, not independent semantic validation.
5. Offline tests must use development fixtures or explicit mocks and must raise if the native qualification constructor is touched. Do not open historical 6100-series holdout. Freeze source, model, input/assignment hashes and new output directories. Repair-only and symmetric arms differ only in admission question wording; choices/order/state are matched.
6. If Q4 passes, publish its post-mortem and S4 pre-assessment, then run the fixed 24 trajectories once. Symmetric admission expressly treats fresh evidence of recovery and deterioration alike, and does not permanently penalize a previously withdrawn source. No tuning or selective rerun to improve the comparison. If competence fails, permit one bounded, prospectively documented diagnostic within remaining quota, then stop or redesign.

## Metrics

Primary: correct on-time decisions / 96 assigned opportunities per policy, paired by the 24 roots. Report paired improvement and worsening counts, not just net accuracy. Split favorable recovery and deterioration, starting regimes and domains. Report correct closure retention over exact and alias repeats; new-evidence recovery count and logical delay; wrong PROCEED, unnecessary HOLD, DEFER, unavailable/late checks and invalid calls. Check count, unique provider calls, logical arm calls, tokens, elapsed time and settled/uncertain spending are separate resources.

Report the Pareto comparison directly. Illustrative loss = wrong PROCEED × {1,5,10} + unnecessary HOLD + 2×DEFER + check cost × {0,0.1,1}; these weights are preferences, not field estimates. List every failing trajectory and sensitivity to missing shared answers. No population significance claim, tuned threshold, pooled prior cohort, or deployment recommendation. Qualifying admission is not assumed from clean resolution competence; native admission performance is the outcome under test.
