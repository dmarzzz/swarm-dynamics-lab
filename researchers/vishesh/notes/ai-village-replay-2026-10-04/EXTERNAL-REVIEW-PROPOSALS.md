## Additional gaps and interpretation

Constructed-information replay can test memory transformations on real text but cannot establish original agents’ access or historical causality. A computer-turn/action extension has separate privacy, rights and truth requirements; do not infer actions from narration. Full corpus access alone does not produce an eligible independent episode population.
