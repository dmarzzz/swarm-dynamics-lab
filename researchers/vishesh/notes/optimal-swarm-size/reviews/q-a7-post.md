## Decision and next design implications

This is a useful mixed diagnostic result: operand binding improves arithmetic on these cases but does not establish reliable joint answers and provenance. Accept the previous post-mortem's priority of diagnosing worker inputs; reject automatic promotion to larger N because the declared reliability gate failed. Preserve both numerical and provenance outcomes. A future proposal should test explicit leaf-source semantics and stronger simple baselines, with independent cases and an unchanged evaluator; do not launch it automatically.

For the realistic outage direction, [O1's post-mortem](outage-o1-post.md) identifies a separate priority: strengthen fixed-team coordination through explicit non-overlapping ownership before attributing contraction benefits to changing conditions. O1's contraction also fired in the stable control. These are prospective design suggestions, not proof that either repair will improve performance. Any successor needs its own concrete owner-approved plan and refreshed admission.
