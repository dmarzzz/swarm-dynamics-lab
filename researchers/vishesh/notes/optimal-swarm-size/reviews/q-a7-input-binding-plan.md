## TLDR

At N=1, compare the unchanged work prompt with a prompt that repeats the requested rule, its public numeric operands and its declared prerequisite artifacts in an explicit binding object. Two fresh development roots, parallel/chain structures, two matched arms: eight assigned width-16 episodes. Measure final correctness, local arithmetic consistency, correct items/second, calls, tokens and dollars. Small competence discriminator, not a size or population-effect estimate.

## Question and prediction

Q-A5/Q-A6 errors arise in workers. Existing records cannot discriminate operand selection from arithmetic/context interference. Redundant explicit input binding is a smaller intervention than changing model, adding tools or increasing N. Prediction: binding improves item correctness if locating and applying public operands is a bottleneck. Null/adverse results end this diagnostic line; do not buy a larger N sweep. A positive finding justifies fresh qualification of the revised interface, not a claim about optimal size.

Never compute the correct output in that object. Record the binding hash/byte count and ordered full serialized request hash/byte count, actor and phase. Evaluator truth remains separate until termination. A deterministic exact arithmetic/scheduler reference is an offline scoring/instrument check, not a paid arm or a model competitor.

## Metrics

Primary: paired final correct-item fraction and quality-adjusted throughput = final correct items / measured episode seconds, counting unsuccessful tasks. Full-task on-time success, wrong-value/provenance counts, worker→final transitions, local arithmetic mismatches against observed prerequisite inputs, provider calls/input/output tokens/cost, critical-path/service time and binding/context hashes are secondary. Throughput is episode-level verified output throughput, not per-item completion-time truth; correct items per dollar is descriptive and includes planning/integration charges. Caps match; actual compute may differ and must be reported. No claim that token differences isolate a biological/physical law.

All assignments enter reconciliation. Interrupted/missing outcomes are explicit unknowns, excluded from no denominator silently; show assigned/started/terminal/valid/graded/analyzed separately. Futility-stopped root7 outcomes remain unobserved. Do not combine Q-A6's different intervention with Q-A7 as independent replication.

Interpretation: support requires improved bound quality in each observed root, mean paired improvement at least.125, and bound mean quality at least.90 with no worse structure mean. This is a conservative development-screen decision threshold, not statistical significance or formal core qualification. Null = no improvement; adverse = degraded quality/throughput; inconclusive = mixed, low precision, missingness or fault. Even support requires fresh qualification before a size comparison; otherwise park broader scaling.
