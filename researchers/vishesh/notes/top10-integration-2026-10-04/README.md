## Evaluation and presentation changes

Use the compact [scale and claims checklist](../../../../tooling/agent-experiments/SCALE-AND-CLAIMS.md) in existing setup records. It asks what N means, which mechanism changes, which resources remain fixed, how contexts/memory reset, and what worked cases/strong controls establish. No extra infrastructure or universal 24-root/300-agent requirement.

The source’s default weights allocate **40% to promise/interest/traction/scale and 60% to work/visual/memeability**. That is an editorial ranking, not scientific confidence or an owner-approved launch order. Commit count is sunk effort, not evidence. Keep a claim deck and a demo sequence separate; choose evidence headlines from verified results with denominators, and visual examples from assets whose provenance is known.

These are caption requirements for reusing existing assets, not newly rendered films or a claim that every historical visual has been repaired. The study notes link the relevant reporting/post-mortem evidence.

## Corrections and limits

- Do not promote PC9/PQ-04 interface progress to broad scientific qualification; do not run leaked C4. Current PC10, C5, RD6 and A8 closeouts supersede the source’s pending/blocked labels.
- Do not copy the source’s Antsy scores, old Influence call count or portfolio total blindly. Its 19 + 62 + 50 components sum to 131, not 132; use the current registry for totals rather than patching one number into a different cutoff.
- Claims that a study was the only independently reviewed instrument are not established here. Same-author recomputation is useful but not independent research review; waived review is not a pass.
- Source price forecasts, account-limit dates and a reported Dmarz fleet pause are historical reports. They neither override current owner-scoped instructions nor establish fresh costs/access. Local inference and a diagnostic are not automatically free.
- New broader experiments need their corresponding authority. Necessary bounded diagnostics retain standing authority plus a prospective plan and normal admission. Existing approved scopes remain intact; no machines or runs were started by this incorporation.
