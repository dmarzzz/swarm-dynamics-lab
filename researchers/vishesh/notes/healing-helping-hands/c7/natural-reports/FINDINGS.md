## Concrete case quality

- Decision relevance: suitable for a curation system deciding whether a numeric statement is current, stale or unsupported. It does not establish collective-agent value.
- Labels/answerability: every supported/refuted query has exact source spans and an explicit scope/cutoff; missingness is packet-relative. All18labels manually checked before comparison. Same-author audit; independent adjudication absent.
- Amendments vs contradiction: authentic revisions present; **zero genuine unresolved same-vintage contradictions found**. These sources give precedence explicitly. A synthetic same-date-conflict mutation tests failure handling only and is excluded from the real corpus.
- Scope: real GDP versus nominal GDP, quarter versus month, annualized rate versus job count, and payroll/establishment versus unemployment/household measures remain distinct. Source text includes adjacent-period and survey distractors. Unit-test mutations of geography/metric/publisher abstain.
- Challenge: natural source prose, explicit revisions, reused values, a downward-then-upward January revision, and overlapping releases are more meaningful than renamed grammar rows. Still highly standardized agency prose and easy future-period controls; likely insufficient difficulty for a broad reasoning claim. No difficulty was inferred from model performance.
- Independence:6incident roots are a bookkeeping count, **not6established independent samples**. BLS month cases reuse releases, BEA quarters share revisions/methods, and economic series can covary. Two publisher/series families, zero randomly sampled independent document families. Query variants and re-executions must remain within incident/publisher blocks.
- Holdout: all6incidents/18queries were inspected during development. None may be renamed a test holdout.
- Source integrity: [sources.json](sources.json) freezes attributed excerpts, dates and hashes. Three BEA raw HTML downloads succeeded, with normalized-text/whole-download hashes. BLS direct downloads returnedHTTP403; publicly accessible web-rendered line extracts supplied the evidence, with capture hashes and exact line ranges. BLS raw-HTML hashes are explicitly null, not fabricated. Frozen excerpt hashes support exact offline replay; a full-document extraction study would need complete archived pages.
- Rights: text only, no photos/logos. [BEA reuse policy](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/145) and [BLS copyright policy](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm) permit public-domain textual reuse with attribution.

## Simple baseline and actual implementation

[baseline.py](baseline.py) extracts current BEA headline measurements and BLS headline/revision measurements from the **same natural excerpts** available to models. It does not read gold answers or hand-entered fact records. The reconciler filters publisher, metric, period, geography and cutoff, selects latest applicable date, and abstains on absent or unresolved same-vintage values. [cases.json](cases.json) contains separately hand-audited targets and evidence spans; [actor-inputs.json](actor-inputs.json) omits gold/rationale fields. The parser is purpose-built on this development set, not a pretrained general extractor.

Eight offline tests pass: all18gold labels; snapshot hashes; source order/duplicate invariance; metric/geography/publisher mismatch; historical cutoff changes; synthetic unresolved-conflict handling; unknown-format abstention; evaluator-field isolation. The old-vintage/cutoff test confirms January's353,000 is supported at the February release and refuted as latest by April. These are software/label checks, not native model replication. Unknown prose currently becomes abstention, which can hide extraction misses; score extraction coverage separately from correct epistemic abstention on any future evaluation.

**Literal extraction plus deterministic evidence reconciliation is the proper strong simple baseline for this source class.** Audit parser extraction scope/value/provenance as well as final labels. The shared model contract is label-only to accommodate Jev; label-only errors cannot by themselves identify extraction versus reconciliation failures. Any richer extraction arm needs its own explicit qualified contract. A hand-curated fact table can be an oracle decomposition only; it must not be called an end-to-end competitor. No useful need for a multi-agent ensemble is established when the source parser already solves the candidate set.
