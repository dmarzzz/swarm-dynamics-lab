# Natural-report candidate collection — prospective source and label contract

Offline only; no model queries, funded packet or native qualification. Retain C7's synthetic18-case suite as a contract regression suite. Build the following real-source candidate set before inspecting any model answers: BEA real-GDP revisions for2024Q1,Q2,Q3; BLS payroll revisions forDecember2023,January2024,February2024. Select these contiguous early2024 reporting windows for available revision histories, not model difficulty. They are a convenience development collection, not a random sample or sealed holdout.

Use first-party dated agency news releases only, with public-domain reuse policies recorded. Preserve source URL, publication date, retrieval timestamp, exact source excerpt, content SHA256 and full-download SHA256. Prefer the release's own text explaining previous/current estimates. Cases ask about what the named authority reported **as of a fixed release date**, not today's value or unobservable economic truth. GDP is real quarter-on-quarter annualized percentage change; payroll is seasonally adjusted monthly total nonfarm job change, not unemployment or household employment.

Freeze label criteria before case construction: SUPPORT when the latest applicable agency vintage in the supplied source packet as of the cutoff states the claimed value; REFUTE when that exact series/geography/period/vintage has an explicit different value; UNCERTAIN when no applicable measurement is supplied or equal-authority same-vintage incompatible evidence has no explicit resolution. A newer explicitly revised estimate supersedes an older estimate for latest-vintage claims; an original-vintage historical claim can still be true. A different quarter, metric, population or survey is not a contradiction. Do not silently use source facts outside the packet or after cutoff. No invented contradictory document and no relabeling to balance outcomes.

Construct three queries per incident: correct latest value, a documented superseded value incorrectly asserted as latest, and an unsupported future-period query. These are18dependent query variants over6incident roots, not18independent reports. Fixed schemas carry query metric, period, geography, cutoff and requested value as actor inputs; gold label/rationale/provenance annotations remain evaluator-only. Real prose excerpts plus dated source metadata are the evidence. Audit every gold label against exact spans. Separate lexical extraction from deterministic scope/vintage reconciliation, and compare with a naive first-number/first-vintage extraction to demonstrate why reconciliation matters without making it the main competitor.

Record dependency: all BEA quarters share a statistical production pipeline; BLS months share a pipeline and overlapping releases, and macro series can be economically dependent. There are at most6incident roots within2publisher/series families; no defensible claim of6independent population samples. Reused releases and derived claims stay grouped. Duplicates are identified by canonical URL and excerpt/content hashes. This collection contains authentic amendments; whether it contains genuine unresolved contradiction is an audit finding, not an assumption.

Acceptance: every quoted span matches downloaded text, dates and metric units are explicit, all applicable current/previous numbers manually checked, labels reproduced by a visible-source extractor plus reconciler, no evaluation labels in actor serialization, and unknown cases distinguish packet insufficiency from a false real-world proposition. Failed access or missing support is recorded, not replaced silently. Document extraction brittleness and unknown-source abstention. Do not claim independent adjudication from a same-author audit.
