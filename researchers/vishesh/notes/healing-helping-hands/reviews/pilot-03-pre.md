Scattered erasure was weak. Change erasure to a seeded contiguous 5-column × 8-row block (40 scouts), without wrapping; top-left location is seeded. Same block, withdrawn roots, initial recipients and tapes across all matched worlds. At round 10 erase memory/notices, deliver any withdrawal notices, record a pre-exchange event snapshot, then perform the usual exchange and measure the frame. The primary metric remains mean post-exchange atlas error at rounds 10–23. The additional event snapshot verifies that damage occurred and distinguishes instantaneous repair from no damage. No change to withdrawal amount or no-event controls.

Strongest comparator: exact semantic extraction under identical policies. Primary mechanism contrast remains evidence+withdrawals minus evidence-only, paired within seed and arm. Source-balanced evidence consensus is the gold, not scientific truth. Three corpus seeds yield descriptive paired mean/range, no significance/CI claim. Classifier errors and propagation errors are separate. Jev-vs-exact checks the cost of semantic extraction; it cannot establish that heterogeneity helps.
