# Successor feasibility: remain parked

Current decision: do not spend more on this two-pass Haiku agreement policy. Main agreement detected 0/13 first-pass errors against frozen labels and cost35.95×always-Jev. Frozen-label results and the subgroup ambiguity sensitivity remain separate in the [post-mortem](C6R2-S1-POST.md). No later run supersedes that evidence.

The economics fail even before any Jev referral: the two Haiku passes cost USD0.318277 versus USD0.008854944 for always-Jev over432reports, a35.94×floor. One Haiku pass alone costs17.97×always-Jev. Merely improving referral selection cannot make this configuration cheaper at the observed usage and tariffs. This is a retrospective observed-cost calculation, not a fresh price quotation.

A successor is only potentially useful if it changes a concrete decision, such as whether to accept or escalate an evidence claim after a correction or retraction. It would need independently sourced natural reports with explicit claim scope, adjudicated gold answers and a collection-level holdout; complementary access to the primary result and correction history rather than two order variants of identical text; and a selective checker whose full acquisition, routing and verification cost can compete with the best same-evidence baseline. Different information access must have a same-evidence ablation so an acquisition benefit is not mislabeled a coordination benefit.

None of those conditions is currently demonstrated. Zero independent natural-report units have been collected or adjudicated for a successor. Twelve authored families and432nestedC6cases do not supply that sample. The six completed offline development rows (five distinct inputs, one reused control) clarify scope, conflict and missingness; they are inspected development fixtures and never a holdout. They are insufficient reason for a new native run.

If a concrete corpus and low-cost mechanism become available, prepare a prospective plan specifying independently sampled source-report clusters, adjudication disagreement handling, a precision-based sample count, collection-level holdouts, complementary-evidence controls, per-stage missingness/stopping and the cumulative resource envelope. Seek the corresponding owner decision only then. At present **FINISH / PARK**, with no allocation, reservation or launch requested. The USD50 ceiling remains cumulative and is not a target to spend.
