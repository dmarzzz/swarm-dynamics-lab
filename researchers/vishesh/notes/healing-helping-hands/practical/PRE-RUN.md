- Healing Helping Hands / vishesh/codex-regrowth-docs / S0 architecture diagnostic.
- Parent: pilot-03 and its preserved post-mortem. Status: **diagnostic-only**, ready for this bounded saved-tape run; no permission to escalate Qwen efficacy or confirmatory research.
- Question: when does local evidence repair add value over ordinary central evidence indexes, and what breaks when provenance or availability is missing?
- Expected result: verified notices help remove stale sources and reject mismatching issuers. Strong central verification may dominate connected mesh communication. Missing target lineage should defeat selective removal. These are predictions, not results.
- Uninformative outcome: unnoticed failed event injection, using gold to choose deletions, silently missing assignments, or a baseline denied evidence available to the intervention.

## Design and assessment

[PLAN.md](PLAN.md) is protocol H3, committed before implementation. The PI Conditional B feedback is incorporated with a central append-only comparison plus the stronger version-aware central control, benign updates, missing/misleading lineage and an explicitly modeled central-service availability boundary. Five arms, six scenarios, three known semantic tapes with two paired placements each = 180 assignments. Three corpus clusters; layout sensitivity is nested, not independent replication. No held-out corpus is opened. No cross-experiment pooling with Immune Response.

All arms share source observations, recipients, publication schedule and extraction tape. Central reads are deliberately immediate and bulk; peer packets are limited to four previously unseen items per directed edge per round. Traffic is measured, not asserted equal. No-event/new-learning control, false notices without real withdrawals, and targetless genuine notices identify restraint and lineage boundaries. Gold is computed separately from original synthetic labels; actor records contain only the saved semantic output and public metadata. Qualification is inherited solely as an input-provenance check, not claimed fresh.

Primary: mean incorrect-or-missing curator-query fraction over 20 post-event rounds. Main contrast central-append minus peer-verified, scenario-wise; absolute and relative differences plus final retention guardrail. Strong central-verified comparison is mandatory even if it defeats the proposed intervention. A correct UNCERTAIN answer without any evidence is scored missing. Twenty claims and 200 repeated queries are not independent samples. Explicit null recovery when the common >=95% threshold is not reached. Thirty logical rounds; runtime measured separately.
