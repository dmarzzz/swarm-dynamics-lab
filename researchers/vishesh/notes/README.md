Working notes and hunches. Nothing here is a hypothesis under the lab's prior-art gate; the surveys come first.

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|

| `agent-swarm-influence-research.md` | Hunch and proposed study: whether preferences inferred from one agent predict and shift a collective's choices. Its seven references are catalogued in `library/`. |

Conventions: plain prose, numbers with their source, measured separated from claimed, no personal information about anyone beyond what they publish.
