# P30-C: smaller staged collective comparison — prospective only

This is an unimplemented amendment for decision, not an admitted run. Preserve [P30-B](NEXT-P30-PLAN.md) and all historical evidence. Keep the 30-member initial roster and three arms: shared cache only (C), development-selected fixed capabilities/routing/procedures plus cache (F), and adaptive differentiation (A). All receive identical evidence, action affordances and competent exact solvers. Test whether adaptation adds useful savings beyond caching and fixed engineering, especially after demand or evidence changes.

## Worlds and repetitions

First stage: four independently constructed challenge worlds, one each targeting redundant retrieval, stable procedure compilation, changing interpretation/version dependencies, and supplier loss/recovery. These are prospective mechanism strata, not four existing validated worlds. Repeat every world in a fresh native execution, with cleared learned state/cache and fresh stochastic provider output. Pair identical scenarios and initial conditions across arms; precommit balanced arm execution order. Agents and jobs are nested observations, not independent sample units. Stage one has independent n=4 and two executions per world; a second execution is not another independent world.

A separate stage decision could add four new worlds (one per stratum), also each executed twice, yielding independent n=8. Never expand based on a favorable effect alone. Stage one must first show interpretable traces, answerable tasks, faithful controls and information that justifies the new cases. Freeze labels and holdouts before native dispatch; current inspected development fixtures are never promoted to holdouts. Do not expose owner/PI conversations to experimental agents.

## Smaller execution contract

Each world-arm execution has three epochs: 30 initial warm-up jobs (one per agent), 12 reuse jobs and 12 shifted/recovery jobs. After each of the first two epochs, at most six adaptive proposal slots are offered to agents selected by predeclared public local-cost statistics, ties broken by stable ID. Agents without a proposal slot still execute assigned work. This is an explicit change from the earlier proposal process and must be qualified; it restricts how fast differentiation can emerge.

Each job has at most four physical model calls and each proposal at most one. No unbudgeted coordinator. Discovery, interpretation, action, verification and recovery must fit those finite slots; alternatively amend and reprice the plan before running. Proposal calls include controller advice. Any changed-context native requalification consumes job/proposal slots and is recorded separately. Offline compilation by F is recorded as development work, and any model-assisted compilation is charged within the same envelope or explicitly added before admission. Communication, API requests, cache operations and local runtime are instrumented for every arm. Missing/unstarted calls and failed jobs remain in denominators. Four calls are a ceiling, not a requirement to waste calls in a deterministic baseline.

Four worlds × two executions × three arms × 54 jobs = 1,296 jobs. At four calls per job: 5,184 calls. Four worlds × two adaptive executions × two boundaries × six proposals = 96 additional calls. Stage-one ceiling: **5,280 physical calls**. No main retries are assumed; qualification's retry permission does not carry over. This cheaper packet trades fewer nested jobs and fewer adaptation opportunities for repeats across all mechanism strata. It might be too short for differentiation; establish that from offline trace coverage before allocation.

## Analysis and stopping

Primary comparisons are paired A−F and A−C total cost per correct task, with quality, evidence freshness, communication, concentration of skills/tools/models, recovery loss and latency reported separately by world and epoch. Include discovery and maintenance costs in totals; do not start the accounting clock after specialization. Report each execution and within-world repeat difference. Aggregate at the world level; with n=4 the uncertainty is large, and a 95% correctness target or observed ≤2 percentage-point loss is not a noninferiority proof. Retain the earlier 15% cost-reduction practical target as a descriptive decision threshold, not a guaranteed detectable effect. Before native admission freeze exact estimators, uncertainty method, per-world labels and missingness rules in the full amended instrument.

Stop on exhausted reservations, contaminated holdouts, violated information parity, unqualified action contract, or corrupted request/effect/charge tracing. A valid null/negative result can end the study. Do not repair scores or select favorable worlds after inspection. Score all fixed policies, including deterministic solutions; if F already solves the world cheaply, that is the substantive comparator rather than a reason to cripple it.
