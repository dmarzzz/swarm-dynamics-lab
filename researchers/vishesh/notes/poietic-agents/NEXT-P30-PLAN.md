# Prospective P30-B comparison and replication proposal

**Status: proposal, not implemented or allocated.** Q30-04 remains frozen. This proposal answers the PI's scientific-baseline correction; it does not expand the active packet, declare baseline completion or authorize another native call. Read its final outcome before selecting any route. A repeated refusal ends Q30-04 and parks that route rather than triggering substitution.

## TLDR

Can 30 initially identical agents learn a useful allocation of model, tool and shared-processing responsibilities that beats a competent fixed system when operational demands change? Compare shared caching, development-selected fixed capability assignments, and adaptive specialization with matched information, initial capabilities and budgets. Use eight independently constructed held-out worlds, three arms, four epochs and 30 jobs per epoch; repeat two preselected worlds in fresh native executions to measure stochastic stability separately. Measure exact service quality and fully amortized cost, plus executed specialization that survives the workload change. This is a bounded mechanism pilot; it cannot certify population-wide reliability or a two-point quality margin with eight worlds.

## Question and prediction

The useful empirical unknown is whether learning which transformations and evidence sources recur produces a lower-cost competent service than selecting cheap executors, caching and fixed routing in advance. Cache hits or a graph with different labels do not answer that question. The current three arithmetic templates are insufficient: a hand-authored public-data controller already answers them exactly. Running thousands of model calls on those same templates would primarily compare avoidable model overhead.

The changed instrument must introduce interpretable operational instructions whose meanings, units, exceptions and source-version applicability require contextual interpretation. Ordinary cache hits remain available in every arm. Some recurring interpretation/normalization can be compiled into verified reusable procedures; novel or conflicting instructions still require a generative executor. The expected advantage, if any, is fewer unnecessary model invocations after warm-up, with correct handling of changed evidence and useful fallbacks. Fixed routing may win, and that is a valid result.

Primary comparison: adaptive specialization versus the competent fixed-capability system. Shared-cache generalists provide an additional reference. Success means at least 15% lower total deployment cost per assigned job, quality at least 95% in both primary arms, and no more than a two-percentage-point observed quality loss, with qualifying executed specialization. Report uncertainty separately; these point thresholds do not turn an imprecise interval into non-inferiority proof.

Support motivates a larger fresh-world replication. A precise cost null or worse adaptive cost favors the fixed system. Harm rejects the adaptive configuration. Inconclusive intervals or missing worlds finish this pilot without claiming an advantage; they do not trigger an automatic sample expansion or reroll.

## Setup

Keep 30 initially identical agents and the qualified GPT-OSS model contracts if available. Only the experimental observation builder supplies agent context; operating-assistant conversation, portfolio messages and protected labels remain inaccessible. All arms receive the same task facts, tools, source versions, permitted procedure language, storage limits and service capacity. Shared version-aware caching is enabled in all arms. Every live model call uses the same provider-start pacing and records requested/served identity, usage and transport status.

Before native implementation, author four development worlds and eight held-out operational worlds. Each root is a separately constructed workflow/evidence bundle, not a new random identifier on one template. The eight holdouts comprise two independently written bundles in each challenge stratum: recurring policy interpretation; demand moving between specialties; unit/schema/exception changes with legitimate historical evidence; and capacity-sensitive sharing with competing evidence freshness. Each world contains answer-changing matched controls: current versus stale instructions, useful versus redundant material and a legitimate exception versus a superficial near-match. No intentionally broken baseline or unavailable required evidence is allowed.

Labels come from a protected typed source-of-truth representation and an independently written reference calculation checked against manually inspected development examples. Model-generated answers are not truth. Every required fact must be traceable into the delivered bundle. The text and parser must not share hidden answer shortcuts. Audit ambiguous labels, unit conversions, timestamp applicability and leakage before sealing holdouts. A shared generator or author remains a source of dependence and is reported.

The three arms are:

- **C: shared-cache generalists.** Same cache, source access and tool limits; capabilities remain unchanged.
- **F: fixed capability assignment.** Use development data to choose the least expensive competent combination of cheap executors, service owners, supported deterministic procedures and fallback rules. Freeze it before holdouts. Ordinary fixed selectors may route by declared task type and current validity; do not sabotage their access or omit an obvious rule/program that solves the work.
- **A: adaptive specialization.** Start with the identical generalist state, then allow local structural proposals at the same boundaries. Model changes, capability shedding, procedure installation and shared service delivery must actually execute. One proposal per actor per boundary, with no privileged access to future jobs or labels.

All arms start epoch one with identical full capabilities. At its end F applies its preselected assignment and A may adapt. Epoch two measures reuse, epoch three introduces the predeclared shift, and epoch four measures adaptation/recovery. C remains fixed. Report initial warm-up, steady work, shift and recovery separately and together. A's extra proposals are charged, not granted free compute. Report cost-matched curves and assigned-job completion under a common hard cap; do not claim a fixed-compute comparison merely because nominal agent counts match.

## Protocol

Eight independent holdout worlds × three arms × four epochs × 30 jobs = 2,880 primary assigned jobs. Two world IDs, selected before sealing as one demand-shift and one instruction-change world, receive one additional fresh execution of every arm: 720 replication jobs. Total 3,600 jobs across 30 world-arm executions. Independent-world n remains eight; the two repeated worlds estimate execution variability only. These repeats are new hosted model executions with fresh state and call IDs, not replay or continuation.

Pair task/evidence realizations across arms, rotate arm order in a balanced schedule and keep state isolated. Allow at most four model actions per job and one adaptive proposal per actor at each of three boundaries. Include every assigned cell after any stop. Keep the per-job deadline from actual release, and separately measure queueing/end-to-end service latency. A global five-second provider-start interval constrains practical throughput; do not simulate concurrent work by resetting its clock.

Before freezing, the offline acceptance gate must show: all reference solutions are answerable; positive/negative controls change the correct decision; all three arms have matched evidence/access; fixed capability assignments are selected only on development data; and meaningful semantic challenge remains after competent rules, caching and fixed routing. If a simple deterministic controller solves every bundle at negligible cost, stop and revise the question offline instead of hiding that comparator.

Native admission additionally needs successful current interface qualification, a finite changed-context check if the semantic task contract changes, the complete instrument/runtime, new immutable registration and current exclusive approved-account allocation. Qualification and any changed-context allowance must be explicitly included in the named PI allocation. Q30-03 and Q30-04 alone do not qualify unimplemented semantic instructions. Holdouts remain unopened until these gates pass.

## Metrics

Primary outcomes are exact correct service per assigned job and total real model/tool/allocated-infrastructure cost per assigned job, including proposals, verification, failures and warm-up. Show incremental comparison costs and an equal allocation of historical study overhead as a sensitivity analysis. No synthetic tool price may be presented as a real monetary saving. Report tokens, calls, latency and resource inventory alongside dollars.

Executed specialization requires a before/after capability or backend change, recorded service/procedure use by other agents, reduced repeated generative work, and maintained exact service quality after the shift. Distinguish useful new reusable processing from cache hits, unused proposals and mere role names. Inspect all failures and a prospectively balanced trace sample of successful source-to-answer and structural transitions. Retain all effective inputs and visible responses for full replay.

Analyze paired world-level A–F differences and A–C secondary contrasts, with per-world results and intervals. Bootstrap whole worlds within the declared challenge strata, show leave-one-world-out sensitivity, and disclose that two worlds per stratum yield coarse estimates. Do not use thousands of jobs as independent n. An empirical zero-variance interval is not evidence against rare failures; report the limited world coverage explicitly. Report fresh-repeat agreement and dispersion separately, with n=2 repeated worlds and no claim of broad stochastic stability.

The pilot can reveal a large consistent cost advantage, a clear cost disadvantage or a specific failure mechanism. It cannot promise tight null precision in advance. A follow-up sample-size proposal must use observed between-world variability and a new decision; do not quietly widen this packet to obtain a favorable interval.
