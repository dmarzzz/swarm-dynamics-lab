# Skeptical research context and review

Forty selected critiques: **24 atlas questions, 12 contributed extensions and four design documents**, connected to **all sixteen original project briefs**. This is a targeted editorial pass, not a review of every question, a new forty-item hypothesis bank or a formal gate verdict. The reviewed repository had zero registered hypotheses; tentative predictions inside questions and notes are not accepted hypotheses.

The purpose is to improve decisions, not reward blanket pessimism. Each critique preserves a useful strength, identifies a concrete concern, proposes the smallest discriminating revision and states when to narrow or stop. A concern may already be acknowledged by a design; the review asks how that acknowledgment becomes an executable control.

## Most consequential decisions

1. **Fix Scheme C before running it.** VX-03 / D-SEO retain a concrete unanimous-commit counterexample. This is stronger than a generic request for more evidence. The sample-size arithmetic also needs a fully specified clustered analysis; assumed effect sizes are not observed precision.
2. **Narrow the immune-response claim.** D-IMMUNE / SEC-06 / SEC-07 should begin with a reservoir mechanism comparison. Generic repair already exists. The promising question is what breaks under undeclared ancestry, semantic retries and loss of useful state, with existing mechanisms as faithful baselines.
3. **Separate discussion from extra resources.** SOC-09 / D-DOSE / D-SUITE need an equally funded independent-work comparison if the claim concerns a social mechanism. A dose study can still be useful when explicitly interpreted as a total-resource effect.
4. **Merge overlapping experiments.** VX-01 into SEC-03, VX-14 into the detector-intervention program, and PX-08 into SOC-10. Preserve IDs and ablations; reduce duplicate headline claims and implementations.
5. **Ship safeguards without inflating novelty.** EX-03 and EX-07 can justify conventional transaction/deduplication checks. Their research boundary is uncertain invariant or operation identity, not whether a supplied perfect validator follows its rule.
6. **Measure operational behavior rather than hidden motive.** EX-24 and SEC-48 cannot establish maliciousness or alignment from finite observations alone. A useful outcome may be justified fallback, restricted capability or calibrated uncertainty.

For a hackathon, the first useful deliverables are a stopping-rule counterexample suite, one memory-recurrence fixture with private/shared state, and one matched-budget decision task. These are priorities for review and qualification, not authorization for model spending. The NCA lesion comparison remains interesting but is deferred unless checkpoint and damage controls can be audited in time.
