## Additional gaps and interpretation

Information-equivalent advice can affect a bounded model through salience; it is not mathematically guaranteed to produce zero behavior change. The meaningful defect is a mismatch between the advertised information mechanism and actual intervention. Oracle affected-record sets are upper bounds, not deployable detectors. A safe provider error taxonomy must retain useful categories without arbitrary provider bodies or secrets.
