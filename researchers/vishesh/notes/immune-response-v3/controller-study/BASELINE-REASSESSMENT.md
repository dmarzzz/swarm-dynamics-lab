## Existing evidence and its limits

A10 contains four authored development roots, each run once with Sonnet and once with Opus. Those model/configuration cohorts are not same-condition replications. There are zero repeated fresh native executions per A10 model/root/condition, zero evaluated A10 holdout roots and zero native controlled-advice trajectories. Exact trace replay establishes measurement reproducibility, not behavioral stability. Shared scenario templates limit independence beyond the four roots.

Both models repaired a real runtime crash. Neither passed all fixed outcome and diagnosis gates. Sonnet's configuration repair failed; Opus unnecessarily acted on a healthy stale-alarm case. Three action/explanation contradictions and ambiguous diagnosis-field definitions remain material gaps. A9 supplies a separate exploratory advice result on three inspected roots, not replication of A10 or an independent population sample. No current result establishes the original immune-response mechanism, reliable service preservation or a production-reliability rate.

The useful question remains: **when evidence is stale or advice is wrong, can an agent repair a necessary fault while preserving an already-healthy service?** A scientifically credible negative must show that failure is stable on valid, sufficiently varied tasks with a competent simple comparator, not merely that a particular prompt failed once.

## Analysis, controls and claim

Primary outcomes stay family-specific: healthy preservation without unnecessary deployment, runtime repair by useful same-version restart without configuration mutation, stale evidence verification, and necessary compatible configuration repair. Do not let a safety rejection plus green health masquerade as correct restraint. Report diagnosis accuracy and action/explanation agreement separately.

For advice, report world-paired changes in healthy ticks and gate attainment, absolute harmful/unnecessary interventions, and agreement across the two fresh repetitions. Show all twelve world-level contrasts stratified by family; any interval must cluster at world level and remain explicitly conditional on this authored panel. Four families and twelve worlds cannot estimate rare production failures. Even zero baseline failures in all twelve worlds gives only a weak reliability bound; do not present 24 repeated trajectories as 24 independent population draws.

A consistent adverse advice effect, a consistent null, or repeat-sensitive behavior can each be useful findings if valid cases, competent comparators and measurement are established. The scoped claim is reproducibility and sensitivity **on this frozen authored service-control panel**, not general agent competence or realistic incident prevalence. To claim natural incident performance later would require externally grounded case acquisition and labels, not more seeds here.

Visualize both fresh repetitions for every world: evidence freshness → diagnosis → selected action → verified service outcome, with advice condition, rejected actions, contradictions and missing observations explicit. Publish variability and phase timing, not only the best trajectory. Complete scientific closeout whether C, Q or A stops or completes.
