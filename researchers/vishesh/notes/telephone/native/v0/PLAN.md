# Telephone V0 native source meaning pilot

2026-10-04, Vishesh / codex-village-fit. Owner approved the real AI Village scope as well as the separate authored diagnostic. This is the first bounded real-source qualification pilot. It does not launch the original 24-component evaluation automatically. Researcher review is not required.

## TLDR

Test whether native three-hop handoffs preserve reported claims, plans and uncertainty from eight real AI Village chat records. Compare prose P, structured S and exact original-source access R, with 72 maximum calls and 25 predeclared critical obligations. Sources are supplied explicitly, so the experiment measures constructed replay fidelity, not historical actor visibility, real-world truth or causal transmission in the Village. All eight records are development material; none is represented as an untouched holdout.

## Question and prediction

Do source-linked structured handoffs preserve reported meaning better than prose after three fresh-context hops? A source-access control may prevent accumulated drift. The practical diagnostic is whether retelling turns a plan into completed work, a preference into a commitment, or a reported claim into established fact. Lossless copying is the strong ceiling, so this study cannot establish that a model is necessary.

Favorable, null and adverse results determine whether this interface preserves meaning on these specific source cases and whether a later cohort warrants development. Inconclusive or ceiling results do not authorize more collection. Natural historical transmission remains an untested question.

## Metrics

Primary descriptive endpoint: per-source supported retention of the frozen critical meanings at hop three; equal-source paired S minus P difference. Report all eight paired differences, condition/hop scores and missingness. Add unsupported assertions, critical commitments, lost uncertainty, citation validity, latency, tokens and cumulative cost. Whole-output assertion review is required before using T1 semantic scoring; critical source-copy checks alone do not establish factual precision.

All 25 critical targets are supported as source meaning under the reviewed labels. Literal copy and exact lookup preserve the source and meet the critical-retention ceiling of 1.0. This is not a complete factual-precision annotation receipt. World-truth accuracy cannot be established from chat assertions alone. Do not score an unverified real-world claim as true merely because it was faithfully copied.

Qualification screen: all arms retain at least 85% of their critical obligations at hop one; every source uncertainty/intent control preserves its status; no unsupported critical commitment. Review every trace and miss. A parse pass does not pass this screen. The pilot can finish with a valid ceiling, null or adverse result. Broader claims and a new evaluation cohort require their own prospective preparation.
