## Protocol

Build paired three-hop assignments with seeded arm order and unique call IDs. Prose and structure receive source records at hop 1 and only their previous handoff thereafter. The retrieval diagnostic receives the original permitted records again at each hop; on a three-record authored archive this is exact lookup, not a test of search quality. That is an explicit information-access advantage and differs from the eventual T0 BM25 corpus condition.

The same 512 output-token cap applies to all arms. Use an 8192 input-token ceiling, enforce a conservative serialized byte limit with framing reserve, and verify actual usage. No silent truncation, provider memory, thinking blocks, tools, response repair or automatic retry. Stop on transport error, truncated output, route drift, malformed structured response or budget violation; retain the failed request reservation and leave remaining assignments unstarted.
