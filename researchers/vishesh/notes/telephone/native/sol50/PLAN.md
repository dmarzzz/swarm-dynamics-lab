## Qualification, stopping and what failure means

Before the 50-agent chain, use two separate fresh GPT-6 Sol qualification agents on compact GO and insufficient-evidence HOLD controls. These add 2 calls; they are not part of the 50-agent chain. Qualify route/schema/accounting and the explicit decision rule, including one uncertainty control. Preserve the original A1 failed fidelity screen as history. This successor prospectively measures content drift as an outcome: it does not require the 50-hop chain to exhibit zero drift as a condition of being analyzed.

A qualification decision failure stops before the chain. During the chain, semantic errors are retained and transmitted without intervention; do not repair the message. A transport, route, output-schema/truncation, source/parent, time or budget failure stops generation, preserves uncertain reservations and marks downstream nodes unstarted. No retries, replacement identities, alternative providers or automatic successor. A ceiling or adverse trajectory can finish the study.

## Metrics, units and judgment rules

Primary: retained source-meaning targets /12 at each hop, and first observed loss. Secondary: GO/HOLD/UNKNOWN recommendation against the frozen release rule, false-GO count, unsupported critical commitments, lost qualifications, valid source ancestry references, words/tokens/latency/cost. Do not convert absent outputs into zero-retention scores. Show the full 50-hop path and available/missing denominator.50 model calls are nested observations from one source root, with no population confidence interval or swarm-superiority claim.

Annotate every factual assertion against source spans; two same-operator passes with disclosed uncertainty. Separate literal report preservation from added verification status: a source statement plus an extra unverifiable caveat can preserve the reported content while separately adding an unsupported status. A new narrowed review scope is scored as an added unsupported assertion; omission of an explicit passed result loses its target. Gold targets specify the required entailment and tolerable paraphrases prospectively. Preserve strict status-integrated sensitivity instead of deciding labels for a preferred outcome.

Strong baseline: forward the full packet verbatim for 50 hops, at the same permitted handoff size; source-target retention remains 1.0. Exact rule controller applies the supplied release rule directly to source evidence; it establishes answerability, not autonomous natural-language competence. One-step model baseline is the chain's first agent, compared descriptively with later agents on the same case. No native P/S/R comparison or causal identity-size claim is made. Source-reopening effects were observed descriptively in A1, not retested here.
