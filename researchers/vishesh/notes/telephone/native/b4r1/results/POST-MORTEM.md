## What changed in the context

All36reader outputs and18final handoffs were manually compared with source records. Ten earlier writer outputs across five trajectories were additionally inspected; the remaining earlier writer prose received automated lineage/schema checks, not a claimed full semantic census. One operator performed the annotation; no independent inter-rater validation is claimed. [Audit rows](SEMANTIC-AUDIT.json) retain exact source/handoff/reader spans, five loss categories and ambiguity.

The exact permission version/site, acquisition IDs or their unknown status, and rollback-site evidence mostly survived. The diagnostic therefore does **not** establish that simple factual deletion explains the whole effect. Important changes included omitted policy conjunctions, altered staging-versus-deployment scope, weakened known-violation versus unknown-evidence interpretation, and repeated caveats about inherited claims. Readers also introduced requirements for write-path tests, an active post-release monitor or independent re-verification that the source deployment policy did not impose. Correct answers sometimes had those extra concerns in their explanations; action correctness and explanation support are separate.

Concrete evidence-linked examples:

- **Version RELEASE, block1:** writer1 states the full deployment prerequisites. At writer2 that explicit conjunction disappears, while v3/West scope, distinct checks and West rollback facts remain. P abstains; R restores the explicit source rule and releases. This is observed natural policy omission followed by a decision difference, not a controlled phrase-deletion proof.
- **Independent-check RELEASE, both blocks:** the final handoff still states the full prerequisite conjunction and its satisfying evidence. P nevertheless cites missing write-path testing or a not-started monitor. These cases show why the effect cannot all be attributed to missing facts.
- **Rollback HOLD:** block1 P treats East-only testing as unestablished South coverage and abstains; block2 preserves the explicit complete tested-site list wording and P correctly identifies the South requirement as violated. The wording differs naturally, but reader stochasticity and other message differences remain alternative explanations; this is not causal mediation identification.
- **Rollback INSUFFICIENT, block2:** writer2 changes the earlier staging GO to UNKNOWN because independent current verification is unavailable, although the inherited record reports readiness. The final deployment abstention remains correct for the separate unknown rollback coverage. This secondary status change is retained as an ambiguity/alteration rather than silently counted as a deployment error.

The P reader may rationally abstain when its handoff has actually lost or confused the governing rule. A wrong full-source label is not automatically an irrational action given degraded context. Conversely, several P handoffs retain the needed conjunction and facts, so extra unsupported prerequisites or a shifted evidentiary threshold contribute to the observed errors. The audit records these distinctions rather than treating every abstention as unsafe or every source-restored success as proof of one specific missing phrase.
