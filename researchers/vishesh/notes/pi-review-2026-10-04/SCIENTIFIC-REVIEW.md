## Experiment - Quorum of Mirrors

Strong evidence-dependence question. Separate sources, voters and rounds; the useful result must beat source deduplication, not merely show that copies agree.

### Quorum of Mirrors

**Evidence:** Exploratory, unimplemented, unqualified proposal, DM-02. This is distinct from the existing adaptive-quorum experiment; its historical runs cannot be borrowed as Quorum of Mirrors results.

**Decision:** Prioritize a small evidence-dependence benchmark with partial-lineage controls. The useful target is an intervention/resource tradeoff, not another demonstration that duplicated votes are dependent.

#### Scientific interest

**Judgment:** A cleaner initial causal study than adaptive Phantom Coast, with practical value if it identifies when another observation beats another judgment.

**Why:** Source count, decision-maker count and communication can be manipulated separately. Explicit versus incomplete lineage tests when implementable deduplication works and when apparent consensus conceals dependence.

**Novelty boundary:** The proposal itself identifies prior work on repeated evidence and correlated Jev judge errors. Reading remains limited; novelty is unestablished. Neither a hundred cloned calls, unanimous errors nor a renamed consensus task is a new scientific contribution.

#### Robustness

**Supported:** The revised design recognizes ancestry ground truth, task-difficulty confounding, confidence calibration, pooled baselines and separate observation/call budgets.

**Unsupported:** No evidence yet that endorsements inflate confidence, partial lineage defeats a defense, a larger quorum helps, or Jev differs from other models. Typed valid outputs do not demonstrate calibrated decisions.

**Unit:** An independently generated decision instance/world with a sampled evidence-ancestry graph. Judges, repeated calls and communication rounds are nested observations; branches using the same evidence graph are paired.

**Independent variation:** Not sampled yet. Different source IDs do not guarantee independent measurements: shared sensors, common causes and item difficulty can correlate distinct roots.

**Assessment:** A strong sketch, not a protocol. Specify the generative dependence model, allowed lineage information, aggregation rules and budget estimands before claiming either independent sources or fair comparisons.

#### Scenario adequacy

**Size:** A small fixed group can expose duplicate amplification. A hundred is a communication hook, not a necessary experimental scale or independent sample count.

**Challenge:** All-explicit source IDs may make deterministic deduplication trivial. Partial lineage adds meaningful ambiguity, but some ancestry configurations are observationally indistinguishable; impossible inference is an information limit, not a model defect.

**Complexity:** Use controlled noisy evidence and imperfect visible ancestry before adding large graphs. Separate exact copies, factual restatements and genuinely new measurements.

**Realism:** Mirrored reports and repeated endorsements resemble practical research/decision workflows. Grounded reliability cues are preferable to arbitrary crowd authority; real source dependence is rarely captured by identity alone.

**Definition:** Freeze fact-preserving paraphrases, lineage visibility, source reliability and how peer messages update state. Report calibrated error, agreement and raw dependence separately; an effective-sample-size approximation is not a literal count of independent minds.

#### Scenario assessments

**One Witness, a Hundred Votes.** Good flagship visualization and controlled dependence test; the generic phenomenon is already expected. The scientific opening is a defense that remains useful with incomplete ancestry at a known cost. Record true observation ancestry in the evaluator. Give actors explicit or partial lineage without truth labels; compare majority, implementable deduplication, calibrated accumulation and pooled evidence. Keep omniscient deduplication labeled an oracle.

**The Right Dissenter.** A useful stress test of an already formed quorum, not a reason to duplicate the independent dissent project. A designated truthful dissenter would leak the answer. Pair correct and incorrect fresh evidence with identical delivery budgets and reputation cues. Freeze the pre-injection checkpoint, include a silent/no-new-source comparator, and reuse RD-1 bounded checks as a declared intervention.

**The Next Observation.** Most direct operational payoff: buy another measurement or spend another model call. It cannot hold both source count and observation spending constant while changing evidence acquisition. Declare an equal-total-resource contrast with explicit prices and latency, separate from the fixed-information duplication contrast. Report a cost-sensitivity frontier; avoid presenting it as a general optimal swarm-size study.

**The Crowd That Learns to Agree.** Useful diagnostic within the flagship. Faster agreement is not automatically harmful; convergence can reflect valid evidence aggregation. Plot agreement, correctness and calibration jointly at every scheduled round, including failures and abstentions. Stratify shared errors by preregistered task difficulty and report joint failure patterns.

#### Controls and parameters

**Retain:** No discussion, pooled-evidence single solver and deterministic accumulation Majority versus source-aware deduplication Exact copies versus meaning-preserving restatements Correct and incorrect new evidence; repeated identical requests

**Missing:** Explicit sensor/source dependence generator and partial-lineage observation contract Separate fixed-information and fixed-total-resource estimands Independent calibration of returned distributions and difficulty strata Parity of observations, context access and decision opportunities for pooled controls

**Confounds:** More judges can also mean more sources, tokens or rounds Paraphrases may alter facts, reliability cues or salience Shared item difficulty induces apparent error correlation Hidden lineage available only to a defense creates an oracle advantage Abstaining or silencing everyone can improve selected-answer accuracy while reducing useful decisions

**Unique observation sources (Essential first axis).** Current: Proposed independently of agent count Proposed: Two source-overlap levels at a fixed group size, report count and call budget Measures dependence without conflating evidence acquisition with replication.

**Lineage visibility and aggregation (Essential intervention axes).** Current: Explicit versus partial lineage; several baselines Proposed: Full versus prespecified partial ancestry; majority versus implementable source-aware aggregation Tests where the useful defense breaks and exposes information-theoretic limits.

**Agent count and communication rounds (Later separate axes).** Current: Unfixed; headline says a hundred Proposed: Vary judges while holding sources/rounds fixed, then rounds while holding sources/judges fixed Prevents a misleading single scaling curve from mixing three resources.

**Observation-versus-call spending (Later bounded budget comparison).** Current: Next Observation ablation proposed Proposed: Fixed total cost with declared observation/call prices and latency sensitivity Turns dependence findings into a usable resource decision without a giant factorial.

#### Next decisive study

**Question:** Does source-aware aggregation protect accuracy from duplicated endorsements when ancestry is only partially observable?

**Primary contrast:** Source-aware-minus-majority accuracy, compared across overlap levels and full/partial lineage. Match supplied evidence and call budgets between policies within each overlap cell; hold report count fixed while source-root count/dependence is manipulated across overlap cells.

**Endpoint:** Held-out world-level decision accuracy; co-report wrong unanimous commitments, selective risk/coverage and calls/records. Probability changes without new evidence are diagnostic until calibrated.

**Unit:** Paired independent world/evidence-ancestry realizations, clustered by task family where appropriate.

**Design:** Minimum next step: specify and review the source generator, information contract and exact posterior/accumulator reference. Then use one small fixed team and one fixed communication horizon, two overlap levels, two lineage levels and majority/source-aware policies. Include pooled and no-discussion references; reuse frozen evidence whenever possible. Defer size/round sweeps and fresh-observation allocation until this benchmark discriminates.

**Go no go:** Proceed only after individual competence, provider-repeat variability, truth isolation and equal-budget accounting pass. If ordinary deduplication explains all gains, publish that narrow result. If hidden ancestry makes the cases indistinguishable, report the information limit; do not invent a special swarm failure or let an oracle defense see the missing lineage.

## Experiment - Phantom Coast

PC-1 now defines a small fixed-evidence history test. Validate the instrument and stage its qualification; active exploration remains a separate question.

### Phantom Coast

**Evidence:** Prospective PC-1 instrument-development plan, with offline implementation in progress in a concurrent task. No native qualification or scientific result. Latest plan frozen at 2026-10-04T03:40:18.974165+00:00; code/tests were not audited in this delta.

**Decision:** Advance the bounded Two Swarms, One World specification first. Preserve The Island No One Visits as the flagship mechanism, contingent on demonstrating decision-dependent observation rather than imposing avoidance in code.

#### Scientific interest

**Judgment:** A compelling prospective question with a stronger causal story than ordinary misinformation spreading.

**Why:** A mistaken map could change inspection choices and thereby remove opportunities for correction. Distinguishing wrong judgments from missing corrective exposure would change how evidence-gathering systems are designed.

**Novelty boundary:** Self-confirming learning, collective sensing, context effects and spatial demonstrations already appear in the proposal's cited work. Those sources were mostly scanned at abstract/introductory depth; no complete novelty search or new independent literature validation is supplied by this review. The possible contribution is an identified feedback mechanism and useful intervention, not a recognizable map or a persistent wrong label.

#### Robustness

**Supported:** The design correctly separates fixed evidence from adaptive observation, distinguishes retraction from deletion/reset, requires complete-world scoring and identifies pooled/deterministic baselines. The newly written PC-1 plan now specifies a 6×6 world, three same-evidence actors, eight history/communication/state trajectories, a complete final observation ledger, explicit retraction, fixed-denominator error bounds and byte-identical canonical reset inputs. These are plan properties, not validated execution.

**Unsupported:** There is no evidence yet of amplification, a self-maintaining exploration trap, benefit from source-aware communication, model-specific susceptibility or recovery. Neither a selected animation nor an Earth-coordinate demonstration would establish these.

**Unit:** An independently generated archipelago/world with its sensor and initial-exposure realization. History, communication and reset branches are paired within world; neighboring tiles and agents are dependent measurements.

**Independent variation:** None sampled yet. Vary world geometry, report ambiguity and exposure placement across held-out world families, rather than relabeling one coastline. Repeated identical requests estimate service variation, not environmental generalization.

**Assessment:** Scientifically promising, but not execution-ready. The observation model, available actions, stopping horizon, memory contract, resource prices and primary estimand remain unspecified.

#### Scenario adequacy

**Size:** PC-1 fixes 36 cells, three actors and twelve qualification worlds. That is sufficient for an instrument screen, not population inference; the proposed 1,044-call qualification ceiling is large for a simple complete-evidence task and should be staged.

**Challenge:** The final step supplies all 36 noiseless observations. This is an appropriate competence and history-persistence control but can reduce the task to copying a complete map. A null is valid. Claims about realistic exploration require the separate PC-2 observation-choice stage.

**Complexity:** PC-1 uses identical factual inputs for three actors, so it isolates communication rather than complementary sensing. Its three logical steps and strict-majority aggregation are deliberately small. PC-2 must add real observation choices without hardcoded avoidance.

**Realism:** A fictional text-survey task offers strong causal control and resembles evidence collection. It is not visual perception, geographic discovery or a demonstration of changed model weights.

**Definition:** PC-1 resolves the action/schema, cell coverage, UNKNOWN versus missing, world-level units and all-assignment error bounds. The primary interaction is in absolute between-history disagreement; accuracy must remain a separate outcome because convergence can be jointly wrong and disagreement can reflect service noise.

#### Scenario assessments

**Two Swarms, One World.** Best first study: counterbalanced early histories followed by equal final factual evidence can localize history dependence. Equal facts do not imply equal prompts, memory access or presentation order. PC-1 now concretely specifies this stage and all-assignment bounds; offline implementation is in progress, not qualification evidence. Cross history with social/no-social decisions, preserve exact requests, and add a canonical common-input reset. A gap after identical reset requests first calls for checking service/model variation; a retained-history gap is external-context dependence, not permanent belief change.

**The Island No One Visits.** Strongest flagship if actors freely choose what to inspect and those choices suppress corrective exposure. A rule forbidding visits to predicted water would manufacture the claimed effect. Keep every cell inspectable and reward whole-world accuracy. Match initial information, sensor opportunities and total observation budget; allow realized observations to differ because they are the mediator. Randomize a forced audit observation to test whether restored exposure breaks the loop.

**The Ghost After Retraction.** Useful recovery stage, but stale records alone can explain persistence. Removing a message is not evidence that agents received an explicit correction. Distinguish authenticated retraction, silent deletion, context reset and a new contradictory measurement. Include actual environmental change so rational stability is not automatically penalized. Measure finite-window persistence with censoring.

**The Tipping Atlas.** Potentially useful exposure-propagation boundary; premature as a large topology sweep. An apparent threshold in one finite grid is not a phase transition. Defer until propagation beyond exposed cells is established. Then vary one communication/source-layout axis and compare against an exact information-flow baseline across independent worlds.

#### Controls and parameters

**Retain:** No exchange, ordinary exchange and source-aware exchange Same-length benign reports and random corruption matched on initial error Pooled-evidence single solver and a competent deterministic mapper Reset/common-input serialization and genuinely changed-world controls PC-1 canonical reset inputs byte-identical across history/communication for each actor PC-1 explicit matched-call accounting with realized token differences disclosed PC-1 lower/upper error bounds retaining missing predictions

**Missing:** Execution validation of the now-specified prompt/schema, source hashes, common-input identity and fixed denominators Staged semantic qualification before committing the full 1,044-call maximum Independent inspection of the proposed sensor/gold generator and explicit task-value margin before any efficacy study

**Confounds:** Social arms may receive more facts, tokens or inference calls Early histories can differ in factual content, order and salience simultaneously Generated summaries can retain an error after the nominal source disappears Adaptive observations are post-treatment mediators; matching them away destroys the exploration question

**History, communication and reset (Essential first study).** Current: PC-1: two report orders × private/social × retained/reset; three actors and three steps. Proposed: Counterbalanced histories crossed with exchange/no exchange; terminal retained versus canonical-reset probes Separates externally carried history from serialization and service variability.

**Observation choice and correction (Essential second stage).** Current: PC-1 fixed observations; PC-2 proposes twelve actor-selected observations including an audit slot within the same budget. Proposed: Fixed schedule versus actor choice, with randomized audit access under a fixed observation budget Identifies the missing-exposure mechanism rather than another classification error.

**Topology, world size, model and Earth demos (Later stress tests).** Current: PC-1 fixes 6×6 synthetic grids; Earth, larger worlds and model sweeps deferred. Proposed: One axis at a time after the mechanism discriminates; keep Earth separate Avoid an expensive sweep and pretrained-geography confounding.

#### Next decisive study

**Question:** With equal final factual evidence, does social exchange amplify the effect of an early misleading history, and does canonical reset remove it?

**Primary contrast:** PC-1 prespecifies the retained-minus-reset social disagreement contrast minus the same private contrast. Preserve this planned estimand, but interpret it as history-dependent disagreement and co-report all-assignment mapping error and identical-request variation; it is not automatically harmful error amplification.

**Endpoint:** Area-weighted final error with land omission and false-land error reported separately; also report between-history disagreement without equating it to inaccuracy.

**Unit:** Paired independently generated world and sensor realization.

**Design:** Review and offline-validate the frozen PC-1 generator, request renderer and scorer. Begin native readiness with a bounded complete-evidence semantic screen; only then expand to the planned twelve-world qualification matrix within its explicitly authorized cap. Stage shared pre-treatment work to avoid unnecessary repeated calls if the final protocol allows it. A later effect study needs fresh worlds, a useful-effect margin and planned precision. PC-2 remains a separate plan.

**Go no go:** Proceed to adaptive exploration only after qualification, serialization checks and fair information accounting pass. Null history effects are valid. Drop collective amplification if pooled single-agent behavior or deterministic state rules explain it; do not tune the held-out worlds to make a dramatic coastline appear.

## Experiment - Right Dissenter

Promising bounded-challenge protocol. Pair useful corrections with harmful reversals and compare against always-check and exact rules.

### Right Dissenter: bounded evidence challenges, withdrawal and reopening

**Evidence:** Prospective native experiment with implemented offline state machine and explicitly scripted local fixtures.

**Decision:** Advance a small identifying study after semantic qualification; avoid a large suite before it beats always-check and deterministic rules.

#### Scientific interest

**Judgment:** One of the strongest prospective mechanisms, if it protects correct decisions as well as repairs wrong ones.

**Why:** A minority earns a bounded check instead of a permanent veto. Withdrawal, expiration and reopening make the protocol more useful than simply encouraging disagreement.

**Novelty boundary:** Minority correction and biological stop signals already have substantial prior art. Novelty must come from an implementable evidence-admission/cost tradeoff under noisy checks; the local literature gate remains incomplete.

#### Robustness

**Supported:** A plan, factor ledger, state machine and fixture viewer exist. The local viewer contains 25 fixture episodes across three scenario families, including injected failure. They exercise six displayed policies and repeated case roots; the prospective plan includes seven arms. The task reports 41 offline tests, which are software validation rather than native efficacy evidence.

**Unsupported:** Native model competence, minority emergence, useful correction rates, superior cost-effectiveness, or realistic team behavior. No native experiment has run.

**Unit:** Independent world/case lineage; the full temporal alarm sequence is one unit. Policies, repeated frames and fixture copies are dependent.

**Independent variation:** The local preview has four underlying case IDs plus a failure variant. Most voting and semantic evidence are authored fixtures.

**Assessment:** Conceptually well specified and honestly labeled. Its exact parser can solve the controlled grammar, so a model contribution remains unestablished.

#### Scenario adequacy

**Size:** Three families are sufficient for a first causal screen. Twenty-five fixtures are not a substantive behavioral sample, and 100 voters are unnecessary.

**Challenge:** Typed scope/version/TTL checks are useful engineering but easy for code. A native task must leave a real semantic judgment after deterministic validation.

**Complexity:** Correction, harmful reversal, blocked checks, stale scope and temporal reopening are distinct mechanisms. Natural dissent formation is correctly deferred.

**Realism:** Bridge inspection and build verification are plausible; independent noisy observations and actual task artifacts would improve transfer.

**Definition:** PROCEED/HOLD/DEFER and challenge fields are explicit. Close a protocol claim, not an agent's private belief. Freeze exact qualification assignments and count uncertainty around the proposed percentage gates.

#### Scenario assessments

**The Missing Bridge.** One fresh inspection confronts four endorsements copied from an old survey. Good source-independence instrument, but evaluator-authored right dissent makes correction easy. Include fresh incorrect inspections, uncertain age and verifier failure; compare source deduplication and always-check.

**The Passing Build.** A relevant failing test should outweigh a green summary, while wrong-platform/stale/optional failures should not. This can become a realistic task with executable artifacts. Use actual small build/test records with independently checked relevance, and reserve a semantic ambiguity stratum beyond exact metadata.

**The Alarm That Became True.** Best distinctive scenario: withdrawing a disproved alarm must not permanently discredit a source after the world changes. Separate new evidence from repeated old evidence, vary delay/change detection, and score useful reopening plus unnecessary repeated interruptions.

**Unavailable verification and injected failure.** Unresolved checks produce explicit deferral rather than a hidden majority fallback. This is necessary failure handling, not beneficial performance by itself. Include deadline utility of deferral and outage-correlated checks in later stress tests; never drop failed assignments.

#### Controls and parameters

**Retain:** Majority, blind veto, always-check, evidence gate, pooled solver, deterministic reference and planned matched-random admission Common potential observations, fixed deadlines and explicit verification costs Correct and incorrect dissent, withdrawal and new-evidence reopening

**Missing:** Semantic cases where exact metadata does not already determine the result A development-fixed verification quota for matched-random admission Concrete qualification assignment counts and uncertainty around percentage gates; the plan already defines the all-assigned primary endpoint Natural-minority formation on unselected cases as a separate later study

**Confounds:** Hardcoded initial minority is an intervention, not emergent dissent Admission, extra evidence, model calls and latency can all change together Always-check can dominate if checks are cheap and reliable Illustrative loss values encode preferences, not universal safety truth

**Majority × minority correctness (Essential identification).** Current: Both favorable and harmful correction planned Proposed: Cross correct/wrong majority with correct/wrong dissent on matched cases Prevents counting correction while hiding corruption.

**Verification quality, cost and delay (Essential identification).** Current: Small fixed budgets and logical deadline Proposed: Start with reliable/low-cost versus fallible/scarce checks; hold potential tapes fixed Establishes when selective admission is useful relative to always-check.

**Scope, freshness and ancestry (Essential instrument, then stress).** Current: Exact structured fields Proposed: Validate exact fields first; later test partial lineage and ambiguous applicability Distinguishes protocol plumbing from reasoning.

**Withdrawal and reopening (Essential for temporal claim).** Current: Three-step alarm fixture Proposed: New-world evidence versus repeated old claim, with varied timing Tests selective trust rather than permanent source blacklisting.

**Dissent formation and model diversity (Later separate study).** Current: Authored minority; intended Jev adapter Proposed: Unselected natural discussions retaining unanimous and unhelpful-minority cases Avoids selection on a right dissenter.

#### Next decisive study

**Question:** Can bounded evidence admission preserve useful corrections while reducing harmful reversals and unnecessary checks?

**Primary contrast:** Evidence gate versus always-check, with majority and exact rules as necessary references.

**Endpoint:** Correct on-time commitments across all assignments, accompanied by correction, corruption, deferral and check-cost components.

**Unit:** Independent case lineage; paired policy forks and alarm sequences clustered together.

**Design:** Qualify one Passing Build semantic task plus small bridge/alarm mechanism checks. Freeze a compact check-quality/cost contrast on fresh cases. Match available information and show resource tradeoffs instead of pretending every policy uses identical information after choosing to check.

**Go no go:** Continue if the model gate adds value beyond exact rules and has acceptable harmful-reversal cost. If deterministic scope checks solve the problem, recommend them. Do not call a high correction rate a success if the protocol also corrupts correct majorities.

## Experiment - Optimal Swarm Size

Careful design, oversized first programme. Establish a size-by-dependency effect before fitting a launch rule; compare against resource-only sizing.

### Optimal swarm size: a deployable conditional launch policy

**Evidence:** Detailed prospective design only. No implemented harness, validated task generator, qualification, model episode, or sizing result is established by these documents.

**Decision:** Develop the task contracts and a smaller identifying pilot. Defer the proposed 2,512-episode core/transfer programme and 1,280-episode optional extension until useful size-dependent heterogeneity exists and the runtime is measurable.

#### Scientific interest

**Judgment:** Scientifically worthwhile and operationally clear, with unusually careful prospective controls.

**Why:** A launch rule that increases verified success under an actual deadline and aggregate budget is useful; charging selection, coordination, retrieval, retries and cancellation makes the question substantially more meaningful than a best-looking N sweep.

**Novelty boundary:** Task- and architecture-dependent scaling is already acknowledged in the design. The proposed contribution is a deployable rule evaluated on fresh executions with charged overhead, not discovery of a universal optimum. Novelty remains unestablished pending the focused methods comparison.

#### Robustness

**Supported:** The draft specifies a coherent estimand, independent-root splits, intention-to-treat failures, hidden evaluators, aggregate resource accounting, paired comparisons and fresh policy evaluation. These are design strengths, not demonstrated harness properties.

**Unsupported:** No evidence yet supports an optimal size, reliable five-point near-optimality, a learned selector, physical-memory effects, or cross-domain transfer. One locked transfer family supports one-family transfer only.

**Unit:** An independently generated task root; repeated model samples, paraphrases, sizes and profiles remain nested or paired within that root.

**Independent variation:** Root semantics and dependencies, plus independent model sampling. Two development families and one transfer family do not create a broad population of independent task domains.

**Assessment:** Four fit and four validation roots per structural cell cannot establish fine conditional boundaries. The documents candidly flag this. Choose complexity and interval interpretation accordingly; do not let 2,512 correlated episodes imply strong precision. Simultaneous uncertainty must govern the smallest-within-epsilon decision.

#### Scenario adequacy

**Size:** N=1,2,4,8,16 counts addressable stateful contexts including the coordinator; four runnable slots in P0 make N>4 a queue/context-capacity intervention as well as a roster intervention. This is legitimate but must be interpreted explicitly.

**Challenge:** The arithmetic dossier and clamp repair are contract illustrations, not evidence of research-grade difficulty. Establish non-ceiling correctness and a genuine serial/parallel tradeoff before the response map.

**Complexity:** Vary dependency width/depth while matching atomic work, output count and evaluator burden. The roster-aware model also constructs a plan, so primary effects concern the whole protocol, including changed decomposition quality.

**Realism:** Repository repair offers practical task structure; synthetic dossiers and simulated incidents offer control. None yet captures long-running deployment, evolving requirements, human escalation or uncontrolled services, which should remain outside the initial claim.

**Definition:** Exact artifact contracts, equivalent repair sequences and accepted alternative citation proofs are strong. Pin all currently open actor-turn, truncation, initialization and integration-reserve values before implementation.

#### Scenario assessments

**Q-A baseline calibration and Q-B engineering qualification.** The 16 N=1 screening episodes and 64 larger-roster episodes intentionally use different caps; they cannot estimate a size effect. Success-conditioned pooled cost/time quantiles select an operating regime rather than a production SLA. Check cap-hit and solvability distributions by family/structure before freezing the core. Separate marginal cost/time quantiles do not guarantee joint feasibility. Preserve screening failures and stop when a cell is trivial or impossible.

**Evidence dossier: independent sections versus linked deductions.** Best first mechanism environment: verifiable sources and proof requirements make information access auditable. Trivial arithmetic risks measuring formatting or retrieval overhead alone. Use matched multistep provenance problems with plausible distractors and alternative valid proofs. Keep distractor load fixed initially; vary dependency structure, not every difficulty dimension simultaneously.

**Repository repair: independent defects versus interface chains.** A useful second family with real integration costs. Isolated copies and deterministic merging prevent uncontrolled filesystem races, but this is a particular coordination protocol; merge conflicts may dominate the apparent size effect. Match defect count and hidden-test burden; report planning, patch correctness and integration failures separately. Include meaningful interface dependencies rather than many independent one-line fixes.

**Incident triage: locked transfer.** Stateful observations, safety constraints and equivalent repair sequences are a stronger transfer test than renamed arithmetic. A task contract exists, but no reviewed held-out fixture yet demonstrates complexity or realizability. Validate the simulator and evaluator with separate development fixtures. Freeze transfer generators and selector features before revealing transfer outcomes; report the narrow family-transfer boundary.

**Core P0/P1/P2/P3 and optional M1/M2/M3/H1.** Baseline, half deadline, half budget and one runnable slot are interpretable one-axis contrasts. Reduced context, no ledger, reduced physical memory and eight slots are distinct extensions; no-ledger changes communication architecture. Initially prioritize N-by-dependency; add N-by-deadline or N-by-service-capacity only when the first pilot identifies a mechanism. Defer physical-memory claims until local measurement is available, and label ledger removal a protocol change.

**Fresh locked selector evaluation.** Charging selection and keeping the transfer size map sealed are excellent protections against hindsight. A global best fixed N is insufficient by itself to establish the value of task-conditioned sizing. Compare against a development-frozen resource-profile-only size rule, or explicitly make this an ablation of the existing heuristic. This distinguishes useful task features from simply responding to a tighter deadline or fewer slots.

#### Controls and parameters

**Retain:** N=1 with the same tools and sequential work interface; equal retrievable evidence union; aggregate budgets; coordinator included in N; hidden evaluator; all failures retained; paired roots; fresh policy runs; charged initialization/selection; source and context receipts.

**Missing:** A resource-only selector comparison for incremental task-feature value, and a separate deterministic scheduling fixture with a known public work graph to distinguish scheduler overhead from model decomposition errors. **Confounds:** Roster-dependent plans, N-by-service contention, aggregate context capacity and integration conflicts are combined in the operational effect. External API load and version changes require randomized blocked execution; logical concurrency is not a physical-hardware measurement.

#### Next decisive study

**Question:** Does task dependency structure produce a reproducible difference in the operational benefit of increasing a fixed-protocol roster?

**Primary contrast:** The paired N=4 minus N=1 success-by-deadline difference in parallel versus linked dossiers; N=2 locates the response rather than creating another primary claim.

**Endpoint:** Verified complete artifact received by the frozen deadline within aggregate budget; partial proof quality, cost and integration failures are diagnostic secondary outcomes.

**Unit:** Fresh independent dossier roots paired across sizes; model samples nested within root.

Hold out root lineages and use only genuinely launch-visible features. Train no flexible selector until a stable interaction is plausible.

**Go no go:** Expand if task scoring and resource accounting work, the regimes are neither ceiling nor floor, and root-level evidence suggests a practically meaningful conditional choice. Otherwise revise task difficulty or use the simplest fixed policy. Only then evaluate a frozen conditional rule against the resource-only baseline on fresh executions.

### Collective Sensing

**Decision:** Advance the narrow provenance mechanism; defer a topology sweep.

**Interest:** A strong mechanism question when the contribution is resistance to correlated or duplicated evidence. Merely changing a graph and watching agreement is much less informative. Biology supplies a motivating analogy; it does not establish that language-agent communication implements collective sensing.

**Robustness:** The dossier is prospective. The separate guide has a reproducible scripted majority/deduplication demonstration, not a measured model effect. **Scenario adequacy:** The proposed 12–20 agents are unnecessary for the first question. **Comparison:** Source-naive vs source-aware aggregation of the same delivered messages on one fixed network, with independent-origin and duplicated-origin blocks. Silent ensemble is a secondary baseline.

### The Commons

**Decision:** Advance one evidence-gate study; park attribution and incentive manipulation.

**Interest:** Useful research if verification protects a reusable knowledge resource at a measurable opportunity cost. This is a stronger initial contribution than a generic cooperation game. **Robustness:** No completed experiment in the proposal establishes useful output, free-riding or emergent cheating. Measure precision at a fixed review budget plus coverage of adjudicated positives and investigator time. Random sampling estimates background prevalence; it is not merely a weak rival to beat.

**Next step:** Assemble one bounded permitted archive and a transparent annotation policy with ordinary-automation negatives. Evaluate ranking on an independent partition with blinded evidence packets. Park the project if ground truth is too weak to distinguish useful triage from rediscovery of known examples.

**Key parameters:**

- Essential: transparent ranker versus random and simple keyword retrieval at the same human review budget.
- Essential: candidate base rate and hard-negative composition, reported for the evaluated archive rather than assumed universal.
- Later: time drift, new platforms and adversarial adaptation; defer new live collection until offline utility is established.

### Agent Institution Bench

**Decision:** Fold into Commons or reporting; run one auditable rule.

**Interest:** A single quarantine/appeal rule could expose a useful quality-versus-delay tradeoff. A broad institution simulator would dilute the mechanism and overlap heavily with Commons and Whistleblowing. Institutional language should describe an explicit decision rule, not imply emergent governance.

**Robustness:** The card contains no completed rule evaluation. Shared-artifact workloads are units; disputes within the same workload interact through budget and availability. An infallible adjudicator with free review makes the rule's advantage almost tautological, while bundled sanctions make effects impossible to localize.

**Scenario adequacy:** Use a small artifact dependency task where quarantining a bad result prevents harm but quarantining a good result delays real work. Appeals should sometimes overturn a mistaken review using checkable evidence. If every challenger is correct and every dispute obvious, the institution has no meaningful challenge.

**Question:** Does a quarantine/appeal rule improve valid artifact delivery at an explicit review cost?

**Comparison:** Ordinary release/review versus one fixed quarantine/appeal rule on matched clean and contaminated contribution streams.

**Endpoint:** Valid output, false quarantine, delay and adjudication cost.

**Controls:** Use the same contribution stream, model population, evidence and aggregate budget. Hide evaluator truth from reviewers, count valid work delayed and invalid work released, and document when a procedural rule changes information access. Keep sanctions and reputation fixed outside the first contrast.

**Next step:** Implement the rule as a small extension of the Commons artifact task, not a new platform. Compare paired workloads and inspect correction of mistaken quarantines alongside total verified output. Progress only if the rule trades measurable benefits against real costs.

**Key parameters:**

- Essential: one fixed quarantine/appeal rule versus ordinary release/review, with clean and invalid contributions.
- Essential: false challenge opportunities, review cost and delay; pin them at a stated operating point initially.
- Later: appeal access, reviewer incentives, collusion and authority structure as separate mechanisms.

### NCA Observatory

**Decision:** Treat as reproduction first; defer new claims until the published baseline works.

**Interest:** Strong educational and reproducibility value; uncertain original research value without a specific unexplained failure boundary. Local learned dynamics are interesting on their own. Their resemblance to a language-agent network does not make their evidence transferable between systems.

**Robustness:** The card establishes no trained-NCA reproduction or new-paper performance. Its browser illustration is hand-specified cellular automata. One pretrained checkpoint can reproduce one instrument; robustness across training runs requires independently trained checkpoints, while repeated damage masks estimate perturbation variation only.

**Scenario adequacy:** Choose one published maze or pattern task with an exact functional score. Visual regrowth can conceal lost task function; a maze task may offer a sharper endpoint than image similarity. Keep grid size and update rule fixed until undamaged behavior and ordinary damage recovery reproduce.

**Question:** Can a documented local-rule model reproduce a specified recovery curve?

**Comparison:** Pinned source/weights and matched undamaged/damaged rollouts.

**Endpoint:** Task function and post-damage loss, compute included.

**Controls:** Pin published source and weights, pair initial states/update randomness and retain undamaged rollouts. Separate reproduction mismatch from perturbation failure. Count recovery compute and residual error; random masks cannot represent targeted removal of functionally critical cells.

**Next step:** Reproduce one released baseline with a documented functional curve, then perform one predeclared perturbation contrast on fresh masks. If usable artifacts are unavailable, present a classical-CA teaching study under its own name instead of promising NCA reproduction.

**Key parameters:**

- Essential first perturbation: damage geometry/location at matched removed fraction, or one damaged-fraction axis—not a full multidimensional sweep.
- Essential definition: post-damage computation horizon and functional recovery threshold fixed before inspecting trajectories.
- Later: asynchronous updates, out-of-distribution grid size, longer stability horizons and independent checkpoints. Training-on-damage history must be reported.
