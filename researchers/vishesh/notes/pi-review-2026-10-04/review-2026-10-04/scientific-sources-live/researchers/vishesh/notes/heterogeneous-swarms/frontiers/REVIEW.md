# Editorial review of the biological revision

Reviewer: vishesh/codex-heterogeneous. This is self-review, not approval by another researcher. No formal review scores were fabricated.

The initial bank emphasized allocation, control and robustness. The new set shifts the unit of explanation to inherited policies, persistent pairs, habitats and cultural lineages. That follows the owner's preference without changing existing rubric weights or erasing historical scores.

| Item | Editorial verdict | Main reason it could fail as new research |
|---|---|---|
| HX-31 | Highest priority for methods review | Cost-free pruning produces no real selection mechanism; fixed division of labor may explain everything. |
| HX-32 | Retain, narrow sharply | Organelle formation and evolutionary constraint already occur in digital-life work. Only interface-mediated constraint and release are candidates. |
| HX-33 | Retain conditional on reciprocal effects | Artifact inheritance is directly discussed already. Generic memory benefits are not the gap. |
| HX-34 | Reserve | Mind-virus research occupies propagation; benign replication–utility conflict needs a sharper independent contribution. |
| HX-35 | Priority for methods review | Emergent language and social differentiation are known; translator-resistant procedural recombination failure is harder to demonstrate. |
| HX-36 | Reserve | Frequency-dependent selection is classical, and Digital Red Queen warns that competition can converge behavior. |
| HX-37 | Retain; relatively tangible intervention | Ordinary retrieval from partial trajectories may explain all gains; stable coexistence is a substantially stronger claim. |
| HX-38 | Close alternate to HX-37 | Roles after scaffolding can be memory persistence; functional differentiation must survive explicit-memory controls. |

No design is ready for a novelty claim. Each has a null result worth keeping: no selected dependence, freely interchangeable partners, generic habitat benefit, hybrids fixed by a dictionary, or consumer utility explained by extra attempts. These would narrow the biological analogy rather than count as execution failures.

The scores measure appeal under the owner's rubric. They do not encode acceptance probability, literature novelty probability or a biological effect size. A high visual score requires an event-to-frame mapping in the eventual run, not an attractive invented animation.

The existing `heterogeneous-methods-review` follow-up should include these eight cards, especially full readings of Kelley, GlossoGen, MATM and the Artifact Ecology preprint, then the bacterial cross-feeding lead. Formal promotion still needs the repository's survey gate and a reviewer from another researcher. No peer review, saturation or completed full-paper reading is claimed here.
