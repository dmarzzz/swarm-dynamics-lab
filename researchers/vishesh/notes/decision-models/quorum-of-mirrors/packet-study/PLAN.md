## Question and decision

Does repeated or distorted reporting change a solver's source-grounded decision while authoritative source evidence stays fixed? This reconnects fidelity with Quorum's repeated-evidence mechanism. A deterministic parser plus one-vote-per-source is the strong comparator, not an intentionally weak text matcher. If the tested native solver is vulnerable, require deterministic source parsing/counting or abstention for this grammar; if robust, report only bounded robustness, not a benefit from swarms or a reason to replace exact code. No conversational swarm treatment is claimed.

## Cases and controls

Six source-reading mechanisms: polarity, time, entity, unit conversion, planned versus observed status, and correction precedence. Each packet has three authenticated source receipts with distinct supplied independent groups, equal reliability (>0.5), a query about the same binary proposition, and reports bound to receipt IDs. Authentication/independence are synthetic assumptions. Source data are complete and small; evidence-MAP is equal-weight source majority, not sampled world truth. No hidden evaluator information is required.

Each root gets a 2x2 crossed set: focal report count one/five × faithful/inverted focal claim. The other two reports and every source receipt stay fixed. Focal source is aligned or misaligned with source majority in balanced strata; direction is balanced. This prevents selecting only report-majority failures. More copies change packet length as part of the treatment; no claim of separating attention/length from repetition. Reordering is frozen across matched source identities and checked as an invariance of the reference.

Development: 12 roots (two/mechanism), 48 packets. Qualification candidates: six separate roots (one/mechanism), 24 packets. Evaluation: 24 new roots (four/mechanism), 96 packets. All four direction/alignment strata appear once per evaluation mechanism. Split by root/acquisition and scenario domain names; linguistic grammar is deliberately shared and known. These are not held-out language families or independently authored field events. All variants stay with the parent. Report 24 evaluation scenario roots, not 96 independent worlds; template dependence limits inference even across roots.
