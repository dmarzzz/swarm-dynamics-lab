# Eight-case external-label development baseline

2026-10-04. **Completed offline; zero model calls and zero new paid cost.** Owner/PI direction limits this step to inspected development controls. The60-agent successor remains unfunded. This report contains scientific findings only; the previously blocked cross-task spending disclosure has not been retried or rerouted.

## Result and recommendation

Six of eight published labels are defensible against the supplied actor-visible QA evidence; one is limited and one ambiguous. This is an owning-agent evidence audit, not a new independent human annotation or model performance result. All eight were already inspected development controls and remain excluded from fresh qualification/evaluation. No external source pages were reverified; the audit evaluates the supplied dataset packet, not present-day factual truth.

**No paid exchange experiment is proposed from this audit.** Useful semantic mechanisms exist, but baseline headroom has not been demonstrated. Several decisive QA answers restate the claim, say yes/no, explicitly announce uncertainty, or state the annotation conclusion. Increasing the number of agents would not establish usefulness on those cases. Preserve Quorum as a provisional research direction, not a final portfolio selection or paid approval.

## What was checked mechanically

The current actor projection was reconstructed for all eight source rows. Actor fields and nested evidence fields match their allowlists; gold label, evaluator justification and original source URL paths are absent. Every cited evidence ID exists. Actor hashes and canonical document groups are recorded. Archived aliases normalize consistently, while semantic URL parameters remain distinct. All17 existing contract/intake checks pass.

These checks do not detect every semantic answer cue. QA questions and answers are themselves annotations and can contain the verdict reasoning. Hiding metadata cannot make annotation-guided evidence into unannotated primary evidence. Similarly, document grouping is not authenticated independent acquisition, and the current board groups identical cited evidence sets rather than proving that outlets have separate origins.

## Strong baseline and remaining empirical unknown

The strongest relevant economical baseline is a **single-context evidence reader** with all the same QA evidence, explicit entity/date/status/scope checks, literal evidence citations and an uncertainty option. Compare its initial answer with its own bounded reconsideration before paying for peers. The existing exact grammar parser cannot solve arbitrary entailment; conversely, lexical retrieval or literal citation validation alone cannot certify a verdict. No invented classifier score or owning-agent audit is reported as native single-agent accuracy.

These packets mostly ask a reader to find or combine an already supplied answer. The potentially valuable mechanisms—conflicting versions, nonexhaustive totals, causal alternatives and common-source dependence—do not yet have both clean targets and demonstrated baseline failures. There is no evidence bottleneck requiring several agents to discover different information in the present complete-context contract.

A future economical exchange-versus-private test would need: independently supported unambiguous targets; source-linked evidence without an explicit annotator verdict; clean event-separated cases; a strong single-context baseline that is competent yet has consequential residual errors; and a specific reason peer evidence can correct those errors. Removing decisive QA conclusions or changing claims creates new packets whose external labels cannot be inherited automatically. Those conditions are not met here, so this audit does **not** propose a funded follow-up or another qualification by default.

## Portfolio disposition

**PARK paid expansion; retain reusable evidence-handling components and the limited semantic research direction.** Controlled grammar qualification is complete and the exact-parser comparator is sufficient in that domain. Realistic swarm benefit remains GAP. The strongest argument for eventual continuation is source/version/scope handling in natural language; the strongest argument against allocating one of four slots now is that its proposed cases do not yet distinguish collaboration from simply reading the supplied answer well.
