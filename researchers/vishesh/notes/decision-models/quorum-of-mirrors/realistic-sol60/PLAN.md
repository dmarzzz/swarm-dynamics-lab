## TLDR

Can 60 GPT-6 Sol reviewers reach a better evidence-grounded verdict on real claims than one or six reviewers, and does source-aware exchange outperform the same 60 agents privately reconsidering? Use externally annotated AVeriTeC claims, retaining published human verdicts. Compare correct grounded answers, harmful confident errors, abstention, latency and cost. Start with six qualification claims, then at most eight fresh claim clusters in a feasibility pilot. Sixty agents are a committee size, not 60 independent observations. Public benchmark contamination and annotation-guided evidence limit generalization.

## Question and prediction

Practical use: an analyst receives several reports that repeat a claim, mix dates and entities, or rely on the same underlying evidence. Produce a traceable verdict and explain what additional evidence would resolve uncertainty. Repeated agreement alone must not turn an unsupported claim into a supported fact.

Primary contrast: 60 source-aware communicating reviewers versus the identical initial 60 reviewer checkpoints continued privately, with equal additional call/output budgets. Prediction: evidence exchange can correct missed qualifications; it can also propagate a persuasive error. Either direction is informative. Secondary: 1 and 6 agents, plurality voting, citation deduplication and retrieval-only references. Do not claim a model-strength effect from historical Sonnet versus current Sol: task and model change together.

SP-02 established exact clause selection on five authored grammars (40/40). Its exact parser already solves those grammars. It did not establish natural-language entailment, a real-world verdict, source independence or benefits of agent communication. These are the reasons for new data; no additional aliases of the old grammar are used.

2. Qualification Q-R60: six distinct claims, each one complete-evidence reader plus six independently initialized reviewers =42 calls. No peer exchange yet. Qualification must subsequently include a scripted peer-context contract test offline; a small native exchange check is reserved within the pilot's first root, not an undeclared extra call allowance.
3. Pilot P-R60: eight fresh clusters, all conditions assigned before launch. For each: one agent gets two turns; a six-agent group gets one independent turn and one peer-review turn (12 calls); a separate 60-agent group gets one independent turn, then each checkpoint forks into source-aware exchange and own-only reconsideration (180 calls). Total 194 calls/cluster, 1552 pilot calls. The first pilot root is an operational sentinel; finish its assigned arms, stop remaining roots on protocol/transport failure, and retain it in analysis. Never use its scientific score to choose whether to continue.
4. Total maximum 1594 physical calls including qualification; zero automatic retries, relabels, model switches or repair calls. Agents have distinct IDs, stateless initialization and no operator history/gold. Same underlying evidence in every committee size. Fixed deterministic document-order rotation balanced across agents; forked continuations share the exact first-turn state. Concurrency at most 6; 60 logical participants do not require60 simultaneous hosts.
5. Each reviewer returns verdict, confidence, cited evidence IDs plus literal supporting spans, unresolved question and concise justification. Keep model interpretation separate from mechanically verified citations; an exact quotation is not proof of entailment. The general-language mapping cannot reuse SP-02's grammar parser as an oracle.
6. Source-aware exchange: deterministic board from first-round answers groups identical evidence-record IDs and preserves dissent and conflicting statements. No gold, evaluator judgement or model-authored coordinator injected. Board selects at most eight representative groups, one report per (verdict, evidence-ID set), tie-broken by deterministic agent ID; round-robin verdict categories retain minority categories; omitted counts and omitted categories recorded. Whole underlying evidence remains accessible to every agent. Fixed board and total context token caps; overflow blocks a root rather than silently dropping its primary evidence. An additional count-all plurality computed from the same first-turn votes costs no extra calls.
7. Own-only fork: exact same first-turn state and evidence; ask the reviewer to check its own evidence and possible counterarguments, without peer answers. Same request/output ceilings and revision opportunity. Token usage may differ, so this is matched call/output entitlement, not identical realized compute. Report actual costs.
8. Aggregation: deterministic plurality of valid verdicts, ties become abstain; all 60 seats remain in denominator. Fewer than 54 valid decisions in a 60-agent arm (6 in the 6-agent arm) makes that arm incomplete. Missing/invalid is not a vote for a label. Present count-all and evidence-ID-deduplicated outputs as engineering baselines, not proof of independent sources.

## Metrics

Primary: claim-cluster paired difference in correct verdict rate, communicating60 minus own-only60; report eight paired rows and an exact paired interval/test without treating agents as n. Eight clusters give a coarse feasibility result (one claim=12.5 percentage points), not robust superiority. Even 8/8 successes have a two-sided 95% binomial lower bound about 0.63 under IID assumptions; purposive strata/related claims weaken that population reading further. A larger confirmatory sample needs pilot discordance and separate planning.

Also report correct+grounded rate (verdict plus evidence entailment audit), citation validity, confidently wrong rate (confidence>=0.8), abstention, revisions toward/away from gold, unsupported peer claims propagated, class/complexity breakdown, per-root tokens/latency/dollars and answer at each stage. A lexical retrieval baseline ranks evidence; it must not be reported as solving semantic entailment. Strong native single/six/own-only controls receive the same full evidence.

Human benchmark verdicts score exact labels; unchanged labels are hidden from actors. Entailment assessment of model-selected spans requires a separate blinded annotation pass or clearly labeled owning-agent audit, with disagreements visible. Do not call that audit independent human labeling. No accuracy claim from a citation-only score. Preserve invalid, interrupted and unstarted assignments; primary complete-pair analysis plus worst/best missing-outcome bounds and all-assigned table.

Decision: useful support means a positive paired effect with grounded examples and a credible cost advantage/decision benefit; with eight claims this motivates a larger study, not deployment. Null/adverse favors the cheaper baseline or private review. If all controls saturate, stop scaling. If baseline competence fails, repair cases/interface; do not interpret treatment effects. No automatic confirmatory collection.
