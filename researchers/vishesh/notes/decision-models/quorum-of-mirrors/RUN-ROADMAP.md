## C1: does discussion add more than another pass?

Four uninspected seeds 41000–41003; q=.65 on even seeds and .80 on odd seeds. Sample truth uniformly, three conditionally independent root bits with accuracy q, favored root uniformly, and two concealed roots independently of truth/favored root. Retain every world including unanimity; never replenish easy or null cases. Freeze RNG/version and manifest before generation. Four worlds, two per q stratum, support a feasibility report only.

For each world cross repetition (3,3,3 versus 7,1,1), ancestry (full versus partial), and instruction (standard versus source-aware): eight cells. Keep nine reports and three actual roots; do not tell partial-lineage actors the hidden root count or graph. All five judges in a cell receive the reports. Pair permutations, facts and model settings across instruction arms. No rationales, tools, persistent memory or new observations.

Each cell makes five round-0 calls. Freeze these outputs and branch each judge into two separately executed round-1 calls: one sees all five original slots; the other sees only its own original slot and is asked to reconsider. Never feed either branch into the other. Both branches use the same model, output cap and initial decision. Randomize branch dispatch order with a frozen schedule. Each trajectory has five initial plus five second-pass calls; shared initial results save dispatches without making branches independent. Across branches this costs 15 calls/cell, 120/world. The comparison controls call count, not prompt-token count; record actual tokens/costs/latency and prohibit silent truncation.

Each repetition × ancestry × instruction cell also gets a pooled initial/self-review pair: sixteen calls/world. Both pooled instruction variants match their committee variants. Total: 136/world × four = 544. Retain fixed five-slot quorum: commit ZERO or ONE only with at least three votes, else DEFER. Failed slots are unavailable and never shrink the threshold. Round-0 vote is a no-extra-call reference. Count-all, visible-root deduplication, capped-opaque, the distribution-aware report-count shortcut and evaluator-only true-root oracle are deterministic references, not native randomized arms.

Primary outcome is correct commitment against sampled truth / all four assigned worlds for each arm/branch. Report peer minus self-review within each cell, plus the instruction-by-repetition interaction separately in both branches and lineage conditions. Show every constituent count; a favorable interaction with worse absolute accuracy is not success. Report wrong commitments, DEFER, unavailable assignments, validity, round transitions, wrong unanimity and resource use. Root-MAP agreement is secondary. Choice scores are not world probabilities; no Brier/calibration claim. Judges, branches and calls are not independent worlds; no significance, power or general efficacy claim.

If completion/identity/accounting fails, stop and preserve missing outcomes; no scientific retry allowance. If peer results do not improve correct commitments over self-review AND the instruction-matched pooled option, do not recommend more judges on this evidence. If they do, require no increase in wrong commitments and inspect per-world/token costs before proposing a larger replication. If too few cells discriminate, report uninformative and do not replenish.
