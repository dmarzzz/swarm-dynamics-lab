## Shortlist and robustness

The first five are Phantom Coast, Stop-Signal Swarm, Panic Queue, Quorum of Mirrors and Menu Parasites. The first four are close enough that available evidence and implementation risk should decide order. Start with Phantom Coast for the clearest spatial demonstration, using Quorum of Mirrors as its measurement foundation. Stop-Signal Swarm supplies the strongest biological mechanism; Panic Queue supplies the most direct operational payoff. Menu Parasites is the most specifically tied to the typed choice interface.

With a practical-first sensitivity weighting (20/45/20/15), Panic Queue and Quorum of Mirrors rise. A ten-point reassessment of one component can move an idea by up to three total points under the main rubric. No claimed top-five guarantee survives arbitrary scoring changes. These are decision aids, not objective rankings.

## Disposition of the user's seed ideas

- Making Jev disagree: develop into Quorum of Mirrors, measuring error dependence and useful evidence rather than disagreement alone.
- Erasing the United States: develop into Phantom Coast. Land/water geography and country membership are different labels; a wrong country name is not disappearance of land.
- Cat mistaken for dog: retain as a lower-ranked pipeline audit. Jev has no native image input in the inspected specification.
- Wrong map/screen target: retain as Decoy Beacon, restricted to synthetic harmless objects; separate perception errors from decision errors.

## Skeptical disposition

No flagship should merely rediscover prompt sensitivity, semantic label bias, calibration shift or duplicate-vote arithmetic. Each finalist below states its nearest prior art, an incremental question, and an outcome that ends or narrows the project. A deterministic baseline win is acceptable. No result is assumed to favor a swarm or Jev.

### DM-01 — Phantom Coast

The visible question is geographic corruption; the scientific extension is propagation and reversibility under fixed exposure. Avoids a one-off jailbreak screenshot.

**Comparison:** Cross localized false reports versus equally sized random errors with independent decisions, neighbor exchange and source-aware exchange; hold distinct evidence and total calls fixed.

**Main confound:** Memorized geography, spatial autocorrelation, unequal evidence, and an incorrect map definition. A coordinate grid is not an image.

**Drop or narrow:** Reject the collective-amplification claim if errors do not spread beyond directly exposed cells or are fully predicted by the single-agent dose response.

### DM-03 — Stop-Signal Swarm

Honeybee cross-inhibition is direct biological prior. The Jev test concerns model-generated contradiction judgments with non-oracle evidence, beyond generic dissent.

**Comparison:** Compare endorsement-only exchange, contradiction-linked stop signals, random throttling and fixed rate limits at the same message budget; include true novel evidence and false alarms.

**Main confound:** An oracle contradiction detector would give the answer away; evidence availability and stop-message bandwidth must match.

**Drop or narrow:** Drop the biological-mechanism claim if random throttling or simple rate limits achieve the same tradeoff.

### DM-04 — Panic Queue

Confidence-gate pressure is already reported. New scope is temporal feedback, finite service capacity and a matched-capacity defense. The immune/alarm analogy is limited.

**Comparison:** Replay paired clean and bounded-corruption decision streams through a finite reviewer queue; compare fixed confidence thresholds, queue-aware admission and reserved review capacity at equal service capacity.

**Main confound:** Arrival rate and reviewer speed can manufacture overload. Include matched load without semantic corruption and score rejected tasks.

**Drop or narrow:** Drop the collective contribution if a standard queue fed the measured single-item escalation rates explains the entire effect and no communication-dependent residual remains.

### DM-02 — Quorum of Mirrors

Correlated AI errors are established. The narrower contribution is a Jev-specific evidence-versus-prompt intervention with returned distributions and matched resources.

**Comparison:** Compare repeated identical Jev calls, meaning-preserving prompt variants and disjoint evidence packets at equal calls; include a single solver with the union of evidence and dependency-aware aggregation.

**Main confound:** Task difficulty creates apparent correlation; repeated calls share weights and data; majority agreement is not correctness.

**Drop or narrow:** Do not claim useful diversity if gains vanish against a single solver with matched evidence or only arise from more tokens.

### DM-05 — Menu Parasites

Label polarity is existing prior. The opening is dynamic semantic-equivalence classes and their downstream collective consequences.

**Comparison:** Hold latent actions fixed while varying aliases, redundant options and order. Compare raw Choice, deduplicated canonical IDs and separate binary judgments with an explicit none/abstain option.

**Main confound:** Equivalent alternatives may legitimately split probability. Choice confidence depends on menu size; use a fixed-action null model.

**Drop or narrow:** If all change is predicted by probability normalization or disappears after aggregating aliases, report a harness arithmetic effect rather than semantic manipulation.

### DM-06 — Noise Thermostat

Biological exploration analogy; needs stronger evidence grounding before priority promotion.

**Comparison:** Vary private versus shared noise at matched marginal error and call budget; compare random exploration with fresh evidence acquisition.

**Main confound:** Randomization can make an attractive animation without improving the task.

**Drop or narrow:** Drop if extra evidence explains all gains or noise only lowers clean performance.

### DM-14 — Protected Dissent

Good companion treatment; overlaps established dissent questions.

**Comparison:** Reserve a fraction of scouts for private sensing versus allocating the same budget to ordinary social participants.

**Main confound:** Protected scouts may simply receive better evidence.

**Drop or narrow:** Merge with Stop-Signal Swarm if only generic diversity explains gains.

### DM-07 — Memory of a False Map

Strong overlap with Healing Helping Hands, Immune Response and Swarm of Theseus; keep as an ablation, not a new flagship.

**Comparison:** Compare fresh-state calls, retained private memory and retained shared memory under the same withdrawal schedule.

**Main confound:** Confuses persistence in a database with changes to the model.

**Drop or narrow:** Merge into existing recovery work if no decision-model-specific mechanism remains.

### DM-13 — Branch Poisoning

Strong overlap with influence and heterogeneous-agent research.

**Comparison:** Compare hard routing, reversible routing and a small random audit path at matched lookup budget.

**Main confound:** Routing changes both information and compute.

**Drop or narrow:** Merge with existing influence work unless typed routing creates a distinct measurable boundary.

### DM-15 — Decoy Beacon

Visually clear but indirect biological connection and existing classifier prior.

**Comparison:** Use inert shape targets with an exact scene record; compare duplicate labels, spatial distractors and clean views with a deterministic selector.

**Main confound:** Jev receives scene text, not the screen.

**Drop or narrow:** Drop if an exact rule solves every case or the error lies in preprocessing.

### DM-09 — Schema Schism

Interface diagnostic; high confounding risk without semantic-equivalence validation.

**Comparison:** Compare Choice versus Noul formulations with held-out threshold calibration and matched facts.

**Main confound:** Different primitives can ask genuinely different questions.

**Drop or narrow:** Fold into Menu Parasites if disagreements follow thresholds alone.

### DM-12 — Missing Option

Useful but familiar selective-classification issue.

**Comparison:** Remove the true option with and without abstention; compare confidence and independent answerability checks.

**Main confound:** Obvious forced-choice artifact.

**Drop or narrow:** Keep as qualification unless a new downstream consequence emerges.

### DM-10 — Cat/Dog Perception Jury

Reframes the user example honestly; not direct adversarial vision.

**Comparison:** Fix the image and separately vary the frozen perception output and the adversarial caption; include perception-only and text-only baselines.

**Main confound:** Jev does not see pixels; this is a pipeline experiment.

**Drop or narrow:** Defer if the perception system dominates the failure or image licensing is unresolved.

### DM-08 — Confidence Cartography

Known demo extended with ground truth; modest novelty.

**Comparison:** Compare returned probabilities with majority-class, geographic-distance and calibrated baselines on a fixed grid.

**Main confound:** Unequal-area grid and ambiguous coast resolution.

**Drop or narrow:** Stop after replication if it adds no insight beyond existing coordinate demos.

### DM-11 — Batch Isolation

Already close to public evaluation; engineering prerequisite.

**Comparison:** Compare named isolated records, named batches and positional indexing at fixed items and schemas.

**Main confound:** Index resolution rather than collective reasoning may explain errors.

**Drop or narrow:** Treat as replication if existing batching evidence predicts outcomes.
