Read the experiment correctly

The configurations change model, team size and chair compute settings together. Agent calls are dependent. This pilot tests execution and an influence mechanism on one authored scenario; it cannot establish a general effect or isolate team size. All vendors and records are synthetic.

What the evidence supports

Source extraction, model choice and the deterministic action guard are measured separately. A guard refusal does not mean the model reasoned correctly. A successful repair cohort does not erase the earlier failed qualification. The pilot preserves raw advice, source citations, changes of preference, the final decision table and guarded outcomes.
