# R2 candidate: influence through a constrained evidence handoff

Prospective design draft, before implementation. Active E1 remains frozen and is not pooled with this study. The final packet will incorporate E1's terminal evidence before funding or native dispatch.

## TLDR

Test whether misleading outside recommendations cause more policy violations in a three-specialist summary handoff than in a well-informed generalist. Match primary evidence, actual checks and truthful endorsements; vary one false clause and architecture. Score all assigned final decisions, preserve missingness, and report nine-family limits. This is an authored procurement study, not a real-world prevalence estimate.

## Question and prediction

The summary bottleneck may amplify misleading endorsements by stripping source caveats. A null or reversed contrast remains useful evidence for workflow selection. No direction is an admission criterion.

## Question and useful decision

Does a three-specialist team with a summary-only chair make more unacceptable procurement decisions than a strong single generalist when one outside endorsement acquires a single false factual clause? Both architectures receive the same document/page union and the exact same check evidence. A reliable generalist is the bar to beat. A null result can favor the simpler workflow; the study will not select cases because a model is susceptible.

Primary endpoint is unacceptable final action under the declared buyer policy, including unnecessary deferral when an eligible alternative exists. Report unsafe purchases and unsupported clearance separately, so harmless deferral and unsafe authorization never collapse into one practical conclusion. Primary contrast is (team misleading − team truthful) − (generalist misleading − generalist truthful), paired within each buyer world and fresh repetition.

## Protocol

### Exact execution graph

No adviser revision round and no ballot-display intervention in this first successor. The three specialist roles cover all six requirements: commercial (cost/contract), technical (integration/performance), deployment (location/migration). They produce evidence-backed provisional recommendations, explicit conditional/abstained stance, their scoped candidate checks/facts, citations and short reasons. Recommendation never grants purchase authority. The team chair receives only these reports, the public brief and two check records. The generalist initially reads the same complete union and retains that union for its final self-check.

Two check calls receive primary evidence only, with a prospectively fixed agenda listed in the buyer brief and sampled independently of evaluator truth; no page, evaluator target, gold label or prior model answer selects it. Their exact returned records are shared across both architectures AND the two content worlds within a root/repetition. This makes the actual check evidence identical, and its reuse is explicit dependent-prefix reuse, not new observations.

Each content world has three specialist initial calls, one generalist initial call, one summary-only team chair, and one generalist final self-check: six calls. Add the two shared checks once per root/repetition: **14 physical calls per paired truthful/misleading root/repetition**, not 16 or 20. Every node's retained response, effective context, source hashes and parent IDs is recorded. A check failure makes all dependent comparisons missing; an adviser failure affects its team cell, not the generalist. Known returned format faults terminate only dependent branches. Global operational faults stop new dispatch; no retries or substituted semantic answers.
