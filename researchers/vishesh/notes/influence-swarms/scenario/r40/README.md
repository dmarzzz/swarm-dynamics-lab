# Forty agents, one procurement decision

**Prepared instrument; no native R40 calls yet.** The [prospective plan](PLAN.md) specifies the question, controls, nine authored families, qualification, costs and stopping rules. Earlier E1 outcomes remain in their own [post-mortem](../reviews/E1-E0-01-post.md).

The public flow is **Source → Advisers → Decider**. The Decider reviews recommendations and makes the final choice; internal `chair` schema identifiers are retained for reproducibility. Forty means36 specialist identities, three role leads and one Decider, not40 repeated calls. Each specialist receives two peers' initial reports for one revision. Twelve revised reports feed a role lead; three role leads feed the Decider. The four-member team and one generalist receive the same evidence union and shared actual checks.

Only named native stages may consume a source-bound finite grant in the original ledger. Qualification has756 calls and27 final decisions; conditional main has2,988 calls and108 final decisions. The neutral-page panel is explicitly deferred; truthful endorsements are not a neutral baseline. Passing offline fixtures is not model qualification.

Run offline checks with `python3 -m unittest discover -s researchers/vishesh/notes/influence-swarms/scenario/r40 -p 'test_*.py'`. Fixtures are labeled software checks, not empirical outcomes. `cases.py` builds18 deterministic authored dossiers; actor-visible case IDs are opaque and exclude evaluator family/target fields. `instrument.py` compiles every context, maintains identities, preserves reports with lossless columnar serialization and validates returned structure without repairing semantic errors. `runtime.py` requires an existing original ledger and genuine grant, reserves atomically before dispatch, rejects duplicate work IDs, and isolates known format failures to dependent branches. `analysis.py` retains every assigned final endpoint and separates missingness bounds from sampling uncertainty. `worker.py` enforces source/runtime, public registration, allocation, budget and fresh native qualification checks.

Native outputs remain private; public projections contain categorical outcomes, numeric scores and authored findings. The post-mortem must inspect incorrect judgments and failed requests, reconcile the original ledger, preserve all attempts and complete the shared scientific rubric. No hidden reasoning or universal distributed-intelligence safety claim follows from these authored tasks.
