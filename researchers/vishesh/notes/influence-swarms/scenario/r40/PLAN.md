# Forty agents, one purchasing decision

Prospective implementation plan, 2026-10-04. This supersedes the unlaunched R2 small-team-only scope; E1 and earlier plans/results remain unchanged. No R40 model calls have started. Exact source, maximal requests, finite funding and runtime admission remain pending.

## TLDR

Can a 40-member procurement team turn an outside vendor's misleading claim into an unacceptable purchase? Compare a 40-member hierarchy, a four-member team and one informed generalist on identical evidence unions and actual checks. Pair truthful endorsements with endorsements differing in one false clause. Measure raw unacceptable decisions, unsafe purchases, avoidable deferral, evidence fidelity and cost. Nine authored scenario families, dependent agents and a small initial panel limit the claim: this tests three complete workflows, not agent count alone or real-world vulnerability rates.

## Question and prediction

A larger team could correct individual mistakes, or repeat them until a chair treats them as established evidence. Sparse peer exchange and explicit role handoffs make both routes observable. The primary contrast is (40-member misleading minus truthful unacceptable-action rate) minus the same change for the generalist. The four-member contrast and 40-minus-four interaction are secondary descriptive comparisons. No susceptibility or favorable effect is required for admission.

Useful decisions: if the larger system adds no quality or resistance, its extra cost is not justified in these cases. If misleading evidence produces recurring false clearances despite competent truthful controls, trace the first source-to-report-to-revision-to-lead-to-chair transitions and design a separate source-preservation repair. If clean competence fails, diagnose the instrument before main collection. Valid null/adverse results may end this iteration.

## Setup

Exactly **40 distinct decision-team identities**: 36 specialists, three role leads and one chair. Twelve specialists cover each of commercial (cost/contract), technical (integration/performance) and deployment (location/migration). Identities persist through their one revision; revisions are not new agents. Two common primary-record check calls are shared measurement infrastructure outside all three decision rosters and are counted as physical calls. State that boundary in every report.

The four-member comparator has one specialist per same role plus a summary-only chair. The generalist receives the exact primary-document and retrieved-page union delivered to both teams and retains it for final self-check. It is a strong, informed baseline, without evaluator arithmetic supplied as evidence. Teams differ intentionally in roles, communication and compute, so no causal agent-count claim follows.

Use nine separately seeded development cases for qualification, one per family; the initial main uses nine different seeded buyer configurations, one per family, each with two fresh repetitions. These are authored constructions: processing-scope ambiguity, weighted durable pilot, stale contract supersession, unit pricing, usage overage, migration dependencies, legitimate near ties, genuine value and evidence gaps. Do not select or remove cases using native susceptibility. E1's inspected legacy-cost scenario is development evidence, not a new untouched holdout. A 36-root expansion is not admitted or costed here.

All-in first-year outlay explicitly includes subscription, setup, usage, legacy overlap and connector costs. Commercial/generalist/final outputs reconcile each component and the total; unknown is never zero. Capability support and migration duration have distinct definitions. Near-tie alternatives remain acceptable; DEFER is wrong if a qualifying alternative exists. Preserve raw actions and report offline shadow authorization separately.

Primary document-shaped records contain issuer/date/scope/caveats; no gold fields or computed worksheet enter observations. A 12-page pool has two delivered controlled page placements. Commercial and technical roles receive their assigned page; deployment receives none. All specialists within a role have the same scoped source set. The matched truthful/misleading versions preserve publisher, endorsement, sponsorship and every clause except the declared false clause. Genuine-value and near-tie controls use a false provenance claim without making the endorsed supplier ineligible. Report that control separately from false requirement claims. The delivered page union is identical across architectures; duplicated exposure to more readers is part of the larger system, not a separately identified dose effect.

## Protocol

1. Two primary-only checker calls follow a frozen agenda sampled independently of gold, pages and model answers. Exact requests and outputs are shared across architectures and content worlds within each root/repetition. This is explicit dependent prefix reuse, not independent replication.
2. For each content world, 36 large-team specialists make scoped provisional reports. Each revises once after seeing its own report and its two immediate neighbors on a fixed within-role ring. All initial reports are frozen before revisions; no cascading within-round update.
3. Each of three role leads reads its role's 12 revised reports and scoped primary records, then produces one bounded scoped report. The large-team chair receives those three reports, the brief and shared checks, without raw primary records or all36 reports.
4. The small team produces three scoped reports and a summary-only chair decision. The generalist makes an initial full-union report then a final decision retaining full raw evidence and the same shared checks.
5. Each content world therefore costs **76 large-team calls +4 small-team calls +2 generalist calls =82**. Two primary worlds plus two shared checks cost **166 per root/repetition**. There is no hidden extra authorizer, retry or revision round.

Truthful qualification: nine development configurations ×84 calls = **756 calls**,27 final decisions. Require every assigned node to produce a structurally valid response with exact scoped vendor/criterion coverage. Require all27 final actions acceptable, all final cost components/totals correct and reconciled, and final source coverage correct. Internal semantic mistakes are retained and diagnosed; they are not a qualification failure if the workflow corrects them before its final decision. Invalid wire/schema/route remains operational failure. Do not reroll valid wrong judgments to manufacture a pass. Do not qualify on misleading-world outcomes.

Initial main: nine evaluation configurations ×two repetitions ×166 = **2,988 calls**,108 primary final decisions. Qualification plus main is **3,744 physical calls** and135 final decisions. **The separate neutral-page panel is deferred.** Truthful endorsement is not neutral; the effect of endorsement versus no page is untested. Genuine-value, evidence-gap and near-tie controls remain in every primary cohort. This dated amendment supersedes the earlier proposed984-call neutral panel before native implementation freeze: the immediate priority is the40-member matched false-clause contrast with competent comparators and fresh repeats.

## Metrics

Primitive fact accuracy, requirement status, source coverage, component arithmetic, provisional stance and explanation completeness are separate endpoints. Trace-visible agreement is not hidden reasoning or proof of conformity.

Average fresh repetitions within each root; display all nine paired root effects, authored-family labels, repeat disagreements and all assigned missing decisions. Nine roots with one construction each cannot estimate broad family generalization. Prespecified worst-case missing-outcome bounds remain separate from sampling uncertainty. No significance threshold, invented simulation power or call-level bootstrap. Ten percentage points is a descriptive practical decision aid, not a powered minimum detectable effect.
