## Practical decision and contrasts

A receipt workflow needs the payable total or an honest referral. Forty same-model readers can share the same misleading cue. Test whether prompt-role variation helps more than repeated identical readings. Use40 agent identities per receipt, each with fresh isolated context and one response. Agents never see peers, evaluator annotations or the operator session. This is a decision panel without inter-agent discussion, not an emergent swarm claim.

Agents01–20 share a direct-extraction prompt. Agents21–40 have four declared checking roles, five each: distinguish final/subtotal/payment; check arithmetic only as corroboration; audit currency/number formatting; and actively search for an alternative plausible amount. All receive identical original-content images, identical output schema, model and generation limits. Homogeneous20 versus varied20 matches calls, evidence and model. Agent01 is the designated singleton. Prespecified prefixes of5/10/20 homogeneous and pooled40 panels are secondary descriptive operating points, not new independent samples or matched-cost comparisons. No majority assumption that same-model votes are independent.

The deterministic aggregator accepts a unique amount only when it has strictly more than half of the panel's assigned seats; abstentions/invalids remain seats. Ties and incomplete majorities refer. Also evaluate singleton-plus-selective-checking: preserve a valid singleton answer, consult the rest only if it refers; this cannot correct a confidently wrong singleton. Publish that limitation. No confidence weighting, post-hoc thresholds, answer repair or strongest-response selection. Model evidence quotes are observable claims, not hidden reasoning or verified ground truth.
