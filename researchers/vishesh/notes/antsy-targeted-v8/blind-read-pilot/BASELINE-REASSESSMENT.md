## Evidence actually available

The original Haiku panel tested two receipts with40 responses each. The locale diagnostic reused those same two receipts with16 calls; it did not provide new independent cases. Literal Q1 exposed one new receipt before stopping on a noncanonical baseline response. Literal Q2 used that receipt plus three others: four paired receipt units,20calls. ## Remaining scientific gaps

- Cases are real but narrow: Indonesian receipts, a tiny partly reused cohort, little quantified quality/layout diversity. No second-locale corpus is frozen.
- Dataset labels plus spot pixel inspection are useful but do not yet meet the prospective source-only adjudication protocol, particularly for ambiguous totals.
- The baseline's strong normalization failure motivates decomposition, but a competent literal-only comparator already explains all observed gains. The new verifier must beat or safely trade coverage against that comparator, not merely outperform the weak direct baseline.
- Same-model contexts share weights and visual input. Blind reading isolates information access operationally; it does not establish independent errors or alternative architecture/evidence.
- Qualification failures, controls, missingness and costs are recorded reliably; new execution-contract tests, challenge validity and repeated fresh dispatch remain needed.
- Four paired units cannot support a population or stability claim. No title, chart or trace completeness changes that limit.

## Scoped analysis and reproducibility criterion

Primary endpoint is the paired difference in wrong acceptance, blind versus conditioned, on48scorable receipt units, reported for each execution and pooled only with receipt-cluster uncertainty. Show correct service and referral alongside risk; do not reward universal referral. Analyze literal-only and direct agreement as comparators; independently score each reader. Treat policy/repeat outputs as nested, never96 or672independent receipt samples. Cluster by merchant/layout family where present and disclose the actual effective cluster count.

Report all paired counts, per-stratum denominators, per-receipt repeat transitions, decision/token agreement across repeats, and intervals for paired outcome differences. A fresh-run change of sign, materially different harm rate, or persistent execution incompleteness blocks a stability claim. The pilot's advancement heuristic is not a significance threshold. Pre-register a practical margin and required precision after offline corpus feasibility and before R1; do not select a margin from its results. With zero wrong accepts on48independent receipts, the simple one-sided95% upper bound is still about6.1%, before clustering; this can support a bounded mechanism/feasibility claim, not production payment safety. A large uncertainty interval means inconclusive, even if mean direction is attractive.

A scientifically useful negative would demonstrate repeatedly, across fresh challenging cases with competent literal extraction and valid controls, that conditional checking does not improve the prespecified risk/coverage tradeoff. A useful positive would show repeatable paired rescues without unacceptable added wrong accepts, with uncertainty and coverage disclosed. Either requires the new evidence; Q2 alone supplies neither. Keep Antsy under consideration pending case-quality and instrument work, without presuming it deserves a top-four slot.
