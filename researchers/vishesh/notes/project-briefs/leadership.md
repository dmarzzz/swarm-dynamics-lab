# leadership

Status: HUNCH, not a hypothesis; no survey has passed the gate. Author's own triage, not a team ranking: Lift Medium · Difficulty Hard · Novelty Focused extension · Event fit Strong · ~14–24 builder-hours (estimate).

## Background

Talkativeness, centrality and influence are different. A coordinator may send many messages without changing the outcome; a quiet agent may supply the one decisive observation.

## Closest prior work

- **Embodied LLM Agents Learn to Cooperate in Organized Teams · 2024** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12482  
  Tests organization structures and designated leadership in embodied tasks. It provides an intervention-based precedent for comparing teams.
- **Anthropic’s multi-agent research system · 2025** — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system  
  Describes an orchestrator-worker production design. Task decomposition and delegation are concrete mechanisms to inspect.

## Where it applies

Identify bottlenecks, replace fragile coordinators and understand whose evidence actually changes a shared decision.

## The angle

Compare a communication graph with matched runs in which one message is withheld. Use interventions on synthetic tasks before interpreting real trace centrality.

## What to watch

Withholding a message changes downstream histories. Use repeated matched runs and describe the intervention precisely.

## Research question

Whose suggestions become others’ actions, and which apparent leaders mostly narrate?

## Dossier design sketch

Carried over from the [interactive dossier](../swarm-ecology-dossier.html#leadership). This is an unexecuted hunch, not an approved experiment. Counts and treatments below are planning choices; the lab’s survey and hypothesis review gates still apply.

1. Extract proposals and assignments in a bounded collaborative goal.
2. Link later acceptance, rejection and verified work to each proposal.
3. Compare proposal adoption with message count and formal or self-declared roles.

## Measures

- Verified adoption per exposed proposal
- Task completion following assignment
- Concentration of influence
- Rejection and correction rates

## Controls

- Normalize for activity and room membership
- Distinguish accepted authority from successful execution
- Review action links manually
- Avoid stable personality conclusions from one task

## Minimum useful output

One goal, a proposal-to-action timeline and a ranking with denominators.

## Optional extension

Compare leadership changes across tasks or agent arrivals.

## Interpretation risk

Claims of completion are not completion. Causal influence remains uncertain without controlled exposure.

## Demo narrative

Contrast the most talkative agent with the agent whose proposals actually changed recorded work.

## Review update October 3

Message volume and centrality are descriptive. A causal leadership claim requires a controlled intervention or must remain an observational association.

**Decision to resolve before promotion:** Does removing or delaying a message change outcomes beyond what its information content predicts?

If the simple baseline explains the result, or the necessary evidence cannot be obtained, narrow this to a replication or park the hunch. A toy animation is not evidence that the proposed intervention works.
