# What went wrong in Antsy v3, and what changes now

The earlier384 blocks were repeated views of12 engineered tasks, not384 independent real problems. Adaptive produced96 ineligible choices despite zero execution errors. The source/decision records show a specific cause: false supplied facts survived into the latest-visible table, two apparent roots corroborated them, and the policy relaxed its admission threshold. The deterministic guard correctly checked the observed table; that table was wrong. Laya was neither the unique cause nor a corrective mechanism: every policy choice matched symbolic execution.

There are two distinct failures. **Evidence failure:** agreeing roots are false, so clean constraint logic cannot establish truth. **Stopping failure:** the policy either commits before corrective information or waits long enough to adopt later misinformation. In clean stalled cases the fixed-three rule abstains despite an adequate correct two-source decision, exposing a deliberately overconservative baseline. These fixtures illustrate the definitions more than discover how practical agents perform.

V4 therefore removes hand-authored source truth, fixed target labels, predetermined corrective arrivals and latest-report overwrite as its main source of difficulty. Externally collected receipt images and reference annotations determine actual OCR errors. Three executable configurations produce real outputs, with quality and latency measured before policy comparison. Actors may inspect imperfect confidence signals or spend limited QA credits to learn whether a sampled region is actually correct. The reference-check tool models a human QA service; it is an explicit idealization, not a free production oracle.

A bad decision in V4 will be diagnosed as (a) confident but inaccurate OCR, (b) a check on an uninformative region, (c) failure to check a consequential alternative, (d) overgeneralization from a checked region, (e) premature stopping, or (f) actor/execution failure. Use counterfactual measured outputs to quantify recoverable regret and replay the actual observation history. Do not call every non-oracle choice irrational: a policy cannot know hidden quality for free.

The necessity test is now falsifiable. If the best fixed configuration is effectively as good as a per-receipt oracle on calibration data, there is little routing value to recover and no justification for an expensive swarm. If routing value exists but simple confidence or deterministic QA selection captures it, the model or swarm is unnecessary. Those negative results must survive the evaluation.
