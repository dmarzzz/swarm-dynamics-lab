# Scenario grounding

Opened 2026-10-04 UTC; this is a focused design check, not a completed prior-art survey.

- [CORD primary repository](https://github.com/clovaai/cord), README and dataset specification read. Authors provide receipt images, OCR annotations and parsing labels; the released sample has 1,000 Indonesian receipts. CC-BY-4.0. [Official v2 dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2) and API revision/license inspected. Use only validation 100; test stays unopened. Dataset attribution: CORD: A Consolidated Receipt Dataset for Post-OCR Parsing, released by the CORD authors/NAVER CLOVA. The data are external to this experiment; configurations' winners are measured, not authored by us.
- [Tesseract command-line documentation](https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/tessdoc/Command-Line-Usage.html), language selection, TSV and segmentation sections read. [Quality documentation](https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/tessdoc/ImproveQuality.html), segmentation/input-quality discussion read. These motivate comparing segmentation configurations on unchanged receipts. No claim that these modes represent different commercial APIs or independent OCR model families.
- [AWS Textract quotas](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/textract/latest/dg/limits-document.html) and [asynchronous processing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/textract/latest/dg/async.html) opened for scenario framing: input format, size and operation mode are real routing constraints. We do not call AWS or transfer its limits/performance to our local configurations. We narrow this experiment to measured extraction quality and QA budget rather than inventing provider privacy policies.

The biological connection remains active information gathering before commitment. We do not claim receipt QA implements an ant colony's recruitment mechanism. The scientific need is empirical: determine whether imperfect, inexpensive quality signals plus a few targeted reference checks can approach an expensive per-receipt oracle, and whether a multi-agent coordinator improves that allocation over a single agent or rule.

## Biological precedent and a warning against constructed tradeoffs

Fresh abstract/selected-methods reading of [Pratt and Sumpter, 2006](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1635101/) complements the existing [[pratt-2006-tunable]] catalogue entry. Their ant study compares forced versus unforced emigration, with search and acceptance behavior as well as quorum-based commitment. Antsy inherits a question about when additional assessment is useful; five prompts are not five biological scouts, and v4 has no urgency manipulation.

Fresh selected-methods/figure reading of [Marshall, Dornhaus, Franks and Kovacs, *Noise, cost and speed-accuracy trade-offs*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1578745/) is particularly relevant to this redesign. In their analysis, a parameter change removed an apparent speed–accuracy tradeoff in an earlier model; explicit assessment noise and cost were essential to understanding the problem. Design implication for Antsy: demonstrate task headroom and report costs separately, rather than treating a tunable threshold as proof of a necessary tradeoff. Our perfect regional QA and unmeasured human-review cost remain limitations. Neither reading is claimed as a fresh full-paper replication or evidence that an LLM committee will help OCR.
