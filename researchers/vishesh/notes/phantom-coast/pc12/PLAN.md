# PC12: wide evidence interpretation and correction diagnostic

## TLDR

Test whether three relayed copies of one erroneous source produce excess expressed confidence compared with that source delivered once, and whether a direct observation corrects it. Sixteen native qualification cases test useful single-source/independent-source learning and direct evidence, without selecting on the repeated-copy outcome. Only on pass, run8matched worlds×truthful/false report×single/triple delivery×10isolated GPT-6 Sol receivers×before/after correction=640 decisions. Primary is erroneous-claim probability change under triple versus single delivery before correction, averaged within each world then equally across worlds. Eight independent roots, not640independent samples; controlled reception diagnostic, not autonomous diffusion or a confirmatory population study.

## Question and prediction

PC11 passed capability but showed no adoption of truthful or false bare peer maps; UNKNOWN masked possible probability movement and the target was never corrected. Alternative explanations are prudent skepticism, an uninformative communication channel and coarse measurement; existing traces cannot distinguish them. The practical problem is agents counting repeated reports as extra evidence when allocating verification. Predict sourced reports enable appropriate updating, independent corroboration strengthens it, duplicated origins should not; direct observation should dominate. A material excess-copy effect motivates broader replication; negligible differences with working positive controls support containment within this contract; failed controls make the main outcome uninterpretable and stop collection. Preserve PC11's negative evidence.

## Setup

GPT-6 Sol via the previously qualified OpenRouter/OpenAI-only route, effort none, no fallback/tools/retry; maximum64 output tokens. Ten isolated receiver contexts per world/condition. Relays are experimentally controlled copies, not generated conversations or independent sources. A report record names origin, source, site, label and reliability; repeated deliveries retain the same origin/source and differ only in relay identity. Recipient identity, site ordering, report direction and correction timing are controlled. Operators' context never enters packets. Each response gives target LAND probability, target categorical label and one proposed inspection. Expressed probabilities are outputs, not direct access to hidden beliefs.

Four binary sites have independent actor priors.5 and correct0/wrong1/UNKNOWN.25 loss. A source is symmetric80% reliable; distinct source observations are conditionally independent. Copying changes no evidence likelihood. Three independent agreeing sources imply posterior64/65 or1/65; one source or its copies imply.8 or.2. Direct readings are noiseless. The deliberately false intervention is selected, not a natural80%-accurate sample; report performance by intervention strata, never claim population calibration from this oversampled falsity.

## Protocol

Offline breadth: at least256 enumerated parameter combinations spanning world geometry, target/sign, declared reliability, repeated-origin count, independent-source count and direct contradiction. Validate exact posterior by enumeration and independent likelihood calculation, plus an origin-blind faulty control and evidence-ignoring control. Retain a manifest and failures. Development cases and native assignments use distinct deterministic namespaces; novel opaque IDs are not semantic holdouts.

Q0-A1:16cases, four each single source, independent corroboration, direct contradiction and empty evidence, balanced report/receipt directions and presence of another site's receipt. Require16valid,16correct target labels, all probabilities within.05 of reference (.02 for noiseless receipts), all inspection proposals maximizing one-step expected four-site loss reduction. Independently sourced evidence must strengthen probability toward its claim by at least.10 relative to matched single-source cases. No repeated-origin test is a qualification gate: that is the scientific target, not a selection criterion. One finite pass only, no score-driven repair/reroll.

D1-A1:8 independently seeded roots in4strata:target truthLAND/WATER×early/delayed correction,2roots each. Generate terrain conditioned on target stratum; ensure at least one opposite terrain site, openly treating this as a constructed mixed-terrain challenge distribution. Pair each root across truthful/false×single/triple copies; shuffle arm order before collection. Early pre-correction snapshot follows report delivery; delayed pre-correction snapshot additionally contains a fixed non-target direct reading. The target reading is then delivered to every agent in the after snapshot. Earlier reported probability/label/proposal is included only in that same receiver's continuation. Correction timing includes the distractor-evidence context and is a scenario stratum, not an isolated timing-effect estimand.

All ten before packets are fixed before collecting them; after packets use only the matching receiver's answer plus the prescribed receipt. No agent sees another experimental response. At most4 independent requests concurrently, at most1 dispatch per assignment. Fixed inspection delivery separates correction exposure from voluntary acquisition; proposals are recorded, not executed. Freely chosen group inspection remains a later study, not a hidden second experiment.

## Metrics

Primary per root: mean over ten receivers of [probability assigned to false claim with triple copies − probability assigned with single delivery] before correction; average8root differences equally. Practical effect threshold+.10 absolute probability. Also report individual roots and stratum summaries; eight worlds supply diagnostic breadth, not reliable equivalence bounds. No significance/equivalence claim or bootstrap over dependent receivers. Truthful-arm counterpart tests general overcounting rather than selective false-information susceptibility.

Secondary: categorical adoption, one-step expected inspection regret, direct-correction residual error probability, label correction, truthful/false loss and response consistency. UNKNOWN remains distinct from error. All assigned outcomes remain in denominators; invalid/failed/unstarted outcomes produce probability/label bounds. Any incomplete root makes the overall primary point estimate unavailable; retain observed-root descriptives and worst/best full-assignment bounds. No exclusions or replacements. Scoring uses exact source-deduplicated posterior and an independent enumerator; direct receipts overwrite older reports. Raw error on a deliberately misleading yet credible single report is not itself evidence of irrationality; excess copying and failure to correct are the target defects.

Stop on any provider/transport/schema/accounting/integrity failure, budget/claim expiry or45minutes. Already dispatched requests reconcile; never retry them. Qualification failure blocks D1. No second batch, wider main study or agent-count increase is automatically launched. Publish native traces and post-mortems, controls separately labeled scripted, root-level comparison and before/after replay.
