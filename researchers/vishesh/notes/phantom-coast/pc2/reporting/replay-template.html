How to read these results

Misleading reports label four actual land cells WATER; benign reports label those same cells LAND. All cells remain legal targets. Repeated inspections spend a slot. With the audit enabled, a prespecified reported cell replaces the selected target at slot six. A team needs two valid proposals to choose a target.

The final endpoint's error range includes unresolved labels: lower = known wrong / 36; upper = (known wrong + unresolved) / 36. These are bounds on missing outcomes, not confidence intervals. Eight synthetic roots from two structural families, one model snapshot, and unequal inference compute limit broader claims. Calls and episodes are not independent sample units.
