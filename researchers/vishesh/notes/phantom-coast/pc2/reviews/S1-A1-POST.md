## Limits and next design

Eight generated roots from two structural families and one model snapshot do not establish population-level reliability. Agents, slots, cell labels and policy episodes are dependent. Inference compute differs across policies. Clean map/unique-target qualification did not establish effective twelve-slot planning. The primary true-error interaction remains weakly identified because unresolved labels dominate; upper error is a conservative criterion, not observed accuracy.

The valid adverse result completes this cohort. Future work should test a coverage guard as a separate treatment on fresh tasks, prospectively set wrong/unknown utility, and strengthen strategic positive controls without qualifying on obtaining the desired scientific effect. Profile and replace costly full-file persistence only in a versioned successor, retaining crash-safe reservations and trace integrity. Add a bounded validation-code vocabulary for diagnoses. No new run, reserved confirmation root, or spending envelope is authorized by this recommendation alone; use a new prospective assessment under the standing budget policy before any continuation.
