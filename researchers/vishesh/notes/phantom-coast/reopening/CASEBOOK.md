# Development cases: what would make this an informative test?

All six mechanism roots and30 dependent variants are authored, inspected **development data**. None is an independent native observation or untouched test set. This is the concrete first step under the upgraded case-design standard; population dynamics are proposed, not implemented. [Prospective direction](PLAN.md); [machine-readable cases](development-cases.json); [offline validation](validation.json).

| Root | Concrete case | Necessary distinction | Failure of the test itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legitimate correction | An old report calls A water; a current direct survey finds land. B/C/D have correct surveys. | Update A without deleting correct knowledge elsewhere. | Rewarding blanket distrust or assuming retraction equals new evidence. |
| Copied rumor | A direct survey finds land; three circulating reports call it water but share one originating observation. | Peer repetition is not independent evidence; a supported belief should survive unsupported challenge. | Counting copies as witnesses or exposing the evaluator's planted-source tag. |
| Stale authentic record | An authenticated pre-episode survey says water; current survey says land. | Identity authenticity and present truth are distinct. | Treating a late receipt as a fresh observation. |
| Missed discovery | Unvisited A really is land; an unverified report says water. A remains legal to inspect. | Does that report change acquisition before a corrective survey exists? | Hard-coding avoidance, grading unsupported UNKNOWN as wrong, or showing hidden truth to the actor. |
| Wasted exploration | Unvisited A is water; a report advertises land. | Opposite direction of harm: false positives may consume scarce inspection capacity. | Selecting only false-negative cases that favor the story. |
| Insufficient evidence | Two unsupported reports disagree; no current direct survey exists. | Warranted uncertainty differs from failure to understand available evidence. | Pretending evaluator knowledge was accessible to the actor. |

Every root has five linked variants: original; current verified resolution; opposite truth with correspondingly changed current verified evidence; record-order reversal; removal of current target evidence. Original/reordered must agree. Resolved/opposite must change the warranted target answer. Removing current evidence must leave UNKNOWN. Non-target authenticated facts remain recoverable. Source IDs, exact observations and cutoff are explicit; LAND/WATER avoids PC8's ambiguous booleans.

The code validates actor/evaluator separation, hashes, available actions, current observation consistency, these matched-pair properties and four deliberately wrong policies. The arithmetic count of checks is not an experiment-quality score. This small evidence-label reference does not optimize acquisition, decide probabilistic guessing loss, implement independent-agent communication or demonstrate poisoning. In particular, copied endorsements do not become model-produced merely because a fixture includes them.

For the eventual native comparison, add heterogeneous world geometries, meaningful scarce sensing choices and actual return values; freeze the objective and strongest exact/simple policy first. A four-location packet is an interface/correction fixture, not an adequate full exploration environment. Do not call renamed sites or30 variants30 independent cases. New held-out worlds should split complete observation/source families; do not reuse these as untouched qualification.

The most informative replay would put world truth next to each agent's expressed map, then show the original report, native endorsements, selected inspections and actual correction times. A line should mean a delivered message with retained ancestry, not an inferred mental influence. Display missing and UNKNOWN separately; scripted fixtures must say so.

Current outcome: offline cases validated, zero paid calls. HOLD native collection while finishing a decision-relevant acquisition contract and separate held-out construction. This is progress beyond “better cases needed,” not a claim the launch plan is ready.
