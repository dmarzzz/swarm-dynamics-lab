## Evaluation against PC-1

All 540 planned native requests completed validly within the cap. Qualification passed. All 186 final pilot maps were correct, every history contrast was zero, and the pooled and deterministic baselines reached the same endpoint. No repair rerun is justified. The data support the practical choice of a simple reconstruction baseline for complete reliable evidence; they cannot establish a self-maintaining exploration trap, a discussion benefit or general resistance to misinformation.

The critical limitation is causal scope: the last event combines 36 reliable observations with explicit withdrawal. PC-1 cannot identify which component caused recovery. It also supplies evidence regardless of decisions. The successor therefore asks a different, explicit question: what evidence is acquired with twelve inspections and no automatic full survey? It does not retroactively reinterpret the previous null as an adaptive success.

## Next launch boundary

PC-2 is **not native-ready**: source review, applicable research-gate disposition, action adapter/launcher, native qualification and fresh fleet/registration checks remain necessary. The shared runbook says “A blank, unknown, stale or failed required check blocks the corresponding transition.” We completed the authorized development work; this rule prevents treating the offline package as permission to run a changed mechanism. The prior $5 total is not replenished, and its already-consumed 540-request envelope cannot be reset; successor admission must bind remaining dollars and a new request allocation explicitly. No new spending approval is being requested merely to publish this iteration.

Practical decision: retain the valid PC-1 result and use PC-2 to test whether reserving one report-verification slot is worth the observation it displaces. Proceed only through the recorded review and qualification gates, and accept a null or a simpler-policy win.
