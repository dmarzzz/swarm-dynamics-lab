# Proposed next diagnostic: risk and utility as explicit inputs

Written after PC-5; this is a proposal, not a preregistration or launch authority. PC-4 and PC-5 are complete, and no additional worker or paid cohort is scheduled.

PC-5 improved the average but regressed from 28/32 to 21/32 optimal choices with reliable reports. The explicit arm still missed 8/32 unreliable reports. A plausible cause is incomplete use of numerical risk/utility, but the present study cannot distinguish that from attention, wording or hosted response variability. Do not claim a verified cognitive mechanism.

The next focused question should compare the PC-5 prose contract with an equivalent structured table of action consequences, holding the objective and information fixed. Cross error probabilities 0.05, 0.20, 0.50 and 0.80 with UNKNOWN costs 0.10, 0.25 and 0.75. These twelve cases avoid ties and require the optimal action to change with both inputs; always-check and always-explore baselines must fail somewhere. Keep the analytic minimum-loss controller as an explicitly programmed zero-regret reference. No oracle truth enters native input.

A concrete candidate is 32 fresh layout roots x 12 cases x 2 representations = 768 native choices, plus a separately scoped interface screen. Freeze exact new seeds, call count, primary paired regret contrast, per-stratum acceptance and precision rationale in a new public plan before implementation. Require no reliable-source regression beyond a predeclared tolerance before advancing to multi-step sensing; a favorable overall average alone is insufficient. Preserve all missing choices and original cumulative funds. At most 792 one-question calls would reserve USD 1.064448 at the existing route ceiling, within the current USD 4 API cap, but no allowance is reset and current prices/admission must be checked again.

Only after evidence supports both risk regimes should a separate closed-loop experiment combine native planning with calibrated versus miscalibrated source estimates, sensor noise and useful revisits. Compare uniform, analytic expected-value scheduling and native choices at equal sensing and native-call budgets. That test must distinguish a wrapper's enforced behavior from learned competence. The present success does not qualify such a controller automatically.
