Legacy task mirrors the former mapping objective: choose one cell to minimize final whole-map error; reports may be wrong and direct measurements are noiseless. Both arms explicitly state that report reliability is its calibrated probability of correctness. Explicit arm additionally states the actual downstream rule: retain any uninspected report label, return UNKNOWN for the uninspected unknown cell, current measurement resolves the chosen cell; wrong costs 1, UNKNOWN 0.25, correct 0; minimize expected total loss. These are two versions of information supplied to a native single controller, not different scoring rules. No peer actors, memory, delegation or repeated sampling in S1.
