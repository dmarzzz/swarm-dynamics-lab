## PI critique and verified design defects

The frozen parser searches the entire report for `unverified` before recognizing verified evidence. Thus the preliminary-impression clause makes all verified prose records look like challenges. Its observed failures are EXCLUDE, not the intended ambiguous-polarity UNKNOWN behavior described in the pre-run clarification. This is a verified comparator implementation defect, not evidence that arbitrary semantic normalization requires a model. Offline checks covered development wording but did not exercise the newly introduced preliminary/final authority pattern. The challenge expansion outran validation.

Structured booleans `outcome` and `preliminary_unverified_outcome` also lacked an explicit true/false semantic legend. Prose spells out agreement/disagreement and final authority. Although information parity was intended, equality of explicit instruction is not established. This is a design gap and possible contributor to native errors, not a proven causal diagnosis. The model also retained stale records despite explicit cutoff rules; a legend alone has not been shown to repair qualification. Do not characterize this result as intrinsic model inability or a pure prose-versus-JSON effect.

A prospective saved-data repair note preceded a separate reporting-only parser candidate. It removes the explicitly delimited preliminary clause before applying the original parser. On the same Q0 packets this gives96/96 correct, zero protected errors and zero first-action regret; eight development checks and eight preliminary-clause perturbations also pass. This was tuned after seeing outcomes. It demonstrates a removable baseline defect on authored grammar, not independent holdout performance. The native source and original scores are preserved, and S1 remains unopened.

The empirical question was better targeted than earlier supplied-minimum tasks, but the small authored grammar and weak comparator cannot justify model deployment. Four mechanism families and only two reserved phrasing styles are insufficient application realism. A superficially compelling prose accuracy gain would have overstated practical value; downstream regret exposes its small size.
