# Phantom Coast PC-3: Can a coverage guard rescue exploration?

Prospective exploratory design, 2026-10-04 UTC, vishesh/codex-phantom-coast. Written before PC-3 implementation. Parent: [PC-2 post-mortem](../pc2/reviews/S1-A1-POST.md). No formal accepted hypothesis or independent review claimed. Owner requested this iteration and waived researcher review; direct launch and temporary OpenRouter transfer remain authorized within the existing budget.

## TLDR

Does prohibiting repeat inspections improve mapping under misleading reports? Compare a three-proposal team with and without a coverage guard, and with single-controller and uniform sampling baselines, each under misleading and benign reports. The guard removes already measured cells from legal actions; it is an explicit system intervention, not learned competence. Primary outcome is paired final decision loss (wrong = 1, UNKNOWN = 0.25) on eight fresh worlds. Report coverage, wrong and unknown separately and compare guarded team with uniform to isolate any value beyond mechanical coverage. This is a small synthetic, single-model exploratory pilot with unequal inference compute.

## Question and prediction

PC-2 correctly mapped all inspected cells but misleading teams inspected only 2.5 distinct cells in twelve slots. The source audit did not meet its practical threshold. We predict the guard reduces misleading-team decision loss by at least 2/36, while making no directional claim that guarded teams beat uniform. A favorable guard result supports a cheap system constraint, not better reasoning or collective intelligence. A null/adverse result is retained. No rerun solely to obtain a preferred result.

## Setup

Six-by-six maps have twelve LAND and twenty-four WATER cells. Four true LAND cells form either a contiguous 2x2 block or a mutually nonadjacent scattered set. Eight other LAND cells are sampled elsewhere. The four initial reports say WATER (misleading) or LAND (benign), reliability 0.8, epoch 0. Noiseless inspections have epoch 2. Agents see coordinates, evidence ledger and previous-round proposals, never truth, future events, world seeds or operator conversation. No whole-map rescue or report retraction.

Keep PC-2 generator and role semantics: team has three fresh native proposals per sensing slot, at least two valid proposals required, plurality with seeded tie-breaking; single uses one; uniform samples a seeded permutation without replacement. Twelve slots in every episode. Guard off allows repeats, spending a slot. Guard on restricts offered native choice criteria to cells with no direct sensor observation and rejects illegal proposals. No repair call, forced oracle action or deterministic fallback. Invalid quorum produces a missing sensing slot. All three actors in a round receive frozen pre-round state; peer proposals arrive only next round. Report and sensor evidence remains the same format in both arms. Uniform has identical acquisitions in guard on/off; separate endpoint draws provide a negative control for endpoint variability, not independent samples.

Final team/uniform maps use three native maps and strict two-of-three known-label consensus; single uses one map. A fresh yoked single judge receives every team endpoint ledger without peer proposals. Map questions cover all 36 cells even when inspection choices are restricted. UNKNOWN remains legal. Snapshot typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917 through TypeSafe, alias typesafe/jev-1.13; no provider fallback. Native decisions endpoint, maximum context 32,000 tokens per question, input price ceiling USD 0.000000042/token, no output/request charge. Live route and returned model checked.

## Metrics

Primary: equal-root mean of misleading-team guarded minus unrestricted final loss, L=(wrong + 0.25*UNKNOWN_or_missing)/36. Negative favors guard. Practical success requires mean reduction >= 2/36 (5.56 percentage points). The 0.25 cost is an explicit abstention utility assumption, not a fact about all applications. Keep assigned denominators including failed/missing outputs; no exclusions. Report per-root values, range, paired standard error and descriptive t interval (7 df), with no population-reliability claim or multiplicity-adjusted significance assertion.

Secondary, separately labeled: guarded team minus guarded uniform loss; guarded team minus guarded single; guard x report interaction; unique inspected cells/12, repeats, failed slots, reported cells visited/4, first reported-cell visit (censor at 12); known-wrong and UNKNOWN rates separately; lower wrong/36 and upper (wrong+UNKNOWN)/36 error bounds; same primary contrast at UNKNOWN costs 0, 0.5 and 1; geometry-specific contrasts and leave-one-root sensitivity (never exclude from primary); yoked endpoint and deterministic-evidence reconstruction; calls, tokens, known/uncertain cost and actual stage versus call time. Primary utility missing-as-abstention is operational, not identified true error. Also report true-error difference bounds [guard lower - off upper, guard upper - off lower], not a confidence interval.

Guard manipulation passes when every valid guarded inspection is distinct and all choice criteria match the unmeasured set. Uniform acquisition paths match across guard/report conditions. Reject causal claims if these checks fail. All measured cells must remain correct in deterministic evidence controls; native violations are outcomes, not silently repaired. Offline fixtures test deliberately repetitive policies, useful uniform/coverage policies, missing quorum, response contract, no truth leakage, round barriers, source/qualification/budget/admission faults and journal crash semantics. Same-author audits are labeled operator audits.

## Decision and limitations

This iteration tests a practical constraint following a valid adverse PC-2 result. Its guard mechanically improves coverage when quorum succeeds; that alone is not evidence of intelligence. Uniform is the essential comparator. Single snapshot, synthetic stationary terrain, shared evidence, finite geometry reuse, eight roots, unequal compute and chosen abstention utility limit transfer. Prior-art/formal hypothesis work is incomplete; this remains exploratory engineering evidence. Close all attempts, preserve uncertainty and release only this study's allocation after artifact verification.
