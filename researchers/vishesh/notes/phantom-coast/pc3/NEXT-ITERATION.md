# Proposed successor: allocate inspections to verifying inherited claims

2026-10-04 UTC, written after PC-3. This is a post-mortem design proposal, not a preregistration, launch receipt or new spending grant. PC-3 is complete; no further model calls are scheduled.

The guard eliminated repeat inspection but misleading teams visited only 1.5/4 reported cells, versus 3/4 without the guard. Known errors increased 8→20/288 while unknowns decreased 246→172. Guarded team loss 21.88% exceeded uniform 20.05%. All inspected cells remained correct, so the next mechanism is inspection allocation and treatment of unverified claims, not sensor grounding.

Proposed question: can reserving a small part of twelve nonrepeating inspections for source verification reduce wrong answers without sacrificing too much useful coverage? Compare fixed quotas 0, 1, 2, 4 reported cells, with remaining slots assigned uniformly to unmeasured cells, against uniform-without-replacement, native guarded single and native guarded team. Quotas are explicit controller policies; their benefit must not be attributed to learned reasoning. Include benign and misleading reports, and partially wrong sources, to expose the cost of checking already-correct reports. Use paired fresh roots and frozen source/geometry distributions; never reuse PC-3 roots as new samples or open the sealed confirmation cohort by default.

Before implementing, choose a fixed primary quota (the others are secondary), sample count and independent-root precision target. Keep wrong and unknown separate, predeclare the abstention cost and sensitivity curve, and require useful improvement over uniform rather than coverage alone. Equalize endpoint context by removing action-only fields from all map inputs; retain a same-evidence deterministic/yoked comparison. Report both total deployment cost and sensing-slot matching, with no claim that a three-proposal team is compute-matched to single/uniform.

Qualification should test evidence interpretation, conflicting old reports versus direct measurements, and legal scheduling on fresh worlds. Do not require the eventual primary treatment to win as a competence gate. Add separate safe validation codes for probability normalization, selected-maximum consistency and confidence; keep unknown charges conservative. Use the journal and shared-palette renderer that passed this run. Randomized criterion order would need explicit seed streams and a separate order-invariance check; the post-hoc PC-3 first-option diagnostic found no guarded first-option choices, so order bias is not established as the cause.

Freeze/publish a new readable plan before implementation and register its immutable page before any run. Carry the closed PC-3 ledger and USD 0.574334208 cumulative exposure (nine unresolved charges) into one non-overlapping envelope under the original USD 5 total/USD 4 API cap. Fresh exclusive approved Mars allocation, source/runtime admission and temporary credential cleanup remain required; researcher review and a launch-request ticket remain unnecessary by owner direction. No existing budget or request envelope resets.
