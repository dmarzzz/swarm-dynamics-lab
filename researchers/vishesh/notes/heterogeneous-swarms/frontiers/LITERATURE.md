# Prior-art boundaries and biological crosswalk

**Priority update:** [owner feedback](PRIORITIES.md) narrows the active project to HX-31, consolidates HX-37 and uses HX-33 as a supporting study. The source findings below remain relevant; the original symbiosis/speciation suggestions are not active priorities. This update adds no fresh literature findings.

This is screening and synthesis, not a systematic survey. Most new papers were read at abstract depth, with selected full-text passages for GlossoGen, Lai and Kelley. See `sources.json` for per-source access. “Known” below means reported in the cited setting, not universally replicated.

## A particularly close predecessor for ecological inheritance

[Zheng et al.'s author-uploaded June 2026 preprint](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405920320_What_Should_Agent_Evaluation_Measure_When_Agents_Change_Their_Environments_A_Survey_of_Agent-Environment_Co-Evolution), *What Should Agent Evaluation Measure When Agents Change Their Environments?*, explicitly describes artifact ecology and compares reset, persistent, governed-persistent and held-out inheritance protocols. The abstract and selected protocol passages were read. This makes “the environment is inherited memory” an insufficient novelty claim for HX-33.

The candidate delta is **reciprocal model-family × habitat effects that feed back into population composition**, rather than persistence alone. This source is retained in the supplemental ledger with its ResearchGate DOI, author metadata and access limits; its bibliographic identifier has not been independently verified. The OpenReview PDF did not render. Do not mistake a preprint upload or conference-format document for acceptance.

## Biological sources and unresolved leads

Black Queen reasoning gives a testable cost–benefit mechanism, rather than an instruction to make agents artificially helpless. The mutable unit would be a tool policy or skill bundle. Foundation-model weights remain fixed. Selection, copying and reproduction are controlled finite operations of a future evaluation harness.

For food webs, [Rosenzweig et al. (1994), DOI 10.1093/genetics/137.4.903](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7982572/) is a close biological lead: its indexed primary abstract describes reconstructed coexistence through differential metabolite use. The direct PubMed page did not expose the abstract, PMC showed a browser challenge, and Europe PMC did not load. It is **not** counted as an opened/read library record. HX-37's formal literature review must obtain it and follow cross-feeding citation trails. The Morris paper is adjacent public-good biology, not a substitute demonstration of metabolite cross-feeding.

[Symbulation](https://github.com/anyaevostinar/SymbulationEmp) and its [documentation](https://symbulation.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) were read at README depth, not run. They establish an available artificial-life framework for symbiosis; they do not validate the proposed LLM transfer. The older *Endosymbiosis or Bust* paper (Johnson, Welch, Dolson and Vostinar, 2022; preprint DOI 10.32942/osf.io/yzce3) is an unresolved methods lead after direct access failures. Kelley supplies a freshly accessible and even closer digital-life predecessor.

Information differs from biological nutrients: an artifact can be copied without consumption. “Cross-feeding” therefore requires measured producer/transformer dependence and scarce compute/context budgets; diagrams of arrows between agents are insufficient. Likewise, “speciation” here means operational incompatibility of inherited procedures, not biological reproductive isolation or a claim that models are alive.

## What would make this work unnecessary

Find direct earlier experiments on capability deletion with helper-removal rescue; co-adapted heterogeneous agent interfaces with partner swaps; habitat-by-model reciprocal transplants with recruitment feedback; translated procedural hybrid breakdown; or failed-artifact consumers with resource-withdrawal tests. Full-methods and forward-citation searches targeting those interventions are still outstanding. A match should downgrade the relevant question to replication or a narrower extension.
