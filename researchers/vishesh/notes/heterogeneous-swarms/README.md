## What the research changed

A direct September 30 Jev missing-answer study caused HX-04 to fall from the initial shortlist; Boolean verification and development-tuned thresholds are now required baselines. Fast/slow control already has precedent and a memory-confound counterexample, narrowing HX-01 and lowering its novelty assessment. Generic mixed-model debate, routing, private judgments and capability markets are retained as controls or replications.

The five retained opportunities are conditional mechanism tests, not claims that nobody has studied them.
