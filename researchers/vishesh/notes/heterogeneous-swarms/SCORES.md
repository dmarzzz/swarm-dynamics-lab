## Sensitivity

A five-point change in practical judgment changes the total by 1.5 points. Therefore the order of the top three is not robust to modest editorial disagreement. Holding the rubric fixed, we lowered HX-01’s novelty from 85 to 70 after finding close control work, HX-04’s from 86 to 45 after direct Jev rejection evidence, HX-30’s from 74 to 45 because it overlaps that extension, and HX-24’s from 60 to 40 after a direct market-for-lemons predecessor. These revisions express reduced distinctiveness of the scoped contribution; literature novelty remains a separate, unresolved assessment.

For implementation simplicity, HX-04 can still be a useful early qualification test even though it is not a leading new research direction. Operational usefulness and research novelty should not be confused.
