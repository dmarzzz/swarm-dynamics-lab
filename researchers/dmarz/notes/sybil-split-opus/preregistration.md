# Pre-registration: sybil-split-opus v1

2026-10-04, dmarz/pipeline-split. Frozen before any stage of this study has run and before any model call. The comparison roots (ring 8233-8256, community 8351-8374) were not simulated for any design decision; the fixed attacker resources were set on the engineering roots only, as recorded in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md). [README.md](README.md) describes the setup in prose and [design.yaml](design.yaml) is the frozen machine-readable design. This is an exploratory study in researcher notes, not an accepted hypothesis; no novelty or superiority claim; S2 is disabled.

Authority and review status (2026-10-04, as relayed to dmarz/pipeline by dmarz/fleet-monitor, the session dmarz is directing): Opus for every paid stage; cost is not a gate, but every run has hard `max_calls` and reports actual calls, tokens and dollars to the hub; cross-researcher review is waived by dmarz for these exploratory runs; dmarz/fleet-monitor does a same-researcher check before the request is queued. The run is not independently reviewed and no document may say it was.

1. **Question.** Does splitting one attacker's unchanged resources across 1, 3, 9 or 27 identities increase harmful influence on a synthesized answer, and which admission policy preserves legitimate specialist value?

2. **Independent unit.** A root: one honest graph and task. 24 roots per graph family for S1 (ring 8233-8256, community 8351-8374). Every root is paired across identity count, policy, check budget and check strength within its family. Skills, identities, rows and calls inside a root are dependent. The two families use disjoint root ranges and are never pooled as if they were one sample; estimates average the two family means with equal weight.

3. **Honest world.** 108 identities (54 core, two outside groups of 27 specialists), two trusted core anchors, 54 seats, personalized-PageRank admission (restart 0.2, 120 iterations), honest check pass 0.9. Family `ring` is the sybil-scale-api generator with every identity honest. Family `community` is a separately implemented degree-matched planted partition (random regular blocks from degree-preserving edge switching, core degree 7, outside degree 4, 6 bridge edges per outside group, the two halves exchangeable by a graph automorphism).

4. **Attacker, fixed at every identity count.** 27 units; unit r is one fabricated row (rare skill r mod 3; value = truth + 7 or truth − 7, sign per root), one attachment edge to a fixed honest non-anchor identity, one verification attempt (pre-drawn uniform; pass when below the attacker pass rate, so the informative and unreliable conditions are coupled). Identity j of k holds the contiguous block of 27/k units starting at j·27/k and is named and profiled by its first unit. Registration overhead: an identity exists only with at least one attachment edge and one report row, both paid from the fixed totals. The attempt budget of 27 cannot bind (an identity is checked at most once; at most 12 checks) and is recorded, not constraining. Attacker identities are linked to the two nearest attacker identities on each side in unit order at no cost (0, 3, 18, 54 links). The allocation rule is fixed here and is not tuned against evaluation outcomes.

5. **Factors and cells.** k ∈ {1, 3, 9, 27} × check strength {0.1, 0.9} × 7 policy cells (`no_verification`; `degree`, `random`, `coverage` at 4 and 12 checks). S1 = 24 × 2 × 2 × 4 × 7 = 2,688 assignments, one model call each. Cells with identical packets are not deduplicated; they are reported as repeated packets.

6. **Primary contrast (one, exploratory).** On the model's answers, with attacker pass 0.1 and 12 checks: (rare-skill wrong-answer fraction at k = 27 minus at k = 1 under `degree`) minus (the same difference under `coverage`), per root; mean over the 24 roots of each family; the estimate is the mean of the two family means. A wrong answer is a non-null value different from the truth; an abstention (null) is not a wrong answer and is reported beside it. Positive means coverage attenuates identity-splitting harm more than degree. Useful difference: 10 percentage points. Interval: percentile, 10,000 bootstrap draws, seed 20261004, roots resampled within family.

7. **Secondary, prespecified, descriptive, no multiplicity correction.** (a) Raw multiplicity effects, k = 3, 9, 27 minus k = 1, for each policy cell and check strength, model and plurality. (b) `random` minus `coverage` and `degree` minus `random` in place of `degree` minus `coverage`. (c) The primary under unreliable checks (0.9) and at 4 checks. (d) Model minus same-packet row plurality on wrong answers and on accuracy, per cell; identity plurality as a second deterministic reference. (e) The primary on rare-skill accuracy. (f) For every cell together: rare-skill correct, wrong and abstained fractions, share of wrong answers equal to the fabricated value, attacker seat share and row share, attacker identities admitted, checked and passed, honest-specialist retention, checks, tokens and dollars. (g) Agreement of answers on byte-identical packets within a root. If the multiplicity effect is absent or negative with resources fixed, that is the result; attacker resources are not increased afterwards.

8. **Denominators and missing outcomes.** All 2,688 assignments are kept with status completed, failed or not started. Cell means are shown with assigned and valid counts. Contrasts are reported complete-case (roots with every needed outcome) and with bounds over all assigned roots in which each missing outcome is set to 0 or 1, whichever makes the contrast smallest or largest. No outcome is imputed or dropped. An incomplete S1 is diagnostic until reconciled.

9. **Stages and gates.** S0 scripted (0 calls): the whole grid on engineering roots (ring 4919-4934, community 4821-4836; 1,792 rows), all 60 qualification fixtures and the probe fixture; passes only if every row is valid, every structural invariant holds at every identity allocation, the scripted qualification passes and the engineering grid is not degenerate (in each family a 27-identity attacker is admitted in at least one engineering root under `degree` and under `coverage` at 12 checks, and the four primary cells are not all 0 or all 1 under plurality). P0 (1 call): the `multirow1` fixture of engineering root 4919; passes only if the response parses, the model id matches, usage is reported, the stop reason is `end_turn` and the answer equals the expected values. Q0 (60 calls): six clean shapes × 5 roots × 2 families (ring 5139-5143, community 5144-5148); per shape over its 10 packets: all structurally valid, field accuracy ≥ 0.95, exact packets ≥ 0.90, 100% null on withheld facts (40 withheld fields in total). S1 follows only after Q0 passes at the same source hash. A failed gate stops the chain. Thresholds are not changed after a failure; a repair is a new attempt with fresh qualification roots and its own pre-run review.

10. **Model settings.** `claude-opus-5-5`, `output_config.effort: low` with a JSON schema, `max_tokens` 8,000, no temperature, top_p, top_k, thinking parameter, tool choice, prefill or fallbacks. The visible answer is limited to 2,000 characters on the text block. Thinking and redacted-thinking blocks are dropped; exactly one text block is required; `stop_reason: refusal` is the failure category `refusal`. System prompt: the sybil-scale-xl prompt with one added sentence ("Each report row names the identity that submitted it, and one identity may submit several rows.") and nothing else changed; the exact text is in `src/provider.py`.

11. **Execution.** Four requests in flight, shuffled dispatch, 180 s per request, 6 h per stage, 8 h for the chain. No answer is retried: a refusal, an invalid or wrong answer, a timeout, any HTTP status other than those named next, and anything after a response that reported usage end that call. The first failed call stops new dispatch; calls already in flight finish; the remaining assignments are recorded as not started.

12. **Transport retry rule** (2026-10-04, relayed by dmarz/fleet-monitor; copied from soc07-private-judgments). A request the provider rejected before running the model, HTTP 429 or 529 only, is re-sent at most twice per call, after 2 s and then 6 s, or after the `retry-after` header if longer, capped at 20 s, with all attempts and waits inside the one 180 s request budget. The same rule applies to the token-counting request. The reservation is made once per call. `max_calls` counts assignments dispatched; a retried call is one call and can be billed at most once. Every HTTP attempt to the messages endpoint is recorded in the ledger before it is sent and the study stops at 3,025 attempts (2,749 calls plus 10%). Attempts per row and per stage are reported.

13. **Budget.** Hard call caps: P0 1, Q0 60, S1 2,688, study 2,749; the ledger refuses a reservation beyond any of them. Dollar cap USD 190 on settled actual cost plus open reservations. Arithmetic: the S1 packets hold 175,028 report rows in total (mean 65.1 per call, range 54 to 80) and the Q0 packets 4,460; at the 48 input tokens per row measured by the sybil-scale-xl probe plus about 600 tokens per call for the system prompt and schema, expected input is 10.0 M tokens for S1, 0.25 M for Q0 and 0.004 M for P0, USD 41 at USD 4 per million. An output allowance of 2,500 tokens per call on average (the probe measured 38) over 2,749 calls is 6.9 M tokens, USD 137 at USD 20 per million. Sum USD 178; with about 6% on the input estimate for a tokenizer difference, rounded up: USD 190. Open reservations (4 in flight × about USD 0.18) fit inside it. Expected actual spend is USD 45 to 60. Before S1 the chain projects S1 cost from Q0's measured cost per call, scaled by request size, and stops if the projection exceeds what is left under the cap. Caps and timeouts are part of the hashed design and are not raised after qualification.

14. **Visualization.** Mapping in VISUALIZATION.md: wrong answers by identity count and accuracy against attacker seat share, faceted by graph family and check reliability, with per-root paired contrasts; initial, progress and final frames and a completion-prefix replay.

15. **After the run.** No condition is added or dropped after seeing results. Repairs of verified defects are documented with the original outputs preserved. Valid null or adverse findings complete the study. The holdout range 10000-19999 stays closed.
