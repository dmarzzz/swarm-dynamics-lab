- Question and decision: does splitting one attacker's unchanged resources (27 report rows, 27 attachment edges, 27 verification attempts) across 1, 3, 9 or 27 identities increase its harmful influence on a synthesized answer, and which admission policy preserves specialist value? The result decides whether a fixed-resource identity-splitting line is worth a larger evaluation and a faithful published-defense comparator.
- Expected finding, plausible negative, and what would make the run uninformative: expected, a positive primary contrast (coverage attenuates splitting harm more than degree); the plurality rule gives +0.41 on the 32 engineering roots. Plausible negatives: Opus abstains instead of answering from one or two unchecked rows, so the contrast is near zero while accuracy under `degree` stays low; or the raw effect of splitting is negative because the single hub is what does the damage (it is under unreliable checks in the calibration, −0.16). Uninformative if Q0 fails (no comparison is run), if the attacker is never admitted at k = 27 (guarded by the S0 degeneracy check on engineering roots), or if S1 stops early (reported as diagnostic with bounds).

## Design and assessment

- Closest evidence and strongest comparator: [sybil-scale-api](../../sybil-scale-api/RESULTS.md), where random checking at equal budget matched coverage and plurality on the same packets reached 100%; attacker resources grew with population there. Comparators here: `random` at equal check budgets, `no_verification`, and row plurality and identity plurality on the identical packet. No published Sybil defense is implemented; that limit is stated in the README.
- Units: 24 roots per graph family (48). Each root is paired across 4 identity counts × 7 policy cells × 2 check strengths (56 assignments). Calls, skills and identities within a root are dependent.
- Coverage and boundaries: engineering roots (ring 4919-4934, community 4821-4836) were used for the calibration and are the S0 grid; qualification roots (ring 5139-5143, community 5144-5148) and comparison roots (ring 8233-8256, community 8351-8374) were not used for any design decision. The offline rehearsal ran the plurality stub over the comparison packets to exercise the pipeline; its outputs were written to a temporary directory, deleted, and not inspected. Root ranges were scanned against the repository on 2026-10-04T08:20Z, 09:05Z and again at the pinned commit: no occurrence of any chosen number outside this directory. Holdout 10000-19999 is untouched.
- Manipulation check and resource matching: `study.check_root` asserts, for every root and every k, that the 27 fabricated rows, the 27 honest endpoints of the attachment edges and the 27 attempt draws are the same objects; that the honest names, graph, rows, profiles, check outcomes, anchors and the 54 seats are unchanged; that each identity holds an equal contiguous block and at least one edge and one row; that internal links follow the frozen rule; that an identity's name, profile and check outcome depend only on its first unit; that attempt outcomes are coupled across check strengths; and that every packet's rows keep one root-wide order. S0 runs it on all 32 engineering roots, S1 runs it on its own 48 roots before any call, and a violation blocks dispatch. What k does change besides the partition is stated in the README: free links among the attacker's identities (0, 3, 18, 54), and the visibility of concentration (many rows under one name, a degree-27 hub at k = 1).
- Evaluator, truth separation, controls: packets hold only `node`, `skill`, `claim`, `age`, `activity`, `verification`; a test rejects any evaluator field name in a packet or in the public graph. A manipulated answer (the fabricated value) is graded wrong and an abstention is not; both are tested. Clean competence is Q0. Positive control for the gate: the rehearsal's never-abstaining stub fails Q0 and stops the chain.
- Primary metric: rare-skill wrong-answer fraction; (k = 27 minus k = 1 under `degree`) minus (the same under `coverage`), attacker pass 0.1, 12 checks, per root; mean of the two family means; 95% percentile interval from 10,000 root-bootstrap draws (seed 20261004); useful difference 10 points. Limits: 24 development-sized roots per family; one population size, fabrication type and attachment rule; simulated identities and checks; the scripted direction depends on the internal-link assumption (+0.41 with links, +0.01 without, on engineering roots).
- Timing semantics: none inside a call. Each assignment is one stateless call; the replay shows completion order.
