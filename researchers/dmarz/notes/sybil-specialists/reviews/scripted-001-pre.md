## Design and assessment

Question: can a bounded verification policy preserve honest specialist capacity under graph admission? Main practical decision is whether this instrument is fit for a later model-assisted or mechanism-comparison experiment. A favorable treatment effect is not a qualification condition. Null or adverse differences remain valid scripted outcomes.

Paired world is the analysis cluster. Policies, check budgets and graph conditions sharing a seed are dependent. S0 world seeds 100–103 are development controls; S1 uses fresh 200–211. Reserved holdout 10000–19999 remains inaccessible. Every graph has matched outside-community profiles and degree-preserving modularity interventions. The verifier is an explicitly simulated external service; equal honest/attacker pass probability is an uninformative control. All policy comparisons use one common ranking and 18 seats, with equal paid-check counts for degree/random/coverage.

Scoring checks six objective claims and separates useful rare-task completion from malicious admission. Truth labels, answer values and future probe outcomes stay outside the policy interfaces. No ownership detector, SybilShield implementation, exact vertex-cut mechanism or LLM behavioral claim is implied. Those comparators and a second graph generator remain formal-promotion requirements.

Primary S1 development contrast and all denominators are in README.md and design.yaml. The 12 worlds support descriptive inspection, not a power claim. The engine records every assigned outcome and preserves all invalid rows. No automatic scientific retries.
