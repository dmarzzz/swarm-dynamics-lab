## Design and assessment

Unchanged from the Haiku study's [S1 assessment](../../sybil-newcomer-api/reviews/s1-001-pre.md) apart from the model: 24 paired worlds 7100-7123, three identity counts, three audit policies (random, renewal, reputation), clean/sleeper/relapse conditions, three sampled model rounds, 1,944 assigned calls. The world is the analysis cluster. Assignment ids, packets, expected answers and dispatch order are identical to `sybil-newcomer-api` S1, so each cell pairs world-by-world with the Haiku cohort. The two cohorts are reported separately and never pooled.

Primary (unchanged): renewal minus reputation specialist accuracy at 16 identities, sleeper attack, round 8; 10 percentage points is the practical margin. Cross-model contrast (secondary, descriptive): Sonnet minus Haiku on the same cell, paired by world. Null or adverse valid outcomes finish the experiment; they are not repair targets.
