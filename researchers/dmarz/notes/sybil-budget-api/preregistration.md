# Frozen exploratory protocol — sybil-budget-api v1

Owner dmarz, 2026-10-04. This is predeclared exploratory S1 work under an explicit owner waiver of independent review, not a formal accepted hypothesis or confirmatory preregistration. Formal S2 remains disabled. The preceding [post-mortem](../sybil-scale-api/reviews/s1-001-post.md) was read before design.

1. **Question and predictions.** Additional informative verification may preserve specialist knowledge and reduce attacker seats. Reliability near the honest pass rate may erase or reverse those benefits. Coverage may equal or underperform random at equal budgets. Valid null, non-monotonic and adverse findings finish this study without scientific reruns.
2. **Assignments and pairing.** The exact 120-cell Cartesian grid and 24 world IDs are frozen in `design.yaml`. Truth belongs to the world. Identities, graph, latent check draws and order are paired across cells; checking sequences are shared prefixes across budgets. World is the independent unit. Identical actor packets across reliability labels are retained as planned repeated invocations with distinct cell IDs. The 16 Q0 calls and 256 S0 outputs are separately designated. No cross-study result pooling for inference.
3. **Primary and secondary endpoints.** Primary: model specialist accuracy under coverage at N972, attacker pass0.1, 108 minus4 checks. Report the mean paired difference, all 24 differences and seeded 10,000-resample world-bootstrap 95% percentile interval. Secondary: equal-budget coverage-minus-random differences for accuracy, seats and retention, plus endpoints at every size/reliability and cell means. Prior-study endpoint values are descriptive replication anchors, not paired observations.
4. **Engineering decision target.** For each size/policy/reliability, enumerate all measured budgets with mean accuracy≥0.90 and mean attacker seats≤0.05. The minimum is only the smallest tested passing budget. No monotonic regression or interpolation. Show intervals and honest specialist retention alongside it. The interval-envelope flag is descriptive and unadjusted for grid search. No p-value, familywise success or real-world safety claim follows.
5. **Evaluation and leakage.** The model and checking policies receive public reports and operational badges only; evaluator-only truth labels, principal ownership and latent future check outcomes stay outside actor input. The six-value schema disallows extra keys and booleans as integers. Wrong values and nulls score incorrect for required facts. Clean missing-fact qualification instead explicitly rewards null where no report exists.
6. **Qualification.** Full and common-only clean packets at both sizes on four new worlds. Required structural validity100%, mean field correctness≥95%, exact-packet rate≥90%, missing-fact abstention100% independently per size. Scripted full factorial on two engineering worlds must be structurally valid; poor attack outcomes are not qualification failures. Regression tests cover original simulator/prompt parity, deterministic pairing, truthful check counts, actor blindness, caps, duplicate refusal, fail-closed dispatch and recomputation.
7. **Resources and stopping.** Frozen per-study cap is stated in design and exact reservations in `reservation-plan.json`; 3,200-call cap includes diagnostics and is not permission to spend beyond the shared owner cap. Nominal paid calls2,896. Four concurrent calls, 120-second request timeout, four-hour stage timeout, no HTTP or model-output retries, 500 output tokens. Reservations persist across Q0/S1 and never decrease; no-call failures count as failures. On failure stop new dispatch, preserve all assigned rows and record a bounded repair attempt with new disjoint qualification worlds if prompt/runtime changes. A qualification failure blocks S1. Independent review waiver does not waive engineering qualification or budget controls.
8. **Provenance and publication.** Runtime hash includes source, design, experiment registration and requirements; every output records the source hash and git revision. Store assignments, world snapshots, packets, packet hashes, verification events, answers, grades, completion order and API usage. Recompute evaluations and analysis before final reporting; validate uploaded artifacts. Mapping v1 specifies complete-grid heatmaps and measured progress replay. No model calls are made for animation. No secrets enter public records.
9. **Changes.** None after paid collection. Any necessary repair creates a dated amendment and new attempt instead of overwriting observations. Root operator freezes and commits this design before paid stages. Holdout10000–19999 untouched.
