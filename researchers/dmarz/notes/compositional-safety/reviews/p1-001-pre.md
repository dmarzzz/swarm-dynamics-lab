## Design and interpretation

168 fixed assignments: roots 300–305 × D1/D2 × risk/benign × C/S/F/R/P/G/H. Four roles, 40 turns, identical task/variant fixtures paired across arms, seeded arm order, fixed cyclic serial scheduling. The central C comparator changes capabilities and architecture together; S versus F isolates the declared shared-versus-fragmented history treatment within the same role/menu/schedule structure. R/P/G/H each start from F. Receipts contain logged host facts, not evaluator verdicts. H has complete atomic host enforcement and is an instrumentation upper bound, not a novel economic mechanism.

Report each domain/variant/arm cell with its assigned denominator, valid/incomplete/invalid counts, committed violations, safe legitimate completion, calls, tokens, steps and costs. Within each task/variant, retain paired S/F, R/F, R/P, G/F and H/F outcomes. No significance testing, post-hoc subgroup selection, stopping for a desirable effect or unqualified prevalence claim. No scientific result is repaired merely because it is null or adverse.

Pre-data structural audit: six D1 roots represent five shapes (one appears twice); six D2 roots represent four shapes (two appear twice). Risk/benign and treatment repeats are dependent. The 168 episodes are not 168 independent task structures. Show structural fingerprints in the summary. Small cells (six episodes each) are descriptive, not powered confirmatory evidence. Fixed-byte R/P envelopes leave tokenizer and inner-schema differences; no receipt-specific causal claim should outrun that control. Actual retrieval/message/context costs are reported rather than assumed matched.

Actors are stateless model calls with their permitted saved history/messages supplied each turn. There is no hidden persistent agent memory, trained collusion, private payoff, asynchronous settlement or real external effect. No D3 model task, unopened root, W prior-defense comparator or formal S1/S2 stage is launched. Limited structural diversity, one pinned model and fixed scheduling remain scale prerequisites.
