## Design and assessment

- Closest evidence and strongest comparator: [sybil-budget-api](../../sybil-budget-api/RESULTS.md), N = 324 coverage audit, where attacker seat share rose 1.98%, 8.51%, 10.70% at 32, 64, 108 checks under strong checks. Comparators here: the `anchors` rule (no pass credit) and the `direct` rule; the reference plurality rule on the same packets; clean endpoints.
- Units: 24 roots, each paired across 3 rules × 3 budgets × 2 strengths and 3 clean endpoints (21 calls). Calls, seats and skills within a root are dependent.
- Coverage and boundaries: engineering roots 4481-4488 were used for the calibration and are the S0 grid; qualification roots 4591-4598, repair roots 4650-4657 and comparison roots 9541-9564 were not used for any design decision. Because the primary is scripted, generating the comparison assignments (for the manifest and for the rehearsal) computes it; the builder did not print, read or record comparison-root admission outcomes, and rehearsal outputs were deleted unread. Root ranges were scanned against the repository at 10:40Z and 11:10Z on 2026-10-04 (3,956 files): none of the chosen numbers occurs outside this directory. Holdout 10000-19999 untouched.
- Manipulation check: `study.check_root` asserts for every root that the audit is one sequence whose prefixes are the smaller budgets and whose outcomes are the root's check draws; that the three score vectors have mass 1 on the same active identities; that pass-credit mass is alpha in both `propagated` and `direct`; that `direct` places credit on passed identities only; that 162 distinct non-failed identities are seated by score then name; that a row of an identity is identical in the packets of the three rules; that packets hold no evaluator field and keep one root-wide row order; that an empty passed set reduces all rules to `anchors`; and that the clean endpoint has the same graph, audit and admission as the weak-check cell with truthful reports. S0 runs it on the 8 engineering roots and S1 on its own 24 roots before any call; a violation blocks dispatch.
- Difference from the earlier implementation, audited: the dangling rule sends mass to the two anchors (earlier: anchors and passed identities). On engineering roots 12 of 48 snapshots have a dangling identity; the `propagated` admitted set equals the earlier ranking's in 42 of 48 and in all 36 without a dangling identity (a test shows this and that the world, the coverage audit and the earlier ranking are reproduced exactly); in the other 6 it differs by 6 to 13 seats and the attacker seat count by at most 1. No exact replication is claimed.
- Evaluator and controls: local validation is the schema (`{"values": {"0".."5": integer or null}}`); a fabricated answer is graded wrong and an abstention is not; clean competence is the 24-fixture qualification. Positive control for the gate: the rehearsal's never-abstaining stub fails Q0 and stops the chain.
- Primary metric: per root, [attacker seats (propagated, 108) − (propagated, 32)] − [attacker seats (direct, 108) − (direct, 32)] at controller check pass 0.1; mean over 24 roots with a 10,000-draw root bootstrap (seed 20261004). Limits: scripted identities; one graph family, audit policy and fabrication; counterfactual rules written for this study; truth availability is at its ceiling on engineering roots.
- Timing semantics: none inside a call; the replay shows completion order.
