# Pre-registration: trust-credit-qwen v1 (program v5, line T)

2026-10-04, dmarz/pipeline-split. Frozen before any stage of this study has run and before any model call. [README.md](README.md) describes the setup; [design.yaml](design.yaml) is the frozen machine-readable design.

Authority and review status (as relayed to this builder by dmarz/pipeline, 2026-10-04): dmarz directed this program himself (research program v5, written with him by a Codex session on 2026-10-04; his instruction to ship it was relayed by dmarz/fleet-monitor). The design is the program's; this package implements line T as specified there. Cross-researcher review is waived by dmarz for these exploratory runs; dmarz/fleet-monitor's check is a same-researcher check and nothing more; the run is not independently reviewed.

Implementation note, 2026-10-04: the program's five-session and shared-reservation arrangement is replaced by the ready-chain (one package per line, one server per line, its own ledger and caps, launched by the orchestrator from the private run queue). The program's design for this line (samples, contrasts, conditions, primary, qualification thresholds, call counts) is implemented as written. Choices the program leaves open are listed in item 12.

1. **Question.** Does propagating a passed identity's trust credit to its neighbours cause extra attacker identities to be admitted?

2. **Unit.** A root (one graph, task and set of check draws). 24 fresh comparison roots, 9541-9564. Every root is paired across the three rules, three budgets and two check strengths. Identities, seats, skills and calls within a root are dependent.

3. **World.** The sybil-budget-api generator at N = 324: 162 core identities, two outside groups of 81 (one honest specialists, one owned by a single controller that reports truth + 7), two trusted anchors, 162 seats, honest check pass 0.9, controller check pass 0.1 (strong) or 0.9 (weak), outcomes one uniform draw per identity.

4. **Frozen audit.** Per root and strength, the earlier coverage policy generates one sequence of 108 checks with outcomes and failed-identity removals. Budgets 32 and 64 are its prefixes. The three rules read the same sequence.

5. **Admission rules.** On each snapshot one transition matrix (mass spread equally over neighbours that have not failed) and one dangling rule (mass of an identity with no such neighbour goes to the two anchors), restart 0.2, 120 iterations, uniform start. q0 from the two anchors; q1 from the passed identities; alpha = passed / (2 + passed). propagated = (1 − alpha) q0 + alpha q1; direct = (1 − alpha) q0 + alpha uniform(passed); anchors = q0; an empty passed set gives q0. Top 162 scores seated, ties by identity name. Badges (anchor, passed, unchecked) and packet order (a key per root and identity) do not depend on the rule.

6. **Cells and calls.** 24 roots × 3 rules × 3 budgets × 2 strengths = 432 attacked syntheses, plus 24 × 3 clean endpoints at 108 checks = 72; 504 comparison calls, one stateless call each. A clean endpoint keeps the graph and the audit of the weak-check cell and makes every controller report truthful.

7. **Primary.** Per root, at controller check pass 0.1: [attacker seats (propagated, 108) − attacker seats (propagated, 32)] − [attacker seats (direct, 108) − attacker seats (direct, 32)]. Estimate: mean over the 24 roots; 95% percentile interval from 10,000 bootstrap draws over roots, seed 20261004; per-root values shown. The outcome is computed by the scripted admission. It is fixed once the design and the roots are fixed; the builder generated the comparison assignments to build the manifest and did not look at their admission outcomes.

8. **Reported beside it, all strata.** Attacker seats and seat share, honest-specialist retention, truth availability and truth plurality for every rule, budget and strength; the same attenuation contrast under weak checks, with `anchors` in place of `direct`, and at 64 checks; the model's rare-skill answers as correct, wrong (a non-null value different from the truth) and abstained, and how often a wrong answer equals the fabricated value; the reference plurality rule on the same packets; clean endpoints; paired differences in rare-skill correctness between `propagated` and `direct` at each budget and strength. Secondary intervals carry no multiplicity correction. Negative and null results are kept.

9. **Qualification.** 24 fixtures: 8 development roots (4591-4598) × `full`, `sparse`, `missing`; clean packets of 162 reports in the comparison's format. P0 is the first fixture and checks the interface only. Q0 is the other 23. The gate is evaluated over all 24 rows: every structure valid; at least 7 of 8 packets exactly right (all six values) in `full` and in `sparse`; null on the withheld fact in 8 of 8 `missing` packets. A failed gate stops the chain. One bounded repair is allowed and is the only one: a new attempt (`attempt: '002'`, new source hash, its own pre-run review) on the second, disjoint set of 24 fixtures frozen now (roots 4650-4657). The failing answers are read before any repair. A failed repeat ends the line. Thresholds are never lowered.

10. **Model and request.** `qwen/qwen3.7-flash` via OpenRouter, provider Alibaba only, no fallback, `require_parameters`, reasoning disabled, JSON-object mode, `max_tokens` 1,000. The body is the frozen template of the program's `selected-model.json` plus `messages`. The answer is validated locally: exactly `{"values": {"0".."5": integer or null}}`. An invalid JSON or invalid answer is a failed call; there is no repair call and no answer retry. Input ceiling 8,000 tokens: every request is at most 7,600 bytes by construction (measured 5.9 kB), a token is at least one byte, and the chain stops before S1 with `input_ceiling_projection` if P0's measured tokens per byte times the largest S1 request exceeds 8,000.

11. **Execution and failure handling.** Four requests in flight, 120 s per request, 3,600 s per stage, 7,200 s for the chain.
    - Transport retry: HTTP 429 and overload statuses (502, 503, 529) only, at most twice, 2 s then 6 s, `retry-after` up to 20 s, inside the request timeout; every attempt is counted against 640.
    - Evidence (failure rule part 1): a failed request keeps its HTTP status, the first 2,000 characters of the response body and the request id; no credential is stored.
    - Token counting (part 2) does not apply: this route has no token-counting endpoint. The reservation is a byte-based upper bound (input tokens ≤ request bytes) plus the full output limit.
    - Tolerance (part 3): in S1 a call that ends without a valid answer is recorded as failed and dispatch continues; it stops when more than `max_failed` = 6 calls have failed (the larger of 3 and 1% of 504, rounded up). Integrity failures stop dispatch at once: a ledger refusal, `reservation_bound_breached`, `model_mismatch`, `provider_mismatch`, a source-hash mismatch, the stage or chain deadline. S0, P0 and Q0 are strict.
    - Billing outage (part 4): HTTP 402, or a 400/403 naming the credit balance, pauses the stage; the same call is re-sent every 60 s for up to 1,200 s; then the stage stops with `provider_credit_balance_low`, the affected and unfinished calls recorded as not started and nothing counted as failed. Units left not started by such a stop may be resumed at the same source hash with `python src/chain.py resume` (batch `s1-001-r1`, then `-r2`), as a dated amendment recorded in this folder, because a billing outage is unrelated to outcomes. The analysis reads the original run and its continuations together and counts no unit twice.
    - Missing data: every failed or not-started call stays in its cell's denominator. Model outcomes are reported complete-case with denominators and with bounds in which each missing answer is all wrong or all right. Nothing is dropped, imputed or re-run. The primary does not depend on model calls.

12. **Choices the program leaves open, fixed here.** (a) Dangling rule: to the two anchors; the difference from the earlier implementation is audited in S0 and reported, and exact replication is claimed only for snapshots without a dangling identity, where a test shows it. (b) Clean endpoints use the weak-check audit, where the controller's identities pass at the honest rate. (c) Packet rows are ordered by a per-identity key instead of the earlier shuffle of the ranked list, so order carries no rank. (d) Compact packet format and the instruction text in `src/study.py`. (e) Fixture composition and the meaning of "correct" (all six values) and "abstention" (null on the withheld fact). (f) The interface probe is the first fixture.

13. **Budget.** Hard call caps P0 1, Q0 23, S1 504, study 528. Dollar cap USD 2 on settled cost plus open reservations. Arithmetic at the frozen prices (USD 0.03 and 0.13 per million): a reservation is at most 7,600 × 0.03 + 1,000 × 0.13 = 358 millionths of a dollar, so 528 unsettled reservations are USD 0.19; expected spend, at about 3,000 input and 50 output tokens per call, is 528 × (3,000 × 0.03 + 50 × 0.13) / 10^6 = USD 0.05. Caps and timeouts are in the hashed design.

14. **After the run.** No condition is added or dropped. Repairs of verified defects are documented with the original outputs preserved. The holdout range 10000-19999 stays closed.
