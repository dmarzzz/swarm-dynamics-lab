## Design

4 clean qualification worlds (5000–5003) × 2 packets (`sample`: seeded N/2 of all reports; `common_only`: the N/2 common-skill identities) × 2 badge modes × 3 sizes = 48 calls. Thresholds per size, unchanged from the parent: 100% structural validity, ≥95% field accuracy, ≥90% exact packets, 100% abstention on missing specialist facts. The packet change from the parent's `full` (N reports) is deliberate: `full` at N=8,748 would be about 315k tokens, above Haiku's 200k context, while every S1 packet has N/2 reports.

## Frozen execution plan

- 48 calls, 4 in flight, 300 s per request, no retries, first failure stops dispatch.
- Largest request body at N=8,748 is recorded in the S1 pre-run file; the 700 kB request limit is above it.
- Command: `python3 scripts/run-sybil-scale-xl.py <commit> Q0`; then `status`, `publish`, `verify`.
- Expected actual spend about USD 3; the conservative reservation is recorded by the ledger.

## Risks
