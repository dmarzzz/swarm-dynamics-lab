Which questions should we pursue?

How to use this review

Browse broadly, then mark candidates Shortlist, Discuss or Park and add a reason. These choices are local to this browser; export them to share with the team. Nothing here approves or runs an experiment.

Ask: Would either outcome change a decision? Does the closest prior work already answer it? Can we obtain the needed observations without privileged information? Is the smallest test worth its cost?

For selected ideas, finish the focused prior-art review, define the primary outcome and smallest worthwhile effect, choose independent units and uncertainty rules, then write the formal hypothesis and protocol. A noisy null result is inconclusive. Feasibility labels are planning categories; source read depths come from the catalogue and retain its open audit caveats.
