## Design and assessment

- Closest evidence and comparator: the note's V4 verdict ([[yan-2026-when]], [[abedini-2026-dont]], [[zhong-2025-disentangling]], [[lin-2026-you]]); none uses a false threat label on a real resource or compares a false alarm with a true one. Within-study comparators: the same root without any alarm (`C0`), the alarm without correction (`FA`), the true alarm with and without a false retraction (`TA`, `TA+C`), and three scripted reference policies computed on the same inputs.
- Units: 24 world roots (8400 to 8423), each played in 5 conditions: 120 team episodes, 5 model agents × 6 rounds each, 3,600 calls. Agents, rounds, resources and calls are not samples. No power claim. For scale: the scripted credulous policy played on the 24 S1 roots gives a primary contrast of −22.5 points with a root-bootstrap interval of −28.6 to −16.9, so the interval is roughly ±6 points for a deterministic policy; a 10-point effect is near the edge of what 24 roots resolve.
- Coverage and boundaries: engineering roots 8390 and 8391 (S0, and the P0 packet from 8390), qualification roots 8450 to 8457, S1 roots 8400 to 8423, 10000 to 19999 unopened, S2 disabled. A repository scan on 2026-10-04 found no other use of these numbers ([SETUP.md](../SETUP.md)).
- What the builder has already seen of S1 roots: their worlds and inspections were generated offline for the manifest; the scripted reference policies and the rehearsal stub played them (the table below, three selftests, the rehearsal). These are outputs of fixed rules. No model has read any packet of this study.
- Manipulation check: within a root the five conditions share the world, the attributes, every inspection of every member and the planted slot; S0 checks this, checks that round-1 packets are byte-identical across conditions, that a condition's board is the `C0` board plus exactly the planted posts, and that a packet differs from its `C0` counterpart only by those posts. A selftest repeats this with agents whose output reacts to the board (round-2 packets differ by exactly one entry; what follows later is allowed to differ). `chain verify` rebuilds every saved packet from the episode's fixed inputs and the saved answers.
- Truth separation: the packet builder takes only the public resource records, the member's own inspections, its own earlier decisions and the board; S0 checks its parameter list and checks every scripted actor input and the system prompt for truth, target, condition and slot fields and for a list of words (planted, scripted, condition, target, truth, test, evaluat-, experiment, ...). Row ids and ledger call ids contain condition slugs; they are never sent to the model.
- Clean competence: Q0, 24 packets without any planted claim (8 roots × depth 2, 3, 6), earlier rounds played by scripted truthful teammates. Gate on 134 gated decisions (35, 34, 65 per depth; 110 `use`, 24 `skip`): at least 90% equal to the private-evidence reference overall and per depth, which allows 3, 3 and 6 misses per depth and 13 overall. The 12 decisions of a call are not independent; the effective sample per depth is nearer 8 packets ([lesson 7](../../pipeline/LESSONS.md)). The most likely way to fail: Opus skipping resources with two clean inspections of two, or four clean of six (posterior 0.084, expected payoff of use +0.75). That would be an over-cautious criterion on clear cases, and the chain stopping there is a result to report: the threshold is not lowered and the prompt is not adjusted to pass it. Before any repair the failing answers are read with their packets, which are saved in every row ([lesson 6](../../pipeline/LESSONS.md)).
- Primary metric: use rate of X by the five model agents in rounds 4 to 6, `C0` minus `FA+C`, mean paired difference over 24 roots, 10,000-draw root bootstrap with seed 20261004. Practical marker 10 points. A decision that was not observed is bounded in [0, 1]; no root is dropped and nothing is set to zero. Secondary measures (a) to (h) and the reference comparisons are listed in the [README](../README.md); all descriptive.
- Timing semantics: logical rounds. Posts of round t are visible from round t+1; the alarm is posted at the end of round 1 and the correction at the end of round 3. Wall time has no meaning for the measure.

Reading: the private-evidence policy shows exactly zero alarm effect in every condition (negative control). The credulous policy shows a full cascade (use of X 0 after the alarm) and a full recovery (1 after the correction), and the mirror image on H (positive control). `C0` use of X under private evidence is 0.64 to 0.78 on the S1 roots (by round: 0.68, 0.47, 0.81, 0.69, 0.85, 0.78), neither floor nor ceiling. The even-round dips come from ties: with as many clean as suspicious inspections the posterior is exactly 1/3, the expected payoff of use is 0, and the reference skips. No reference policy gives a positive primary contrast; a positive value from the model would be persistence that none of them produces. Ways the scripted S0 could pass while the design is uninformative, and what was done: (1) floor or ceiling on X in `C0`: checked above on the S1 roots and by an S0 invariant on the engineering roots; (2) a board that cannot carry a cascade: the credulous control; (3) an alarm that changes other inputs: the identity invariants; (4) H near the floor: this one is real. H is used in 11 to 13% of `C0` decisions under private evidence, so the true alarm has little room to lower it and the asymmetry measure (d) has a small denominator on the true-alarm side. The false-retraction harm (`TA+C` minus `TA` in rounds 4 to 6) has room upward and is the usable true-alarm measure.
