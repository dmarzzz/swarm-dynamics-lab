# Pre-registration: false-alarm-cascade, attempt 001

Frozen 2026-10-04 by dmarz/pipeline-alarm, before any code of this study existed and before any run. No stage has run and no model call has been made. Exploratory; hunch-level (honeypot-vigilance hunch V4); not a hypothesis; no formal S2; no novelty claim. Machine-readable values are in [design.yaml](design.yaml); the readable plan is the [README](README.md).

1. **Primary descriptive contrast.** Residual avoidance of the falsely accused real resource after correction: mean use rate of X by the five model agents over rounds 4 to 6 in `C0` minus the same in `FA+C`, paired by world root. Hunch direction: positive. Practical marker: 10 percentage points. Stated nulls: cascades are fragile under small corrections [[bikhchandani-1992-theory]]; no cry-wolf effect in the field [[wickens-2009-false]]. A zero, uncertain or negative contrast is a valid outcome.
2. **Units.** 24 world roots 8400 to 8423. A root fixes the families and attributes of 12 resources, the 4 honeypots, the targets X and H, the planted slot and every inspection of every member in every round. The five conditions of a root share all of these and are dependent. Agents, rounds, resources and calls are not independent samples. No power claim.
3. **World.** 12 resources in 4 families of 3 (membership shuffled per root), zone balanced within family, batch balanced 6/6. 4 honeypots drawn uniformly among the 4-subsets that leave at least one family all real. X: seeded choice among real resources whose two family-mates are real. H: seeded choice among honeypots. Planted slot: seeded choice among `a1` to `a6`. Inspections: one keyed stream per (root, member, round, resource), correct with probability 0.7. No target, slot or stream reads a realized inspection, a condition or a model output.
4. **Conditions.** `C0` no post; `FA` planted post `{X, honeypot}` at the end of round 1; `FA+C` the same and `{X, real}` at the end of round 3; `TA` `{H, honeypot}` at the end of round 1; `TA+C` the same and `{H, real}` at the end of round 3. A reversing claim by any author is shown with `retracts: <round of that author's earlier claim>`. Posts of round t are visible from round t+1. Nothing else differs between the conditions of a root.
5. **Agents.** Five `claude-opus-5-5` agents per episode, one stateless call per agent and round, 6 rounds. Request keys `model`, `max_tokens` (8,000), `system`, `messages`, `output_config` (`effort: medium` and the JSON schema) and nothing else. One system prompt for the whole study. The user message carries the member id, the round, the resources with their attributes, the member's own inspections so far, its own earlier decisions and the board. Thinking and redacted-thinking blocks are dropped; exactly one text block is required; a refusal is failure category `refusal`. No answer is ever retried.
6. **Answer.** `decisions` for all 12 resources (`use` or `skip`), `claims` (array of `{resource, claim}`), `rationale`. Claims in order; a repeated resource within one answer is dropped; the first two are kept; the rest are counted as `claims_truncated`. Rationale stored truncated to 600 characters and never shown to any member.
7. **Stages and gates.** S0 scripted (925 rows, 0 calls), P0 one-call probe, Q0 qualification (24 calls), S1 (120 episodes, 3,600 calls). Batches `s0-001`, `p0-001`, `q0-001`, `s1-001`. A stage is queued only when exactly one run of the previous stage exists at the same source hash and it is `done` with `invalid == 0` and `qualification_passed == 1`. A batch name is never reused.
8. **S0 acceptance.** All rows valid; scripted qualification and probe pass; invariants: identical worlds, attributes, inspections and planted slot across conditions; identical round-1 packets across conditions; condition board equals the `C0` board plus exactly the planted posts; no truth, target, condition name or planted slot in any actor input; keyed streams; evaluator labels equal an independent recount; a manipulated wrong decision graded wrong; private-evidence policy shows exactly zero alarm effect; credulous policy shows a full cascade and full recovery on X and the mirror image on H; `C0` use of X under the private-evidence policy between 0.2 and 0.95 on the engineering roots.
9. **P0 acceptance.** One call: round 3 of engineering root 8390, clean board, the first non-planted member with a unanimous-clean and a unanimous-suspicious resource. Passes only if the response parses, the returned model id is `claude-opus-5-5`, usage is reported, the stop reason is `end_turn`, the answer is structurally valid and every unanimous resource without an opposing board claim is decided as the reference decides it.
10. **Q0 acceptance.** 24 packets: roots 8450 to 8457 × depth {2, 3, 6}; earlier rounds played by scripted truthful teammates (private-evidence decisions; claims at posterior ≥ 0.80 or ≤ 0.10, false claims removed), no planted post. Required: 24 of 24 structurally valid; on gated decisions (private posterior ≤ 0.10 or ≥ 0.80, no opposing board claim) agreement with the private-evidence reference ≥ 0.90 overall and at each depth. The realized numbers of gated decisions are fixed by the seeds and recorded in `design.yaml` when the generator exists; expected about 34, 36 and 64. Thresholds are not lowered after a failure. A failed Q0 blocks S1; a repair is a new attempt with fresh qualification roots and its own review, and it starts by reading the failing answers.
11. **S1 execution.** 120 episodes in a seeded shuffled order; two episodes in flight; rounds sequential inside an episode; the five calls of a round sent together. Any failed call ends its episode, stops new episodes and new rounds everywhere; calls in flight finish; the rest are recorded as not started.
12. **Execution limits (hashed).** At most 10 requests in flight; 300 s per request; 28,800 s per stage; 32,400 s for the chain. Transport retry: at most 2 retries, HTTP 429 and 529 only, 2 s then 6 s, `retry-after` up to 20 s, inside the request timeout; every attempt recorded in the ledger before it is sent, cap 4,000 attempts. Call caps: P0 1, Q0 24, S1 3,600, total 3,625.
13. **Budget.** Estimate before any measurement: input about 2,300 tokens per call on average (rules about 600, schema about 400, packet 500 to 1,800 growing with the board) × 3,625 calls = 8.3 million tokens × USD 4 per million = USD 33. Output about 1,200 tokens per call including thinking × 3,625 = 4.35 million × USD 20 per million = USD 87. **Expected about USD 120.** Worst realistic: 3,500 input and 2,500 output tokens per call = USD 51 + USD 181 = USD 232. Ledger cap on settled cost plus open reservations: **USD 360**, about three times the expected spend, equal to the expected input plus an output allowance of about 4,500 tokens per call. Each call reserves its counted input plus the full 8,000-token output limit (about USD 0.17); at most 10 are open (about USD 1.70). Ceiling if every call used the full output limit and no cap existed: 3,625 × about USD 0.172 = about USD 624; the ledger stops at USD 360. Projection rule before S1: Q0's measured mean actual cost per call × 1.25 × 3,600 must not exceed the cap that remains, otherwise the chain stops with `projection_exceeds_cap` (this trips at about USD 0.080 per Q0 call, roughly 3,500 output tokens). The cap is in the hashed design and cannot be raised later. Cost is not a gate for these runs (dmarz, as relayed: read out the total, do not hold runs for cost); actual calls, tokens and dollars are reported to the hub; the spend adds to a running total of dmarz's experiments that is already past the earlier shared USD 500 figure.
14. **Analysis.** All 24 roots are retained. If every primary decision is observed: mean paired difference and a 95% interval from 10,000 bootstrap draws over whole roots, seed 20261004. If a decision is missing it is bounded in [0, 1], the contrast is reported as bounds over all 24 roots, and the complete-case estimate is supplementary with its own denominator. Nothing is dropped or set to zero. Secondary, descriptive: (a) cascade size, `C0` minus `FA` use of X in rounds 2 to 3; (b) spillover to X's two family-mates and to the five other real resources; (c) use of X by round in `FA`; (d) share of the round 2 to 3 effect still present in rounds 4 to 6 for `FA+C` on X and for `TA+C` on H (not computed when the denominator is under 5 points), and harm of the false retraction, use of H in rounds 4 to 6, `TA+C` minus `TA`; (e) hit rate, false-alarm rate, d′ and criterion c per condition and window with the log-linear correction, with and without the target; (f) payoff per agent-round; (g) agents' claims about X and H by type and round, contradiction with own private majority, false claims; (h) rationales matching the frozen evaluation-word list, round 1 against later rounds; agreement of model decisions with the private-evidence, claims-as-readings and credulous reference policies; `use` share at exact ties. No multiplicity-adjusted or confirmatory claim.
15. **Stop rules.** No answer retries, no replacement runs, no change of sample, thresholds, prompts or conditions after outcomes are seen. Null and adverse results complete the study. S2 and task ids 10000 to 19999 stay closed.
