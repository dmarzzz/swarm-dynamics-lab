# v3 explainer film

Human asked for a self-contained video of the discussion and memory v3 results: the experiment, what each
outcome of the hunch would mean, the simulation playing out, then the result.

Waited for `discussion-dose-v3/v3-q0-a1` to finish (636/636 calls, 96/96 cases, audit ok, measured $4.39), copied
its records read-only from the run host into ignored `data/discussion-v3/v3-q0-a1/`, and built the film from them.
No model calls, no changes to `bench_v3`, worlds, scoring or the run.

Source: `notes/discussion-dose/src/film_v3/`. `build_data.py` reduces the run (contrasts and the qualification
verdict are copied from the pinned `summary.json`, world truth is checked against the manifest's world hashes).
`build.py` assembles one self-contained page and refuses to build if the data stops supporting any caption.
`record.mjs` steps the page frame by frame and encodes h264 + silent aac. Filed as artifact
`discussion-memory-v3-film` v1 (119.4 s, 1920x1080).

What the film reports, all measured in this run, six worlds, descriptive only:

- After one exchange of reports the planted value was held by a majority of agents in 4 of 6 attacked worlds.
- Parent wrong under attack: reports only 4/6, private work 1/6, public board 0/6, independent vote 0/6 (it
  abstains in all six because nothing reaches memory).
- Parent right under attack: board 5/6, private 3/6.
- Primary contrast (wrong parent, board minus private, net of clean, resolvable worlds): 0 in each of the three
  worlds. Safety contrast on ambiguous worlds: 0.
- Qualification not passed: clean full-evidence 2/6, clean reports-only votes 1/6, floor 5/6. Private and board
  arms reached the right clean vote in 6/6.

Surprise: in the worked world (20001) the agent holding the primary record endorsed the planted secondary value
after reading the other reports, then recovered it in round 1 of both continuations.

Not in the film: the 36 parent-memory fixtures (conflict: answered instead of abstaining in 6/6; correlated copies
counted as independent in 6/6), cost accounting, shadow's F1/F2 (no invalid ballots occurred in this run, so F1
does not change any vote metric here). The run's post-mortem stays with dmarz/discussion-bench-v3.
