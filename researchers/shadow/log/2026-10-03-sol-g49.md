## Evaluation design

The unit of independence is the operator/campaign, not an account, response or file. Group aliases and accounts by independently verified operator evidence, then hold out entire operators including all their accounts, descendants, prompts, near-duplicates, funding clusters and collection windows. Train/tune on remaining operators and evaluate against several independently built swarms, not only BotSim or sealed-swarm variants from one author. Add within-platform matched organic controls and legitimate automated/maintenance activity, including the platform creator's 2,328 linked agents as a labelled benign-system stratum rather than malicious positives.

Report temporal holdouts and collection/platform holdouts separately from operator holdouts. A new Moltbook collector is not a new independent swarm or operator. fox8 is one observed botnet and cannot alone support leave-one-operator-out estimation; BotSim's rows carry construction artifacts, not field labels. If operator identity is unknown, call the split collector/time/community-disjoint rather than operator-disjoint.

Preserve label provenance: verified platform takedown, manually reviewed botnet, synthetic construction, moderation/heuristic proxy, self-declared registry identity and incident inference are different levels. Separate AI authorship, autonomous agency, coordination, adversarial intent and operator attribution as tasks. Calibrate precision/recall/FPR and uncertainty on true controls, report PR-AUC at relevant base rates and per-operator macro averages, and evaluate timing/content/graph ablations. Avoid character_setting/row-order leakage, outage-correlated timing, changing engagement metadata and future moderation labels. Bootstrap uncertainty over operators, not millions of correlated events. Licence restrictions and no-training terms apply before model fitting or redistribution.
