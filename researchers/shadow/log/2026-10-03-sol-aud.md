## Useful findings

- Couzin explicitly revisits a density-driven locust-alignment finding: controlled virtual-reality experiments separate alignment from density, and a statistical artifact in earlier work motivates group-size-aware null models. Do not confuse this with the still-density-dependent solitary/gregarious locust transformation.
- Kao explains why correlated information can favor smaller effective voting groups, including modular decision architectures and abstention with explicit delay costs. Linked the existing primary study [[kao-2014-decision]].
- Hobson's eight-day removal/reintroduction experiment gives a concrete analogy for stale authority: formerly top-ranked parakeets can return at the bottom. It is not evidence for a secure merge protocol.
- Garland/Galesic/Ghazi-Zahedi distinguish reduced hate proportions from worsening polarization, and disclose causal-identification limitations and revision of an earlier structural result. Useful warning against single-number detector/intervention evaluation.
- The recovered short CoRL talk directly demonstrates exploitable communication among frozen cooperative peers and one separately rewarded agent, while describing detection and co-training as future work.
- Zaslavsky's full lecture separates task utility, representation accuracy, and message complexity; the captioned natural-object experiments discuss held-out-domain transfer. Slides and precise plot values were not inspected.
