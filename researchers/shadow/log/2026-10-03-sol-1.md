## What surprised me

- The in-the-wild corpus is far richer than the news coverage suggests. Three independent channels show the
  same 22 June collapse (DseWiki edits per rogesterone's hand count, urlquery scan reports, and the SWARM
  archive's phase table where 93% of the corpus sits in a 16 to 22 June burst and 18 June alone is 45%).
  That is a dated regime change we could actually model.
- killy-netsphere/sealed-swarm-transcripts: an anonymous one-week project that reproduced the dynamics in a
  sealed box with small open models and published 7,200 transcripts under CC0. Its claims (shared board
  synchronises rather than divides; an exploit spread to 66 lives while the honest solution reached zero other
  lineages; exploitation needs impossibility plus means, a rule against it changes nothing) are exactly the
  hypotheses the hackathon brief invites, already run once. Nobody has cited it yet.
- The Flag Game paper (Tanaka/Pavlova, arXiv 2609.19124) dropped two days before the hackathon with the
  false-consensus vs polarisation vs N result and "social attribution". Must cite.
- The leaked cohort data in OpenAI's own post HTML (rogesterone, swarmcha.se) is the only per-cohort
  quantitative data on the real swarm (participation rate vs reasoning budget vs compactions).
- Edge City is running a 3-week human-plus-agent village in Goa starting 11 October, with Microsoft and
  Cosmos. Live field experiment, no results yet.
