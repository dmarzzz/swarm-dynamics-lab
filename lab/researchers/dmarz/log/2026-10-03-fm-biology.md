**Surprised me.** Biology's defence against corrupted merging partners is almost never a k-of-n vote. It is a credential gate at the moment of fusion (Fu/HC, tgr genes, vic/het loci), plus population structure. The measured threshold-like result is Cortesi 2001: each mismatched vic locus independently cuts virus transfer (to 21 percent at vic2, no effect at vic4) and effects add roughly on the logit scale. The strongest attack shape is not aggressive: Neurospora cheaters lose the ability to initiate fusion, let healthy partners fuse into them, and end up over-represented in spores, but only while below about 30 percent frequency. In Botryllus the victim's body keeps its own genotype while its gonads are fully replaced, so a surface check passes. Ant parasite queens take on the killed host queen's chemical label during the takeover.
