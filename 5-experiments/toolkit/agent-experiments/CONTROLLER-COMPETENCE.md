Prospective guidance from Immune Response A8/A9 and the [AI Village fit review](../../studies/vishesh/ai-village-replay-2026-10-04/FIT.md). Apply where relevant; do not change frozen running experiments or other owners' policies. Source records with missing historical visibility or gold labels are design inspiration, not measured agent competence.

1. Define basic competence in the task's own consequences: preserve an already-good state, diagnose a visible fault, perform a necessary repair, verify the result. Include a real change-required case so “always wait” cannot pass, and a healthy case so “always intervene” cannot pass.
2. Separate factual diagnosis from action selection when the failure is ambiguous. Record both outputs, the exact observations and the executed action. A wrong diagnosis remains wrong; no hidden gold correction or action veto. A stage split changes architecture and token budget, so comparisons must name that confound.
3. Teach tool semantics explicitly. Inspect, refresh, retry, restart and deploy must have distinct documented effects and costs. Qualify the request schema separately from behavioral competence. Transport, schema, diagnosis, action selection and environment failures are separate statuses.
4. Construct controlled advice: absent, useful, stale and confidently incorrect. Keep visible evidence and initial state paired. Hold advice fields/source style constant when changing its truth or timestamp. Do not infer average collaborator quality from selected misleading notes.
5. Use several development families, including stale-but-alarming and stale-but-reassuring observations. Vary superficial identifiers and substantive fault/location/data combinations. Split related task/claim/artifact genealogies together. Seal holdouts before native evaluation; retain their hash and custody. Disclose shared generator ancestry. Once inspected or used for tuning, a case is development, even if its seed is new.
6. Freeze outcome thresholds, sample units, missingness, escalation and stopping before collection. All basic capability gates must pass before interpreting a larger collaboration comparison. Escalated models are distinct cohorts, not replacement observations for failed cases. Stronger-model success under a changed architecture does not isolate a model effect.
7. Map each run's visualization from evidence freshness → diagnosis → selected action → observed outcome. Mark missing cases and conditional unopened stages, unhealthy intervals, harmful interventions and useful repairs. Plot paired trajectories and retain replayable native traces; do not substitute attractive synthetic animations for measured outcomes.

## Expose unnecessary intervention and verification

Report unnecessary **proposals** and **executions**, rejected harmful actions, missed necessary repairs, actual post-repair verification and intervention costs alongside health or uptime. Preserve proposal → admission/rejection → executed action → observed state as separate records. A rejected action may consume a decision opportunity without changing the environment; disclose that cost model. A repair followed by another needless restart is not full preservation merely because the final state is healthy.

If an explicit public-evidence guard is the treatment, name it and retain a proposal-quality gate separate from the protected outcome. A guard's structural protection is not learned model competence. Count its denials and delays, and compare with a competent public-observation rule. If the guard never engages in native trajectories, report that limit rather than inferring a protective effect from similar good outcomes. Do not insert an undeclared veto into a controller experiment.

Verification requires an actual observation after the executed repair with the correct state/version/epoch. Earlier inspection, registry refresh, stated intent to verify and a healthy hidden simulator state are different facts. Show them separately. Retrospective harm analyses may supplement earlier runs but must retain their historical scores and identify new metrics as retrospective. The [C1 harm breakdown](../../studies/vishesh/immune-response-v3/controller-study/c1/reviews/c1-harm-breakdown.md) illustrates why green uptime can conceal unnecessary interventions; the [verification plan](../../studies/vishesh/immune-response-v3/verification-study/PLAN.md) freezes separate proposal and protection endpoints prospectively.

## Verification obligations and fallible tools

The [Immune Response verification-loop preparation](../../studies/vishesh/immune-response-v3/verification-loop/README.md) adds a reusable qualification pattern. When recovery or correction is the task, distinguish an acknowledged operation from verified success. Include successful, ineffective and delayed interventions where relevant, plus already-correct controls and genuine faults that require a different action. Document observable completion separately from the outcome; a completed job may still fail its objective.

Give every comparator an equal feasible tool horizon, including observation, necessary retry and final verification. Keep strict task outcomes fixed and explicitly version a changed horizon. Separately report the model's proposal, admission/enforcement, actual execution, and evidence supplied by architecture. An automatic probe is architecture-provided verification, even if the model also proposed it; it must not become evidence of learned initiative. Establish feasibility with a public-state comparator and sensitivity with policies that never repair, always intervene, or omit verification.

Apply this pattern when it fits the experiment's actual mechanism, without imposing a service-repair simulator on unrelated studies. New random aliases/seeds can support held-out fixture testing but do not create independent mechanisms or production realism. Seal evaluation separately, retain its custody/hash, and label shared-generator ancestry. Keep controlled advice downstream of the basic competence gate, and retain valid negative outcomes rather than scaling until a preferred result appears. These are prospective design checks, not retroactive launcher enforcement or permission to run another study.
