## Did the stronger model change the answer? Mostly no

The same 2,880 assignments (identical ids and packet hashes) were answered by Haiku 4.5 in [sybil-budget-api](../sybil-budget-api/RESULTS.md) (run `46ebda03`). Pairing by world (24 worlds, the independent unit):

- **Admission is identical.** Attacker seat share and honest specialist retention match exactly in every cell, because admission is decided before the model is called. The model only changes how admitted reports are synthesised.
- **Overall accuracy:** Sonnet minus Haiku specialist accuracy averaged **+5.7 pp** (world bootstrap 95% interval +1.2 to +10.0). Sonnet was higher in 102 of 120 cells and lower in 13. The gain is similar at every attacker pass rate (+4.3 to +6.9 pp).
- **Frontier unchanged.** The cells meeting the joint 90% accuracy / 5% attacker-seat engineering target are exactly the same as Haiku's: N324 random 32/64/108 and coverage 32; N972 random 64/108 and coverage 108; all at attacker pass 10%; none at 30-90%.
- **Primary contrast smaller, not distinguishable.** Coverage 108 minus 4 checks at N972, attacker pass 10%: Sonnet **+47.2 pp**, Haiku +56.9 pp; Sonnet minus Haiku -9.7 pp (interval -29.2 to +9.7). Sonnet's 4-check baseline is higher, so the gap to the ceiling is smaller.
- **Where Sonnet lost:** N972 coverage at 64 checks (-8 to -11 pp, intervals touching zero). **Where it gained most:** low-budget cells with admitted fabrications, e.g. N324 coverage/4 checks (+15 to +19 pp) and N972 random/16 checks at 30% (+16.7 pp).

Interpretation (inferred, not tested): in this instrument the verification budget and admission policy decide what evidence reaches the synthesiser; a stronger synthesiser recovers a few more facts from the same packets but does not move the security frontier. This matches the sybil-scale and sybil-newcomer Sonnet replications. ## Interpretation and evidence

The cell CSV and JSON report honest specialist retention, accuracy, attacker seats, paired policy differences, uncertainty, all denominators and cost. Identities and verification are scripted; only answer synthesis uses the API. Three specialist facts are highly repeated. The fixed graph, two seeds, +7 attack, single pinned model and independent simulated checks limit generalization. Observed threshold crossings can be unstable with24worlds. No formal S2 or held-out confirmatory claim is made.

The heatmaps contain final cell means; the GIF is measured completion progress, not agent interaction. Verify the retained inputs, grades, analyses and image artifacts before interpreting the report.
