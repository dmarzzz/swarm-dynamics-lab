# Visualization mapping v1: sybil-split-xmodel

Follows [RUN-VISUALIZATION.md](../../../toolkit/agent-experiments/RUN-VISUALIZATION.md). Renderer: `src/render.py` (PIL only, 1800×1200 RGB). Nothing has been rendered from a model run; the frames checked so far come from the scripted stage and say so.

- Mapping version / source: v1, part of the hashed source (`src/render.py`); pinned commit and source hash are in [reviews/chain-001-pre.md](reviews/chain-001-pre.md). It is the parent's mapping ([sybil-split-opus VISUALIZATION.md](../sybil-split-opus/VISUALIZATION.md)) unchanged except the run binding and the header label, which names the model and its route.
- Run binding: one hub run per stage and model, `sybil-split-xmodel/<run id>`, params `stage`, `batch` (`s0-001-<tag>`, `p0-001-<tag>`, `q0-001-<tag>`, `s1-001-<tag>`, tag `qwen` or `sol`), `model`, `source_hash`, `code`. Frames of the two models are separate runs and are never combined in one image. Each row binds graph family, root, identity count k, policy, check budget and attacker pass rate.
- Behavior to make visible: whether holding the same attacker resources with more identities raises wrong rare-skill answers, under which admission policy, and at what cost in attacker seats and lost specialists.
- Views: a live progress image during the stage, a final frame, and a replay over completion prefixes.

| Recorded signal / exact field | Unit, denominator or derivation | Visual encoding / legend / scale | Actor-visible or evaluator-only | Missing/failure display |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `evaluation.rare_wrong` | fraction of the 3 rare skills answered with a non-null value different from the truth; cell mean over completed roots | upper row of panels, y from 0 to 100%, x = identity count 1, 3, 9, 27 (evenly spaced); one line per policy, colour by policy (coverage teal, random gold, degree violet, no check gray); solid = 12 checks, dashed = 4 checks | evaluator-only | a cell with no completed root is not drawn; never zero |
| `scripted_evaluation.rare_wrong` | the same outcome for the row-plurality rule on the same packets | hollow squares on the degree and coverage lines at 12 checks | evaluator-only | not drawn when the cell is empty |
| `evaluation.rare_accuracy` against `admission.attacker_seat_share` | rare-skill accuracy (y, 0 to 100%) against attacker identities among the 54 seats (x, 0 to 25%, clipped), cell means at 12 checks (no-check baseline at 0) | lower row of panels; one path per policy through k = 1, 3, 9, 27, points labelled with k | accuracy evaluator-only; seat share evaluator-only (ownership is never shown to the model) | missing cell breaks the path |
| panel facets | graph family (`ring`, `community`) × attacker pass rate (10%, 90%) | four columns, same scales in every column | design labels | all four always drawn |
| primary contrast per root | (wrong at k = 27 minus k = 1 under degree) minus (the same under coverage), attacker pass 10%, 12 checks, from `analyze.contrast` | right strip, y from −2 to +2, one open circle per complete root, colour and column by family, white bar = family mean | evaluator-only | roots with a missing term are not plotted; the count of plotted roots is printed per family |
| primary estimate, interval, bounds | mean of the two family means; 95% percentile interval from 10,000 root-bootstrap draws; bounds over all assigned roots with missing outcomes at their extremes | text line under the panels | evaluator-only | "no root complete yet" until one root is complete |
| status counts | completed / failed / not started / pending out of the assigned total; elapsed wall seconds | header line | operational | always shown |
| `accounting` and ledger totals | stage calls, input and output tokens, settled dollars; study calls, settled dollars, dollars committed against the cap | footer line | operational | zeros before the first call |
| qualification cells (Q0, and the S0 fixtures) | per shape: packets completed of 10, field accuracy, exact packets, null rate on withheld facts, pass or not | text table (Q0 and P0 frames have no curves) | evaluator-only | counts show what is missing |

- Time axis: completion order. The replay cursor is the number of terminal calls; there is no within-model telemetry and no simulated time. Comparison across arms is by cell, not by time.
- Event markers: none inside a stage. In S0, P0 and Q0 a failed call stops dispatch; in S1 dispatch continues past failed calls up to 27; the header shows failed and not-started counts, and a billing pause is named in the hub progress message.
- Cadence and bounds: the progress image is re-rendered at most every 20 seconds while calls complete and once at the end; the replay has at most 33 frames (initial, 31 evenly spaced prefixes, final), 450 ms per frame and 2.5 s on the last; each frame is one 1800×1200 image. Rendering one frame takes well under a second and uses no experimental random stream. The bootstrap in the frame uses its own fixed seed.
- History for replay: `episodes.jsonl.gz` holds every row with `completion_index`, `elapsed_seconds` and the ledger totals at that moment; `assignments.jsonl.gz` and `worlds.jsonl.gz` hold the inputs. Any prefix can be re-rendered from them.
- Artifacts per run: `initial_frame.png`, `progress.png` (latest live frame), `final_frame.png`, `replay.gif`, and the data files. PNG and GIF are the publicly embeddable types; data files stay team-only. There is no interactive player; the GIF with the completed-count in its header is the supported form.
- Why a completion replay and not a time series: each assignment is one independent call; the only thing that evolves is how much evidence has been collected. The frame says so in its last line.
- Public-safe content: no secret, address or credential is drawn. Truth, ownership and fabricated values appear only as aggregate outcomes, never in anything the model receives. Policies are distinguished by colour and by legend text; budgets by line style.
- Scripted frames carry the label "SCRIPTED PLURALITY RULE - NOT MODEL EVIDENCE" in the header.
- Synthetic-trace validation (selftest): frames for an empty run, a partial run with one failed and many not-started rows, a complete scripted run, empty and complete qualification and probe stages, and a run with the truetype fonts unavailable; plotted cell means compared with an independent recomputation; the replay GIF decoded frame by frame.
- Acceptance checks after a run: the final frame's primary estimate and cell means equal `analysis.json`; the header counts equal `summary.json`; every GIF frame decodes; `chain.py verify` confirms the uploaded image checksums.
- Rendering failure policy: a rendering or upload error during the stage is recorded in `summary.json` under `reporting_errors` and does not change any row or the stage outcome; if the final frames cannot be rendered they are absent from the hub and `chain.py verify` reports it; an artifact upload the hub does not acknowledge ends the stage as failed with its rows preserved on disk. The operator who closes the run owns the visual audit in the post-mortem.
