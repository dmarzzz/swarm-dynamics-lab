# Direction for the Swarm of Theseus film

dmarz asked on 2026-10-04, right after the narrated market-split film: "make one more for the ship of thesues work
by vishes". No new sketch round was held. The direction is the one he gave for the market-split film the same day
("a youtube video narrating an agent society run", the point of view, "a bunch of simulations then zoom into that
one"), applied to vishesh's study `5-experiments/studies/vishesh/swarm-of-theseus`.

## What the film shows

- Source: the completed turnover pilot, attempt S1-a1 (36 runs, 864 model calls, Claude Haiku 4.5). The later v2
  design and its diagnostics have not run a turnover experiment, so they get one closing line each and no pictures.
- The wall: all 36 runs, one tile each. A tile shows the three crew slots, the four cases and the receipt phrase at
  every step.
- The run it enters: seed bank, world 200, notes plus one question. It is the first scenario and the first seed, and
  was fixed before any run's records were read. Its neighbour is the same world with nothing handed down.
- The point of view: the seeded notebook, a newcomer's question, the departing founder's answer and the team's
  notebooks, and what the newcomer returned, all copied from the saved requests and responses.
- One metaphor: a crew slot is a plank, white while a founder holds it and teal once it has been replaced.

## Whose work this is

The study, its design, its runs and its review are vishesh's. This film only reads his committed results and the
filed evidence archive. It changes nothing in his folder. The limits in the last part of the narration are taken
from his own critical review and evidence rating, and `make_film.py` checks the narrated facts against his records.

## Deliberately left out

- The four qualification attempts before the pilot, the cost and token accounting, and the bootstrap interval.
- The observatory and repair-dock procedures in detail. They appear on the wall and in two result lines.
- Any claim about culture, identity or emergence. The study itself declines them.

## Carried over from the market-split film

Spoken narration with matching subtitles and a synthetic voice (Kokoro `af_heart`); more than 120 seconds. Both
depart from the film playbook's defaults on dmarz's earlier instruction.
