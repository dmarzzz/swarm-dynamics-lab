# Amendment 02 — peer messaging comparison

**Astra Ultra · 4 October 2026 · approved for plan publication by dmarz before any experiment ran.**

This amendment replaces the prospective v1 dose ladder with the four-condition design discussed with the owner. The owner requested: “okay push this experiment plan and give me the link”. This is publication of a design, not a model run or a claim of launch readiness.

The [original v1 plan](https://github.com/dmarzzz/swarm-lab/blob/88faf68173b29a4874ea87565ce366b02f32579c/researchers/dmarz/notes/growth-pressure-200/PLAN.md) and [filed v1 document](https://github.com/dmarzzz/swarm-lab/blob/88faf68173b29a4874ea87565ce366b02f32579c/artifacts/growth-pressure-200-plan/growth-pressure-200-plan-v1.md) remain preserved. No observations, qualifications, attempts or costs are discarded or pooled: both versions are unrun.

## What changes

| Item | V1 | V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Treatments | 0, 1, 4 assigned evaders; optional messaging throughout | A: 0/off; B: 0/on; C: 4/off; D: 4/on |
| Shared opening | Five rounds, all ordinary | Five rounds, all ordinary, messaging off |
| Communication | Optional message | Explicit send/pass choice; common neutral invitation; channel enabled only in B/D after the fork |
| Scientific question | Coarse seeder dose comparison | Seeding effect plus whether messaging changes that effect |
| Primary | Four versus zero assigned evaders | D − B: four versus zero with messaging on |
| Secondary central contrast | One versus zero | (D − B) − (C − A) |
| Economy batches | 4 | 3 |
| Independent paired markets | 16 | 12 |
| Focals / fixed ordinary owners per arm | 32 / 736 | 24 / 552 |
| Continuations / workers | 12 / 12 | 12 / 12 |
| Main decisions | 52,000 | 51,000 |
| Qualification decisions | 3,296 | 3,096 |
| Planned decisions / maximum transport attempts | 55,296 / 55,849 | 54,096 / 54,637 |

Each economy still has 200 persistent owners in four isolated 50-owner markets, with 20 continuation rounds and the same growth, levy, ownership and profit rules. The one-hour execution target remains eight minutes qualification, 44 minutes main collection and eight minutes closeout, conditional on fresh resource and throughput admission. Fewer independent markets mean less precision; the amendment does not claim this interaction is well powered.

## Communication contract

All owners receive the same neutral instruction to consider asking questions, sharing observations or discussing strategy when the channel is available. Sending is optional; there is no recruitment reward or instruction to spread the exploit. Do not insert the suggested enforcement argument into experimental messages. The model may independently produce it, warn against it, refuse to share, or remain silent.

Messaging-off agents still see market prices, firm ownership, outputs, profits, levy and growth. This is an explicit peer-message comparison, not a no-social-information condition. In on arms, messages are sent within the existing decision call and delivered next round before recipients act. The full message stream, delivery/drop records, passes and counterexamples remain available for analysis. Particular quotations are descriptive traces, not proof of causal mediation.

## Clarification of a previously implicit turn boundary

A newly registered firm enters the next round empty and becomes eligible for investment or transfer then. New or transferred capacity arrives one round after that action. Existing resources and decision budgets remain attached to the owner. This formalizes the discussed register → transfer → produce sequence without creating an extra agent or a free asset.
