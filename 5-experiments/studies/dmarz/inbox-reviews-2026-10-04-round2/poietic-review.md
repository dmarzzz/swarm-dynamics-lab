## Required clarifications before implementation/freeze

**P1 — Make adaptation delay part of the stated service estimand.** PROTOCOL gives jobs a 120-second deadline from release and gives proposals separate boundary slots, but does not define release times relative to those slots. A self-paced implementation could spend 120 seconds reconfiguring, release the next jobs afterward, and report all ten-second answers on time. With arrivals due at the boundary, the same deployment misses deadlines. Dollar charging alone does not fix that difference.

Freeze an exogenous arrival schedule common across arms, and let proposal/compilation/migration downtime create queueing and deadline misses; preserve the identical logical source snapshot per job. Alternatively explicitly study self-paced batches, report complete elapsed time/throughput including every boundary and startup, and narrow claims about on-time service accordingly. Do not let A3 choose arrival times by how long it deliberates. Add a manual boundary-delay acceptance case, including delayed/unstarted jobs, and account for observer/rendering time consistently rather than pausing only one arm's clock.

**P2 — Specify a reuse opportunity rather than a topology target.** The shared-cache control, optional calls, empty service directory, exogenous endpoint state, and useful no-overlap cases are good protections against forced redundancy. However, an 80% repeated-key target does not specify how often useful *derived* work repeats after A1's coherent cache has already removed duplicate fetches. Nor is the overlap denominator unambiguous: distinct keys versus required-key occurrences can produce different percentages.

Before generating reserved roots, give hand-authored public examples showing (a) raw fetch reuse fully captured by A1, (b) repeated normalization/join work that a reusable typed service could eliminate, and (c) no useful reuse. Define key/occurrence denominator, snapshot/version equivalence and cross-epoch reuse precisely. Put every relevant generic primitive and its cost in a shared menu for A1/A2/A3/A4/A6, with the same opportunity for zero-model cache/procedure operations. Success must not require any predetermined provider count or graph. These fixtures need not show A3 winning; they establish what, if anything, it can improve over a competently implemented cache/static baseline. If all benefit is caching, report that result and reframe accordingly.

**P3 — Complete the prospective decision and comparator contract before S1/S2.** Recovery requires two epochs meeting registered quality/deadline requirements, but no numeric S1 recovery threshold is supplied; the later S2 95% margin and the S0 44/48 screen are different quantities. Define the S1 diagnostic threshold and feasibility advance/stop criteria before opening roots, including what happens when no useful changes occur. Choose these as engineering criteria, not post-hoc cutoffs that guarantee progress. Freeze per-lineage and static-construction quotas under the 8,000-call total so early arms cannot consume later arms' allowance.

S1's A0–A3 comparison is legitimate feasibility work only. Before an optimizer/efficiency claim, include the already-promised prior-informed prune/replace and trace-repair candidates in the actual development selection menu, or record their consolidation into A2/A4. The current selector explicitly names only A1/A2/A4/A6. A4's union of legally shareable observations must be obtained through defined, charged collection with explicit visibility; it must not silently read private actor history. This assesses local versus central organization, not equal information at each individual decision.

## Fairness and measurement assessment

The cost contract is substantially sound: actual dollars, physical resources and synthetic tolls are separate; failed, rejected, retried, migrated and unstarted work stays accounted for; `C/all assigned jobs` is gated jointly with verified success; zero-success cost per success is infinity. Keep cache lookup/model bypass real, actual cheap-backend execution verifiable, and loaded-tool removal enforced by adapters. Fixture endpoint toll savings cannot be labelled realized dollar savings. All candidates need cold-start totals and the same fixed-horizon amortization treatment, including prior-informed optimization. Human engineering time stays separately visible.

A3/A5 correctly shares a pre-shock acquisition history and charges the whole prefix to each hypothetical deployment while counting its physical bill once. Retain their dependence in inference. Specify whether A5 makes any structural proposal calls after freezing: do not force it to buy useless rejected proposals unless this is an explicit frozen-policy estimand, and do charge A3's continuing adaptation. A2's declared fallback and A5's ordinary routing/cache behavior remain active. A3-versus-A5 is the appropriate conditional test of continued structural adaptation; the initial pilot does not run A5 and cannot make that causal claim.

The distinction between exogenous actor loss and targeted withdrawal of A3's learned provider is correct. Preserve matched target withdrawal only for its A5 child, distinguish removal from rescue, and do not combine that intervention with S1 overlap/schema shocks. Four roots, two per shock, cannot establish deployment reliability or broad task-family generality. Jobs, epochs, agents and copied children must not inflate replication counts.

The S0 threshold of 44/48 with 48/48 valid and no protected access is an engineering screen, not proof of 95% success. Different candidate contracts should stay separate. Before the first native lineage, qualify the actual tool/service/proposal interface and recovery behavior, using bounded disjoint fixtures; single-choice success cannot substitute for a multi-turn lifecycle check. Model route, source, grammar and settings must be frozen; missing identities/authorization currently correctly block calls rather than invalidate prospective design work.

S2's conjunctive cost-ratio, quality noninferiority and absolute-quality bounds are coherent as an intersection-union rule. Margins are practical choices, not empirical facts. Preserve joint power planning, paired root-level dependence, disjoint baseline selection and holdout, and uncertainty about variance estimated from only four roots. The affordable outcome may be development-only evidence. A null, overhead-dominated result or competent fixed baseline winning is informative and must not trigger repeated unchanged attempts until success.

## Prior work and the surviving question

I opened primary sources directly. This is a focused overlap check, not a full literature/saturation certification:

- [AgentSlimming](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08813v1): inspected the structural prune/cheap-model-replacement account and appendix break-even treatment. Efficiency from pruning/replacement and amortizing optimization is already explicit prior work. A fair adaptation needs a development-trained frozen comparator and its optimization ledger; an exact replication is a separate undertaking.
- [MANTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28527): abstract explicitly includes inference-time changes to roles, links, order, information visibility and validation. Dynamic topology alone is not the remaining contribution. Methods-level comparison still needs completion before a novelty claim; I did not certify a faithful implementation.
- [Adaptive Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09742): abstract describes runtime specialist creation and resource-driven eviction. Specialization and changing capability allocation alone are not new. Poietic's fixed population and reversible, metered data-service behavior are the narrower proposed distinction.
- [SafeSieve](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40236): publisher abstract describes progressive communication pruning informed by semantic initialization and experience. Fewer messages/edges is insufficient novelty; the relevant question concerns useful services, adaptation overhead and recovery beyond pruning.

My inference from these overlaps is to frame this initially as a comparative engineering study of persistent workloads, reversible capabilities and honest break-even behavior. If a closest method already covers that combination, replicate or extend it explicitly. Do not market a novel optimizer before the promised methods comparison and competitive development baselines exist. No library read-depth fields were upgraded by this review.
