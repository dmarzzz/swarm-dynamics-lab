**2. Randomization addresses the trace objection only for the intervention actually varied.** Randomizing injection identifies an assignment effect under the design's assumptions. It does not alone identify transmission through peers. In the old proposal, P2 minus P1 can include access to others' evidence, additional inference and social presentation. Keep unexposed recipients' private judgments and final outcomes separate from the scout's. A replay with a claim removed measures the effect of that deletion under the replay policy; it does not establish that nothing else contributed.

The successor's shared-prefix chair forks answer a more defensible question: the effect of displaying preliminary choices/confidences, conditional on the same recorded evidence and checks. That is an intervention on the displayed recommendation package, not proof of a psychological conformity mechanism. ## Changes needed if the old proposal is revived

**The Scheme C brake rejects unanimity for every finite positive root count.** It requires `C <= C*`, where `C* = 1 - 1/(1 + kappa*N_eff)`. For finite positive `kappa*N_eff`, `C* < 1`, but a unanimous point distribution has `C = 1`. At `kappa=1` and four equally represented roots, `C*=0.8`; unanimous correct evidence still cannot commit. This is an algebraic consequence of the written rule, not a simulation result. Add clean-unanimity, unavailable-new-evidence and legitimate-superior-target cases before treating the brake as useful. Choose a rule that tests support sufficiency without forcing correct consensus to become less confident, and measure abstention cost explicitly.

**Root concentration is not proof of independent evidence.** The written `N_eff` is an effective count over assigned root labels. Equal counts on four labels yield four whether their content is independently obtained or copied. Keep oracle ancestry, observable provenance and inferred provenance as separate inputs. Include common-owner false negatives, unrelated-source false merges and incomplete citations. The proposal already names these controls, but its “deployable mechanism” claim should wait for results without privileged labels. The successor's observable same-publisher syndication comparison is narrower and better specified.

**Do not import robustness guarantees into the weighted probability rule.** Trimming the scaled vectors `w_i*p_i` changes their order and geometry; normalizing afterward does not establish a theorem for that estimator. Specify trimming and tie rules, empty evidence sets, zero totals and what the fault count means when legitimate agents share false evidence. The cited [Yin et al. primary abstract](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/yin18a.html), re-opened for this review, concerns robust distributed gradient descent, not this probability aggregator. [[yin-2018-byzantine]] Treat the proposed rule as a heuristic unless its own assumptions and guarantee are established.

**Resolve the sample and endpoint contradictions before preregistration.** The old protocol table describes five assessments, the primary contrast fixes `N=32`, and the minimal pilot uses five agents. W8 is called co-primary while the primary block names only W3; the metric and power sections also call regret primary. Select one executable pilot contract and identify primary, safety and exploratory endpoints. The 35-task power illustration depends on an assumed large effect and within-task correlation; it is a planning approximation, not established power. Plain McNemar analysis over all repeated seeds would ignore clustering. Use task-level paired analysis consistent with the sampling scheme; any later power calculation should reflect that exact estimator and dependence. The successor's six authored pairs support descriptive case-level reporting, not a population significance claim.

**Remove literature outcome matching as an acceptance gate.** The old plan requires rerunning above a borrowed 65.2% experiment-recognition rate and treats failure to reproduce another paper's topology ordering as proof the harness is wrong. Those are external empirical outcomes, not engineering invariants for this task. Freeze locally justified competence and validity rules, record recognition diagnostically, and retain valid contrary results. Only demonstrated implementation or qualification defects justify repair. The successor's preservation of adverse Q2 results is the right practice.
