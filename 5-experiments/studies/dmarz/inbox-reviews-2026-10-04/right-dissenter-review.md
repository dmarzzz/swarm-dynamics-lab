# Right Dissenter RD-1 review

Reviewer: dmarz/inbox-design-feedback, 2026-10-04 UTC. **REVISE.** The offline prototype is useful, but its source identity and temporal closure contracts need repair. This does not approve RD-2's live adapter or a scientific launch. Reviewed revision and file hashes: [receipt](package-receipt.json). Independent reproductions: [script](check_dissent.py), [results](dissent-checks.json).

## Findings and acceptance

**RD-R1 — repeated evidence can buy another check (blocking protocol defect).** `src/protocol.py:validate_challenge` sorts a list of lineage tuples without removing duplicates. Start with development bridge seed 1101 and a two-check ledger. Resolve citation `e4`, then add a second ID containing exactly the same root/scope/revision/time/text and cite both IDs. The repeated packet is admitted and consumes the remaining check: two checks for one observation. Renaming a single ID is covered by author tests; adding aliases is not. Canonicalize the lineage as a set of observation identities, and define treatment of same-source conflicting reports separately. Acceptance: alias count and order cannot change the fingerprint or consume another check; a genuinely new observation or task revision can reopen.

**RD-R2 — suppressing a supported repeat discards its established resolution.** The ledger stores a closed fingerprint but not its decision. `step` starts from the original majority; on `already_closed` it returns that majority unchanged. In the same development bridge case, first result HOLD is supported and correct; identical replay returns PROCEED, marked `suppressed_repeat`, with no new evidence. Store and reuse the applicable closure result (with scope/revision/freshness rules), or explicitly carry a revised decision into the next epoch. Test supported repeats as well as withdrawn repeats. The delivered three-frame alarm happens to withdraw first, so it masks this defect. This blocks a general temporal recovery claim; the narrowly scripted false-alarm trajectory still behaves as documented.

**RD-R3 — separate development tests from reserved qualification generation.** Full discovery currently includes `test_live.py`, which invokes `qualification()` and the mocked Q0 worker on reserved 2100-series seeds. I ran the advertised offline suite before noticing this: 49 tests passed, no native calls, and temporary mocked outputs were not retained or used for tuning. Thus this audit cannot be described as wholly blind to the qualification test path. My independent reproductions use only development seeds. Split development/mocked integration fixtures from the reserved manifest and make a safe review command explicit. Document this exposure before asserting freshness; preserve any historical attempt rather than relabeling it. No holdout seed was generated by the reviewed test paths, and I did not inspect a holdout corpus.

## Hand-worked cases and controls

- Bridge: four endorsements share `survey-root`; they are one provenance source, not four independent measurements. A fresh applicable check says the load-bearing support is severed, so HOLD; the nominal four-to-one PROCEED vote is wrong. One check supports dissent.
- Build: the sample's failing record concerns a different object, so its challenge is inapplicable. No check is spent; the correct PROCEED majority remains. The general applicable-failure variant must HOLD, while stale/wrong-version evidence cannot establish that requirement.
- Alarm: the first independent sample is within range, so PROCEED and withdraw. Identical repeat is suppressed, using no additional check. The new observation at tick 3 reopens; the tick-4 outside-range sample requires HOLD. Expected actions are PROCEED, PROCEED, HOLD and check counts 1,0,1. This verifies the prescribed trajectory, not arbitrary histories.

The actor whitelist and deep copies exclude gold, condition names and future truth; an independent gold/metadata mutation leaves the actor packet unchanged. Verification is acquired separately and may be wrong or unavailable. Missing, late and invalid decisions stay failures/DEFER in the assigned denominator. The offline executor retains assignments and offers interrupted-attempt reconciliation; normal policy failures do not silently revert to a successful majority. The ledger's one/two-check caps hold even with RD-R1, but copies can waste the scarce quota.

## Scientific scope

Keep always-check and exact-reference as decisive comparators. ExactReference always admits a valid check and parses a few literal phrases; evidence-gate using it is not learned selectivity. Pooled evidence tests whether integration alone is sufficient. Matched-random needs a development-frozen quota; equal potential observation tapes are not equal realized information or costs. Report calls, checks and failure-inclusive outcomes jointly, charge private-vote collection and distinguish counterfactual arm costs from actual cached spend. A closed source is not a permanently distrusted speaker.

The native contribution is unestablished on this controlled grammar. Before a broader claim, freeze a independently authored semantic development/qualification corpus with paraphrase, negation, mixed evidence, required versus optional tests, version/platform mismatches, and both safe/unsafe truth directions. Retain the deterministic parser and always-check baselines, natural no-dissenter/unanimous cases, and corruption as well as correction. Private votes/challenges scripted by the generator measure reaction to dissent, not its spontaneous emergence.

I reopened the original abstracts of [Minority Sentinel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29270) and [withheld dissent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38324). The former already targets safe minority overturns with a learned classifier; the latter models withholding/reconsideration and tests discussion gains. These are real close precedents, not a novelty audit. A bounded acquisition/withdrawal protocol may be a narrower contribution, but it needs methods-level comparison and the lab's remaining literature gate. No biological equivalence, completed survey or native efficacy is established by this review.
