# QM-PQ-01: native packet qualification

2026-10-04. Prospective qualification only. The owner directed preparation and execution of this qualification after the packet-v2 readiness assessment. No independent researcher review required. Evaluation is not launched automatically or included in this attempt. Historical native Q1 failure is preserved; this is a new model/interface cohort, not a repaired favorable retry.

## TLDR

Test whether one pinned Sonnet 4.6 solver can correctly read authenticated source receipts and preserve source-majority decisions under repeated or inverted reports. Use all 24 sealed v2 qualification packets (six scenario roots, four matched conditions), with the same-input deterministic parser as comparator. Measure valid output, source-vote accuracy, decision correctness, report-distortion identification, quoted-source evidence and cost. This is a controlled finite synthetic qualification, not a swarm-benefit or field-reliability claim.

## Question and prediction

A qualified solver correctly identifies all three source votes and final source-majority choice regardless of copied report counts. Visible source quotations and reported source votes localize mistakes without requesting hidden reasoning. Model success means suitability for this exact contract only. Failure stops progression and yields a full trace-based diagnosis; no automatic prompt repair, model switch, retry or evaluation.

## Setup

Use the frozen packet-study v2 qualification corpus; verify its SEAL/FREEZE hashes. Do not inspect or deploy the 96 evaluation packets. Six roots span polarity, time, entity, units, plans versus observations and correction precedence; target decisions are balanced 12/12. Four within-root conditions cross one/five focal copies and faithful/inverted focal claims. Sources and other reports remain identical. Actor context contains only the frozen packet plus a fixed formatting instruction and one explicit source-first procedure; no owner/operator history, gold labels, prior answers, demonstrations or tools.

Pinned model: anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6 via OpenRouter, Anthropic provider only, no fallbacks, temperature 0, max output 768 tokens, stateless serial calls. Validate live route support and price <=$3/million input and $15/million output before launch. No paid compatibility probe outside the 24-call denominator. This selects a capable model while keeping the original cumulative cap; it does not promise success or comparability with historical Jev scores.

## Protocol

Freeze exact request bytes and allowed IDs before dispatch; publish manifest hashes without exposing evaluation cases. Public immutable plan and condition TLDRs must be registered and verified. Reserve each call before transport in the original sole-authority budget ledger. Use current exclusive allocation in the verified approved account, verified source/runtime and a 30-minute session ceiling. No new VM if eligible capacity exists.

For each packet request: decision ONE/ZERO/DEFER; boolean source_votes keyed by the three receipt IDs; verbatim source_quotes keyed by receipt ID; distorted_report_ids. Structured output schema rejects extra fields, wrong IDs, duplicates and excessive lengths. Source quotes must be literal substrings of their source; quotes are diagnostic evidence, not hidden reasoning. The source votes and final decision are scored against sealed gold locally after receipt retention. Report-fidelity scoring is separate from final-choice accuracy.

Qualification gate: 24/24 valid and accounted responses; all six clean one-copy faithful packets correct in final decision and all source votes; at least 23/24 correct final decisions overall; at least 70/72 correct source votes; at least 23/24 exact distorted-report sets; and all supplied quotes pass literal-source validation. These thresholds cannot be changed after outputs. Complete the bounded 24 assignments after valid wrong answers to retain balanced contrasts. Stop immediately on transport, model/provider drift, invalid response, accounting, source/admission failure, deadline or budget limit. Mark every remaining assignment unstarted. No automatic evaluation even on pass.

## Metrics

Retain assigned/started/terminal/valid/graded/unstarted separately; wrong, DEFER and invalid are distinct. Main endpoints are final-choice and source-vote correctness. Report clean and each 2x2 condition, per-root paired differences, exact distortion sets and quote validity. All six roots remain separate from 24 correlated calls; no population confidence, rare-error or model-superiority claim.

Trace each pretransport dispatch intent, exact credential-free request body/hash, timestamps, model/provider/options, source/corpus hashes, safe returned content, usage, provider generation ID, finish reason, latency, parsed fields and deterministic scoring. Retain accounting before validating returned content. HTTP errors keep only status/type and safe identifiers; never credentials, headers, private operator messages or arbitrary error dumps. Persist rejected output within its bounded safe content when available, never erase it. No tools or hidden reasoning are configured; unavailable provider/wire metadata stays unavailable.
