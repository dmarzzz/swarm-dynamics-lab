## Question and prediction
Can a single literal-clause representation retain evidence for both observations and explicit non-observations without requiring the model to switch to null? All15 SP-01 misses were the same representation mismatch; every quoted plan decoded safely to unknown/null and DEFER, with no false evidence promotion. Source-plan quotations were present in the raw responses, not inferred from missing traces. The nullable-schema/model preference mechanism is not established, and no HTTP error occurred.

The repair removes only the actor's null-selection branch. Sources and reports both return `{id,quote:string}`. For sources, choose the corrected/current query-matching observation when available; if the only relevant statement is a plan, quote that full plan clause including Plan:. For reports, retain complete asserted, hedged or planned statements as before. The decoder derives mode and treats source plans/hedges as unknown/null. It never substitutes a better clause, promotes plans to evidence, or invents a source observation. Prediction: this aligns the extraction request with the evidence retained while preserving source-grounded correctness.

SP-01 remains a failed25/40 exact-packet qualification under its original rule. A retrospective re-interpretation would not prove native adherence to the revised contract; fresh collection is necessary. Pass supports only this authored interface. Misses identify a selection boundary; a transport/contract error stops. No further paid loop merely to obtain a pass.
