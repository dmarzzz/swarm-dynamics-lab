## Question and prediction

For a frozen benchmark of source-linked, multi-document evidence decisions, does peer exchange improve exact target accuracy relative to equal-turn private reconsideration without increasing unsupported assertions? The primary practical decision is whether a source-aware review step merits its latency and coordination overhead after every reviewer has the same evidence. Possible improvements arise from identifying missed qualifiers, dates or incompatible scopes; possible harm arises from persuasive shared errors. No benefit is assumed, and no single scenario supplies independent n.

One/six-agent comparison is secondary and deliberately added-capacity. The primary6-agent peer/private comparison shares exact initial states and matches call/output entitlement. Do not infer a model-strength effect from historical Sonnet cohorts or a60-agent scaling law from this study.

## Protocol and agent/context contract

Model exactly GPT-6 Sol, medium reasoning, pinned provider and served revision where available; no alias/model fallback. All agents receive the same complete evidence, no evaluator labels, original annotated answers, operator conversation, previous cases or knowledge of arm scores. Freeze a deterministic document-order schedule balanced across agents. Source/context hashes, model settings, schemas and evidence-document groups are recorded per request. Any required evidence exceeding the4000-input-token cap blocks the packet; it is not silently truncated.

Per event/execution: one single-context reader receives an initial turn and a private reconsideration (2 calls). Six additional reviewers each produce an initial answer (6 calls); each initial state then forks into peer exchange and private reconsideration (12 continuations). Total20 calls. Each fork retains exactly the same first-turn answer and evidence. A deterministic peer board groups document-linked claims, retains contradictory statements and reports omitted categories; it must not collapse different claims merely because they share a document. No evaluator or gold-derived coordinator. Private review asks for counterarguments and checks with the same output allowance. Equal entitlement is not equal realized reasoning tokens; report actual compute/latency.

Every answer contains four-class verdict, confidence, citation IDs and literal spans, unresolved issue and short justification. Literal citation validation is separate from semantic entailment. Aggregation is frozen plurality with tie abstention, and incomplete seat counts remain visible; all six final replies are required for a complete committee endpoint. No missing reply is converted into a vote. No retry or replacement runs; systemic transport, accounting, source or isolation failures stop the stage and preserve all partial records.

Q-B32:8 separate qualification events ×20 calls =160. Validate the exact native peer/private continuation contract. All calls must be accounted and schema/citation valid; single-context and private committee must each answer at least7/8 correctly, including at least one justified uncertainty case. Do not demand a favorable peer effect as a qualification gate: a harmful but faithfully executed peer condition is potential scientific evidence, not an automatic repair target. Any mechanism with an unanswerable or mislabeled clean case blocks comparison until fixed prospectively.

E-B32-R1 and E-B32-R2:32 frozen evaluation clusters ×20 calls each =640 calls/execution,1280 total. Both independent executions are preassigned and required regardless of the first scientific outcome. The second starts fresh conversations and model calls, preserving source/model/interface and evidence ordering; do not replay or resume first-run responses. Hosted seeds do not guarantee determinism. Repetition estimates execution variability on the same32 events; it does not double independent n. If source/provider changes, report a new configuration, not same-contract replication.

## Metrics and sample precision

Primary endpoint: event-level difference in exact target accuracy between peer6 and private6, averaged over the two executions, with an event-cluster bootstrap95% interval and complete paired rows. Primary independent n is32 conditional on completed event audit, not64 or thousands of agent outputs. Report all-assigned denominators, arm missingness and worst/best missing-outcome bounds. A complete-pair analysis is secondary when any outcomes are missing.

Secondary: single-context comparator; literal citation validity; blinded semantic support audit of every final condition result; confidently wrong rate; dissent retention; revisions toward/away from truth; share of failures propagated by peers; cost/latency. The owning-agent semantic audit is disclosed and cannot replace independently published labels. Neither a correct label with a fabricated span nor a literal quote with an incorrect interpretation is called fully grounded.

Replication report: per-arm accuracy in each fresh execution; paired difference per execution; per-event verdict agreement; sign and magnitude stability; confidence calibration changes; missingness. Show stable wrong outcomes as failures, not reproducibility successes. Predeclared instability flag: the peer-minus-private effect changes sign between executions and at least one absolute effect is >=0.10, or any arm has <0.80 per-event verdict agreement. Report exact counts and intervals even if the flag is not triggered; a non-trigger does not prove stability. A triggered flag makes the outcome unstable/inconclusive rather than averaging it away.

Precision sensitivity uses the discordant-root rate q for paired binary outcomes, not agent count. For n32 and q0.10/0.25/0.50, a simple normal half-width is approximately0.110/0.173/0.245 before repeat covariance. This is a planning approximation, not a promised power calculation. The study can establish a reproducible benchmark result or a substantial effect, but often cannot exclude a10-percentage-point advantage. Predeclare10pp as the practical benefit margin: a robust no-material-benefit conclusion requires the upper interval below+0.10, competent controls and stable fresh-run findings. Otherwise a null is inconclusive, not proven equivalence. Publish actual intervals even when wide.

A stable adverse effect is a useful negative if the controls and labels pass; a deterministic-parser ceiling only supports the restricted grammar. Neither failed qualification nor a one-run null constitutes the requested scientific baseline. Success means credible cases, reproducible measurement and interpretable stable performance, not necessarily peer superiority.
