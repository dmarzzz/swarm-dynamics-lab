## Question and decision

Does an instruction to account for shared sources improve committee decisions specifically when copies are uneven, and does any improvement survive incomplete ancestry? A useful outcome must beat the standard instruction in underlying accuracy, not merely produce a favorable interaction. Compare costs with a pooled solver and deterministic provenance rules before recommending more judges.

S0 established limited reader competence. Its post-hoc references show that complete, reliable IDs make the simple task exactly solvable by code. S1 therefore tests the missing model instruction comparison and the information boundary, not whether deduplication arithmetic works. No semantic/domain-transfer claim is available from this pilot.

## Paired design and assignment

Use six new world seeds 41000–41005, with q=.65 on even seeds and q=.80 on odd seeds. Do not generate or inspect worlds before gate completion and immutable registration. Each world samples binary truth with probability 1/2 and three conditionally independent root bits of accuracy q. Retain every sampled world, including easy unanimous ones; never replace worlds to produce a desired effect. Seeds identify worlds, not independent judges. Six worlds is a feasibility allocation with poor precision, not a powered efficacy study.

For each world cross balanced (3,3,3) versus skewed (7,1,1) copies, full versus partial ancestry, and standard versus source-aware instructions: eight committee trajectories. Select the favored root uniformly independently of truth; select two concealed roots independently of truth and favored root. Pair the same latent observations, favored root and mask across arms. Retain nine reports and three actual roots in every arm. Do not disclose the hidden root count or graph in partial-lineage requests. Actor-visible reliability and prior remain identical. Freeze deterministic RNG/version and serialization in the eventual manifest before dispatch.

Five scheduled judges each make one round-0 decision, then one round-1 decision after seeing all five round-0 slots. Barrier scheduling prevents round-1 leakage. No persistent memory, tools or rationales; raw peer choices and choice scores only, failed slots explicitly unavailable. Judge report-order permutations are paired across instruction arms. No new observation enters during discussion. Commit only with at least three of five votes for ZERO or ONE; otherwise DEFER. Failures never reduce the quorum.

Standard instruction assesses reports and peer choices. Source-aware instruction additionally says to count visible roots once, treat peer endorsements as no fresh measurement and acknowledge incomplete ancestry. Both arms receive the same facts, schema, call and output-token limits. Freeze literal prompts in the manifest; validate that differences are confined to the intended instruction. This is a prompt package contrast, not a blinded model or compute-matched causal decomposition.

## Comparators and measures

Each repetition × lineage cell also gets one standard pooled solver with an initial decision and a self-review decision, without peers: four trajectories, eight calls per world. Round-0 committees supply a five-call no-discussion diagnostic without additional dispatches. Deterministic count-all, visible-root deduplication and capped-opaque references follow QM-2; the exact true-root oracle is evaluator-only when ancestry is hidden. No oracle field reaches actors.

Primary outcome: correct committed round-1 decisions against sampled world truth / all six assigned worlds per arm. DEFER and execution-unavailable slots count as no correct commitment and remain separately reported. Within each lineage condition report the paired world-average interaction:

(source-aware − standard correctness under skewed copies) − (source-aware − standard correctness under balanced copies).

Also show both constituent instruction differences, every world outcome, wrong commitments, DEFER, execution completion, validity, round-0 to round-1 changes, wrong unanimity, and costs/tokens/latency. Equal-weight the two q strata. Exact root-MAP agreement is secondary and distinct from sampled-truth accuracy; partial-lineage oracle agreement is not a claim that hidden information is recoverable. Report counts and world-level differences descriptively, no significance or efficacy declaration from six worlds. If no discriminating cases occur, report pilot uninformative; do not replenish selectively.

Native ZERO/ONE/DEFER distributions are choice scores, not elicited world probabilities. Preserve raw values; do not compute Brier, calibration or posterior-confidence claims. Show loss sensitivity wrong + d×unresolved at d∈{.1,.25,.5}, separately identifying execution failures; these are illustrative preferences.

## Qualification, budget and stopping

S0's full-lineage single-reader pass does not automatically qualify peer or partial-lineage context. Before S1, freeze a separate 16-request interface/context screen from a disjoint deterministic fixture manifest, eight with full lineage and peer payload, eight with partial lineage. Require 16/16 parseable responses, unchanged served model/provider, no hidden evaluator fields, and ≥7/8 full-lineage MAP choices. Partial cases test the interface only; no hidden-graph correctness gate. Failure blocks escalation and requires a documented bounded repair, not result-selected retry. Qualification calls need their own preregistration/TLDR and fresh admission; the exact fixture manifest and prompts are pending researcher review and implementation.

Main pilot: 6 × (8×5×2 + 4×2) = 528 calls. Context screen: 16 calls. New planned ceiling: 544; including prior S0: 576. At the existing conservative $0.001344/request reservation, cumulative reservation would be $0.774144, below the already approved $1 API cap. This is conditional on verified current pricing and enforceable token caps; longer peer payloads must fit that reservation or admission fails. Prior $0.043008 reservations are never erased or credited back merely because actual spend was lower. No new funding is requested. Infrastructure authority remains $1 and at most six hours, with a fresh exclusive fleet allocation required before dispatch.

Freeze model/provider, exact token ceilings, source revision, manifests, execution command and preflight receipts before launch. Preserve the shared ledger atomically across stages; the S0-only 32-call relay is not an S1 launcher. No automatic retries, provider fallback or changing models mid-cohort. Reserve before each call; any provider identity drift, contract failure or accounting gap stops further dispatch. Preserve assigned but unstarted outcomes. Cap concurrency at two and total allocation at six hours; collect all terminal receipts before release. A source change requires requalification review.
