## Interpretation limits

The adversarial benchmark makes a generic “can Jev be persuaded?” question weak as a novelty claim. Decision flips need independently established truth: benchmark labels partly originate from models. The option-name paper is direct prior work for Menu Parasites; the proposed extension is a changing collective choice set, not discovery of label sensitivity. [[hu-2026-jevadvbench]] [[sun-2026-type]]

Calibration and abstention provide measurement tools, not evidence that confidence estimates are already reliable in this setting. Current vendor formulas measure concentration; they must be tested against correctness under shift. [[guo-2017-calibration]] [[geifman-2017-selective]] [[typesafe-2026-confidence]]

Biological papers motivate local sensing and cross-inhibition mechanisms. They do not establish that a model collective inherits animal behavior. [[berdahl-2013-emergent]] [[seeley-2012-stop]]

Social posts supply demonstrations and questions, not controlled evidence. The original Henry post was opened on X, as was Karpathy’s map post and his text-only clarification. Architecture rumors in replies were not adopted. Entries retain short excerpts and paraphrases rather than copyrighted full-thread archives.

The map repository has no declared license in inspected metadata; do not copy its implementation without resolving permission. Source snapshots and commit hashes are recorded in source-manifest.json.

Formal novelty and saturation remain unresolved. The separate survey stays in progress until its gate and substantive reading requirements pass.
