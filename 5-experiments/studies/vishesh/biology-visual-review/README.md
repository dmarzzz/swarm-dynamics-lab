This review assesses **301 linked records**: all **214 atlas candidates**, **40 VX extensions**, **14 PX extensions**, **16 original project briefs**, **10 budget/vigilance hunches**, **4 standalone designs** and **3 fork-merge questions**. These overlap substantially; the count is coverage, not 301 independent discoveries. There are **zero registered hypotheses** in the frozen checkout.

## What these judgments mean

**Prioritize** means a well-defined comparison could inform the shared testbed; it does not mean run every such item. The shortlist selects among them. **Develop** means promising but an assumption, definition or infrastructure choice needs resolution. **Foundation** means a validity or measurement requirement, often more important than an additional treatment. **Defer** means the likely scope or confounding burden currently outweighs the incremental answer. These are the reviewer's qualitative judgments, not measured scores or consensus priorities.

**High visual value** means a proposed view can expose a mechanism, failure, tradeoff or competing explanation with inspectable data. **Medium** usually means a compact static diagnostic is more informative than an animated demo. Visual appeal is assessed independently of scientific novelty and feasibility. No figures here represent experimental results.

Every item has an individual assessment and visual specification. Shared mechanism sections provide biological or computational precedents and transfer boundaries. For identity, cryptography, attribution and many statistical questions, the honest assessment is that no strong biological connection was verified. The original computational literature remains the main prior art.

## Scope, snapshot and limits

The inventory records the source Git commit, atlas content hash and SHA-256 for all 26 input files. The 214 source candidates retain their original comparison, confounds, falsifier, feasibility and closest-prior metadata in the structured assessment; VX/PX records retain their own designs and links. Briefs and standalone designs link to the full protocols. The record-level judgments are new editorial work by vishesh/codex-methods on 2026-10-03.

This is a **screening assessment**, not a systematic review, a passing cross-researcher review, a novelty certification, a power analysis or permission to execute. Fresh primary reading is limited to the sections named in the source ledger. Existing catalogue read-depth labels belong to their original cataloguers. Biological transfers are our proposed comparisons, not results established for language agents.

Indexes, search logs, preflight notes and synthesis background were treated as context rather than counted again as separate ideas. Supporting fork-merge and detection syntheses overlap the atlas; the three explicit fork-merge questions receive separate umbrella assessments. This does not assert that every sentence in every repository document was exhaustively reviewed. No other researcher's source documents were edited.

## Reuse and validation

Open review.html directly: its data is embedded, it makes no external requests, and filters work offline. Search IDs, brief names, mechanisms or assessment text. A direct link such as `review.html#DES-IMMUNE` focuses that record. The review is not a new experiment dashboard and does not imply that any run is live.

Run `python3 5-experiments/studies/vishesh/biology-visual-review/check_review.py` from the repository root. It checks coverage, frozen input hashes, links and required fields. If upstream ideas change, regenerate or explicitly revise the snapshot; do not silently reinterpret old judgments as covering new content.
