# Source access and evidence boundaries

Checked 2026-10-03. Fresh reading below is deliberately narrower than a systematic or full-methods review. No new biological experiment was performed. Links are primary papers or the authors’ primary computational article, including third-party mirrors of the actual papers. New biological leads without canonical entries remain source-ledger leads; they have not silently become reviewed catalogue entries.

<a id="bee"></a>
## Stop signals provide cross inhibition in collective decision-making by honeybee swarms

[Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22157081/) · [[seeley-2012-stop]]

Primary empirical study and analytical model; abstract freshly read.

Scouts direct stop signals toward dancers advertising competing sites; the model examines resolution of equal-option deadlock.

**Transfer boundary:** A useful precedent for selective inhibition, not proof that suppressing disagreement improves factual accuracy.

<a id="ant"></a>
## A tunable algorithm for collective decision-making

[Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1635101/) · [[pratt-2006-tunable]]

Primary empirical and modeling study; abstract freshly read.

Ant emigration decisions adjust speed and accuracy with urgency through search, acceptance and commitment dynamics.

**Transfer boundary:** Transfer the threshold-versus-delay comparison, not species-specific rates or claims of universal optimality.

<a id="nca"></a>
## Growing Neural Cellular Automata

[Primary source](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/) · [[mordvintsev-2020-growing]]

Primary computational work; introduction and selected model sections freshly read.

A shared learned local update operates on visible and hidden cell channels; growth, persistence and repair are distinct training challenges.

**Transfer boundary:** Repair demonstrations already exist. Randomly masked parallel updates do not establish robustness to stale reads or arbitrary event-driven clocks.

<a id="repair"></a>
## Macrophages are required for adult salamander limb regeneration

[Primary source](https://humanlimbregeneration.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/couldhumansregrowlimbs_macrophagesresearch.pdf)

Godwin, Pinto and Rosenthal, 2013; primary experimental paper PDF, abstract freshly read.

Macrophage depletion permits wound closure but prevents normal regeneration; replenishment followed by reamputation restores regenerative capacity in the studied setting.

**Transfer boundary:** This motivates separating containment from functional restoration. It does not show that an agent role is equivalent to a macrophage.

<a id="commons"></a>
## Snowdrift game dynamics and facultative cheating in yeast

[Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2888597/)

Gore, Youk and van Oudenaarden, 2009; primary experimental paper, abstract freshly read.

Publicly available products of sucrose hydrolysis support frequency-dependent interactions and cooperator/cheater coexistence under the tested conditions.

**Transfer boundary:** Growth payoffs and diffusion are material processes. They do not establish norms, deliberate deception or economic collusion in agent markets.

<a id="reserve"></a>
## Bacterial persistence as a phenotypic switch

[Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15308767/)

Balaban and colleagues, 2004; primary experimental paper, abstract freshly read.

Single-cell observations connect persistence to preexisting heterogeneous growth states; regrown survivors retain antibiotic sensitivity, distinguishing persistence from heritable resistance.

**Transfer boundary:** A lead for reserve policies and repeated perturbations. Survival is not decision accuracy, and dormant agents need not be trustworthy.

<a id="feedback"></a>
## Robust perfect adaptation in bacterial chemotaxis through integral feedback control

[Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10781070/)

Yi, Huang, Simon and Doyle, 2000; primary theoretical paper, abstract and figure captions freshly read.

Control analysis identifies integral feedback in the Barkai–Leibler model under specified assumptions.

**Transfer boundary:** An explicit state, error signal and stability analysis are required before borrowing a homeostasis claim for an agent controller.

<a id="allocation"></a>
## Interdependence of Cell Growth and Gene Expression: Origins and Consequences

[Primary source](https://garcialab.berkeley.edu/courses/papers/Scott2010.pdf)

Scott and colleagues, 2010; primary study PDF, abstract and opening passages freshly read.

The study relates bacterial growth and gene expression through resource-allocation constraints, including translation inhibition and unnecessary protein expression.

**Transfer boundary:** Resource competition is a useful structural connection; token budgets have different currencies, delays and decision rights.

<a id="network"></a>
## Rules for Biologically Inspired Adaptive Network Design

[Primary source](https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/files/papers/others/2010/tero2010a.pdf)

Tero and colleagues, 2010; primary study PDF, abstract and opening passages freshly read.

Physarum network formation and its model expose tradeoffs among cost, transport efficiency and fault tolerance.

**Transfer boundary:** Transport edges carry material flow; communication edges carry semantic information. Similar graph pictures do not imply identical optimization problems.

<a id="motion"></a>
## Emergence of macroscopic directed motion in populations of motile colloids

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.2017) · [[bricard-2013-emergence]]

Primary experimental/theoretical active-matter paper; abstract freshly read.

Interacting synthetic motile colloids exhibit coherent collective motion, providing a controlled setting for microscopic coupling and macroscopic order.

**Transfer boundary:** This is physical active matter, not living biology. It supports comparative model design, not a claim of intelligence.

<a id="danger"></a>
## The danger model: a renewed sense of self

[Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11951032/)

Matzinger, 2002; theoretical Viewpoint, abstract freshly read.

The Viewpoint proposes damage rather than foreignness as a central organizing signal for immune activation.

**Transfer boundary:** A conceptual contrast for harm versus identity labels; not a direct experiment, settled universal account, or validated agent defense.

<a id="agreement"></a>
## The Byzantine Generals Problem

[Primary source](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/byz.pdf) · [[lamport-1982-byzantine]]

Lamport, Shostak and Pease, 1982; primary paper, abstract and opening problem definitions freshly read.

The guarantee quantifies over arbitrary behavior by a bounded number of traitors under specified communication assumptions.

**Transfer boundary:** This is not a statistical-independence assumption and does not guarantee truth about a shared external source.

## Catalogue-only and inaccessible leads

The original atlas’s `prior` entries and the sixteen briefs’ reading lists remain the closest project-specific prior work. They are carried through in assessments.json or linked originals; their existing `catalogued_depth` is not a claim that this pass reread every paper. Direct computational precedents usually outrank a distant biological analogy for novelty assessment.

The social-amoeba self-recognition paper [[hirose-2011-self]] could help frame recognition versus cooperation, but access returned a browser challenge in this pass. No fresh result claim is based on it. Trained-immunity and monocyte-tolerance leads were located but not successfully inspected sufficiently for use; they do not support the review’s conclusions. Animal leadership, bacterial quorum sensing, ant stigmergy and phylogenetic reconstruction remain targeted follow-up searches where noted; this review does not claim their methods were verified.

Primary search routes used PubMed/PMC, author and university paper mirrors, Distill and arXiv. PDF mirrors for Scott and Tero include adjacent articles; only the named paper’s opening passages were used. The macrophage source was read through the actual paper PDF after PMC access failed. Full methods, replications and forward/backward citation saturation remain required before promotion.
