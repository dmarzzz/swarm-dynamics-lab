# Priorities, biological connections and useful visual evidence

The most coherent project is an **evidence-and-recovery observatory** combining the original collective-sensing, quorum, dissent, memory, regrowth, whistleblowing and institutions briefs. Its central question is whether a group can preserve useful independent evidence, correct a bad contribution and resume productive work without suppressing a correct minority. This is a proposed integration, not a demonstrated immune system or an accepted team hypothesis.

## Where biology helps and where it does not

The useful operation is to translate a mechanism into variables and competing predictions. The [primary source ledger](sources.md) centralizes the actual biological claims and reading depth. The following are our design inferences:

- **Quorum and inhibition:** test whether commitment depends on independent evidence, whether opposition supplies new information, and how the threshold changes with urgency. Do not equate a collective choice with external truth. The bee and ant anchors motivate contrasts, not numerical priors for LLMs. [[seeley-2012-stop]] [[pratt-2006-tunable]]
- **Repair:** distinguish a contained incident from restored task function. Score loss of helpful contributions as a side effect of a defense. An image that regrows, a connected network and a correct answer are separate outcomes. See the [regeneration](sources.md#repair) and [NCA](sources.md#nca) anchors.
- **Heterogeneity:** stress-test shared failure causes as well as random member loss. Bacterial persistence is a lead for reserve strategies, not evidence that diverse models reason better. See [persistence](sources.md#reserve).
- **Resource regulation:** require an explicit measured state, actuator, delay and objective before describing a policy as homeostatic. Plot actual resource currencies separately. See [feedback](sources.md#feedback) and [allocation](sources.md#allocation).
- **Collective networks:** vary connection maintenance, delay and fault tolerance jointly with task utility. A good material-transport network may be a poor information network. See [adaptive networks](sources.md#network).
- **Institutions and identity:** membership recognition, harm detection, truth assessment and permission enforcement are different operations. No verified biological precedent here establishes unique ownership, Sybil resistance, due process or a cryptographic guarantee. Keep these as computational and institutional questions.

## Material corrections and uncertainty boundaries

### Fork-merge Q2: distinguish bounded faults from probabilistic independence

The frozen [fork-merge synthesis](../../../../synthesis/fork-merge-questions.md), in its opening summary and section 3.2 framing, treats classical Byzantine thresholds as requiring independent failures. That is too broad. Lamport, Shostak and Pease quantify over arbitrary behavior by a bounded set of traitors under communication assumptions; their agreement result is not conditioned on statistical independence. [[lamport-1982-byzantine]] [Primary paper](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/byz.pdf).

The useful replacement framing is: **common causes can exceed the fault bound, and honest agreement on bad external evidence is a different problem from protocol agreement**. Independence assumptions must be checked theorem by theorem for stochastic aggregation results. Do not transfer one interpretation across Byzantine agreement, robust gradients, weight merging and semantic voting. This correction is recorded in owned notes; it does not silently rewrite another researcher's synthesis or assert a full audit of every cited theorem.

### Immune response: recognize costs of defense and separate state layers

The immune design should retain clean cases, correct minorities, false reports, independently verified repair and post-reentry challenges. Include shared caches, summaries, private state and stale children in the declared state inventory. A prevention-only arm and a restart/restore arm can explain whether a complex immune policy adds value. A stopped attack with permanently lost useful service is not successful healing.

Do not label every disagreement as infection or every outsider as a pathogen. Use an operational incident label tied to executable truth, permissions or declared task constraints. The danger-model source is a theoretical Viewpoint, not proof of this policy. The observation that one repair phase differs from another does not imply an agent has the molecular machinery of a biological immune system.

### Novelty and mechanism claims remain narrow

NCA growth-and-repair already has a direct computational precedent. Private initial judgments, independent voting, semantic routing, provenance and memory repair also have project-specific predecessors in the source atlas and earlier review. This pass adds cross-connections and discriminating comparisons; it does not certify empty research gaps. “No one has studied this” should become “not located in this search” until the appropriate survey is complete.

The [external-influence design](../seo-poisoning/experimental-design.md) contains models at several fidelity levels. Parameters imported from animal decision systems are illustrative until fitted and validated on agent observations. A scripted quorum simulation verifies code and accounting; it does not establish an LLM response law. Explicitly check acquisition, exposure, interpretation and aggregation instead of attributing every failure to social conformity.

Budget B4's coordinated spending should not be called a cartel solely because expenditures synchronize. Common workloads, budget signals and ledger information can produce the same pattern. The swarm-factory design should separately report ownership, economic concentration, coordinated actions, welfare and detection. Yeast public-goods experiments are a useful structural analogy, not a definition of market collusion.

## Visual design that can distinguish explanations

Use one shared trace schema wherever possible, adapting the existing [agent-experiment tooling](../../../../tooling/agent-experiments/README.md). These are instrumentation requirements, not a request for another runtime:

| View | Minimum recorded fields | Necessary comparison | Visual failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence ancestry | Observation ID, parent/copy edges, observed provenance, evaluator-only truth, agent exposure time | Independent observations versus paraphrased copies | Rendering five copies as five independent votes; showing hidden truth to agents |
| Decision evolution | Pre-exposure judgment, message ID, post-exposure judgment, correctness, abstention, actual cost | Equal preparation, evidence union and total budget | Showing only final consensus or only rescued wrong answers |
| Containment and repair | Incident time, state version, dependency edges, policy action, quarantine/release time, independent functional result | Clean control, no response, restart/restore and targeted repair as specified in the existing protocol | Counting removed messages as recovered function; omitting excluded good work |
| Memory/claim lineage | Original text identifier, claim/uncertainty annotation, supersession link, retrieval event, subsequent action | Append-only correction versus replacement/invalidation, held-out retrieval | Treating text similarity as a proven transmission edge |
| Resource regulation | Authoritative balance, reported balance, reservations, realized consumption, phase, completion state | Fixed/equal allocation, forecast policy and reserve policy with overhead charged | Confusing an advertised token budget with usable context or time |
| NCA perturbation | Model/version, visible/hidden states, lesion mask, update events and reads, functional score | Undamaged, visible-only lesion, full-state lesion, scheduling controls | A pleasing image recovery without a task endpoint or matched randomness |

Replay one trace for explanation, then show **all randomized worlds** in outcome plots. Keep aborted, failed and unfinished runs. Mark examples selected for illustration. Estimate uncertainty at the randomized world/team level; do not count each agent or video frame as an independent replicate. A topology view should encode only observed or declared links and distinguish inferred links visually. A mechanism slider in a toy model demonstrates the assumptions written into that model, not evidence for those assumptions.

## Feasibility and a useful stopping rule

Begin with deterministic fixtures for ancestry, missing evidence, stale versions, negative dependencies, accounting and replay. They can reveal implementation mistakes without model spending. Then, after research gates and budget authorization, use one objectively scored task suite for the first four shortlist comparisons. Keep biological reproduction as a separate lane: a small NCA boundary test has a different data model and training burden from language-agent evidence processing.

For each candidate, stop expanding scope when a simpler control explains the apparent benefit, the necessary labels are unavailable, or the comparison cannot be made at matched resources. A null result that rejects a proposed mechanism can be valuable. Selective reporting or a striking animation of one favorable run cannot make it so.
