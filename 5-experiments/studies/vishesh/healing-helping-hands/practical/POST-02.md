## Quality and interpretation

The data support a transport-sensitive result for this exact programmed protocol. More capacity resolves most dissemination loss, but higher traffic does not buy superiority over the strong central baseline. We do not equate logical rounds, transmitted item counts, bytes, real hardware latency or equal resource budgets. Central bulk reads are deliberately strong; the mesh uses local four-neighbor synchronous exchanges. The outage only removes central connectivity for half the clients while mesh links stay intact. Real correlated link failures, independently authored corpora, semantic extraction errors and cryptographic authenticity were not tested.

No further cap search is authorized by this protocol. The valid adverse comparison is the outcome, not a defect to tune away. All Qwen/Laya limitations remain; saved Jev labels are reused, not a new model qualification. A real curation application should start with a version-aware index and auditable source identity, then justify distributed repair by a concrete availability/latency requirement.
