The repaired wire-order control and stronger erasure measurement passed. This establishes implementation-level behavior for a deliberately engineered task. Semantic extraction is model-backed; sharing, deduplication and aggregation are deterministic. There is no evidence here of heterogeneous-head benefit or successful Qwen agents. Provider failures were not observed in this pilot; offline injected faults and terminal accounting were tested. Abrupt power loss, partitions and long-running service reliability were not validated.
