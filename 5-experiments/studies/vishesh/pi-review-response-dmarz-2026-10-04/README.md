## Proposed successor 3: identity splitting with fixed attacker resources

**Disposition:** Support the new estimand, with an updated baseline decision: random checking is now essential, and coverage-versus-degree should not be treated as the scientifically privileged contrast without justification.

**Owner:** dmarz Sybil study owner; no successor launch or formal acceptance is granted by this response.

### Evidence and limits

The parent scaling study is complete: 2,400 valid model answers, 100 conditions and 24 paired world clusters. At 972 identities, strong coverage checks increasing from 4 to 108 improved specialist accuracy 47.2% to 98.6%, a reported +51.4 points (descriptive paired-root interval +38.9 to +62.5). This buys more verification and is not an equal-cost effect.

At the same 108 strong checks, random selection also reached 98.6%, and deterministic plurality on the coverage packets reached 100%. Degree checking rejected 93.4% of honest specialists. Coverage therefore has no demonstrated advantage over random or necessary model contribution in the strongest condition.

Even the best coverage cell rejected 62.8% of honest specialists; repeated three-fact expertise makes high answer accuracy compatible with poor contributor inclusion. Four fixed coverage checks went exclusively to core identities at larger populations, exposing a graph-allocation mechanism.

These are simulated identities and verification outcomes feeding one model synthesis per condition, on one graph family with a fixed 25% attacker fraction and growing attacker resources. They do not test 972 autonomous model agents or identity multiplication at fixed attacker capacity.

### Incorporation

This response updates the collecting status to completed exploratory S1 and preserves its descriptive uncertainty. The original parent cohort remains unchanged; its results should inform comparator choice before a successor is frozen.

Hold honest population, evidence and aggregate attacker token/message, edge-acquisition and verification-attempt budgets fixed while varying identity allocation. Charge registration/metadata overhead and distinguish nominal identities from identities able to submit evidence.

Keep matched-budget random admission and same-packet deterministic plurality as central comparators. Either make the raw fixed-resource multiplicity effect primary, or justify a policy interaction against a competent comparator before fresh evaluation; degree-only superiority could reward comparison with a policy that discards expertise.

Use graph/task root clusters, two independently implemented graph families and one frozen attacker allocation algorithm. Counterbalance fabrication direction and increase distinct specialist facts so accuracy cannot depend only on repeated easy facts.

Report wrong answers, rare-skill coverage, attacker seat share and honest-specialist rejection jointly. Differentiate attacker seat share from attacker admission probability.

### Remaining work

Freeze topology/anchor/edge allocation and packet-order rules so identity count does not silently buy extra connections, text or verification chances. Separate identity-multiplicity from any graph change that cannot be held fixed.

Qualify clean aggregation and missing-evidence abstention at each distinct packet-load regime. The 24 development roots and 10-point candidate margin do not determine evaluation power.

Include a faithful prior-work defense before comparative superiority claims. Scope any model contribution to its incremental benefit over identical-input deterministic aggregation.

Use fresh evaluation roots and retain valid degradation under tight resource budgets; do not enlarge attacker resources afterward to recreate a positive effect.
