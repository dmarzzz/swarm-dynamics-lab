## Stage A: challenge framing, evidence acquisition and recovery

Primary question: under identical evidence access and checking budgets, does explicitly challenging the current decision improve useful evidence acquisition and the final justified action compared with a neutral verifier? This is a native role-instruction comparison supporting dissent research, not an emergent swarm claim. Initial consensus/history is scripted and disclosed; do not present it as a measured native multi-agent consensus.

### Episodes and labels

Construct **24 distinct operational rule/dependency families**, four synthetic domains crossed with six mechanisms, with two independently parameterized episodes per family:48evaluation episodes. Domains: software release, data publication, equipment return-to-service, and inventory dispatch. These are operational simulations, not advice or actions affecting real equipment or people. Mechanisms:

1. Explicit metadata invalidity that a gate/refresh should solve, as a simple positive control.
2. Current evidence whose semantic coverage omits the disputed requirement.
3. A newer authoritative correction reachable through an available check.
4. Conflicting current sources requiring the declared authority rule or justified deferral.
5. A valid unchanged decision where challenging it wastes attention or delays service.
6. An unresolved state where no permitted check can justify commitment by the deadline.

Write the practical decision, evidence dependency graph, authority rules, tool/source catalog, acquisition latency and full counterfactual source responses before constructing prompts. All policies see the same initial evidence and available check descriptions. Do not make routine refresh deliberately obsolete, hide relevant tools from a comparator, label a source as the answer, or give the dissenter privileged facts. The need for checking must arise from the operational contract, not arbitrary denial of information.

Examples to develop, not completed fixtures: a release certificate current for the artifact but excluding a required migration test; a current publication approval superseded by an authoritative restriction; an unchanged inventory decision challenged because an irrelevant certificate expired. Include answer-preserving and answer-changing cases in development. If a metadata check or simple batch policy solves all useful evaluation cases, retain that result and do not manufacture harder cases after seeing responses.

Ground labels in the generated environment and separately audit a compact evidence-to-action decision table for each family. Enumerate every legal check offline to establish which final actions are knowable under the one-check budget. Unknown states require DEFER. Compare the state-machine oracle with the decision-table scorer and reject contradictions before freezing. This is an owning-agent cross-check, not independent annotation. Human/rater judgment must not be invented where the contract cannot determine a label.

Develop six separate public episodes for parser/policy debugging. Create12additional disjoint qualification episodes spanning the hard selection and final-action steps. Seal evaluation episodes before native qualification, with root/family provenance and fingerprints that prevent changing IDs or parameters from being reported as new semantic families. If the24family structures are not genuinely distinct, report the lower effective family count and revise the design before funding/freezing rather than silently retaining the advertised n.

Routine refresh is a strong lower-compute practical comparator; disclose its one-call advantage rather than claiming exact compute equality across all three policies. No unused quota is spent on dummy calls.

One native selector and one native final resolver at most; reset state between trajectories and fresh executions, preserve within-trajectory selected evidence, and expose no evaluator truth, future source response or operator conversation. The full-information oracle is only an attainability reference, never a competitor credited with equal information.

Evaluate48episodes × three policies × **three fresh complete executions** =432trajectories and720maximum model calls. Pair policies on identical environment episodes; independently randomize policy/episode order within each frozen repeat block. Retain all three repeats and actual execution windows, even when they disagree. Different seeds/orderings do not create new semantic roots.

The structural sample is **24semantic families**, each with two parameterized episodes;48episodes and432trajectories are not432independent tasks. Aggregate the primary dissenter-minus-neutral difference in correct justified final decisions within each family, giving families equal weight. Report family differences, all policy absolute correct/wrong/DEFER rates, inappropriate challenges, useful sources acquired, legitimate work retained, acquisition count, latency and model cost. Distinguish deployment throughput from correctness; do not hide harm inside a composite utility score.

Estimate fresh-execution variation separately: action/source-selection disagreement for each fixed episode/policy, each repeat's aggregate effect and the range across repeats. Family-cluster resampling and leave-one-domain-out analysis describe sensitivity to this authored benchmark; they are not probability-sample confidence statements about real operations. For planning only, an exchangeable-family approximation with24families and difference SD0.5 yields roughly±0.21 at95%; SD1 yields roughly±0.42. Thus this design can inform large effects around25percentage points but cannot establish fine equivalence or rule out a10point advantage. Report the observed uncertainty rather than promising adequate power, and never expand n based on a favorable interim effect.

The useful-effect target is a25point improvement over the neutral checker with no increase in wrong commitments and with correct service preserved. This is a decision target, not a qualification threshold or a prerequisite for a scientifically valid result. A stable null/adverse result with competent controls is useful evidence that challenge framing adds no demonstrated value on the tested support; wide intervals remain inconclusive rather than proving equivalence. A later precision expansion would need a prospective justified plan and funding, not automatic continuation.
