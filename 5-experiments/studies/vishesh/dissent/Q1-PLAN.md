# The Right Dissenter: sufficient-evidence diagnostic

Design RD-3, 2026-10-04. Prospective amendment after [Q0-A1](reviews/Q0-A1-POST.md), written before Q1 implementation. The original [RD-2 plan](LIVE-PLAN.md) supplies unchanged study scope, S1 assignments, policies, stopping rules and research boundaries.

## TLDR

Does explicitly defining a single sufficient task criterion repair Jev's tendency to defer on positive evidence? Pair generic and clarified instructions across 18 fresh clean packets, plus six clarified absent/conflicting-evidence controls. Measure correct and valid actions by domain and instruction arm, and paired changes. This is a bounded instrument diagnostic on repeated synthetic grammar, not a generalization or dissent finding.

## Question and prediction

Q0 had six errors, all DEFER on positive build/alarm reports. If unspecified sufficiency is responsible, clarification should improve positive classification without inducing commitments on missing or conflicting evidence. The generic arm is a contemporaneous diagnostic comparator; it is not pooled with the clarified arm for qualification. A null effect or new errors may reject this diagnosis.

## Setup

Same dedicated host, approved $2 cumulative cap ($1 API/$1 infrastructure), same route and served snapshot as RD-2. Q1-A1 has parent Q0-A1. All actor fields exclude expected answers and condition labels. Instructions define exactly one simulated requirement per domain: bridge meets its required load; build passes the named required compatibility test; alarm sample is within permitted range. Current applicable uncontradicted evidence satisfying that one criterion is sufficient for PROCEED; failing evidence warrants HOLD; absent or unresolved conflicting evidence warrants DEFER. Do not infer additional unstated requirements. This rule is identical across positive and negative examples.
