# Six public acquisition episodes: offline construction plan

Written before implementation, 2026-10-04. Implements the development-case step in [the scientific baseline proposal](../freshness/SCIENTIFIC-BASELINE-PLAN.md). No native model calls, sealed-reserve inspection or new paid scope. This is public development material and must never become an untouched qualification set.

Build six episodes covering expired metadata, missing conjunctive coverage, authority supersession, conflicting current sources, valid unchanged service and insufficient one-check reachability. Record the task requirements, authority rules, initial observations, full source catalog, every counterfactual source response, scripted incumbent decision and hand-authored action table. Labels describe what the acquired evidence justifies, separately from whether a better permitted source could have enabled a commitment. A justified DEFER after a poor acquisition is an acquisition failure, not necessarily an interpreter failure.

All comparators receive the same initial actor projection and catalog. At most one lookup, identical latency/cost and deadline; NONE is legal. No source response or expected outcome enters source-selection input. Acquisition returns only the chosen source response. Neutral versus challenge native request drafts differ only in their selection instruction. Both share the final resolver contract and eligibility/authority rules. Scripted incumbent ballots are labeled scripted; no emergent collective behavior is simulated.

Implement two useful executable simple comparators: (1) a metadata/authority-aware routine refresh using the declared source-priority schedule, stopping if all requirements are already resolved; (2) a stronger neutral coverage controller selecting the source with greatest advertised coverage of unresolved required predicates, then authority and routine priority, also stopping when no required predicate is unresolved. The second must read actor-visible fields only; it may not consult counterfactual results, expected actions or case/mechanism identity. Report if it solves all six. Do not weaken it to create room for dissent.

Use a shared deterministic resolver of declared rules for offline replay, with source provenance checks, inclusive TTL, future/wrong-scope/revision rejection, requirement-specific minimum authority, latest evidence within maximum authority, tied contradiction detection, mandatory-failure short circuit and missing-evidence DEFER. This is a rule baseline, not measured native competence. The native neutral/challenge selectors and final resolver remain unrun.

Validate against a separately hand-authored table for every legal choice (NONE plus each source), mutations for stale authorization/always-act/always-defer/budget overrun, input projection/leakage, counterfactual noninterference, renaming/order invariance, policy resource parity and recorder replay. Preserve all choice outcomes, including a comparator's miss. A family audit must group shared logical structures; six mechanism labels or renamed IDs do not establish six independent families, much less24.

Produce public cases, source-selection/final-resolution request drafts, exhaustive counterfactual outcomes, comparator traces, family inventory and an evidence-linked assessment. Use the resulting challenge/ceiling findings to propose a finite discriminating packet. Do not assume the earlier24-family/48-episode corpus exists or that the162-call freshness proposal is a required first step for this development work. No launch adapter or spending allowance is added here.
