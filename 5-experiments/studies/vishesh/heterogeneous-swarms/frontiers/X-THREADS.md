The useful progression in these conversations is from communication, to durable shared experience, to transmission and selection. The proposed next step is to ask what persists and reproduces: an individual policy, a paired interface, a shared habitat, or a co-adapted collection of procedures. That progression is our synthesis, not a quotation or an empirical result from X.

Searches also returned promotional crypto-agent material, human–AI symbiosis essays and repeated announcements of the same work. Those were not counted as independent evidence.
