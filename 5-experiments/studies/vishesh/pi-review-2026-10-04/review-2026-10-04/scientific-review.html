02 / A standard that fits a small lab

Interesting, identifiable and consequential

Interesting. Name the result that would change a practical decision or distinguish competing explanations. A biological analogy, vivid failure or increase in agreement is a starting point. Novelty requires a close methods comparison; this review does not certify it from an abstract collection.

Robust. Count independently constructed worlds, tasks or adapted lineages. Repeated agents, messages, forks, seeds on the same tiny fixture and generations are dependent. Pair conditions at the level randomized; preserve family and lineage structure in uncertainty estimates. Size studies from a useful-effect margin and pilot variability, not a universal minimum number of agents.

Challenging and defined. Qualify clean competence and show room above the baseline. The task should survive an exact-rule, equally informed single-controller and simple operational baseline. Increasing ambiguity without specifying what an agent could know creates an unanswerable task, not a stronger one.

Controlled. State which of information, calls, tokens, tools, memory, time and money are held equal, charged, or deliberately changed. Match information at the causal branch point. In adaptive exploration, changed observations are part of the mechanism; measure them instead of forcing them equal after treatment.

Realistic enough. Preserve consequential constraints and costs: wrong authorization, missed deadlines, lost evidence, false alarms, interrupted work. Add external inputs after a clean mechanism works. A task can be intentionally synthetic, provided claims stay at that level.

Small enough to finish. Freeze one main comparison and a few necessary controls. Essential identification axes are marked separately from later stress tests throughout this report. A null or a deterministic solution is an acceptable result; it should stop unnecessary expansion.

Agent identity is an experimental variable. Use the agent definition and lifecycle contract: versioned definition, actual initialized context, private/shared memory ownership, access rules, reset/fork semantics and logged state evolution. Reliable initialization means the same declared setup; it does not promise identical hosted-model output. Keep intended configuration separate from the receipt of what each actor actually received.

Experiment - Quorum of Mirrors

Quorum of MirrorsPrioritize a small evidence-dependence benchmark with partial-lineage controls. The useful target is an intervention/resource tradeoff, not another demonstration that duplicated votes are dependent.

Evidence status. Exploratory, unimplemented, unqualified proposal, DM-02. This is distinct from the existing adaptive-quorum experiment; its historical runs cannot be borrowed as Quorum of Mirrors results.

Scientific interest

PI judgment. A cleaner initial causal study than adaptive Phantom Coast, with practical value if it identifies when another observation beats another judgment.

Why it matters. Source count, decision-maker count and communication can be manipulated separately. Explicit versus incomplete lineage tests when implementable deduplication works and when apparent consensus conceals dependence.

Novelty boundary. The proposal itself identifies prior work on repeated evidence and correlated Jev judge errors. Reading remains limited; novelty is unestablished. Neither a hundred cloned calls, unanimous errors nor a renamed consensus task is a new scientific contribution.

How robust is the evidence?

Supported. The revised design recognizes ancestry ground truth, task-difficulty confounding, confidence calibration, pooled baselines and separate observation/call budgets.

Not established. No evidence yet that endorsements inflate confidence, partial lineage defeats a defense, a larger quorum helps, or Jev differs from other models. Typed valid outputs do not demonstrate calibrated decisions.

Independent unit. An independently generated decision instance/world with a sampled evidence-ancestry graph. Judges, repeated calls and communication rounds are nested observations; branches using the same evidence graph are paired.

Actual variation. Not sampled yet. Different source IDs do not guarantee independent measurements: shared sensors, common causes and item difficulty can correlate distinct roots.

Assessment. A strong sketch, not a protocol. Specify the generative dependence model, allowed lineage information, aggregation rules and budget estimands before claiming either independent sources or fair comparisons.

Is the scenario adequate?

Size. A small fixed group can expose duplicate amplification. A hundred is a communication hook, not a necessary experimental scale or independent sample count.

Challenge. All-explicit source IDs may make deterministic deduplication trivial. Partial lineage adds meaningful ambiguity, but some ancestry configurations are observationally indistinguishable; impossible inference is an information limit, not a model defect.

Complexity. Use controlled noisy evidence and imperfect visible ancestry before adding large graphs. Separate exact copies, factual restatements and genuinely new measurements.

Realism. Mirrored reports and repeated endorsements resemble practical research/decision workflows. Grounded reliability cues are preferable to arbitrary crowd authority; real source dependence is rarely captured by identity alone.

Definition. Freeze fact-preserving paraphrases, lineage visibility, source reliability and how peer messages update state. Report calibrated error, agreement and raw dependence separately; an effective-sample-size approximation is not a literal count of independent minds.

Experiment - Phantom Coast

Phantom CoastAdvance the bounded Two Swarms, One World specification first. Preserve The Island No One Visits as the flagship mechanism, contingent on demonstrating decision-dependent observation rather than imposing avoidance in code.

Evidence status. Prospective PC-1 instrument-development plan, with offline implementation in progress in a concurrent task. No native qualification or scientific result. Latest plan frozen at 2026-10-04T03:40:18.974165+00:00; code/tests were not audited in this delta.

Scientific interest

PI judgment. A compelling prospective question with a stronger causal story than ordinary misinformation spreading.

Why it matters. A mistaken map could change inspection choices and thereby remove opportunities for correction. Distinguishing wrong judgments from missing corrective exposure would change how evidence-gathering systems are designed.

Novelty boundary. Self-confirming learning, collective sensing, context effects and spatial demonstrations already appear in the proposal's cited work. Those sources were mostly scanned at abstract/introductory depth; no complete novelty search or new independent literature validation is supplied by this review. The possible contribution is an identified feedback mechanism and useful intervention, not a recognizable map or a persistent wrong label.

How robust is the evidence?

Supported. The design correctly separates fixed evidence from adaptive observation, distinguishes retraction from deletion/reset, requires complete-world scoring and identifies pooled/deterministic baselines. The newly written PC-1 plan now specifies a 6×6 world, three same-evidence actors, eight history/communication/state trajectories, a complete final observation ledger, explicit retraction, fixed-denominator error bounds and byte-identical canonical reset inputs. These are plan properties, not validated execution.

Not established. There is no evidence yet of amplification, a self-maintaining exploration trap, benefit from source-aware communication, model-specific susceptibility or recovery. Neither a selected animation nor an Earth-coordinate demonstration would establish these.

Independent unit. An independently generated archipelago/world with its sensor and initial-exposure realization. History, communication and reset branches are paired within world; neighboring tiles and agents are dependent measurements.

Actual variation. None sampled yet. Vary world geometry, report ambiguity and exposure placement across held-out world families, rather than relabeling one coastline. Repeated identical requests estimate service variation, not environmental generalization.

Assessment. Scientifically promising, but not execution-ready. The observation model, available actions, stopping horizon, memory contract, resource prices and primary estimand remain unspecified.

Is the scenario adequate?

Size. PC-1 fixes 36 cells, three actors and twelve qualification worlds. That is sufficient for an instrument screen, not population inference; the proposed 1,044-call qualification ceiling is large for a simple complete-evidence task and should be staged.

Challenge. The final step supplies all 36 noiseless observations. This is an appropriate competence and history-persistence control but can reduce the task to copying a complete map. A null is valid. Claims about realistic exploration require the separate PC-2 observation-choice stage.

Complexity. PC-1 uses identical factual inputs for three actors, so it isolates communication rather than complementary sensing. Its three logical steps and strict-majority aggregation are deliberately small. PC-2 must add real observation choices without hardcoded avoidance.

Realism. A fictional text-survey task offers strong causal control and resembles evidence collection. It is not visual perception, geographic discovery or a demonstration of changed model weights.

Definition. PC-1 resolves the action/schema, cell coverage, UNKNOWN versus missing, world-level units and all-assignment error bounds. The primary interaction is in absolute between-history disagreement; accuracy must remain a separate outcome because convergence can be jointly wrong and disagreement can reflect service noise.

Experiment - Optimal Swarm Size

Optimal swarm size: a deployable conditional launch policyDevelop the task contracts and a smaller identifying pilot. Defer the proposed 2,512-episode core/transfer programme and 1,280-episode optional extension until useful size-dependent heterogeneity exists and the runtime is measurable.

Evidence status. Detailed prospective design only. No implemented harness, validated task generator, qualification, model episode, or sizing result is established by these documents.

Scientific interest

PI judgment. Scientifically worthwhile and operationally clear, with unusually careful prospective controls.

Why it matters. A launch rule that increases verified success under an actual deadline and aggregate budget is useful; charging selection, coordination, retrieval, retries and cancellation makes the question substantially more meaningful than a best-looking N sweep.

Novelty boundary. Task- and architecture-dependent scaling is already acknowledged in the design. The proposed contribution is a deployable rule evaluated on fresh executions with charged overhead, not discovery of a universal optimum. Novelty remains unestablished pending the focused methods comparison.

How robust is the evidence?

Supported. The draft specifies a coherent estimand, independent-root splits, intention-to-treat failures, hidden evaluators, aggregate resource accounting, paired comparisons and fresh policy evaluation. These are design strengths, not demonstrated harness properties.

Not established. No evidence yet supports an optimal size, reliable five-point near-optimality, a learned selector, physical-memory effects, or cross-domain transfer. One locked transfer family supports one-family transfer only.

Independent unit. An independently generated task root; repeated model samples, paraphrases, sizes and profiles remain nested or paired within that root.

Actual variation. Root semantics and dependencies, plus independent model sampling. Two development families and one transfer family do not create a broad population of independent task domains.

Assessment. Four fit and four validation roots per structural cell cannot establish fine conditional boundaries. The documents candidly flag this. Choose complexity and interval interpretation accordingly; do not let 2,512 correlated episodes imply strong precision. Simultaneous uncertainty must govern the smallest-within-epsilon decision.
