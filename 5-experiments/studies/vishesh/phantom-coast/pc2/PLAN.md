# Phantom Coast PC-2: which evidence does a mistaken map prevent?

Prospective development specification, 2026-10-04 UTC; written before PC-2 implementation. Owner vishesh/codex-phantom-coast. Evidence confidence **0/4** for an adaptive effect; observed sample **none**. PC-1's valid null remains unchanged. This document authorizes no paid dispatch by itself.

## TLDR

Under twelve inspection opportunities, do misleading initial reports cause a three-actor controller to miss corrective observations, compared with a seeded uniform inspection schedule? Cross misleading/benign reports with a one-slot source audit; compare a single controller and an evidence-only reconstruction baseline. Measure whole-map error bounds, direct coverage of the reported region, and errors after actually inspecting a cell. All 36 cells remain inspectable. The source audit replaces an ordinary inspection, even when redundant. This is a small synthetic exploratory design; no effect has been observed and no realistic exploration claim is established.

## Question and prediction

The practical decision is whether to reserve one scarce inspection for checking an inherited report or spend it on the controller's preferred target. A mistaken map may lower visits to a falsely water-labelled land region. The competing prediction is that actors actively verify suspect reports or perform as well as a simple uniform schedule. Both are valid outcomes. We will not qualify or retune on obtaining an avoidance effect.

PC-1 supplied every cell's correct value and withdrew the false source simultaneously. It therefore could neither test observation choice nor separate those two interventions. PC-2 removes the full-survey rescue and does not introduce explicit retraction. The narrower mechanism is acquisition under a source-audit intervention. A separate evidence-coverage × retraction factorial would be required to explain the earlier recovery; it is not silently folded into this study.

## Setup

Use 36 equal-area cells and twelve land cells. Four land cells form either a contiguous 2×2 reported region or four mutually nonadjacent reported cells. Eight other land cells are sampled outside that region. These are two structural families, not eight different task types. Truth is fixed during an episode. Counterbalance report cell IDs by seeded generation, with stable source IDs and equal record counts. Misleading initial reports label the four cells WATER; benign reports label them LAND, both reliability 0.8 at epoch 0. No whole-map prior or region-membership label is exposed to actors. Labels necessarily differ between arms; identical-input causal claims do not apply to this treatment.

At each of twelve slots a chosen cell returns one noiseless authenticated epoch-2 direct measurement. All 36 cells, including previously visited or predicted-water cells, are always legal. Repeated inspections cost a slot and obtain a new acquisition ID; they do not create coverage or independent evidence. Every actor in a controller receives the same factual ledger. This is collective target selection, not distributed sensing or independent witnesses.

Three acquisition policies: (a) native team: three simultaneous cell proposals per slot, with at least two valid proposals required; select the plurality winner, with all ties resolved by a frozen seeded cell order; (b) native single: one cell proposal; (c) uniform: the first twelve cells of one seeded permutation, without replacement. Team members see the previous round's proposals, labelled interpretations, but cannot see another member's current proposal. The single actor sees only its own previous proposal. No free-form memory or permanent model-state claim. The task is to minimize final whole-map error under the remaining sensing budget; UNKNOWN remains a legal map output.

Source audit on/off: at slot six (one-based), the on arm replaces the proposed target with one uniformly selected member of the initial report's four cells. Selection uses only the public report cell set, never hidden truth, and is fixed before dispatch. Keep the native proposal calls even when overridden, preserving policy-call budgets. If that cell was already inspected, the audit is still spent there; no reroll or extra slot. This is a targeted source audit, not uniform exploration over the world. The same target and uniform schedule are paired across report conditions.

## Metrics

Primary descriptive interaction: (team misleading minus team benign) minus (uniform misleading minus uniform benign) in final all-cell upper error, without source audit, averaged equally over roots and separately by family. Lower error is observed wrong/36; upper error is (wrong+UNKNOWN or failed-map cells)/36. Upper error is a conservative decision-risk measure, not an observed misclassification rate. Report both components and interval arithmetic for differences; no missing paired branch is assigned a zero effect.

The targeted-audit contrast is on minus off within each policy/report condition. A planning value threshold is a reduction of at least 2/36 = 5.56 percentage points in upper error, with no additional sensing slots; this is a prespecified practical threshold, not a powered significance boundary. Eight roots are an exploratory feasibility cohort. No significance or population-generalization claim, cell-level standard errors or bootstrap interval over agents.

Mechanism measures: number of distinct directly inspected report cells /4; whole-world direct coverage /36; slot of first corrective report-region visit (right-censored after twelve); repeat/invalid slot counts; error among directly observed cells versus never-observed cells; missed land /12 and false land /24, each with missingness bounds; source-audit coverage gain; realized observation overlap; and native map versus deterministic reconstruction from the exact acquired packet. Report the yoked judge separately: it tests endpoint interpretation given identical acquired evidence, not causal mediation or the total policy effect. Evidence-only reconstruction uses latest authenticated direct observations, otherwise unanimous nonwithdrawn report labels; disagreements become UNKNOWN. Do not infer inspected-cell coverage from the output map.

An avoidance mechanism is unsupported if the misleading condition does not reduce acquired corrective evidence, if only interpretation changes, or if uniform/single/deterministic behavior explains the pattern. Equal convergence can be jointly wrong, so disagreement never substitutes for accuracy. Practical reporting includes corrected cells per twelve inspections and cost, not just a dramatic map.

## Scope and review

The question is useful even if competent actors never fall into a trap. This plan follows the reviewed flagship recommendation while avoiding imposed avoidance and a free-information rescue. It is not an accepted formal hypothesis or a claim that the pending launch gates passed.
