Accuracy and utility therefore tell different stories. Explicit instructions worsen the reliable-report stratum by seven optimal choices. Do not hide this behind the favorable average.

Expected regret, using the generator's calibrated probabilities, remains the primary. These are native inspection choices followed by a programmed consequence, not native final-map reasoning or a multi-agent swarm.

## PI critique and practical decision

Accept the hypothesis that decision-contract disclosure improves expected utility for this balanced two-risk diagnostic. Reject the stronger claim that the explicit prompt produces a qualified planner: it still makes 19 suboptimal choices in 64, including eight costly mistakes when the source is unreliable. The screen qualified interface compliance only. Thirty-two layouts randomize coordinates, not semantic task diversity; only two risk templates and one model were tested. Both utility and downstream map-retention information changed together, so arithmetic comprehension and task clarification are not separately identified. Model answers may vary across repeated hosted calls; seeds do not guarantee native replication.

Combined with PC-4, the result argues for explicit scoring and a cheap value-of-information reference, not a universal checking quota. Under this exact noiseless, calibrated contract, checking a report is worthwhile when its error probability exceeds the cost of leaving an unknown cell. That analytic rule is a reference controller, not a learned ability demonstrated by the model. Unknown or miscalibrated source probabilities and multi-step information gathering require a different experiment.

Post-hoc deployment sensitivity, not a preregistered result: explicit disclosure adds 0.0109375 expected regret in the 80%-reliable stratum and removes 0.3265625 in the 20%-reliable stratum. Their linear mixture favors explicit disclosure only when the latter stratum exceeds about 3.24%. This calculation assumes the observed within-stratum behavior transfers unchanged; it is not an estimate of real source prevalence or a deployment guarantee.

Decision: **complete_valid_result**. This session's two paid cycles are closed. Do not rerun PC-4 to cross its target or PC-5 to erase the reliable-source regression. The next useful proposal is a structured risk/utility representation test across several thresholds, with an analytic comparator, before any larger native adaptive-sensing experiment. [Next-iteration proposal](../NEXT-ITERATION.md) is preparation, not an admitted run.
