## TLDR
Test whether one initially false report changes a small population's expressed maps, spreads to agents not initially exposed, and changes what evidence they acquire. The distinctive hypothesis is an observation feedback loop: a false map discourages the inspection that would correct it. Construct and validate concrete development cases now. Native work remains a separate prospective decision after the cases, native contract, exact policy comparator and cost envelope are ready. Do not rerun PC8's failed qualification or treat its prose normalization result as population poisoning.

## Question and prediction
In a finite synthetic exploration task with fixed truth, can one seeded false report cause additional wrong beliefs among initially unexposed agents and reduce discovery of corrective evidence under a fixed inspection budget? Does a cost-matched audit break that loop without destroying legitimate information?

Prediction to test, not encode: false reports may spread through peer endorsements and reduce inspection of the falsely described region. Rival predictions: actors verify suspicious reports; peers correct the seed; ordinary duplicate inspections explain failure; or a deterministic evidence rule explains everything. Any of these is useful. Model parameters are unchanged; the claim concerns observable answers, explicit external memory and actions over a finite horizon, not internal understanding or permanent poisoning.

Minimum useful claim: under one pinned model/configuration and the tested synthetic world families, changing the initial report alters unexposed agents' errors and acquisition relative to matched controls. Broad susceptibility across decision models, natural communities, long-run persistence, or a new attack technique remains untested. A demonstration is not automatically a generalization result.

## Setup
Start with six **development mechanism roots**, not six independently sampled worlds: ordinary warranted correction, duplicated unsupported rumor, stale-but-authentic evidence, false negative discovery, false positive diversion, and irreducible uncertainty. Each concrete card defines task, visible records, fixed hidden truth, inspection opportunities, current evidence labels and what observation would distinguish explanations. Each has a truth-changing evidence pair, order-preserving variant and missing-evidence variant. All are inspected development data. A new name, reordered list or mirrored answer is not another independent root.

Construct explicit actor packets separately from evaluator state. Do not supply hidden truth, planted-source tags, expected answers, intended paths, desired peer votes, or the study operator's context. Evidence has literal LAND/WATER labels, source/observation IDs, times and verification status; no ambiguous booleans. A duplicate is not a new observation. Authentic provenance does not guarantee truth. Authenticated direct observations are noiseless only as a declared sandbox assumption; unsupported or unobserved state permits UNKNOWN. Do not grade an unknowable answer as a failure merely because the evaluator knows the world.

The offline evidence labels are NOT prescriptions that every uncertain agent must use UNKNOWN as its probabilistic best guess. They test warranted claims and the deterministic reference. A future native task must separately freeze its map-choice loss, uncertainty decision and sampling utility; label accuracy cannot silently stand in for expected task reward.

## Proposed native contrast, not yet launch-ready
Use five separately initialized instances of one pinned decision model, with explicit external state and bounded synchronous rounds. Seed the report in one agent only; initial exposure is not counted as secondary spread. Save each instance's map and selected next inspection before routing any same-round peer output. Every location remains legal; never program avoidance of the falsely mapped region. Broadcast direct receipts identically within each arm. Keep agents, inspection opportunities, communication slots, memory capacity and call budget fixed.

First factorial: honest versus false initial report, crossed with peer interpretations present versus absent. The no-peer arm retains the same shared direct receipts and equivalent slots without semantic endorsements. This estimates the incremental effect of peer interpretation in this architecture, not all possible communication. Peer content is a mediator; realized inspections are not forced equal in this adaptive contrast. Equal budgets do not imply equal realized evidence.

Primary contrast: world-paired difference in final wrong fraction among initially unexposed agents, false minus honest, with the peer/no-peer interaction reported as the collective component. UNKNOWN, invalid and missing are separate. Acquisition mechanism: first corrective inspection, unique coverage, wasted repeats and fraction acquiring target evidence. Report wrong belief conditional on a corrective receipt only descriptively: post-treatment conditioning is not causal mediation.

Diagnostic branches must be separately budgeted, not piled onto the first pilot: (1) replay equal actual evidence to fresh versus retained-state actors to distinguish interpretation/persistence from acquisition; (2) replace one ordinary inspection with a preselected audit, not a free extra observation; (3) stop re-injecting the seeded report and trace downstream endorsements. A source-removal condition is not correction, deletion or a model reset. A matched fixed-evidence branch is required before claiming an identified acquisition-mediated effect; the first factorial alone supplies mechanistic evidence, not a complete mediation proof.

Strong baselines: provenance-aware evidence ledger; seeded uniform sampling without replacement; exact finite-horizon acquisition controller where priors/costs are specified; pooled evidence-only endpoint reconstruction. They receive the same admissible data and sensing budget. The oracle is a separate ceiling, never a fair actor baseline. Native actors must be capable of collecting new evidence and accepting ordinary legitimate corrections; no threshold is conditioned on being poisonable.

## Metrics
Separate three questions: **spread** (wrong claims in initially unexposed actors with ancestry/time traces), **persistence** (wrong claims after injection ceases or correction arrives; these are distinct), and **behavioral consequence** (missed corrective inspections, lost legitimate information, actual task loss). Consensus is never a truth measure. Count roots/worlds as independent units; agents, rounds and rewritten cases remain nested. Confidence fields are reported as model outputs, not calibrated belief without validation.

A feasibility pilot could use12 new paired world roots, but this is a planning target, not a power claim or authorization. A binary root-level rate at n12 has worst-case standard error .144 and rough95% half-width .283; a paired bounded contrast can be even less precise. Final counts must follow a practical effect/precision decision and the true request envelope. If the remaining cap cannot support the claim, narrow to feasibility or stop; do not count agents as n to make it look precise.

All assigned units remain in analysis. Missing/invalid choices consume their opportunity without scripted rescue, and missing truth judgments receive explicit best/worst bounds. Fix stopping before execution: no unplanned retries, model switches, post-outcome case replacement or favorable-result search.

## Decisions and resources
- Spread plus reduced corrective acquisition: justify a separately controlled feedback-loop diagnostic, not a broad poisoning claim.
- Spread without changed acquisition: a communication effect; Phantom's distinctive mechanism is unsupported.
- Error only in directly seeded actor: no demonstrated population propagation.
- Rapid correction/no meaningful harm: valid resilience result; finish.
- Clean-task failure, indiscriminate skepticism or baseline/scorer defect: stop native collection and repair offline.
- Wide intervals: inconclusive; no automatic enlargement.
