## Minimal portfolio discipline

1. Finish required closeout and retain adverse findings from an already authorized attempt before proposing a successor. Do not stop somebody else’s worker from this table.
2. Give one saved-data diagnostic a bounded scope. If the needed records are absent, state the gap; do not generate replacement observations. Reusing inspected evaluation data is retrospective diagnosis.
3. For a selected new run, fill three decision fields in the existing next-run plan: **empirically unknown**, **what support/null/adverse/inconclusive would change**, and **why existing data or a simpler method cannot answer it**.
4. Add expected preparation, analysis/reporting and operator time beside API/infrastructure exposure. These are estimates with assumptions, not measurements inferred from commit counts. Pick the smallest informative stage; exact sample/precision, scenarios, controls and stop rules stay in the one owning plan.
5. Prefer a finished narrow finding and an evidence-linked figure to another list of successors. A presentation change cannot upgrade scientific confidence.
