# Swarm of Theseus: critical review

2026-10-04 UTC. Verdict: **revise before scaling**. This is a retrospective, same-author review, not independent approval or an exhaustive novelty survey. The user is right about the central weakness. Our earlier description of the work as a culture-survival result gave a modest transmission check more conceptual weight than it earned.

## What the pilot actually establishes

Under a specific prompt and one model, supplied procedures were transmitted through bounded text across replacement of three context identities. Notes and notes plus mentoring reached 100% task accuracy, versus 52.08% with neither. This is valid feasibility evidence for the instrument. It does not identify spontaneous culture, cumulative improvement, a distinct benefit of social organization, or a mechanism beyond preserved information.

The execution record is useful: failed qualifications were retained, prospective plans existed, evaluation was deterministic and separately recomputed, and the main study completed. Process quality protects a result from avoidable errors; it does not make the research question novel.

## Mechanistic audit

The [reproducible audit](structural-audit.json) reads saved S1 requests and outcomes. Source inspected: `src/study.py` at executed revision `a773ff5410442fcf351cfc817550b3fc92a88994`. It is new analysis of old evidence, not a new run.

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Every founder begins with the correct executable rule and receipt phrase | The group is not discovering a practice. Copying a short rule is sufficient to solve the task. |
| All 36 recorded mentoring parent edges in mentor/both conditions originate from founders; none originates from a descendant | Complete founder replacement occurs, but mentoring does not test multiple generations of transmission. Notes can still pass through rewritten descendant archives. |
| Seed bank and repair dock recycle all four possible binary feature vectors in each solve | New case IDs and shuffled order do not constitute structural generalization. These are two variants of one rule family, not three independent task families. |
| All 72 post-change repair solve requests contain an explicit bulletin giving the new mapping | This measures following an update despite old notes, not discovering a changed environment. |
| The system prompt orders agents to preserve the procedure and convention; source-ID scaffolding tells actors how to expose their work | Retention is instructed and the instrument helps structure the computation. Useful engineering, weak evidence of emergence. |
| Collective output is a hard-coded majority of three independent answers | No collective practice is necessary for task success. One competent reader with the same rule is an untested sufficient alternative. |
| No-inheritance removes arbitrary world-specific mapping information | The primary contrast combines access to relevant information with transmission format. It cannot isolate a cultural mechanism. |
| A receipt phrase has no task consequence and also disappears among retained founders | Its loss is compatible with salience or output-contract effects. It is not a validated measure of identity, nor specifically turnover-induced loss. |
| Notes, both and verbatim are all at ceiling; two seeds per scenario mostly counterbalance a label swap | More samples of the same design could tighten an obvious contrast without separating explanations. Six world IDs are not six substantively distinct environments. |

The empirical dissociation between correct work and phrase retention is real in this operationalization. Its interpretation is modest: agents can omit an irrelevant string while following a useful rule. The lower repair score among retained founders is an exploratory lead, not evidence that turnover improves adaptation.

## Close prior art checked this session

This targeted check found enough overlap to reject any broad first-of-its-kind claim. It does not establish whether the specific revised factorial comparison is new. No source below is claimed fully read. Existing library records are not promoted to a deeper read level.

- [[perez-2024-cultural]], [Perez et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08882): abstract read. LLM cultural-transmission simulations already manipulate population structure and information transformation. Transmission itself is not a new contribution.
- [[ashery-2024-emergent]], [Ashery et al., 2025 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08948v2): abstract plus selected methods, memory analyses and figures inspected. Their decentralized agents develop conventions through interactions. Our supplied receipt phrase is a weaker construct, not a novel demonstration of emergence.
- [[vallinder-2024-cultural]], [Vallinder and Hughes, 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10270v1): abstract and selected methods/results inspected. Donor-game agents evolve strategies over ten generations, with surviving strategies informing newcomers. Learned social practices and multi-generation turnover are already studied; adding more replacement rounds alone would not establish novelty.
- [Cook et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.00392v2): abstract and selected introduction/comparison text inspected. Cultural accumulation in reinforcement learning is compared with a single lifetime receiving the same total experience. This motivates a resource-matched individual baseline; our pilot has none.
- [Cuadros et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04264): abstract read. This is a Viewpoint with illustrative ecosystem traces, not a randomized efficacy result. It already argues for evaluating shared memory through provenance, correction and institutional selection. “Add memory governance” alone is not a fresh conceptual contribution.
- [The Succession Study](https://successionstudy.org/): public home page read. It explicitly frames files as inheritance across sessions and model changes. Its narrative reports are not equivalent to a controlled benchmark, but they preclude claiming the framing itself is original.
- [Chimento and Aplin, 2024](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/730110): selected abstract/discussion text inspected. Turnover, naive learners and adaptive cultural change are also studied outside LLM research. The effect of turnover on adaptation must not be presented as a newly invented question.

Searches covered LLM transmission and replacement, organizational learning and turnover, and archive/continuity terminology. Remaining work includes full-method comparison, forward/backward citation chasing and the lab's formal saturation requirements. March's original publisher page was inaccessible in this session; this review does not claim to have read its original paper.

## Decision

Retain v1 as a documented transmission baseline and evaluation harness. Do not increase its sample size, produce more cosmetic scenario variants, or frame it as a discovery. Keep the historical results intact and add this assessment to their current interpretation.

Develop v2 around **selective continuity**: can a learned practice cross repeated replacement while descendants preserve what still works and revise what no longer does? The important outcome may be a tradeoff or a negative result: an archive can preserve an obsolete practice perfectly, and a rolling tradition can corrupt a useful one.

The [prospective design](DESIGN.md) makes this discriminating through archive freezing, archive transplantation, multiple generations, functional conventions, unchanged-versus-changed task components, and matched-resource baselines. It is a candidate design under review, not an accepted hypothesis and not a proven novel result. The next scientific milestone is a reviewable instrument that can distinguish copying, reacquisition, adaptation and coordination, before another paid sweep.
