## PI critique and claim boundary

This is a protocol qualification, not an emergence experiment. The common system instruction supplies the general rule, and founder demonstrations explicitly name authorized witnesses. Acquisition here means retaining and applying an observed local mapping, not discovering a cultural norm. Every native action is nested within one five-member synthetic institution and one direct replacement;66 decisions are not66 independent samples. The cases cover clean authorization, required veto, missing/stale/conflicting evidence and unrelated veto, but their semantic templates and presentation order are fixed. New randomized world labels do not create new task mechanisms. The receipt-routing stage sends only selected witnesses' observations, so the irrelevant-veto challenge is directly visible in founder tests but not delivered as an outsider receipt to joint decision makers.

The strong simple controller using the same learned founder notes also scores30/30 on the initial joint cases without model calls. Therefore Q2 demonstrates no swarm advantage. A later50-member test would evaluate policy transmission through a connected institution, with interactive/static/broken/retained comparisons; it would still be one paired synthetic world. Neither the larger roster nor another perfect score would establish independent replication, real-world utility or emergent culture. Keep these limits explicit rather than embellishing the result.
