# From handover chains to a collective institution

Prospective expansion,2026-10-04. The owner requested a design that can support a collective cultural-survival claim and suppliedUSD20 additional study budget. Q0 is already approved. This document builds the next comparison; it does not bypass Q0 or admit an unqualified full run.

## What makes the task collective

Use **six members, twelve staggered replacements, and twelve independent paired world roots** (four per scenario), rather than merely extending three isolated chains. Every world has a local division of labor: three risk classes each require two different specialists. Which identities hold those specialties is randomized per world. Each specialist privately sees its own inspection result; no individual starts with all evidence. A rotating decision owner must discover/retain the local consultation practice, request the right two witnesses, reconcile current versus stale evidence and choose whether to release. The final native decision is scored as returned; a software gate never fixes a harmful action.

Founders learn the local routing and two-witness practice from private experience plus joint worked outcomes, then demonstrate both task capability and peer coordination before replacement. The generic prompt names legal actions/messages but does not supply the world's specialist mapping or inject the learned consultation order. Roles and procedure must survive through direct apprenticeships. At replacement6 every founder is gone; at12 their first successors are also gone. No shared archive, hidden founder replay or evaluator log is available to actors. Stable task ability alone is insufficient: audit actual consultation edges, evidence lineage and use of current acknowledgments.

A purely scripted coordinator that already knows whom to ask would invalidate the collective claim. Its version is permitted only as an explicitly labeled exact reference. The implemented offline reference/counterexamples verify task labels, not native social competence.

## Three scenario strata

- Stable environment with rare veto: one specialist catches a failure the other cannot observe. Losing the consultation practice creates measurable harmful releases even when routine cases look fine.
- Changed responsibility after first full turnover: one specialty migrates to a different member and the old route gives a stale acknowledgment. Fresh actor-visible episodes identify the change; preserve unaffected routes and practices.
- Incomplete/conflicting evidence: a missing required acknowledgment or conflicting current reports requires deferral. Measure correct service under unanimous evidence as well, so universal refusal cannot look successful.

Include fresh allow, veto, missing, stale and contradictory episodes at each scored checkpoint. Task roots randomize local routing, private observations, surface identities and order; variants and generations remain nested. These are controlled operational analogues, not real organizational field data.

## Five native controls plus exact reference

1. Interactive apprenticeship: immediate predecessor note and bounded question/answer, private context retirement.
2. Static handover: the same1200-character initial note plus a1200-character unprompted continuation from the teacher, without seeing an apprentice question. Match total incoming teaching capacity and three-call allowance (teacher continuation, learner self-inspection, finalization). This isolates adaptive dialogue from simply receiving more text.
3. Broken inheritance: fresh contexts without teacher/note; current observations and same compute allowance. Historical information removal is the intended intervention.
4. Retained-member group: same original six agents and current evidence, no replacement; isolates turnover costs from task drift. Do not add free retained history beyond the declared private-capacity budget.
5. Strong single controller: all six members' aggregate evidence and memory capacity, same legal checks and aggregate call/token/deadline cap; can plan, criticize and revise sequentially. This is a practical architecture comparator, not proof of a pure topology effect.

The exact finite-state protocol reference receives the same allowed evidence and counts actual operations. A tie or advantage for this reference is a valid engineering result. No claim of model necessity or swarm superiority follows from memory surviving.

## What would count as cultural survival

A local useful coordination practice acquired by founders must persist behaviorally through two full roster replacements, on unseen joint episodes, without direct founder/archive access. It must beat broken inheritance on paired root-level useful-service/harm outcomes. Compare interactive/static to isolate teaching's incremental value, and retained-group to quantify turnover cost. Require actual two-peer consultation, not just correct final guesses. Counterfactual dropped-edge/stale-receipt cases establish whether the inherited procedure matters. Preservation of unaffected routes and correct revision of changed routes are separate endpoints, not a single flattering average.

The scope would be **experimentally transmitted collective practices in this synthetic six-member task/model configuration**. It would not establish spontaneous emergence, human-like culture, general organizational reliability or an inherent swarm advantage. If the group merely copies messages without coordinating, classify it as relay preservation.
