# Focused research refresh for transmission design

Read scope: abstract/landing page only,2026-10-04. Perez et al., *When LLMs Play the Telephone Game: Cultural Attractors as Conceptual Tools to Evaluate LLMs in Multi-turn Settings*, arXiv2407.04503v4 (2026 revision; ICLR2025 paper): https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04503v4 . The authors study sequential text transmission and attraction in properties including toxicity, positivity, difficulty and length. Agent-to-agent transmission itself is established prior art. Our proposed distinction is useful policy behavior, real context retirement, selective correction and explicit competing storage/teaching routes; novelty remains unestablished.

Search located primary experimental work *Trade-offs, control conditions, and alternative designs in the experimental study of cultural evolution* (2024), https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2322886121 . The indexed abstract describes chains/groups compared with individual learners matched on accumulated time. Full text fetch was blocked; do not claim a methods audit. This motivates conserving total resources and reporting controller contrasts honestly. Another indexed experimental report compares interactive/noninteractive human chains (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.evolhumbehav.2011.01.001); publisher access was denied, so it is a lead, not reviewed evidence or a library entry. This focused refresh is not an exhaustive novelty search.
