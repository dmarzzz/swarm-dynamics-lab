What this establishes

The acquisition repair contained three format failures while retaining 285 of 288 final decisions. It did not eliminate the failures. A social influence effect is not established.

24 authored variants, six construction families and two fresh repetitions do not estimate real-world prevalence. Shared evidence can correlate mistakes across a network; this experiment tests one narrow procurement setting, not a general claim about AGI safety.
