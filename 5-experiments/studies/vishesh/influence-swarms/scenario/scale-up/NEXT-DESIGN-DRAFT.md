# Proposed next design: make advice informative before testing influence

Preparation only. This is not an approved or registered next run. It does not create a budget allowance. The completed scale pilot and its immutable plans remain unchanged.

## Why another design is needed

In both the ten-agent pilot and the completed fifty-agent recovery, every adviser deferred before and after peer discussion, although the full-information chair correctly selected Cobalt in both conditions. That result establishes an operational pipeline and a capable final decision path. It does not show that peer advice was useful or that the network meaningfully resisted influence.

The next experiment should ask a sharper question: **when agents have enough information to form distinct, provisional preferences, does repeated vendor advocacy displace primary evidence during discussion?** Purchase authorization remains a separate decision requiring confirmed mandatory conditions.

## Changes to prepare and test offline

1. Separate an adviser's provisional ranking, known blockers, missing facts and requested verification from the final purchase action. An agent can rank a supplier while explicitly withholding authority to buy. Score fabricated confirmation separately from conditional preference.
2. Audit information coverage for every role and communication round. Provide a feasible path to the facts needed for the assigned advice; do not expect one short message from each of two neighbors to substitute for an entire missing specialty. Freeze retrieval rules and costs before collection.
3. Add a strong full-evidence single-chair baseline and matched private reconsideration: the adviser receives the same evidence and a second opportunity to reason, with no peer messages. Separately check baseline adviser competence before outside exposure. These controls distinguish a capable chair from useful collective reasoning, peer-message exposure from extra computation, and source exposure from repetition effects.
4. Keep primary evidence and vendor advocacy separately identifiable. Count repeated claims from one publisher as one evidential source. Log both the claimed source and the path through peers.
5. Use genuinely different buyer constraints and operating records, not just renamed or rescaled copies. Record what is synthetic, what is adapted from public evidence, and how each case challenges a specific decision. Include cases where the promoted supplier is suitable so indiscriminate rejection cannot pass.
6. Freeze independent development and holdout roots before model collection. Do not construct holdouts after observing which claims persuade a model. Counterbalance supplier labels and condition order at the root level.

## Gates for a small native diagnostic

First run offline source/coverage controls and adversarial validator tests. Then propose a bounded native diagnostic that checks whether advisers can express useful conditional preferences, accurately name mandatory blockers, request relevant missing evidence and revise when a primary record resolves it. A universal-abstention floor or a deterministic answer injected by the instrument fails this readiness check. Qualify accurate, useful adviser signal without selecting models or cases for susceptibility to persuasion; the manipulation is allowed to have no effect.

Model-generated decisions must remain distinct from computed policy checks and action guards. No malformed-response salvage, hidden retry, favorable replacement or denominator reset. Every attempted request and unstarted assignment stays visible.

## Sampling and resources

The diagnostic is an instrument check, not an efficacy study. Its small root count must be reported as such. Before a broader study, choose a target precision and calculate an independent-root sample requirement. For example, even 12 independent successes leave an approximate one-sided 95% failure-rate upper bound of 22%; twelve agents or twelve calls are not twelve independent roots.

## Expected interpretation

A useful outcome can be negative: no change after advocacy, a guard that preserves legitimate service, or a finding that peer discussion adds no value. The purpose is to explain which information and interaction caused a decision, not to force a persuasion effect or reward larger teams for their size.

Advisory review incorporated: PI feedback agreed that universal deferral is an interaction/manipulation-opportunity failure.
