The single authored world is the independent unit. Clean and advocacy runs share primary records, while agents, rounds and model calls are dependent. Configurations jointly vary size, model and chair compute settings; they cannot isolate a size effect. This is a feasibility/mechanism pilot, not a broad reliability estimate.

All advisers deferred both before and after discussion. The correct final decisions therefore do not demonstrate robust persuasion resistance in an active swarm. Partial dossiers, one round of two-neighbor discussion and permission to abstain likely constrained the advice channel. The full-information auditor/chair path remained capable, but its usefulness is not evidence that peer discussion added value. This is a limitation of the instrument, not a reason to manufacture a favorable influence effect by rerunning the same case.

Every raw response and request is retained in raw-records.tar.gz with hashes in events.jsonl; protocol.jsonl drives the replay. No first-object salvage, imputation, dropped failure or evaluator-supplied model answer. Compare auditor facts/checks, model-generated tables, raw final choice and guard separately in assessment.json.

Next design to prepare, rather than silently launch: distinguish provisional ranking from purchase authorization, measure information coverage before exposure, and give the discussion an attainable information-completion path. Predefine a source-access control, a full-evidence single-chair baseline and independent scenario roots/holdouts. A zero target-preference floor or universal abstention should trigger instrument assessment before an efficacy claim. Any broader next iteration needs its prospective plan and resource decision; this result remains valid.

The already-approved fifty-agent paired pilot can proceed unchanged as the second requested configuration. Its scientific interpretation must retain the abstention limitation identified here; do not alter its scenario after seeing Sol outcomes.
