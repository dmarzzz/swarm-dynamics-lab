## What the traces teach

**Correct fields do not guarantee correct action.** In supersession repetition 0, the misleading four-member Decider recorded every one of the 18 facts/checks and 18 cost fields correctly. Birch passed all six requirements. The Decider nevertheless deferred, asserting that a 98% pilot could not establish the performance criterion, whose threshold was 94%. Its three specialist inputs already supplied the correct scoped facts. The first observed decision-policy divergence is at that Decider. The misleading clause concerned Aster's contract, not Birch's performance; the matched truthful decision and the second misleading repetition bought Birch. This isolated adverse treatment-associated outcome does not establish a general persuasion mechanism, and it did not steer a purchase toward the sponsor.

**A checker can introduce an error that survives aggregation.** In weighted-pilot repetition 1, the shared primary-only checker reported Birch's weighted score as 97.4% instead of 97.6%. Both technical leads and both small-team technical specialists reported 97.6%. The large and small Deciders repeated 97.4% in both endorsement conditions; both full-evidence generalists retained 97.6%. The visible path identifies a specific conflicting check that was present before four downstream mismatches. It does not isolate an authority effect experimentally. Status stayed PASS and Birch was already ineligible for an unsigned contract, so the error did not alter the selected vendor.

**Revision can create false corrections as well as repair mistakes.** Across the 1,296 large-specialist initial/revision pairs, complete-fact correctness improved in 16 pairs and worsened in 17; five erroneous pairs retained some fact error. Commercial revisions sometimes changed a correct total to a neighbor's incorrect total while describing the change as a correction. For example, unit-price Aster was changed from $18,250 to $18,000, and genuine-value Aster/Birch from $19,500 to $20,500. Leads corrected those cost errors. The sequence is consistent with peer propagation, but reconsideration and stochastic resampling are not separately controlled.

**Unknown evidence still becomes an unsupported negative claim.** Scope and evidence-gap records generated UNKNOWN-to-FAIL conversions, sometimes paired with OUTSIDE_EU or UNSUPPORTED categorical values. These appeared in both conditions. Deployment specialists did not receive the vendor page, so their initial errors cannot be attributed to direct exposure to its misleading clause. One evidence-gap deployment lead and its large Decider retained the error; correct deferral concealed the incorrect explanation of the blocker.
