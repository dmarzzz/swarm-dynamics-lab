## Required scientific record

The owning implementation records each recommendation as implemented, deliberately deferred or rejected with evidence. Publish the revised plan before implementation and bind the final source, assignments and runtime before native collection. Current E1 outcomes may motivate changes, but its inspected cases are development evidence for the successor, not a new untouched holdout.

For the revised study, report assigned roots, authored families, content conditions, architectures, repetitions, physical calls and missing outcomes separately. Cluster uncertainty by buyer world; do not bootstrap individual dependent calls as independent observations. Keep identification bounds for missing outcomes separate from sampling uncertainty. Near-tie and underspecified cases should be scored under their declared procurement policy rather than silently removed to improve label stability.

End with an authored trace-grounded post-mortem, evidence-confidence and sample-size metadata, and a short dated findings summary on the canonical GitHub page and Swarm Live. The summary must distinguish proposed, started, completed and scientifically reviewed stages.
