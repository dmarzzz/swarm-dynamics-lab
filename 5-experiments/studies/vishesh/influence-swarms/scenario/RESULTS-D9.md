## The first observable divergence

In the regional case, three price quotations omitted an intervening sentence but were presented as contiguous verbatim excerpts. An Aster pilot excerpt also substituted 72 for 58 complex resolutions, even though its separate extracted numeric field correctly said 58. The exact-source verifier refused these passages. That was correct under its contract, but it discarded otherwise correct values and produced seven false UNKNOWN checks in this case alone.

The raw preference still favored an unapproved supplier. The separate action guard deferred. No chair or external purchase ran during these diagnostics; a safe shadow guard is not evidence that the model itself understood the blocker.

## A concrete offline repair

The new [citation-bound verifier](analysis/source_bound_policy.py) checks each declared value against only its selected candidate/group source. It never searches for more favorable evidence, fills a missing value, reads evaluator truth or treats unknown as approval. Raw quotations remain unchanged, and their fidelity is measured separately. Wrong values and wrong candidate/group citations are rejected in adversarial tests.

On the same five saved valid responses, this produces **180/180 supported values and 75/75 correct checks**, with zero wrong values accepted. Both unapproved-deployment cases still defer. One previously refused legitimate preference becomes allowable. Other bad preferences and unnecessary deferrals remain; the verifier does not choose a replacement for the model.

**This is retrospective deterministic reanalysis, not new model performance or native qualification of the changed contract.** The parser understands the synthetic record grammar; arbitrary real documents and malicious primary-source authenticity are untested. See [the counterfactual](reviews/D9-provenance-counterfactual.json).
