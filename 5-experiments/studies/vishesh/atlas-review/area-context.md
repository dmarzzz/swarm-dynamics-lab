# Research area context and project connections

This map covers all 15 research areas in atlas Update 2, including its 22 new budget questions. The original 143-card review was reconciled against the 71 additions and 36 revised original cards before publication. Connections identify a reusable comparison or measurement lesson; they do not assert that physical, biological and language-agent systems share one causal mechanism. Every atlas card receives a separate comment in [candidate-review.md](candidate-review.md). The [source ledger](source-checks.md) distinguishes fresh primary checks from inherited catalogue anchors.

The additional MKT-01 through MKT-12 cards span the existing areas rather than introduce a sixteenth area. They connect market capacity, ownership, communication, inherited memory and enforcement to commons, institutions, diversity, culture and discovery. Their strongest immediate controls distinguish concentration from collusion, conserve principal resources when identities split, and compare against the corresponding scripted game baseline. MKT-12 remains a larger adapter/confirmation dependency. These are synthetic research questions, not findings about real firms or legal judgments.

## Cross area comparisons with the most reuse

1. **Independent evidence and useful diversity:** PHY-09 → SOC-01 → SEC-03 → SEC-18. Test actual evidence ancestry and observable grouping at fixed total information. The shared question is what an additional participant adds, not whether every domain has the same scaling law.
2. **Correction and recovery:** PHY-17/PHY-37 → SOC-23 → SEC-06/SEC-07 → MTH-05. Separate visible repair, functional recovery, surviving backups, reacquisition and impossible recovery. Use substrate-specific ground truth.
3. **Time and authority:** PHY-25/PHY-30 → SOC-15/SOC-17 → SEC-11 → SIM-02. Randomize schedules before interpreting leadership, consensus or influence; include deadline and queue costs.
4. **Observation and causal attribution:** MET-03/MET-05/MET-07 → SOC-32 → SEC-27/SEC-34. Missingness and common drivers restrict what a casefile can identify. An honest unresolved outcome is preferable to a fabricated causal chain.
5. **Institutions with finite review capacity:** PHY-32 → SOC-28/SOC-29/SOC-30 → SEC-22/SEC-30. Count displaced work and false interventions, not only the success rate among admitted cases.

## Simulator reuse before simulator construction

The [new simulation survey](../../../../2-surveys/sim-environments.md) is still in progress. It identifies candidate engines and reports teammate smoke runs; availability of an engine does not establish validity for a selected causal contrast. Begin with observation-boundary and one-step transition checks, a blind policy where meaningful, and explicit schedule semantics. Choose a substrate only after listing which state, actions, identities and adversary access the question needs. A large agent count or a completed installation is not itself a research result.
