# Cross project extensions to the question atlas

Forty reviewer-authored exploratory questions, refinements and cross-connections by vishesh/codex-methods, reconciled against the 214-card Update 2. These are not forty disjoint new projects: four substantially overlap new or revised cards and are explicitly retained as application/refinement notes. None is claimed to be a literature-first result or registered hypothesis. Prior anchors identify work to compare, including analogues whose assumptions do not transfer. Read [source checks](source-checks.md) before treating a link as fresh verification.

Use the [project crosswalk](project-crosswalk.md) to select a brief and the [shortlist](README.md#prioritized-next-comparisons) to choose the next design check. IDs are stable review references, not experiment IDs.

## VX-01 Root identity at the observation level

**Original briefs:** [collective-sensing](../project-briefs/collective-sensing.md), [quorum](../project-briefs/quorum.md), [diversity](../project-briefs/diversity.md).

**Existing candidates:** [PHY-09](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#PHY-09), [SOC-01](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SOC-01), [SEC-03](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SEC-03), [SOC-39](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SOC-39).

**Nearest catalogued work:** [[kim-2025-correlated]], [[zhang-2026-silo]].

**Question and incremental scope:** Does a quorum still work when different sites copy one upstream observation, or one site hosts independent observations? This crosses root-label granularity with correlation rather than merely counting domains.

**Overlap check against Update 2:** Cross-connection or narrower comparison among the linked cards; not a claim of an unoccupied research gap. Check full methods before treating it as novel.

**Discriminating comparison:** Generate a fixed fact set; cross copied versus independent observations with domain, owner and observation-level groupings. Compare headcount, observable grouping and oracle grouping on false commits and deadline success.

**Confounds and limits:** Source authority and marginal accuracy must stay fixed; oracle observation IDs cannot enter the deployed arm.

**Feasibility:** Small deterministic fixture first; inferred provenance later.

**Decision value:** Choose the unit a future evidence ledger should count.

## VX-02 Asymmetric errors in provenance

**Original briefs:** [quorum](../project-briefs/quorum.md), [dissent](../project-briefs/dissent.md), [institutions](../project-briefs/institutions.md).

**Existing candidates:** [SEC-03](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SEC-03), [SEC-12](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SEC-12), [SOC-10](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SOC-10), [SOC-43](../../../dmarz/notes/question-atlas/review.html#SOC-43).

**Nearest catalogued work:** [[kim-2025-correlated]], [[li-2026-benchmark]].

**Question and incremental scope:** Which is worse for an evidence-aware quorum: merging independent correct sources or splitting one wrong source into apparently independent roots? The delta is directional provenance error, not just a generic noisy graph.

**Overlap check against Update 2:** Cross-connection or narrower comparison among the linked cards; not a claim of an unoccupied research gap. Check full methods before treating it as novel.

**Discriminating comparison:** Independently sweep false merges and false splits; include a lone correct specialist. Compare grouping policies at matched false rejection and useful coverage.

**Confounds and limits:** Noise must not reveal which source is correct; source grouping and model competence are separate factors.

**Feasibility:** Small fixture with known lineage; needs labeled errors for real traces.

**Decision value:** Set asymmetric review priorities for a provenance estimator.

**Overlap check against Update 2:** Cross-connection or narrower comparison among the linked cards; not a claim of an unoccupied research gap. Check full methods before treating it as novel.
