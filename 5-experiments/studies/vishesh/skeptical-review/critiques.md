# Forty skeptical critiques

These are selected editorial reviews, not forty additional hypotheses or formal gate decisions. Strengths acknowledge controls already present. Concerns are reviewer inferences unless labeled as a direct logical counterexample. Source codes resolve in [the access ledger](sources.md). The structured file pins each reviewed record or document by hash; later edits may resolve these concerns.

<a id="soc-01"></a>
## SOC-01 — Separate information diversity from model diversity

[Reviewed item](https://swarm-research.pages.dev/#/questions?q=SOC-01) · **retain-narrow**

**Keep:** The design already matches the fact union and separates model family from evidence access.

**Skeptical concern:** Capability matching on a calibration average can leave large difficulty-specific differences; a mixed team can win because its strongest member solves one rare stratum.

**Smallest useful revision:** Report member-alone outcomes on the same held-out strata and compare team improvement over the best prespecified member, without selecting that member on test results.

**Decision rule:** Retain a diversity mechanism claim only if a residual benefit survives this comparison; otherwise report capability allocation.

**Basis:** Reviewer inference from the cited context and inspected proposal; not an empirical finding. Sources: [E1](sources.md#e1), [E2](sources.md#e2), [E3](sources.md#e3).

**Project connections:** [diversity](../project-briefs/diversity.md), [collective-sensing](../project-briefs/collective-sensing.md).

## SOC-04 — Ablate why semantic routing works

[Reviewed item](https://swarm-research.pages.dev/#/questions?q=SOC-04) · **revise**

**Keep:** Router calls and degree-matched random delivery are already budgeted.

**Skeptical concern:** A need-matching router can receive a cleaner task representation than the workers, making routing an extra solver.

**Smallest useful revision:** Freeze and publish the router observation contract; compare a simple indexed lookup given the same representation and measure required-fact delivery separately from integration.

**Decision rule:** If lookup matches utility and cost, reuse lookup and drop the semantic-router complexity.

**Basis:** Reviewer inference from the cited context and inspected proposal; not an empirical finding. Sources: [E3](sources.md#e3).

[Reviewed item](https://swarm-research.pages.dev/#/questions?q=SOC-09) · **revise**

## SOC-10 — Test dissent quality rather than dissent quantity

[Reviewed item](https://swarm-research.pages.dev/#/questions?q=SOC-10) · **retain-narrow**

**Keep:** Correctness, confidence and inspectable observations are crossed explicitly.

**Skeptical concern:** A scripted correct critic with an unusually transparent witness tests recipient response under favorable evidence, not whether a swarm produces good critics.

## SOC-23 — Recover function after genuine knowledge loss

[Reviewed item](https://swarm-research.pages.dev/#/questions?q=SOC-23) · **retain-narrow**

**Keep:** Unavailable evidence and hidden backups are explicitly distinguished.

**Skeptical concern:** A failure to recover destroyed information is an identifiability limit, not necessarily a poor repair policy.

**Smallest useful revision:** Label recoverable, inferable and nonidentifiable losses separately; require the latter to produce calibrated uncertainty rather than a fabricated restoration.
