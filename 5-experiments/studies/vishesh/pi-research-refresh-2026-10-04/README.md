## What changed because of this pass

- **Independent evidence:** retain a fully informed single agent and measure duplication, correlation and handoff loss before concluding that larger swarms help. Task dependency structure belongs in later size and specialization designs.
- **Causal peer effects:** later dissent/influence comparisons need private reconsideration and content controls. An answer flip alone is not an influence mechanism.
- **Useful verification:** Phantom should only reopen for a task where uncertain information acquisition matters. Quorum's distinct source IDs still do not certify statistical independence.
- **Realistic outcomes:** Immune needs verified recovery and collateral service loss; Theseus needs acquired knowledge before inheritance; routing/OCR needs wrong-acceptance risk and total cost.

These are design inferences from the sources below, applied in each linked study note. Existing frozen diagnostics remain unchanged. Missing controls constrain their claims; they are not retroactively invented or quietly added to approved packets.
