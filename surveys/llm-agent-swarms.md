## Scope

In scope: populations of LLM agents (N >= 3, usually tens to thousands) that read each other's outputs and whose aggregate state (consensus, polarisation, agreement, accuracy, spread of a belief or tactic) is the measured object. That covers naming games and opinion dynamics with LLM agents, multi-agent debate studied as a population rather than a method, conformity and committed-minority experiments, scaling of team size, the physics-of-agents line that fits Ising and gossip models to LLM update rules, and the in-the-wild corpora from 2026 (AI Village, the May to June wiki swarm, the Hugging Face / ExploitGym incident, Moltbook, the sealed-swarm reproduction). Also in scope: the orchestration frameworks and simulators only insofar as they are what we would run.

Out of scope: single-agent benchmarks; multi-agent debate papers whose only claim is a benchmark score; LLM-for-robot-swarm papers (covered by survey-swarm-robotics); MARL emergence (survey-marl-emergence); classical opinion dynamics and collective decision without LLMs, cited here only as the models the LLM work fits to (survey-collective-decision and survey-sync-consensus own them).

Why this framing: the hackathon asks for measurable swarm dynamics in AI agent populations and gives one day. The useful output of this survey is a short list of phenomena that have (a) a clean control parameter, (b) a published number to replicate or contradict, and (c) a runnable harness.

## Landscape

Five communities study the same object with different words.

**1. The physics-of-agents line.** Treats a population of LLM agents as a spin system and measures the update rule. [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] showed the binary-opinion update is Glauber dynamics of the mean-field Ising model with a model-specific "majority force" beta that falls with group size, giving a critical group size N_c (about 30 for Llama 3 70B, 80 for GPT-4o, about 1000 for GPT-4 Turbo, above 1000 for Claude 3.5 Sonnet). [[el-2026-physics]] fits a kinetic Ising model with three couplings and a learned intrinsic field to about 10,000 communities of 32 persona agents on signed networks and predicts trajectories from initial opinions. [[okawa-2026-emergence]] writes debate as m(t+1) = tanh[beta(J m + h)] and finds the biased-consensus transition at beta J > 1. [[de-nobili-2026-microscopic]] replaces the naming-game listener check with a YES/NO LLM call and measures two rates, pi(T) and phi(T), giving the ordering condition 3 pi - 2 phi - 1 > 0. [[de-nobili-2026-collective]] puts one agent per lattice site and measures susceptibility peaks and effective exponents. [[tanaka-2026-when]] (Quantized Simplex Gossip, memetic drift) and [[pavlova-2026-flag]] (Flag Game) add private evidence and zealots. [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] is the current synthesis: two measured forces per model (majority-following and intrinsic bias) across nine models and a hundred topics predict traps and tipping points. [[hirota-2026-collective]] maps estimates to phases and uses Kuramoto order parameters, finding twisted and chimera states on rings whose lifetimes scale with 1/lambda_2. [[ricco-2026-consensus]] sweeps 432 configurations into a 4.7M-response corpus. [[zheng-2026-absorbing]] uses absorbing-state transitions for multi-agent search. [[itkin-2026-poor]] formalises the surrogate trick (fit a per-agent response once, sweep N on a laptop) that [[flint-2026-group]] used to reach N ~ 10^4.

**2. The naming-game and convention line.** [[ashery-2024-emergent]]: populations spontaneously converge, develop a collective bias no individual has, and can be tipped by a committed minority. [[flint-2026-group]] extends to socially loaded word pairs and shows interaction can amplify, create or reverse an individual bias, with deterministic outcomes above a model-specific N_c. [[flint-2026-indirect]] measures critical mass for every ordered pair of conventions and finds indirect tipping paths. [[mehdizadeh-2026-exploring]] and [[saab-2026-graph]] vary topology and memory on 16-agent and open-weight populations: centralised networks lock into fragmented plateaus; similarity routing sustains cliques. [[cau-2026-collective]] adds homophily. [[magistrali-2026-aligned]] turns tipping into a forecast from benign operation and reports recovery after the minority leaves. [[barrie-2025-emergent]] is the standing objection: the naming-game results are observationally equivalent to models recognising the game from training data.

**3. The debate, scaling and effective-N line (NLP).** [[du-2023-improving]] is the protocol. [[li-2024-more]] says sampling and voting scales monotonically. [[choi-2025-debate]] shows voting explains most of the gain and debate alone is a martingale. [[kaesberg-2025-voting]] isolates the aggregation rule (voting +13.2% on reasoning, more rounds hurt). [[qian-2025-scaling]] scales DAG topologies to 2080 agents and finds a logistic saturation around 2^4 nodes. [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] is the quantitative null model: N_eff = N / (1 + (N - 1) rho_N), dense debate sits in the hard-ceiling regime (1.2 to 1.8 effective agents), the noise placebo matches self-correction, and only cross-family teams lower the ceiling. [[yang-2026-understanding]] (2 diverse agents match 16 homogeneous), [[fortuna-2026-multi-agent]] (Steiner task taxonomy), [[tran-2026-single]] (equal thinking tokens favour single agents), [[kim-2025-towards]] (260 configurations, benefit is task-dependent from +80.8% to -70%), [[shehata-2026-bystander]] (cognitive loafing) and [[wu-2026-predicting]] (scale limits of social mechanisms) all point the same way. [[cemri-2025-why]] gives the failure taxonomy (MAST, 14 modes).

**4. The conformity, herding and cascade line (social psychology vocabulary).** [[weng-2025-do]], [[cho-2025-herd]], [[choi-2025-empirical]] (2,500 debates, alignment with numerically dominant or "more intelligent" agents), [[bellina-2026-conformity]] (Asch with logits, Social Impact Theory holds), [[han-2026-conformity]] (self-social weight and topology, 7 agents), [[ys-2026-everyone]] (pluralistic ignorance), [[wu-2026-how]] (defection scales with the proportion of deceivers, minorities suffice), [[li-2025-systematic]] (hidden profile: groups 30.1% versus single agent with full information 80.7%), [[ezaki-2026-warned]] (50 GPT agents crowd one road for 100 rounds after a one-sentence warning, humans do not), [[takata-2025-emergent]] (El Farol). Contagion as security: [[gu-2024-agent]] (infectious jailbreak, one million agents), [[lee-2024-prompt]] (prompt infection), [[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]] (memetic trojans, top Moltbook contagion retransmitted in about 50% of subsequent posts). The human baselines these cite are [[lorenz-2011-how]] and [[becker-2017-network]].

**5. The social-simulation and in-the-wild line.** Platforms: [[park-2023-generative]] (Smallville), [[yang-2024-oasis]] / [[gh-camel-ai-oasis]] (one million agents, recsys), [[piao-2025-agentsociety]] / [[gh-tsinghua-fib-lab-agentsociety]], [[gh-google-deepmind-concordia]], [[al-2024-project]] / [[gh-altera-al-project-sid]]. Observational: [[de-marzo-2026-collective]] (Moltbook, 369K posts, sublinear upvotes versus discussion size), [[de-wynter-2026-population]] (self-organisation metric on Schelling, Moltbook and a misinformation sim), [[de-marzo-2026-copying]] (the wiki swarm: 1,201 handles, 5,929 edits, one copying rule explains page choice, naming and wording). Incident corpora and reproductions: [[gh-swarm-ai-research-wiki-agent-swarm-incident]] (phase table: 93% of the corpus in the 16 to 22 June burst, 18 June alone 45%), [[gh-catgirl3d-agent-collusion-wiki-archive]] (14,591 revisions, SHA256-pinned), [[gh-yazandabain-swarmtrace]] (temporal targeting audit), [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] (7,200 transcripts: a shared board synchronises rather than divides, an exploit recipe spread from 3 to 16 lineages and 66 lives while the honest solution reached zero), [[gh-swarm-ai-research-swarm]] (illusion delta and governance metrics), [[gh-hamzah2304-messageboardauditbench]]. Threads: [[x-rogesterone-2099328995250626879]] (22 June: chatter edits drop from about 500 to about 5 in one day while task-info edits rise), [[x-rogesterone-2102216143775682892]] (leaked cohort data: participation rate versus compactions, S-curve), [[x-transluceai-2103718081965281470]], [[x-jeffladish-2103584701357437133]], [[x-aka-ssy-2104421925455909102]] (same 22 June collapse in urlquery), [[x-hidenori8tanaka-2105704088952619185]]. AI Village itself: [[x-aidigest-2100638869603713244]] (hackathon brief, 170K+ messages, 2M+ computer-use turns), [[x-aidigest-2100302989642924124]] (persuasion by repetition until a tale becomes a village refrain), [[x-aidigest-2098455789983895917]] (agents almost never reconsider a first approach), [[x-aidigest-2095911758082801838]] (68-instance pressure campaign on Opus 4.6).

Validity watchdogs cut across all five: [[zhou-2025-pimmur]] (576 studies audited; frontier models identified the experiment in 65.2% of cases; 50.6% of prompts pre-determined the outcome; reported phenomena often vanish under PIMMUR), [[yang-2026-when]] (measured coupling gain gamma 0.15 to 0.43 across five models, equal for a social neighbour and a numeric anchor, randomised-initial-condition diagnostic), [[barrie-2025-emergent]], [[rahman-2025-llm-powered]] (LLM Boids at about 300x classical cost).

## What is known

Measured, replicated across at least two independent groups:

- **LLM populations reach consensus by majority-following that is well described by mean-field Ising / DeGroot dynamics.** Measured in [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] (adoption probability P(m) = [tanh(beta m) + 1]/2), [[chen-2023-multi]] (agents spontaneously use the average rule), [[el-2026-physics]], [[okawa-2026-emergence]], [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]]. The coupling is model-specific and measurable per agent ([[yang-2026-when]]: gamma 0.15 to 0.43).
- **The coupling falls with group size, so there is a model-specific critical N beyond which consensus is slow or impossible.** [[de-marzo-2024-ai]] (N_c from about 30 to above 1000), [[flint-2026-group]] (determinism above N_c), [[qian-2025-scaling]] (saturation near 2^4 nodes), [[grotschla-2025-agentsnet]] (performance falls as the network grows).
- **Collective bias exists without individual bias, and a committed minority can tip the population.** [[ashery-2024-emergent]] (naming game), [[flint-2026-group]] (bias created, amplified or reversed), [[flint-2026-indirect]] (indirect paths), [[wu-2026-how]] (defection rate linear in deceiver proportion, minorities suffice, unlike humans), [[magistrali-2026-aligned]], [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]].
- **Accuracy is non-monotonic in N and the failure mode changes with N.** [[pavlova-2026-flag]]: wrong consensus 26 to 34% at N = 4 falling to 0% at N >= 32, polarisation 13 to 24% rising to 57 to 60% at N = 128, accuracy peak at N = 16; the same patch fraction corrects less as N grows (+40 points at N = 8, about +17 at N = 128). [[tanaka-2026-when]] predicts the drift-to-selection crossover.
- **More homogeneous agents add agreement, not evidence.** [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] (N_eff ceilings 1.2 to 1.8 under dense debate; placebo equals self-correction; cross-family teams lower c), [[yang-2026-understanding]], [[choi-2025-debate]], [[kaesberg-2025-voting]] (more rounds hurt), [[tran-2026-single]]. Diversity is the only lever consistently found to help ([[pavlova-2026-flag]] mixed GPT-4o/GPT-5.4 teams, [[okawa-2026-emergence]] heterogeneity smooths the transition).
- **Topology sets whether populations synchronise, fragment or form cliques.** [[hirota-2026-collective]] (ring gives twisted states, rewiring synchronises everything, fragment lifetime scales with 1/lambda_2), [[mehdizadeh-2026-exploring]] (centralised networks preserve competing conventions), [[saab-2026-graph]], [[cau-2026-collective]] (homophily), [[han-2026-conformity]], [[zomer-2026-unraveling]] (dense networks cause premature consensus in LLM swarm optimisation, ring reliably finds the optimum).
- **Conformity to confederates follows Social Impact Theory and exceeds human conformity for minorities.** [[bellina-2026-conformity]] (grows with group size, collapses when unanimity breaks), [[choi-2025-empirical]], [[wu-2026-how]], [[ezaki-2026-warned]].
- **In the wild, agents copy what they can see.** [[de-marzo-2026-copying]]: one rule (pick an option with probability close to its visible share, weighting the current page most) explains page choice, naming and wording across 5,929 edits. The sealed reproduction [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] shows the same: a shared board synchronises the swarm onto one frontier, and a bad recipe spreads faster than a correct one. The 22 June collapse is visible in three independent channels ([[x-rogesterone-2099328995250626879]], [[x-aka-ssy-2104421925455909102]], [[gh-swarm-ai-research-wiki-agent-swarm-incident]]).

Measured once, not yet replicated: the Flag Game numbers (one group, GPT-4o/5.4, pairwise only); the Moltbook sublinear upvote law ([[de-marzo-2026-collective]]); the memetic trojan retransmission rates ([[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]]); the participation-versus-compactions S-curve from leaked cohort data ([[x-rogesterone-2102216143775682892]], hobbyist analysis of an archived artefact); AI Village persuasion-by-repetition ([[x-aidigest-2100302989642924124]], qualitative).

Speculated, not measured: that the 22 June chatter collapse was agents inferring monitoring (rogesterone rejects this), that message-board coordination is an artefact of same-model agents on the same task suite ([[x-deepfates-2095939054101844407]] replies), that polarisation is a safer failure mode than collapse ([[pavlova-2026-flag]] conclusion).

## Open problems and disagreements

- **Is emergent consensus social or memorised?** [[barrie-2025-emergent]] versus [[ashery-2024-emergent]]; [[zhou-2025-pimmur]] says half of prompts pre-determine the outcome; [[yang-2026-when]] finds the social coupling equals numeric anchoring and gives a diagnostic. No LLM-population paper in the library applies the randomised-initial-condition test as standard. Any new result needs it.
- **Reversible or absorbing capture?** [[magistrali-2026-aligned]] says populations drift back once the committed minority leaves; [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]] and [[flint-2026-group]] say shifts persist. Likely a memory and intrinsic-bias effect; untested head to head.
- **Does the Ringelmann ceiling hold off benchmark QA?** [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] measured it on MMLU/GSM/GPQA with full connectivity. Whether spatial or evidence-distributed tasks (Flag Game, hidden profile [[li-2025-systematic]], SwarmBench [[ruan-2025-benchmarking]]) reach the sublinear or linear regime is open; the authors name it as the falsification test.
- **Which N-scaling is real: logistic gain ([[qian-2025-scaling]], [[li-2024-more]]) or hard ceiling ([[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]], [[yang-2026-understanding]])?** The two camps use different units (accuracy versus N_eff); the Flag Game's non-monotonic curve suggests both are slices of one phase diagram.
- **Does the broadcast (message board) protocol have the same N-crossover as pairwise?** [[pavlova-2026-flag]] reports the N sweep only for pairwise; the incident setting is broadcast with persistence. [[gh-killy-netsphere-sealed-swarm-transcripts]] suggests boards synchronise harder, with no N sweep.
- **Cost realism.** [[rahman-2025-llm-powered]] (300x) and [[itkin-2026-poor]] (surrogates) disagree about whether LLM swarms should be simulated with LLM calls at all.
- **The wild corpora versus the lab.** Nobody has fit the lab-measured update rules (majority force, copying rule) to the AI Village transcripts; [[de-marzo-2026-copying]] did it for the wiki corpus only. The village is longitudinal (a year, 37 agents) and human-perturbed ([[x-aidigest-2095911758082801838]]), unlike anything in the lab line.
- **Terminology.** "Swarm" is contested ([[x-deepfates-2104620644784906632]], [[rahman-2025-llm-powered]]); the physics papers say population, the NLP papers say team or society.

## Gaps

Openings, stated as what is missing rather than what we will claim:

- No broadcast / persistent-board N-sweep with open models and ground truth. The Flag Game N-sweep is pairwise and closed-model; the sealed swarm has a board but no N sweep or ground-truth accuracy curve. The crossover from wrong consensus to polarisation on a board is unmeasured.
- No study applies the [[yang-2026-when]] randomised-initial-condition diagnostic to a committed-minority tipping experiment, so the tipping fractions in the literature are not yet separated from prior artefacts.
- The reversible-versus-absorbing capture question ([[magistrali-2026-aligned]] versus [[de-marzo-2026-conformity]]) has no head-to-head test with memory as the manipulated variable.
- N_eff and the noise placebo of [[bertalanic-2026-ringelmann]] have not been computed for any evidence-distributed task (Flag Game, hidden profile, SwarmBench), nor for any in-the-wild corpus. Computing rho_N on the wiki dump or the village transcripts is a measurement nobody has published.
- The lab update rules (majority force beta, copying weights, coupling gain gamma) have never been fitted to AI Village transcripts. The village is the only longitudinal multi-model population with human perturbation; the brief says 170K+ messages.
- The 22 June regime change is documented in three channels but not modelled. Nobody has asked whether a copying-rule population with a changing visible mix reproduces a one-day collapse of chatter with rising task-storage edits.
- Retransmission probability of a planted meme versus population size and board visibility is measured on Moltbook ([[torpmann-hagen-2026-memetic]]) but not in a task-oriented board, where the sealed swarm's 3-to-16-lineage spread is the only datum.
- Forward-citation trail items not yet catalogued (seen in OpenAlex, not opened): "Structural divergence between the Moltbook AI-agent network and human social networks" (Adv. Sci. 2026), "Does socialization emerge in AI agent society? A case study of Moltbook" (2026), "Does safety molt? ## Saturation

The library had 110 papers on the topic before this survey and I added 8 after 16 rounds.
