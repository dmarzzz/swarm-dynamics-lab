## Summary

Author thread on a single-author preprint. Claim: words that became much more popular in 2023, such as "commendable", signal ChatGPT/LLM-derived text in scholarly papers, and he estimates upwards of 60,000 papers in 2023, and rising. An aside notes one of the highest relative growths was for "outwith". Later self-replies point to a same-day detector-tool study of preprint servers with differential effects by discipline and country, and (replying to others) praise a related excess-vocabulary study and suggest LLM-usage disclaimers may become standard. The 60,000 figure is his estimate from word-frequency shifts, not a direct count.
