## Relevance to us

Byzantine eventual consistency is a direct primitive for fork-and-merge security: replicas that diverge and later merge while some participants may be malicious. The earlier announcement [[x-martinkl-1334166327750221827]] describes the related system as immune to Sybil attacks. The talk is an alternative route into [[kleppmann-2020-byzantine]].
