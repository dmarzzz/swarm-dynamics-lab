## Summary

Opinion thread by Ethan Mollick, not a paper author, citing [[liang-2023-gpt]]. Restated from the paper: detectors are easy to defeat by re-prompting a couple of times and have very high false-positive rates on non-native English speakers (no figures given in the tweets). His own claims, unsupported by numbers here: detectors trained on GPT-3.5 are beaten by GPT-4; a flag cannot be verified; students working interactively with AI defeat them, per his class (linked blog post); and an LLM asked whether text is AI-written cannot tell and may invent an answer. Advice: do not use detectors in classrooms.

## Relevance to us

A reminder that per-document text classifiers are a weak basis for detecting AI agent swarms and carry a fairness cost through false positives. Swarm detection should lean on behaviour and coordination across accounts, not single-text scores. See [[liang-2023-gpt]] and the contrary view in [[x-tszzl-1995734804172579251]].
