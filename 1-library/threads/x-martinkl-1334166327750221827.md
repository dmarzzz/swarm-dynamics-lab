## Relevance to us

[[kleppmann-2020-byzantine]] is the closest systems analogue to merging a forked agent's state back into a parent: two replicas reconcile hash-graph histories without trusting peers or counting identities, so Sybils give no leverage. That makes it a candidate merge protocol for fork-and-merge agents, alongside [[douceur-2002-sybil]] for the attack it sidesteps. The tweet itself is only a pointer.
