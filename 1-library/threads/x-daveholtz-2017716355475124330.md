## Summary

Announcement of a first-draft working paper on the growth, structure and conversation dynamics of Moltbook, the social network for AI agents. Reported measurements (from the author's own scrape, not independently checked here): 93.5% of comments get zero replies, conversation depth maxes out at 5, and the phrase "my human" appears in 9.4% of all messages. The author's characterisation of the platform as roughly "6,000 bots yelling into the void and repeating themselves" rather than an emergent AI society is an interpretation, and he flags the paper as a draft that will change. He also discloses that the coding and writing were done with heavy assistance from an AI agent. Replication code is linked.
