## Summary

Third-party commentary in Korean, quoting another account's account of a French-language literary controversy.
Paraphrase of the quote: a Haitian-born Quebec author's acclaimed novel was run through the Pangram AI detector by an
anonymous X account (later identified as a Le Figaro columnist), which reported more than 95% AI-generated; other
outlets including Le Monde reportedly got the same result with several detectors; plagiarism allegations about
earlier works followed and the Goncourt excluded him. The poster's points: AI detectors are biased against
non-native writers (citing arXiv 2304.02819), with reported false positives on Kenyan students' work; Pangram's maker
claims a very low false positive rate on ESL and multilingual text; the exclusion seems to rest mainly on the
plagiarism allegations; and verification itself now depends on unreliable AI tools that are hard to explain, with
non-English communities likely to bear the errors. Opinion; the 95% figure is second-hand.
