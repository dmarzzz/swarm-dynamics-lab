## Summary

Author thread, verified (James Zou is the senior author). Measured, as estimates from a statistical model: about 17% of recent CS arXiv papers used LLMs substantially in their writing, and about 8% of bioRxiv papers. Certain words ('showcasing', 'pivotal', 'realm') rose sharply in frequency after ChatGPT's launch and are more typical of LLM scientific writing. The estimator fits the full vocabulary at the corpus level, following the authors' earlier method for peer reviews. Opinion: LLM modification can be beneficial but can change tone and interpretation, and transparency matters.
