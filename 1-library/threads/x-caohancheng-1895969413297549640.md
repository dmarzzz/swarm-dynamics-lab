## Relevance to us

[[liang-2025-widespread]] is a population-level estimate of machine-written text, the kind of aggregate signal that can show AI activity in the wild without classifying single documents. Its per-domain adoption curves are a baseline any swarm-detection claim about a platform would need to separate from ordinary assisted writing.
