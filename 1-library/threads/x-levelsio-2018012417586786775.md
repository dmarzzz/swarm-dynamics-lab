## Summary

One heavy X user's count of AI reply bots he detects and blocks by hand: over 6 per day and almost 200 per month at the time of posting, versus "just a few per week" the previous year. This is an n=1 observation on one large account's replies, with manual, unvalidated labelling and no false-positive check. His extrapolation that social media will be "99% AI by next year" and the claim that AI replies are hard for AI but easy for him to detect are speculation. He quotes an earlier tweet offering his roughly two-year "blocked for AI reply" log to X as training data, and in replies says the point is to chart the growth and give X data. He states he is in favour of AI participation that adds to conversations.
