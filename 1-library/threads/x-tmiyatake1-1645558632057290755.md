## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by a third party, not an author. He restates the paper: 25 AIs with their own motivations and memories were placed in a town; they woke up, made breakfast, recognised each other and formed opinions while working. Agents talk in natural language and remember what was said the day before; a memory model decides which memories matter; reflections themselves are stored. The part he found most interesting: Isabella, who wanted to host a Valentine's Day party, told a few agents, they told others, and by the end of the simulation 12 agents attended. He speculates open-world games could become more dynamic.
