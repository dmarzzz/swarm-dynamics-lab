## Summary

Third-party commentary on the linked paper (arXiv 2502.12150). The root says the
attached chart (not archived) shows the actual unique giveaway words for each model relative to each other, as
opposed to folklore words like "tapestry" or "delve". In a follow-up he clarifies that these patterns separate AIs
from each other, not AI from humans, and repeats his opinion that AI detectors cannot tell human from AI writing with

## Relevance to us

Model fingerprinting, telling which model family wrote a text, is directly useful for swarm detection: a swarm run
on one model should share these idiosyncrasies across accounts even if no single post is flagged as AI. The paper
[[sun-2025-idiosyncrasies]] is the source; the thread is a pointer and a caution not to conflate model attribution
with human-vs-AI detection ([[sadasivan-2023-can]]).
