## Summary

Six-tweet author thread by Onur Varol, a listed co-author of [[sayyadiharikandeh-2020-detection]]. Problem: bot classifiers degrade on out-of-domain bots. Method: Ensembles of Specialized Classifiers (ESC) train a separate model per bot class and combine their judgements. Claimed results: significantly better F1 while keeping AUC and precision, tested on their training data and on novel out-of-domain datasets (no figures in the thread); ESC flags mislabeled training instances (about 1%); it can learn new bot behaviours from few examples and grows by adding models. The models were to be deployed as Botometer version 4.

**4. @onurvarol, 2020-06-15 05:31 UTC** (status 1272401292925829126)

**5. @onurvarol, 2020-06-15 05:31 UTC** (status 1272401294511267840)
