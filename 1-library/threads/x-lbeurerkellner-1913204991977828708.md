## Relevance to us

Points to the residual hole in control/data separation defences like [[debenedetti-2025-defeating]]: if untrusted data can steer a tool-composing interpreter, a forked part can still be hijacked. The author's own patterns paper [[beurer-kellner-2025-design]] covers this class of defence; see also [[x-edoardo-debe-1938699790629343297]].
