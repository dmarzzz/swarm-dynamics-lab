## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by Kentaro Matsui, not an author, who wrote an earlier related paper. He summarises the preprint: it processed over 20 million PubMed abstracts from 2010 to March 2024 (14.2 million used in analysis), compares word frequency ratios and differences for 2022 vs 2024, runs earlier year pairs as controls (2018 vs 2020 surfaces COVID-19 words), and breaks down usage by field, country and journal (higher in Frontiers and MDPI; Cureus and Sensors named; lower among UK and native English authors, high among Asian authors; Japan not named). He compares it with his own hand-picked-word study, notes his finding that some terms rose from about 2020 is not covered, and thanks the authors in a reply.
