## Summary

Author thread (James Zou is the last-listed author of [[el-2026-physics]]; the thread says the work was led by @elb4tu). Setup: more than 10,000 communities of LLM agents, each a social network of agents with different personas, discuss questions ranging from math problems to statements such as "Nuclear power is essential for clean energy", talk with neighbours and update opinions over several rounds. Reported: some communities reach consensus and others polarise; an Ising-type model fit to agent behaviour predicts a community's future and generalises to new social graphs and new questions; the stated key insight is that agents drift to low-energy states that minimise social pressure. No accuracy numbers are given in the thread. Calling this important for multi-agent safety is the author's framing. The thread appears complete (5 tweets, the last labelled n/).

## Relevance to us

A fitted energy model of normal agent-community dynamics is a baseline against which injected or coordinated agents could show up as residuals, and a way to predict how many planted agents tip a community (both our inferences, not tested here).
