## Summary

Commentary in Chinese; the poster is not among the paper's authors. He describes a Stanford HCI group's town of
25 freely living AI characters built on a large language model, which produce believable, emergent social
behaviour. Example from the paper as he relays it: starting only from one user-specified idea that an agent
wants to host a Valentine's Day party, the agents spread invitations over two days, made new acquaintances,
invited each other and coordinated to show up together. A self-reply links the online demo town.

## Relevance to us

The party example shows one seeded idea spreading through an agent society without further prompting, which is
the same mechanism by which a single corrupted agent could spread influence. See [[park-2023-generative]] and
[[x-h-okumura-1645697312356966405]].
