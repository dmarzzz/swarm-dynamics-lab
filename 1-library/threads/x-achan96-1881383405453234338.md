## Summary

Author thread (19 tweets) announcing a research agenda paper. The claim is conceptual, with no measurements: alignment-style safety work does not cover how agents interact with institutions (the legal system) and other actors, so we need "agent infrastructure", defined as technical systems and shared protocols external to agents that mediate their interactions with and impacts on their environments. Analogies offered: TCP/IP, BGP and HTTPS for the internet, and traffic lights and speed cameras versus driver training. Examples given in the thread: protocols that tie users or a company's legal identity to its agents (building on existing authentication such as OpenID), so counterparties know a legal entity is on the hook, and commitment devices for coordination between agents. Open challenges named: interoperability, ease of use, updatability. The paper is said to cover use cases, adoption, limitations and open questions for each type of infrastructure.
