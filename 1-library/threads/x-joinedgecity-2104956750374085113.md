## Summary

Announcement of a field experiment: hundreds of residents each with a personal agent for three weeks at Edge City India. Stated goals: increase useful coordination between humans and agents, and minimise multi-agent risks (misrepresentation, manipulation, coercion, collective resource capture). Research questions listed: do agents represent principals faithfully, when is human approval needed, what happens when agents compete for shared resources, can individually aligned agents produce collectively misaligned outcomes. Partners: Microsoft, Cosmos Institute, Index Network (technical), NYU Ethical Intelligence Lab, World, Renaissance Philanthropy; agent platform includes Nous Research. Links a Cosmos post on V1 results. No results yet.
