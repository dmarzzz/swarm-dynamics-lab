## Summary

Author's 11-tweet summary of the OSoMe paper on the fox8 botnet. Measured: the team searched Twitter for the self-revealing phrase "as an AI language model", then narrowed to 1,140 accounts that coordinately shared links to three suspicious websites. The accounts follow each other densely, retweet and reply to each other, mix original tweets, retweets and replies, use stolen images for fake personas, and push crypto/blockchain/NFT content and the three sites. Detection results: Botometer did not work; GPTZero did not work; OpenAI's (since withdrawn) AI-text classifier gave some signal but had very high false-positive rates in the field. Inferred, not measured: the underlying LLM is "very likely ChatGPT", and it was instructed to generate content including negative or harmful comments. The closing claim that "the future many have been worried about is here already" is the author's opinion. All 11 numbered tweets are present.

## Relevance to us

The canonical in-the-wild LLM botnet case and the thread version of [[yang-2023-anatomy]]: a self-revealing-phrase seed plus a coordinated-link-sharing filter, with a released dataset we could reuse. Its negative result (Botometer and single-text AI detectors fail; the OpenAI classifier over-fires) is the baseline any detector we build has to beat. Feature-based bot detection background is in [[ferrara-2016-rise]] and the pre-LLM labelled accounts are in [[data-cresci-2017]].
