## Archived text

By one new count, 31.1% of web text in August 2026 was written by AI.

That number comes from a paper published this week. The team didn't stop at counting. They trained 800 language models on different mixes of human and AI-written web text and measured what happened.

The short version: AI text can help a little, then its value flips.

Train on the web as it is today, and you need 1.6× the compute to match a model trained only on the human part.

The strangest finding: repeating the same human text beat adding fresh AI text.

What this is not: proof that AI is "collapsing". The models were small, up to 973M parameters. The AI share is measured with Pangram's detector, and the work comes out of Pangram. AI text still helped when the goal was predicting AI text.

But the direction is hard to ignore. Every lab wants more data, and nearly a third of what's on the web is now machine output.

Human writing just became the scarce resource.
