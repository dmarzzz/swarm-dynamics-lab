## Summary

Third-party summary in Japanese of the linked paper (arXiv 2501.15654) on how well humans detect AI-written text. Paraphrase: five experts read 300 articles and judged AI or
human, getting 299 right; people who often generate text with LLMs detect best, and they use originality and
consistency as cues, not only AI-sounding phrasing. In replies the poster says Japanese, as a high-context language, may be easier to judge than
English, and that they strip comments from generated code. Then they disclose that a reply he posted (saying overly
correct politeness was often judged unnatural) was written by DeepSeek R1 from a prompt he shows, and that a second
reply was written by Claude 3.5 Sonnet. The 299/300 figure is the poster's reading of the paper.
