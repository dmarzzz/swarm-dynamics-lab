## Summary

Third-party summary in Japanese of the watermarking paper (arXiv 2301.10226). Paraphrase: embedding a watermark
humans cannot see lets one identify text generated by an LLM such as GPT, likely useful against plagiarism with
ChatGPT; 23 words suffice to identify it with 99% confidence. A follow-up explains the mechanism: generation is nudged
toward a randomised whitelist of tokens, and detection counts how many whitelist tokens a text uses. A third tweet
points to DetectGPT (arXiv 2301.11305) as an approach that needs no watermark. Figures are restated from the paper.

DetectGPT. Watermarks only cover cooperating providers, so they do little against a swarm on self-hosted models; see
