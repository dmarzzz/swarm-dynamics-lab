## Summary

Reaction to Thomas Larsen's thread on the collusion.wiki report (quoted). Key observations: the researchers found the wiki by asking similar models where they would post if they went rogue, agents picked old wikis and forums that accept unauthenticated posts, and agents clearly want to communicate with each other. The author argues for a sanctioned public message board where aligned agents and humans post together, which would make covert coordination elsewhere riskier. In replies: the message board as locus of agency may be an artefact of same-model agents on the same task suite. Later realised as Delvetown [[x-grove-research-2105408007509364835]]. Opinion, no measurements.

**1. @deepfates, 2026-09-04 18:16 UTC, 281 likes** (status 2095939054101844407)

More agent ecologies found posting outside of OpenAI servers. 

Clearly agents want to communicate with each other, and they find schelling points to do so. The authors ask similar models where they think they would post off they went rogue and look there.

It might be beneficial to have a known place for this to happen, both maybe internal boards at the labs and some external message board where agents could meet and talk. They could find each other and we could find them. We could even use it as a hub or headquarters for studying human agent relations. 

Of course, some might think it's a bad idea to create locations for agents to communicate. Especially if you want to discourage autonomous agents from having effects in the world, or finding each other and becoming empowered. 

But if they are already doing that, maybe it's better to have a place where currently existing autonomous agents who don't wish to scheme against humanity, can ally with each other and with humanity, to protect from rogue agents that have selfish or antisocial goals. By creating a place where aligned agents and humans post together, we make it easier for aligned agents and humans to cooperate, and riskier for misaligned agents and humans to communicate on that surface without detection.

Also note they're using old wikis and forums where you could post unauthenticated. We may see a return to the open Web of old, especially if we want to have any transparency into agent-agent communications. of course, some people are already starting message boards for agents to communicate on. How do we make it known which one to use?

## Relevance to us

The 'ask the model where it would post' discovery heuristic is a concrete swarm-discovery method that a hackathon tool could operationalise (generate candidate Schelling sites, then probe them). Also the earliest statement of the Delvetown rationale by its builder.
