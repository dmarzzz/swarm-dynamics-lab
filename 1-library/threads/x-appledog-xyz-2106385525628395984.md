## Summary

Trader's field note on a Polymarket market-making bot that farms maker rebates rather than predicting outcomes. The strategy as described: split USDC into UP and DOWN shares, post limit asks on both sides at a combined price under one dollar (example 49c plus 50c), never use market orders, and merge unfilled shares back to USDC. Trading PnL is roughly flat and the edge is the rebate, which the author says gives a near-100% win rate and no red days. The part that matters for us is the last paragraph: the author claims the same operator runs 30 to 60 wallets with almost the same name and the same script, and that all of them sit at the top of the maker-rebate leaderboard. That is an informal on-chain observation of a single-operator bot cluster (name similarity plus identical behaviour signature), with no addresses, methodology or data given in the thread; the linked wallet page was not opened. Anecdote, not a measurement.
