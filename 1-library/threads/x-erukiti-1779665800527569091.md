## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by erukiti, not an author. Three tweets explaining the paper's method: send the same prompt to one LLM several times with randomness (for example higher temperature), then pick an answer mechanically, by majority for multiple-choice or by overlap with other answers for open text; the poster says this alone reportedly improves performance. He contrasts it with the earlier Ask Me Anything prompting (no prompt editing needed) and notes that 'agent' in this paper just means a single LLM or an ensemble. No numbers are given.

## Relevance to us

Sampling-and-voting is the simplest 'swarm' of LLM calls and the baseline any fork-and-merge scheme should beat; it also merges outputs by majority, which a single corrupted branch cannot easily dominate. Primary source [[li-2024-more]].
