## Archived text

Proof of "I'm human" will become the foundation for agents, dating, lending and gating. But the authors of the paper on personhood credentials name 4 risks:

1. Who the system will leave without access

If the barriers created by PHCs are too high, some users may stop using them and thus miss out on the privacy benefits they provide.

2. Will people speak freely under "verification"

It is important to consider how PHCs may reduce people's willingness to speak freely and express dissent online. People may fear that their online statements will be linked to their real-world identity, even if PHCs do not reveal who they are.

3. Who will get power over issuing proofs

One of the significant problems of the PHC ecosystem is the concentration of power in the hands of a small number of institutions. User information is one potential source of power for the issuer.

4. How it will be broken and where it will make mistakes

A PHC system, like any digital system, is vulnerable to attacks and exploits from many actors, first and foremost subversion by the issuer itself, service providers and malicious users.
