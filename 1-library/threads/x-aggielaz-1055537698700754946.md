## Relevance to us

The causal-influence measure is a candidate signal for spotting agents that coordinate: an agent whose actions consistently shift another's is detectable from traces. It is MARL background rather than work on adversaries or merging. See [[jaques-2019-social]] and the co-author's post [[x-caglarml-1055186965493149696]].
