## Relevance to us

Relevant as a counterweight to "more agents is better" ([[li-2024-more]], promoted in [[x-karmedge-1776944056683139156]]): if adding calls can hurt, then a swarm's size is a tunable with an optimum, not a monotone lever. For our work it suggests sweeping swarm size in any fork-and-merge experiment rather than fixing it. Links [[chen-2024-are]].
