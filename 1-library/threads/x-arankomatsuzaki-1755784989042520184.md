## Relevance to us

Points to [[li-2024-more]], where accuracy rises with the number of sampled agents under majority voting. Majority vote over many agent instances is exactly the aggregation step that Sybil copies or corrupted sub-agents would try to swing, so the paper is a useful baseline for how many bad votes a merge can absorb. The tweet adds nothing beyond the title.
