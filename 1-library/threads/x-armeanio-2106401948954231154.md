## Summary

Armeanio separates the low cost of creating AI economic agents from the resource cost of influencing a proof-of-work system. The thread predicts that cheap bots will attack transaction ordering and markets, while PoW-derived ordering preserves a scarcity constraint. It supplies no experiment, quantitative cost model, or proof that this protection carries across layers.

## Archived text

AI can make it almost free to create economic agents.

That means one actor could create thousands of bots to spam transactions, compete for ordering, or manipulate markets etc. at very low cost in many systems. 

PoW creates a different constraint identities are cheap, but meaningful economic influence still requires real world resources.

AI makes participation abundant. 

PoW keeps influence scarce.

Pow derived economic ordering enforces this sybil constraint in other layers. 

Which probably will be a good thing as AI adoption accelerates.
