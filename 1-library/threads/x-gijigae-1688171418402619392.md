## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by a user, not an author. He says MetaGPT handles requirements, design, development and QA with GPT-4 alone and calls it a practical Mixture of Experts. From his own use: a blackjack game with class diagrams, code review and docs took about 10 minutes and $2 or less; GPT-3.5 could not build stable systems; errors such as wrong initialisation order occur. Speculation: MoE evolution leads to AGI, and MetaGPT's goal of auto-developing ~2000-line systems will likely be reached by January. Anecdotal and promotional.
