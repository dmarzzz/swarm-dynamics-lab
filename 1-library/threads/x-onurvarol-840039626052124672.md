## Relevance to us

[[varol-2017-online]] is the origin of the Botometer-style prevalence estimate, and [[x-floriangallwitz-1551857999215484929]] argues such estimates violate base-rate reasoning and mostly count humans. Reading both side by side is the right calibration for any population-level swarm estimate we make; see also [[ferrara-2016-rise]] and the labelled accounts in [[data-cresci-2017]].
