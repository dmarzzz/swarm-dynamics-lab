## Summary

Commentary in Japanese by a third party, not an author. He found the title striking and the paper interesting, and restates its point as: perhaps 40-50% of crowdsourced human annotators are already secretly using GPT or other LLMs, so services that looked automated but were done by humans have actually become AI work. The 40-50% figure is his rounding of the paper's estimate; another post on the same paper quotes 33-46%.
