## Summary

Product launch post from Steve Hind, co-founder of Lorikeet (customer support AI), announcing an "agent traffic detection" feature in what the company calls its B2A (business-to-agent) suite. The one data point: by Lorikeet's own measurement, "depending on how you define it", up to 3% of inbound customer support traffic is now from AI agents and other automated systems, and that share has grown quickly over the past three months. He attributes the expected continued growth to consumer agents he names as Instict (sic), Muse, Dots and Grok bot. The post carries a demo video that the API does not return, so the detection method is not described in the text. Vendor claim, no methodology, no sample size. Still one of the few concrete population-level estimates of agent share in a non-web-crawl channel (support tickets and chats), which is why it is logged.
