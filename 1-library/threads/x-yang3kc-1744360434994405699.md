## Summary

Author thread (7/7, complete) by Kevin Yang; the paper lists Kai-Cheng Yang, and the handle, lab tag and co-author mention indicate the same person. Measured: a dataset, TwitterGAN, of 1,353 fake accounts using GAN-generated profile faces; they pose as humans and spread spam, run scams and amplify messages in coordination through automation, some verified. Because GANs place the eyes in the same spot in every image, a simple face-and-eye locator detects them. From a random user sample the lower bound of active users with GAN faces is 0.021-0.044%, which with an earlier estimate of 40 million daily actives gives 8k-17k accounts (the thread headline says about 10k). The view that AI-powered fake accounts are here to stay is opinion.
