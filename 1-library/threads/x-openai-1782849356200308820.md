## Summary

Organisational announcement by the OpenAI account (the authors' employer). Claims, without numbers: the Instruction Hierarchy is safety research to improve robustness against prompt injection and other ways of tricking LLMs into unsafe actions; models process text from several sources that can conflict, and are taught to follow the highest-priority input. The second tweet also advertises Safety Systems hiring. Promotional framing; results are in the paper.
