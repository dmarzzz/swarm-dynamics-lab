## Summary

Two-tweet author announcement that "BadMerging: Backdoor Attacks against Model Merging" [[zhang-2024-badmerging]] was accepted at CCS 2024. The claim stated in the tweet: in model merging, an attacker who controls only one of the contributed models can compromise any task of the merged model. The second tweet credits co-authors from UCLA, Meta and CISPA. No attack success rates or other numbers appear in the tweets; they are in the paper.

## Relevance to us

This is the weight-space version of our core attack: corrupt one part, and the merge corrupts the whole. [[zhang-2024-badmerging]] says one bad contributor suffices, which argues against merge rules that simply average parts; compare the later [[zhang-2026-roguemerge]] and the merge-time defence literature such as [[yuan-2025-merge]].
