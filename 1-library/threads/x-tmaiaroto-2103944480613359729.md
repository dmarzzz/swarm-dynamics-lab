## Summary

Short three-tweet announcement pointing at the phosphor POISONING.md document ([[gh-hackafterdark-phosphor]]). The author summarises the design in one line: incoming data destined for memory is scanned for secrets, there are levels of human review, and memory entries carry an HMAC to prevent manual tampering. The third tweet records a design decision worth keeping: the author considered letting himself hand-edit and "correct" the agent's memory, then rejected it on the grounds that the editor might one day not be him, which is the threat model behind the tamper seal. The substance lives in the linked doc; this entry exists to archive the pointer and the author's stated rationale.
