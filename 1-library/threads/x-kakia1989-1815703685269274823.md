## Relevance to us

The paper [[pan-2024-sybil]] is our mechanism-design anchor for Sybil-proofness: if agents in a collective bid for a resource, splitting into Sybils is a profitable deviation under most rules except a second price auction. It sits in the false-name-proofness line with [[yokoo-2004-effect]] and [[conitzer-2010-using]].
