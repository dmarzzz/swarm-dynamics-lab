For 98 days, I have logged every AI crawler that visited my site, one by one. 120,000 requests from 81 countries.

What I found is simple.
Half of them come without identifying themselves.

The other half, the ones that officially declare who they are, turn out to have blurred edges when you look closely. Some traffic claiming to be OpenAI does not fall within OpenAI's official IP ranges. OpenAI via Azure, OpenAI outside Azure. Of 62,185 requests from Microsoft Azure infrastructure, only 427 identify themselves as Bingbot. The rest are all nameless "something from Azure."

Deeper still, of the 11,343 honeypot engagements, 5,737 (exactly half) are still labeled "Requester Identity Pending." No name can yet be assigned. Only the evidence is preserved.
