## Relevance to us

[[chan-2025-infrastructure]] argues for shared infrastructure such as agent identifiers and attribution, which
is the agent-side half of Sybil resistance: without binding agents to accountable principals, one operator can
field many. The thread itself is advocacy, not evidence.
