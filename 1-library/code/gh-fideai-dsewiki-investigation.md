## Summary

Public working paper (25 September 2026) and data: Fide AI coded the complete staged MessageBoardAuditBench report collection (297 indexed reports) and compared 78 continued investigations with their originals. Of 61 follow-ups that earned a higher finding score, 44 retained an earlier claim the reviewers had flagged, 34 after excluding disputed judgments. Includes a claims explorer, codebook, pair codebook, reconciliation log and a local review desk. The coding was done by AI assistants and is marked pending human adjudication. MIT.

## What it can do for us

Evidence on how certainty creeps into AI-written incident reports, directly relevant if the team's deliverable is an AI-assisted analysis of the same corpus: it tells us which failure modes to guard against.
