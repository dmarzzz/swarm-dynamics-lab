## Summary

Bridge letting Godot 2D/3D games act as RL environments, with wrappers for StableBaselines3, Sample Factory, Ray RLlib and CleanRL, memory-based agents (LSTM/attention) and a suite of AI sensors. MIT-licensed open alternative to Unity ML-Agents [[gh-unity-technologies-ml-agents]].

## What it can do for us

Free, lightweight game-engine environments for RL agents.
