## Summary

Patchwork is a seven-agent spatial heist simulator with separate agent histories, capability-scoped tools, deterministic intent settlement, and a later private-profit economy. The owner-accessible repository contains the game and an economy pilot. Local, uncommitted extensions contain a restriction-bypass factorial, market-rule conformance tests, and asynchronous causal audits. Those extensions are distinct instruments; results from them are not results from the heist economy.

## Limitations

The bypass pilot uses one model and five replicates per cell. The market suite is scripted conformance under complete mediation, known ownership and atomic settlement. The audit pilot assumes a known simple motif and explicit provenance; it does not establish semantic ancestry of arbitrary model outputs. The economy panel has three cases per communication treatment and does not establish a treatment ranking. Shared public evidence remains an information channel even in the no-messaging arm. Neither the legacy aligned objective nor the private-profit objective establishes malicious intent. Source access, licensing, a frozen revision containing the extensions, and a fresh reproduction must be resolved before reuse.
