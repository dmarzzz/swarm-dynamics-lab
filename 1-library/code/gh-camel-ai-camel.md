## Summary

Coordination model: role-playing dialogues between inception-prompted agents, agent societies, and a workforce pattern for task decomposition, with many model backends, tools, memories and environments. The community's stated aim is to study agents at scale to find scaling laws, and it is the parent of OASIS (one-million-agent social simulator) and the CAMEL paper already in the library as [[li-2023-camel]]. Apache-2.0, 17.8k stars, active.

## What it can do for us

A research-oriented framework whose authors care about large-N behaviour; OASIS is the piece we would actually use for diffusion or polarisation runs.
