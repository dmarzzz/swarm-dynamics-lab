## Summary

Toolkit: primitives for discrete or continuous, single-symbol or variable-length communication between neural agents (FFN, RNN, GRU, LSTM, Transformer), trained with REINFORCE or Gumbel-Softmax; pairs or populations of agents; zoo of signalling, reconstruction and referential games including those from published emergent-language papers; grid-search CLI. LLM-native: no. Weight: PyTorch, CUDA optional.

## What it can do for us

Ready games and channel primitives for emergent-communication experiments in populations.
