## Summary

Platform with three parts: a simulation toolkit with predefined industrial scenarios (container inventory management, Citi Bike repositioning, virtual-machine scheduling, asset management) and reusable building blocks for new ones; a full-stack RL toolkit (agent manager, learners, actors, shapers); and a distributed toolkit for communication, cluster provisioning and job orchestration. Also supports operations-research decision mechanisms.

## What it can do for us

Event-driven resource-allocation scenarios with many interacting decision agents; a model of how to package domain simulators for multi-agent RL.
