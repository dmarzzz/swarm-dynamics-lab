## Summary

Coordination model: environment-driven society. Agents are stateless workspace-bound records driven by Ray tasks over modular environments with hot-pluggable tools, multiple reasoning patterns (CodeGen default, ReAct, plan-execute, two-tier, search routers), trace and replay handles. Papers: AgentSociety (arXiv 2502.08691) and AgentSociety 2 (arXiv 2607.11895). Apache-2.0, 1.3k stars, active.

## What it can do for us

An alternative to OASIS with an urban/mobility environment and built-in replay, if we want agents moving through a space rather than posting on a feed.
