## Summary

Reference PyTorch implementations of the value-decomposition and actor-critic MARL algorithms of the SMAC era (QMIX, VDN, COMA, IQL), with the training loop, replay buffer and config system that many later MARL repos copied; tied to the StarCraft II environment. Apache-2.0, 2.2k stars, last commit 2021-08-08.

## What it can do for us

Background for MARL baselines; superseded by BenchMARL and JaxMARL.
