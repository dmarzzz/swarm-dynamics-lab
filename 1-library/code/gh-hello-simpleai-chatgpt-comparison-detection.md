## Summary

Repository of 'How Close is ChatGPT to Human Experts? Comparison Corpus, Evaluation, and Detection' (arXiv 2301.07597): HC3, paired human and ChatGPT answers to the same questions in English (reddit_eli5, open_qa, wiki_csai, medicine, finance) and Chinese (open_qa, baike, nlpcc_dbqa, medicine, finance, psychology, law), plus RoBERTa and GLTR-feature detectors. On Hugging Face as Hello-SimpleAI/HC3 and HC3-Chinese.

## What it can do for us

Historical first human-versus-ChatGPT corpus, widely used to train early detectors; useful mainly to show how detectors trained on early ChatGPT transfer (badly) to newer agents.
