## Summary

A six-firm simulated supply chain evaluates long-horizon business behavior with different roles and dependencies. The project provides a realistic direction for studying inter-agent contracts, inventory, payments and recovery.

## Limitations and open questions

Role heterogeneity does not itself identify a benefit from mixing models. Full 90-day trajectories are expensive compared with a bounded mechanism test.
