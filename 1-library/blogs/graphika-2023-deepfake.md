## Summary

Landing page and summary of a Graphika report (page metadata dated 7 February 2023) on Spamouflage, the pro-China influence operation, promoting videos of AI-generated fictitious news presenters from late 2022, almost certainly made with a UK company's commercial AI avatar platform whose terms prohibit political content. Graphika calls it the first state-aligned operation it had seen promoting AI-generated fictitious people on video. None of the Spamouflage videos it reviewed got more than 300 views. Within weeks other actors used the same tools for videos about Burkina Faso.

## Key claims

- Commercial AI avatar products let IO actors scale video content.
- The content was low quality and drew little authentic engagement.

## Relevance to us

Background: an early documented case of a known coordinated network adopting generative tools, cited by Meta ([[meta-2024-adversarial]]) and OpenAI ([[openai-2024-covert]]). Detection here came from tracking the known network, not from identifying AI content.
