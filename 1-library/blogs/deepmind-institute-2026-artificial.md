## Summary

This essay treats intelligence as an orchestration of interacting systems and develops a symbiotic framing for agents. It is a conceptual argument, not a controlled demonstration of a major evolutionary transition.

## Key claims

The argument motivates ecological questions; it does not certify any HX frontier as novel.

## Evidence quality

Opinion and synthesis; the essay disclaims representing an official institutional position.

## Relevance to us

Distinguish biological interpretation, behavioral variety, and experimentally isolated heterogeneity.
