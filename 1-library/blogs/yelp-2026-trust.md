## Summary

Yelp's primary release supplies a concrete count of suspected AI-written reviews filtered by its own automated systems, alongside account and coordinated-review enforcement totals. These are observed enforcement outcomes, not classifier-independent labels or a census of all AI-assisted reviews.

## Key claims

- Published 2026-02-25, reporting calendar 2025: nearly 500,000 suspected AI-generated reviews filtered globally; approximately 22 million total reviews contributed globally January 1 to December 31. Their approximate ratio is 2.27%, calculated here, not detector sensitivity or a total AI-prevalence estimate.
- Method: automated recommendation software uses hundreds of quality, reliability and activity signals; dedicated AI-detection technologies flag suspected synthetic reviews. User Operations combines proactive investigations and user reports for broader fraud enforcement.
- Over 1.3 million policy-violating accounts closed (+138% YoY), primarily airline phone scams, not all AI-written accounts. Nearly 2,000 review-ring/suspicious-activity accounts closed in US/Canada; 363 suspicious-review alerts involve single-IP/connected reviewer patterns.
- Global review status: about 70% recommended, 17% not recommended, 11% removed by User Operations, 2% self-removed. These are different categories from AI flags.

## Evidence quality

Primary enforcement counters with explicit geography and dates. Selected positives, proprietary labels and no released per-review ground-truth corpus.
