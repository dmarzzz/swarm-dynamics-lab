## Summary

Originality estimates AI assistance in Amazon reviews with its own detection API, reporting increases after ChatGPT and associations with star ratings and verification. The measurements concern classifier output on a sample, not verified fake reviews or a census of AI accounts.

## Key claims

- Page dated 2025-11-17. Method: randomly selected over 26,000 reviews, cleaned of identifying information, then a representative subset of 2,000 processed by the vendor detector. Introductory wording and 26k source size should not be mistaken for 26k classified reviews.
- Reported approximately 400% increase since ChatGPT from an average annual 0.02 proportion in 2022 at a threshold of at least 50% AI content; absolute final fraction and precise collection end date not supplied in text. Do not invent a current prevalence percentage.
- Extreme 1/5-star reviews 1.3x as likely classified AI; verified reviewers '1.4x less likely'; helpfulness correlation -0.06. Detector probability is not a measured fraction of truly synthetic words.
- Source code/notebooks/cleaned dataset are said to be linked; reuse licence not confirmed here.

## Evidence quality

Primary commercial-detector study, no independent manual labels or confusion matrix for this review domain. Temporal growth is observational.

## Relevance to us

Review-spam prevalence context that needs independent coordination labels. Keep Yelp, X and Bluesky estimates separate rather than extrapolating this Amazon sample.
