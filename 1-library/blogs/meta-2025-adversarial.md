## Summary

Meta describes coordinated inauthentic behavior, criminal scam networks and AI-related threats in a restructured semiannual report. Expert investigations and platform enforcement provide stronger operator-level labels than content classifiers, but account-removal totals are not prevalence estimates for the full platform.

## Key claims

- Report dated December 2025, scope second to third quarters of 2025. Meta moves from quarterly to twice-yearly reporting. Accessed 2026-10-03 through the primary transparency page with Exa text retrieval.
- Almost 500,000 public figures enrolled in a facial-recognition protection program. This is a protection count, not detected AI agents or CIB accounts.
- Method: deep expert investigations combined with scaled integrity and threat-disruption systems, organized around infrastructure, digital assets, engagement, execution and cleanup.
- The retrieved opening sections name CIB cases including Endless Mayfly and Russian use of authentic operators in sub-Saharan Africa. Network/account takedown totals were not present in the retrieved portion and are not invented here.
- Threat indicators are shared through facebook/threat-research, which gives operational links but not a complete labelled session corpus.

## Evidence quality

Primary platform enforcement narrative, partial report text. Attribution and enforcement reveal selected positives, not a representative sampling frame. AI assistance does not imply every coordinated account is autonomous.

## Relevance to us

Supports campaign/operator labels and cross-campaign testing. A detector cannot use takedown dossiers alone to estimate false-positive rates without matched non-CIB controls.
