## Summary

The talk connects biological collective intelligence to embodied robot systems. At 23:37-25:49, TERMES-style construction compiles a target into local growth rules while preserving staircase accessibility for robots that cannot climb vertical walls. Repeated sensing rather than a remembered construction history supports some repair. At 46:21-48:42, implicit visual coordination is proposed as an alternative to underwater messaging, but idealized boids assume sensing and motion perfection that even real fish lack. BlueSwarm therefore investigates minimal sufficient local information rather than claiming thousand-robot underwater deployment. Late Q&A distinguishes fast bridge construction from material efficiency.

## Relevance to us

Mechanism-level examples for local invariants, repair and constrained communication. Highlights the cost of embodying an abstract coordination algorithm and limits of assuming perfect neighbor-state observations.
