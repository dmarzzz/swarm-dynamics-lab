## Relevance to us

A particularly useful correction to naive group-size scaling claims: measure causal sensitivity to information quality separately from density, and include size-dependent null models. It also provides an experimental recipe for separating observational correlation from causal interaction via controlled virtual neighbors and robot surrogates. Do not infer that noise always improves swarms or that biological computational parallels establish LLM-agent behavior.
