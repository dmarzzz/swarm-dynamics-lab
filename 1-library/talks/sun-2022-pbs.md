## Summary

Transcript coverage: 0:00-26:57, including closing discussion when present. Sun uses price discovery and interdependent values to reason about MEV bursts after information disclosure and growth as order flow accumulates. He proposes batch-allocation intervals between price-impact times and fast-finality constraints, citing a subsecond window for rollups rather than presenting a universal fixed constant. The talk then argues that shared coordination layers recover inefficiencies but can capture that surplus as MEV, so moving most activity to L2 does not imply proportionally little L1 MEV. Extortion and monarchy analogies motivate reshaping order-flow demand and commoditizing supply. ## Relevance to us

Useful hypothesis-generating framing for coalition power and shared-layer extraction, not a validated theorem that coordination captures exactly the price of anarchy. Distinguish the argument from deployed PBS guarantees and empirical identification of colluding operators.
