## Summary

At 9:34-13:00 Nagpal frames collective construction as active tile self-assembly: locally stored coordinates and attachment constraints prevent defects such as inaccessible holes. At 18:40-22:30 she describes TERMES hardware combining staircase locomotion, navigation and block manipulation, with continual corrective sensing. At 24:35-28:09 minimal collision-avoidance communication suffices for demonstrated cooperative building, but general compilers for arbitrary survival missions remain a future goal. Biological construction and movie robots are inspiration, not demonstrations of equivalent real-world capability.

## Relevance to us

Example of compiling global geometry into local state and invariants while integrating hardware constraints. Useful for separating minimal task-specific communication from a claim that all collective tasks need no explicit messages.
