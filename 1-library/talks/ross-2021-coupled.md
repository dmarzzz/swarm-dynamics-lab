## Summary

Ross represents two phases on a torus or periodic square. At 6:32-12:29 uncoupled rational frequency ratios yield periodic trajectories, whereas irrational ratios yield nonclosing trajectories dense on the torus, not trajectories passing through every point. At 12:31-16:09 symmetric sine coupling reduces to phi-dot = omega1 - omega2 - 2K sin(phi). The stable phase-difference solution yields equal frequencies with a fixed offset, not necessarily zero phase difference. The locking boundary arises at the corresponding saddle-node condition. A roller-racer example closes the discussion of more general torus flows.

## Relevance to us

A compact derivation of the two-agent phase-locking mechanism and its detuning/coupling threshold. The lecturer informally says all initial states converge, but the explicitly present unstable locked state is an exception; retain the stable-basin interpretation.
