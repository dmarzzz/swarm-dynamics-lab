## Summary

Isa motivates interface-confined active colloids with catalytic swimmers reported before the familiar 2007 bulk Janus-particle experiments. At 01:30-03:00, platinum-containing floating elastomer elements decompose hydrogen peroxide, with wettability-dependent capillary interactions shaping dynamic assemblies. At 03:30-08:00, gas-liquid, liquid-liquid and biological interfaces are introduced. Adsorbed particles can be trapped normal to the interface with energies far above thermal fluctuations while retaining lateral mobility, yielding a quasi-two-dimensional material. At 08:00-10:15, Isa outlines measurements of contact angle and interfacial tension, catalytic Janus propulsion and Marangoni stresses for the later lecture parts. This first part does not contain those later measurement protocols.

## Relevance to us

Good dimensionality and boundary-condition reference. Two-dimensional confinement is not the same as immobility, and interface-specific interactions should not be copied from bulk-particle models. Complements the deformable-boundary caution in [[vlahovska-2025-quincke]].
