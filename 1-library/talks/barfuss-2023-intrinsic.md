## Summary

Barfuss's abstract proposes linking complex-systems science with MARL to make cooperation dynamics more interpretable. Its concrete question is why temporal-difference learners using epsilon-greedy strategies can cooperate. The abstract argues that intrinsic learning-process noise is a positive mechanism for cooperation rather than merely an optimization nuisance. It does not provide quantitative cooperation rates or isolate the conditions under which fluctuations help.
