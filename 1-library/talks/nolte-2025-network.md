## Summary

At 1:50-4:05 the lecturer reduces a two-oscillator system to a phase difference, deriving locking when detuning is smaller than the sum of attractive coupling strengths and a coupling-weighted common frequency. Frequency pulling appears below full locking. At 7:18-10:20 mean-field phasors reduce all-to-all dynamics; at 13:43-15:04 a Lorentzian frequency distribution gives the reported critical coupling 2 gamma and a square-root coherence branch. Finite simulations are compared to this result. The closing explanation incorrectly describes second-order transitions as discontinuous, so that statement should not be reused as a general definition; coherence amplitude is also not generally identical to the fraction of locked oscillators.

## Relevance to us

Compact derivation and numerical intuition, with explicit caution about loose phase-transition terminology. Useful in conjunction with the more careful course [[pikovsky-2017-theory]], not a stand-alone authority for universal discontinuous synchronization.
