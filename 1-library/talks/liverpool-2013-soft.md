## Summary

Liverpool derives continuum descriptions from microscopic driven swimmers while tracking approximation choices. At 12:00-26:00, force-free swimmers are represented by time-dependent point-force distributions and nonreciprocal shape cycles. Thin-film and bulk hydrodynamic Green functions differ, with a viscosity-ratio length separating regimes. At 27:00-40:00, pair interactions, cycle averaging, a mean-field closure and moment expansion yield density, polarization and nematic-order equations. Pusher-type three-bead swimmers can order without excluded-volume interactions in the presented two-dimensional approximation; the analogous three-dimensional terms cancel. At 45:00-53:00, questions emphasize that low-density expansions are reliable near onset, not deep in ordered phases, and spatial perturbations can destabilize homogeneous order. At 54:00-64:00, minimal active oscillators interact hydrodynamically. A nonisochronous coefficient controls in-phase versus antiphase locking; long-wavelength phase patterns rather than rigid synchronous beating are useful for pumping in that model. Questions through 73:00 distinguish active stress and internal energy supply from passive viscous dissipation.
