## Summary

The lecture addresses the simulation-to-reality gap via multiobjective optimization. At 21:55-27:10, nondominated solutions trade off task behavior and transferability rather than collapsing objectives into one weighted score. At 46:39-51:17, behavior vectors comprising travel distance, average body height and final orientation define a Euclidean discrepancy between simulation and reality; similarity to previously transferred controllers supports a surrogate estimate. Closing discussion at 66:41-71:44 cautions that successful transfer on one floor does not establish robustness across friction and environment changes.

## Relevance to us

Adjacent robotics methodology for testing swarm policies: simulated task performance can reward artifacts, and transferability estimates depend on feature choice and tested environments. This lecture does not itself demonstrate a multi-robot swarm.
