## Summary

Transcript coverage: 0:00-26:34, including closing discussion when present. Hasu models a transaction supply chain from users and wallets through searchers, builders and validators. Competition, rather than cryptography alone, supplies low fees and censorship resistance. He argues that MEV makes building a trading problem, encouraging proprietary builder-searcher integration; MEV-Geth is presented as an open alternative. Exclusive order flow can make one builder consistently more profitable, while cross-domain execution benefits can concentrate building across chains. The proposed response is open interfaces and decentralized building that pays users competitively. Q&A acknowledges that private building could still win and that forced-inclusion mechanisms may preserve censorship resistance without preserving users' bargaining power.
