## Summary

Research seminar by Meead Saberi (UNSW rCITI) on calibrating a social force model of pedestrians for bidirectional (counterflow) streams. Only the YouTube page and description were read; the transcript could not be fetched (YouTube bot wall), so no timestamps. The description points to the published version, [[taherifar-2019-macroscopic]] (Transportmetrica A, 2019), whose abstract states the method: calibrate and validate a modified social force model against the pedestrian macroscopic fundamental diagram (p-MFD, an area-wide flow-density relation), and check that the calibrated model reproduces the empirical p-MFD including hysteresis while still producing self-organised lane formation at the micro level. Assume the seminar presents that work at a pre-publication stage.

## Relevance to us

Methodological point worth keeping: fit an agent model to an aggregate, area-wide observable (a fundamental diagram with hysteresis) and then check that the right micro-level emergent pattern (lanes) falls out, instead of fitting individual trajectories. That is a reusable validation recipe for agent-swarm simulations. Base model: [[helbing-1995-social]]; empirical lane formation and walking behaviour: [[moussaid-2009-experimental]], [[moussaid-2011-simple]]. Low priority; cite the paper, not the seminar.
