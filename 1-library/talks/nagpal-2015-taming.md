## Summary

Nagpal asks how local rules can be systematically composed into global construction and shape formation. At 10:52-14:22 she motivates a compiler from desired structures to libraries of synchronization, gradients, localization and movement, while acknowledging incomplete knowledge of which behaviors admit proofs. At 20:45-23:54 the thousand-Kilobot experiments expose traffic jams, stuck robots and rare-error amplification not captured in small simulations. Q&A at 28:20-31:04 explains that message-loss bursts can trigger cascading gradient failures even when small groups converge, leaving large-scale reliability an open research problem.

## Relevance to us

Direct evidence for scale-dependent failure modes and the need to test rare correlated events rather than extrapolate from a small reliable swarm. Biological regeneration is motivation, not a guarantee of robot self-repair.
