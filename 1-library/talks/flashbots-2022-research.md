## Summary

This workshop reviews research proposals rather than presenting completed experimental results. The opening discussion surveys crypto auction design, cooperative strategies, combinatorial bundle selection and computationally tractable approximations. At 19:06 onward a liquidation proposal contrasts fixed-spread rewards with auction mechanisms, and reviewers note that event-only data misses failed attempts and therefore understates competition and gas cost. At approximately 31 minutes the discussion turns to MEV-induced consensus instability, proposed attack thresholds and how conclusions depend on rationality and mining-model assumptions. Around 46:13 onward participants consider alternative consensus architectures, mempool policies and execution rules; BFT systems may shift attacks from safety to liveness. Closing remarks emphasize cross-proposal collaboration and reducing information asymmetry.

## Relevance to us

A research agenda and source of methodological cautions, not evidence that any suggested attack threshold or defense has been validated. The event-versus-trace discussion complements [[raj-2022-case]], while alternative ordering architectures connect to [[adler-2022-exploring]].
