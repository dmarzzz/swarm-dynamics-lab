## Summary

Release from [[li-2023-camel]] (arXiv 2303.17760). 25K conversations between two gpt-3.5-turbo agents, generated by role-playing every combination of 50 user roles and 50 assistant roles over 10 tasks each. A task-specifier step turns each general task into a more specific one. Each record keeps both role names, the original and specified task, all messages tagged by role, and the reason the chat terminated. Synthetic: the card warns it may contain incorrect information.

## Access

https://huggingface.co/datasets/camel-ai/ai_society, not gated. CC-BY-NC-4.0 (non-commercial).

## Relevance to us

Early, large corpus of pairwise agent-agent dialogue from a single model; usable as a baseline of homogeneous "same operator" agent text for detection work, and for termination and role-drift failure modes. Two agents only, so no collective dynamics.
