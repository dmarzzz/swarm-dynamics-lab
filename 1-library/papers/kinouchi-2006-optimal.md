## Summary

Shows that a network of excitable elements has its sensitivity and dynamic range maximised exactly at
the critical point of a non-equilibrium phase transition, giving a concrete example of a biologically relevant
quantity optimised at criticality, with implications for electrical coupling in olfactory and retinal
circuits.

## Contribution

Canonical demonstration of a functional benefit (dynamic range) at criticality, often cited as
the theoretical basis of the criticality hypothesis.

## Key results

- Dynamic range of the network response is maximal at the critical point (branching ratio 1) (model).

## Methods and models

Excitable cellular automata (Greenberg-Hastings type) on random networks with stochastic external
drive. arXiv q-bio/0601037.
