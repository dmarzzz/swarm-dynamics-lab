## Summary

Under a single-common-factor model of LLM-judge panel error, two or more judges and two or more anchors point-identify the quality variance, the common-mode error variance and each anchor contamination correlation in closed form, with diagnostics (covariance dispersion, over-identification, a family-block test) and bootstrap intervals; ordinal-only settings are not identified.

## Contribution

An estimator for common-mode error when the reference set itself may share errors.
