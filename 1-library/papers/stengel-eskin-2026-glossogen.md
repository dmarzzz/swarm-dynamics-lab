## Summary

Agents develop compositional communication in a partially observed coordination world. Strong models can originate conventions that weaker models subsequently learn; efficiency pressure and opportunities to negotiate conventions affect these outcomes.

## Contribution

Language invention, productivity and strong-to-weak transmission already have direct evidence.

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.
