## Summary

Using an offline trading benchmark, the authors examine plausible poisoned social-media inputs that enter only through an analyst evidence stream. Their GMATS framework tracks belief changes at analyst and coordinator levels and changes in backtest performance. They report materially degraded risk-return profiles but also topology-dependent dampening under identical poisoning budgets.

## Contribution

Published evidence or modelling of propagation, containment or evaluation across agent trust boundaries.

## Key results

The abstract reports reduced Sharpe ratios and improved average robustness under some topologies, without numerical effect sizes.
