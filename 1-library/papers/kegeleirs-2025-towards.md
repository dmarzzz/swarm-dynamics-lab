## Summary

A 2025 perspective arguing that swarm robotics has stayed in simulation and on small, weak robots, so it has not
reached commercial use. The authors identify two barriers: the lack of affordable, modern, robust platforms,
and a narrow definition of swarm robotics that excludes useful applications. They propose platforms with
state-of-the-art capabilities (SLAM, computer vision, reliable communication) and a broader interpretation of
what counts as a swarm.

## Contribution

A recent, explicit challenge to the "simple robots" doctrine of [[sahin-2005-swarm]], from the IRIDIA group.
