## Summary

Beni, who coined "swarm intelligence" for cellular robotic systems around 1989, reflects on how the word
"swarm" had come to be applied loosely across biology, engineering and computation, and tries to clarify how
swarm terminology maps onto robotic concepts such as cellular robotics, collective robotics and swarm
robotics. It is a short terminological essay that opened the first swarm robotics workshop proceedings.

## Contribution

Historical and terminological anchor linking swarm intelligence (the optimisation and computation community)
with swarm robotics (the embodied community).

## Key results

- Conceptual: distinguishes swarm-intelligence systems from swarm robotics along with related robotic
  paradigms; no experimental results.

## Methods and models

Essay; abstract only read.

## Limitations and open questions

Terminology has since shifted again (drone swarms, robotic active matter, LLM "swarms").
