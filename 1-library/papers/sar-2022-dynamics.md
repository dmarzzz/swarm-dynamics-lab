## Summary

A short (7-page, 4-figure) perspective that systematically reviews swarmalator dynamics five years after
[[okeeffe-2017-oscillators]]: the effect of different coupling topologies, interaction functions, external
forcing, noise and competitive (time-varying, mixed-sign) interactions on the long-term states of position
aggregation and phase synchronisation, with some open problems.

## Contribution

The first review of the swarmalator literature; now largely superseded by the much longer
[[sar-2026-interplay]] from the same group plus O'Keeffe, Bettstetter and others.

## Key results

- Abstract-level only: catalogues the static sync, static async, static phase wave, splintered and active phase
  wave states and their variants under the modifications listed above. No new results claimed.

## Methods and models

Review of numerical and analytical studies of the 2D swarmalator model and variants.

## Limitations and open questions

Short and pre-dates the solvable-model wave ([[yoon-2022-sync]] onward) and robot implementations after 2022.
