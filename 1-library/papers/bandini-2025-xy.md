## Summary

Bandini, Venturelli, Loos, Jelic and Gambassi study the 2D lattice XY model with interactions restricted to each spin's
vision cone, comparing one non-reciprocal and two reciprocal implementations by Monte Carlo. Configuration-dependent
couplings yield long-range and quasi-long-range ordered phases at low temperature; non-reciprocity is not required for
long-range order, and an unexpected order-by-disorder transition appears. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Tests a common assumption in the non-reciprocal literature ([[fruchart-2021-non]]): vision cones produce long-range
order even with reciprocal couplings.

## Key results

- Long-range order without non-reciprocity (abstract).
- Order-by-disorder transition (abstract).

## Methods and models

Monte Carlo on lattice XY spins with vision-cone couplings; symmetry arguments. arXiv:2412.19297; full text not read.

## Limitations and open questions

Static spins (no self-propulsion).

## Relevance to us

Vision cones are standard in robot and animal swarm models; this separates the effect of the cone from that of
non-reciprocity. Related: [[fruchart-2021-non]], [[lavergne-2019-group]].
