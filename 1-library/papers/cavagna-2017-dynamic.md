## Summary

Spatio-temporal correlation functions of wild midge swarms collapse onto one curve when time is rescaled by a
single characteristic time that grows with the correlation length with dynamical exponent z ~ 1.
Simulations of a self-propelled particle model in its swarming phase give z ~ 2, suggesting natural swarms
belong to a new dynamic universality class; non-exponential relaxation and paramagnetic spin-wave remnants
point to inertial effects. Read at abstract level from arXiv.

## Contribution

First evidence of dynamic scaling in a biological collective; connects to the inertial theory
([[cavagna-2015-flocking]], [[attanasi-2014-information]]).

## Key results

- Measured: dynamic scaling with z ~ 1 in natural swarms vs z ~ 2 in Vicsek-type simulations.

## Methods and models

3D tracking of midge swarms; dynamic correlation functions; comparison with SPP simulations.
