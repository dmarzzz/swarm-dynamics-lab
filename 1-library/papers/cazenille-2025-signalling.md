## Summary

A review in the 'road forward with swarm systems' issue ([[abbass-2025-road]]) on communication in robot swarms where learning and execution happen simultaneously and in a decentralised way, with robots continuously learning from each other through local messages (social learning). The authors highlight how communication interacts with the credit-assignment problem. They propose a taxonomy along two axes, information selection and physical abstraction, running from lossless raw-signal exchange to lossy structured communication models, and they survey evolutionary robotics, multi-agent deep RL, language models and biophysical models.

## Contribution

It connects swarm robotics to cultural-evolution and social-learning concepts and frames decentralised learning as a swarm-level process.
