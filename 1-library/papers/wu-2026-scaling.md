## Summary

The authors compare expanding teams with accumulating experience through memory. Their abstract reports that larger populations do not consistently improve outcomes, while repeated experience can allow smaller teams to outperform larger teams on evaluated tasks.

## Contribution

LLMA-Mem memory topologies evaluated using MultiAgentBench coding, research and database tasks.

## Key results

Nonmonotonic team-size effects and gains from memory are reported without effect sizes in the abstract.

## Methods and models

Assessment based on the source sections specified below; no implementation was run.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only. Experience exposure and training cost must be matched before attributing gains to memory topology.
