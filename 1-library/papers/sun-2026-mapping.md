## Summary

Using 3,389 identified Reddit bots and their full activity histories, the authors cluster temporal, community, linguistic and semantic features into 18 bot 'species', from content bots to moderation and utility bots. Bot numbers and activity grew fast, peaked around the COVID-19 period and began declining before Reddit's 2023 API changes, while the diversity of bot types stayed stable.

## Contribution

An ecological census of declared or identified automation on one platform, useful as a pre-LLM baseline population of benign and semi-benign bots.

## Key results

- 3,389 bots, 18 clusters spanning content-specialised, behaviour-driven and infrastructural roles.
- Bot count and activity peaked around COVID-19 and declined before the 2023 API policy change; species diversity stable.

## Methods and models

Clustering on activity-history features. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Covers identified bots, which are mostly overt; covert LLM agents are probably absent from the sample. Preprint.

## Relevance to us

Baseline of what 'normal' automation looks like on Reddit, against which a covert agent swarm would have to be separated. Pairs with [[la-cava-2025-machines]] on the same platform.
