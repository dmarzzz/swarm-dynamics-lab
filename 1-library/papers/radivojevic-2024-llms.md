## Summary

On a private Mastodon instance, 36 human participants and bots built from 10 personas on GPT-4, Llama 2 Chat and Claude discussed politics over three rounds without knowing the bot-human ratio. Asked afterwards, participants identified whether other users were bots or humans correctly only 42% of the time, and persona choice mattered more than which LLM was used.

## Contribution

A direct measurement that human users cannot tell LLM persona bots from humans in conversation.

## Key results

- Participants correctly identified the nature of other users 42% of the time despite knowing bots were present (abstract).
- Persona choice affected perception more than the choice of LLM.

## Methods and models

Mastodon-based experiment, three rounds, post-round surveys. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Small sample, short exposure, participants not trained moderators; detection here means human judgement only.
