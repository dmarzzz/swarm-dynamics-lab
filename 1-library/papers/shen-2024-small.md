## Summary

Planner, caller and summarizer capabilities are separated and trained in stages. The resulting modular multi-LLM system improves reported tool-use performance relative to a single-model approach on the evaluated benchmarks.

## Limitations and open questions

Division into specialist modules is established; training and scaffold changes confound a simple model-size explanation.
