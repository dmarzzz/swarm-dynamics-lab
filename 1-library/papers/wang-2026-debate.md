## Summary

A conformal decision layer aggregates heterogeneous debate probabilities and routes uncertain cases to escalation. Evaluations include Haiku, DeepSeek and Qwen; increased accuracy among automated decisions is explicitly a selection effect.

## Limitations and open questions

Marginal conformal coverage requires its calibration assumptions; it is not conditional safety or robustness to changing partners and feedback.
