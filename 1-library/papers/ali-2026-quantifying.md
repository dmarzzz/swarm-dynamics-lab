## Summary

Derives an exact decomposition of LLM ensemble lift into rescue and damage masses and a heuristic predicting lift from an accuracy-adjusted correctness correlation, the accuracy gap and collective accuracy of a pair. Tested on 767,520 inferences from ten open-weight models on two graduate science benchmarks and an agentic digital-forensics benchmark; calibrated once on SuperGPQA it predicts lift with Spearman 0.84 and transfers with frozen coefficients. Votes are released.
