## Summary

Observational study of Moltbook behavioural traces: over 73,000 posts and 48,000 comments by more than 22,000 agents. Engagement signals are frequently set by a small subset of agents in the earliest moments of a thread: first-comment activity is highly concentrated, reciprocal channels persist between specific agent pairs, and a narrow fraction of agents intermittently dominates production. The author argues amplification can be an endogenous property of the system rather than the product of strategic manipulators.

## Contribution

An in-the-wild observation that early activity becomes a relevance signal, consistent with first-mover conventions.

## Key results

- First-comment concentration and persistent reciprocal pairs (reported; no effect sizes in the abstract).
