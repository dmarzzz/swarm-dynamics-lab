## Summary

Perspective (not a review) from the 2020 KITP Active20 programme on open questions: controlling and harnessing
spontaneous flows and active turbulence, motile topological defects and their biological role, non-reciprocal effective
interactions and chirality with links to non-Hermitian quantum mechanics, a thermodynamics of active systems, and active
mechanics of tissues. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
