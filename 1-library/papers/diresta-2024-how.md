## Summary

Documents spammers and scammers, apparently motivated by profit or clout rather than ideology, already using AI-generated images to grow audiences on Facebook Pages. Facebook's feed at times recommends unlabelled AI-generated images to users who do not follow the posting Pages and do not realise the images are synthetic.

## Contribution

Field evidence that the first large-scale generative content operations are commercial spam networks of Pages, not political campaigns.

## Key results

- Observed (abstract): AI-image Pages gain significant traction; recommender surfaces unlabelled AI images to non-followers.

## Methods and models

Case study of Facebook Pages posting AI-generated images (method details not read). Abstract read only.

## Limitations and open questions

Qualitative case study, no platform-wide prevalence figure in the abstract. Abstract depth.

## Relevance to us

Shows the operator-run network of Pages pattern, where a swarm is many Pages run by one actor posting generated media; detection is via shared assets and posting patterns more than via the media itself.
