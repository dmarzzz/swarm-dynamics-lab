## Summary

Book treating social insect colonies as problem-solving systems whose intelligence lies in networks of interactions
among individuals and with the environment. Each chapter takes a biological example (foraging, division of labour,
nest building, sorting, cooperative transport) and develops an algorithm, a multi-agent system or a group of robots
from it, emphasising autonomy, emergence and distributed functioning over central control.

## Contribution

The book that named and organised the field of swarm intelligence in its biology-to-engineering form; source of the
response-threshold, stigmergic building and clustering models used across swarm robotics.
