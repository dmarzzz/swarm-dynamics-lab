## Summary

Reviews honeypots, honeytokens and moving-target defence from the late 1980s to 2021 together. It classifies techniques on a two-dimensional taxonomy: which phase of a tailored cyber kill chain they disrupt, and which layer of a four-layer deception stack they belong to. It discusses deception in depth and the deception lifecycle, and lists open directions including quantified deception effects, operating cost and the human element.

## Contribution

A pre-LLM review article covering classic deception methodology (in scope as a review for this task). It is the baseline vocabulary that the LLM-era work ([[bridges-2025-sok]]) extends.
