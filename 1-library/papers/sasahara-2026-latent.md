## Summary

A binomial model of multiple independent AI advisers shows that as advisers are added, visible dissent becomes nearly inevitable alongside a more reliable majority; the two approach certainty at rates that cross at adviser accuracy 0.8, below which visible dissent eventually becomes more likely than a correct majority.

## Contribution

Separates aggregation from disclosure of disagreement in multi-adviser settings.
