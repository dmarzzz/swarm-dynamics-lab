## Summary

Reviews efforts to measure information flow in biological systems, from protein sequence families to
flocks of birds, and the evidence that biological systems are optimised for gathering and representing
information.

## Contribution

Companion review to [[mora-2011-biological]] focusing on information optimisation rather than
criticality.

## Key results

- Review of measured information transmission across scales, including flock velocity distributions (review).
