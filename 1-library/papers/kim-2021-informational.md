## Summary

Proposes comparing non-living and living collectives on a continuum by their information
architecture, meaning how information is stored and processed across degrees of freedom, and reviews
information-theoretic characterisations of diverse collective phenomena.

## Contribution

Framework arguing that information architecture, not just dynamics, distinguishes living
collectives from physical analogues.
