## Summary

Longpre and 48 co-authors (Data Provenance Initiative) audit the robots.txt and Terms of Service of 14,000 web domains behind C4, RefinedWeb and Dolma. In one year (2023 to 2024) restrictions rose fast: about 5%+ of all C4 tokens, and 28%+ of the most actively maintained critical C4 sources, became fully restricted by robots.txt, and 45% of C4 was restricted by Terms of Service. They find inconsistencies between ToS and robots.txt and asymmetric restrictions across AI developers.

## Contribution

Longitudinal measurement of the publisher response to AI crawling, i.e. the demand side for crawler detection.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): 14,000 domains audited.
- Measured (abstract): about 5%+ of C4 tokens and 28%+ of critical C4 sources fully restricted by robots.txt within 2023 to 2024.
- Measured (abstract): 45% of C4 restricted by Terms of Service.

## Methods and models

Longitudinal snapshots of robots.txt and ToS for domains in major pre-training corpora. Abstract only.

## Limitations and open questions

Measures stated preferences, not crawler compliance or detection.
