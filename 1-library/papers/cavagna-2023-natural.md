## Summary

Computes the dynamical critical exponent z of natural insect swarms with a renormalisation group
calculation to first order in epsilon = 4 - d for a field theory with both activity and inertia. A new fixed
point appears where both are relevant, giving z = 1.35 in three dimensions, matching experiments
(1.37 +/- 0.11) and simulations (1.35 +/- 0.04).

## Contribution

A rare quantitative match between renormalisation group theory and biological collective data,
supporting universality in strongly correlated animal groups.

## Key results

- z = 1.35 (RG, d=3) vs 1.37 +/- 0.11 (midge experiments) vs 1.35 +/- 0.04 (simulations).
- Novel fixed point with relevant activity and inertia.

## Methods and models

Dynamic RG, epsilon expansion, inertial active field theory; comparison to midge swarm data and
numerics.

## Limitations and open questions

First-order epsilon expansion; abstract-level read.
