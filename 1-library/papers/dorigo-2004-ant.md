## Summary

Monograph on ACO: translation of observed ant foraging behaviour (shortest-path finding) into optimisation
algorithms, definition of the ACO metaheuristic in the context of combinatorial optimisation, descriptions of all
major ACO algorithms, theoretical results, and applications (routing including AntNet, assignment, scheduling,
subset problems, machine learning, bioinformatics), with exercises.

## Contribution

The standard reference text for ACO; consolidates AS, ACS, MMAS and theory into one framework.
