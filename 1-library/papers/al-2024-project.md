## Summary

Altera's Project Sid runs 10 to 1000+ LLM agents in Minecraft. It introduces the PIANO architecture (Parallel Information Aggregation via Neural Orchestration), which lets agents act in real time with humans and other agents while keeping multiple concurrent output streams coherent. Using "civilisational benchmarks", the simulations show agents autonomously developing specialised roles, adhering to and changing collective rules (for example through voting), and transmitting cultural and religious memes.

## Contribution

One of the largest embodied LLM agent societies; reports emergent specialisation and rule change at population scale.

## Key results

- 10 to 1000+ agents in one environment (abstract).
- Emergent role specialisation, collective rule adherence and amendment, cultural and religious transmission (abstract; quantitative measures not read).

## Methods and models

PIANO concurrent-module agent architecture; Minecraft environment; benchmarks inspired by human history. Industry technical report; code not checked.

## Limitations and open questions

Industry preprint; evaluation metrics are bespoke; reproducibility and the statistical robustness of "emergent" findings are unclear from the abstract.

## Relevance to us

A rare data point on division of labour at hundreds of agents, the LLM analogue of task allocation in social insects; compare the much more controlled [[riedl-2025-emergent]] and [[ashery-2024-emergent]].
