## Summary

GAMMAF provides a shared evaluation environment for graph-based anomaly monitoring in LLM multi-agent systems. It generates attributed interaction graphs from simulated debates and evaluates existing detectors through live node isolation. The authors evaluate XG-Guard and BlindGuard on knowledge tasks and report that effective remediation can reduce operational costs by shortening adversarial exchanges.
