## Summary

Lasry and Lions survey their mean-field approach, by analogy with statistical mechanics, to games with a very large number of rational players who have limited information and choose optimal strategies given macroscopic information resulting from everyone's actions. In three examples from economics and finance they derive a new type of nonlinear differential equations (a backward Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation coupled to a forward Fokker-Planck equation), show well-posedness (unique solutions), discuss limiting cases and list open problems.

## Contribution
