## Summary

A review article that classifies over 260 papers on agentic security under three pillars: applications (agents used for cybersecurity), threats to agentic systems, and defences. It maps which defences cover which attack classes and where in the agent loop they sit, and charts trends in architectures, backbone models, modalities and research volume. It concludes that agentic systems are structurally fragile by default and that defences must span the full agent lifecycle rather than a single layer.

## Contribution

A large mapping of attacks against defences, with a coverage matrix over agent-loop stages.
