## Summary

A broad review of UAV swarms: definitions and types, intelligent path and trajectory generation, trajectory prediction, task allocation, coordinated control, safety, the role of AI and deep learning, and a substantial section on anti-drone-swarm (counter-swarm) techniques for military settings. It ends with trends and research gaps.

## Contribution

A recent general survey; its distinctive part is the counter-swarm coverage, which robotics-focused reviews such as [[abbass-2025-road]] or [[refis-2025-network]] give little space.

## Key results

- Review; no new results. The authors survey trajectory generation, trajectory prediction and counter-swarm techniques as the three main areas (Conclusions).

## Methods and models

Narrative literature review (MDPI Sensors). I skimmed the introduction and conclusions.

## Limitations and open questions

Broad and engineering-oriented, with little on emergent collective dynamics, and the framing leans military. Use for orientation, not as a primary source.
