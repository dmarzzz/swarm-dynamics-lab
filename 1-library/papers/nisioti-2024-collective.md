## Summary

This study measures collective innovation with LLM agents in Little Alchemy 2. The authors vary connectivity and report that dynamically structured interaction can outperform fully connected groups, linking social organization with exploration.

## Contribution

More communication is not uniformly better for innovation.

## Key results

Author-reported findings summarized above; not independently replicated here.

## Methods and models

Groups solving combinatorial discovery tasks under different communication networks.
