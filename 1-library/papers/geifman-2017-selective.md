## Summary

Selective classification permits a predictor to reject cases, exchanging coverage for lower error among accepted predictions. The paper constructs such a classifier from an existing network and evaluates the resulting risk–coverage tradeoff.

## Contribution

Finite review queues need end-to-end completion and delay metrics, not only accuracy among accepted decisions.
