## Summary

A review of recent social bot detection with a focus on generative AI and LLMs. It proposes a bot categorisation meant to reduce class overlap, reviews common datasets and state-of-the-art classifiers, and uses explainable AI and data mining on profile-based measures to find what drives bot misclassification.
