## Summary

Examines fairness, accountability and transparency in social bot detection through three pillars: training datasets, algorithm development and the use of bot agents. The authors survey the datasets behind existing detectors, evaluate them, and draw on accounts of people wrongly detected as bots, ending with recommendations.

## Contribution

Review of the ethics and false-positive harms of bot detection, including dataset audits.

## Key results

- Surveys and evaluates training datasets of existing detectors (abstract).
- Draws on user experiences of being labelled a bot.

## Methods and models

FATe-framed review. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Preprint; recommendations rather than measurements.
