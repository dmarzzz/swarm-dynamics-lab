## Summary

Describes I-24 MOTION, a traffic-science instrument near Nashville: 276 pole-mounted high-resolution cameras covering about 4.2 miles of a 4–5 lane freeway, with vehicle trajectories extracted by computer vision. About 230 million vehicle-miles are travelled through it each year; the first public datasets contain at least 4 hours of trajectories on each of 10 days.

## Contribution

Provides continuous, full-coverage trajectory data for every vehicle on a congested freeway section, enabling measurement of stop-and-go wave formation and control effects at the individual-vehicle level.

## Key results

- Reported (abstract): 276 cameras, about 4.2 miles, 230 million vehicle-miles per year; datasets with at least 4 h per day over 10 days; data planned at i24motion.org/data.

## Methods and models

Camera network, fibre to compute facility, CV tracking. Transportation Research Part C 155, 104311.

## Limitations and open questions

Tracking errors and reconstruction quality are discussed in companion papers (not read). Abstract only.
