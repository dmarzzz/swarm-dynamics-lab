## Summary

Review of microswimmer physics: propulsion at low Reynolds number by helical flagella, beating eukaryotic flagella and
artificial mechanisms; hydrodynamics of swimming; flagellar synchronization; swimming near surfaces; and collective
behaviour of isotropic and anisotropic swimmers with and without hydrodynamic interactions. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

The standard reference for the single-swimmer-to-collective hydrodynamics route, complementing the dry-particle
emphasis of [[chate-2019-dry]].

## Key results

- Covers propulsion mechanisms, surface effects and collective phenomena (abstract).
