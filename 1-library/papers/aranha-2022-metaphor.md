## Summary

Open letter by editors and leading researchers in swarm intelligence and metaheuristics (including Dorigo, the
inventor of ACO) noting that dozens of metaphor-based metaheuristics inspired by natural, artificial, social and even
supernatural phenomena are being proposed without motivation beyond publication. As summarised by
[[kudela-2023-evolutionary]] (which I read in full), the letter identifies four problems: useless metaphors, lack of
novelty, poor experimental validation and comparison, and publication in off-topic journals.

## Contribution

Community position statement that led journals to discourage metaphor-based submissions ([[vermetten-2024-large]]
reports TELO and Evolutionary Computation adopting this).
