## Summary

A review of cooperative MARL organised into five approaches: independent learners, fully observable critics, value-function factorisation, consensus-based methods, and learned communication. For each it explains challenges and how papers addressed them, then covers emerging areas, real-world applications and available MARL environments, and closes with research directions.

## Contribution

The clearest taxonomy of cooperative MARL algorithm families; useful to place swarm methods (consensus, parameter sharing, communication) among them.
