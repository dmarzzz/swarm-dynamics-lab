## Summary

Studies an active-matter model of dense crowds gathered around a point of common interest (heavy metal concerts, Black Friday sales), where motion is governed by physical contact rather than social norms. Borrowing methods from jammed granular media, it identifies Goldstone modes, soft spots and stochastic resonance as structural mechanisms that can trigger potentially dangerous large-scale collective motion.

## Contribution

Connects crowd safety to the vibrational-mode analysis of jammed packings, giving a structural (not behavioural) explanation of where and how dense crowds begin to move collectively.

## Key results

- Simulated and analysed (abstract): Goldstone modes, soft spots and stochastic resonance drive emergent collective motion in the dense active packing.

## Methods and models

Active-matter model of agents drawn to a common point of interest, analysed with methods from jammed granular media (model details not read). PRL 117, 228301. Shares an author (J. L. Silverberg) with [[silverberg-2013-collective]].

## Limitations and open questions

Model study; comparison with real dense-crowd data limited. Abstract only.
