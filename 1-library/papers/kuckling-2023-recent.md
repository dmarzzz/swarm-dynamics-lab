## Summary

A mini-review of automatic design for robot swarms, covering three families: offline evolutionary robotics,
embodied (online, on-robot) evolution, and offline robot learning, including reinforcement learning. For each it
describes recent methods and recurring challenges (reality gap, credit assignment, specifying objectives) and
offers a perspective on how current trends may address the main open problem: designing individual behaviour
so that a desired collective behaviour emerges.

## Contribution

An up-to-date map of the automatic-design literature that follows [[francesca-2014-automode]] and links swarm
robotics to the MARL literature ([[huttenrauch-2019-deep]]).
