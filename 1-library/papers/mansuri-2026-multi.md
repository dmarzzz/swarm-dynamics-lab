## Summary

The authors model withholding, internalization, reconsideration and correction in group discussion. They fit behavioral rates to agent conversations and report smaller discussion gains as withholding rises, with an intervention that encourages expression of dissent.

## Contribution

Makes dissent expression distinct from whether a expressed challenge is correct.

## Key results

The abstract reports a model-dependent critical withholding rate and empirical agreement with its predicted direction. No local replication.

## Methods and models

Behavioral model, Bayesian rate estimation and LLM benchmark conversations.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract only; assumptions and detailed methods require review before transfer to Jev.
