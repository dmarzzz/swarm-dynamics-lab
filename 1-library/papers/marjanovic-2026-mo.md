## Summary

Evaluates 8 model-selection strategies (size, accuracy, answer diversity and others) for routing, majority-voting and LLM-as-judge multi-agent architectures on scientific benchmarks. Expanding the candidate pool often degrades performance below the best single model, and selecting candidates within one model family gives the best relative gain over a standalone model.

## Contribution

A counterpoint to the cross-family lever: arbitrary heterogeneity can destabilise a multi-agent system.
