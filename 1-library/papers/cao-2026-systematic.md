## Summary

This survey analyzes prompt injection as low-integrity information influencing high-integrity control flow, distinguishing that integrity failure from later confidentiality leakage. It organizes attacks by contamination source, attacker capability and persistence, compares four defence lines and audits benchmark reporting.

## Contribution

Analysis of propagation, lifecycle security, containment or trust-boundary assessment.
