## Summary

Tang and colleagues simulate dense mixtures of passive and active self-aligning disks with isotropic or anisotropic
mobility. The passive fraction controls an order–disorder transition that is continuous for isotropic mobility and
discontinuous for anisotropic mobility, a dichotomy captured by a mean-field equation for headings. Near the transition
ordered states can be oscillating or rotating and metastable, depending on the arrangement of passive particles and
lattice defects. Abstract read from the arXiv page in `url`.

## Contribution
