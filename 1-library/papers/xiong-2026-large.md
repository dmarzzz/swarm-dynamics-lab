## Summary

A systematic review of 2019-2024 literature on how LLMs both help and hurt social media information integrity, covering misinformation, disinformation, fake news, social bots and privacy. The authors screened 1,048 studies and analysed 215 in depth, and identify gaps in cross-lingual detection, real-time monitoring and privacy-preserving methods.

## Contribution

The broadest recent systematic review that includes LLM social bots, useful as a citation map.

## Key results

- 1,048 studies screened, 215 analysed in depth (abstract).
- Gaps: cross-lingual detection, real-time monitoring, privacy-preserving implementations.

## Methods and models

Systematic literature review. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Broad scope, so social-bot coverage is one section among several; literature only to 2024.
