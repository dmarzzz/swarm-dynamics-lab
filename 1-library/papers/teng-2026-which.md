## Summary

Argues an LLM team ceiling is set by member capability and by error resonance between members. Offline profiling measures error decorrelation (fewer co-failures) and predictive divergence (strategy diversity); a standardised quality-complementarity objective with greedy search selects a small team from a pool, with reported gains over heuristic selection.

## Contribution

Team selection by measured error decorrelation.
