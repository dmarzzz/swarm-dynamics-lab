## Summary

Wang and colleagues propose "Robo-Matter": micro-robot building blocks (magnetic spinners) with symmetry-breaking active
motion modes and tunable anisotropic interactions, coupled to a programmable spatio-temporal light field that exchanges
information with each block. The collective shows a "robot–matter duality": matter-like behaviour (ultra-fast
self-assembly, adaptivity) and robot-like behaviour (active force output, healing, morphing, infiltration). Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Frames large micro-robot swarms as reconfigurable active materials controlled at the building-block level, between
programmable matter and swarm robotics.

## Key results

- Demonstrations of self-assembly, healing, morphing and force output (abstract; quantitative values not recorded).

## Methods and models

Experiment with magnetic spinner micro-robots and a programmable light field (Nature Communications 2024).

## Limitations and open questions

Control is external (light field), not autonomous; scalability claims not checked.
