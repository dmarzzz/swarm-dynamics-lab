## Summary

Across three studies, seven datasets and over 880,000 texts, links widespread LLM writing assistance to declining linguistic diversity. When LLMs polish or rewrite text they keep content but homogenise style, reducing writing-complexity variance by 21-50% across datasets and models, and amplify features associated with dominant groups.

## Contribution

Quantifies stylistic homogenisation from LLM rewriting, the population-level property that diversity-based detectors would exploit.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): 21-50% reduction in writing-complexity variance after LLM rewriting (p <= .05), consistent across LLMs, prompts and contexts.

## Methods and models

Comparisons of original and LLM-rewritten texts across seven datasets. Abstract read only.

## Limitations and open questions

Mostly controlled rewriting rather than measurement of deployed platforms. Abstract depth.
