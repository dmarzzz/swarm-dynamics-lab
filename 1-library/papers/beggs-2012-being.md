## Summary

A review, written as a dialogue between proponent and skeptic, of the hypothesis that neural networks
operate near a critical point. It explains criticality and power-law avalanches and presents the main objections
(alternative mechanisms for power laws, sampling and thresholding artefacts, statistical testing) with
counterpoints.

## Contribution

Widely cited balanced treatment of objections to criticality claims; the neural analogue of the
skeptical literature relevant to swarms.

## Key results

- Lays out objections and counterarguments to the criticality hypothesis (review).
