## Summary

Uses minimal self-propelled particle models to ask when a local perturbation (sensed by few
individuals) triggers a global coherent turn. The time for a collective directional response grows with system
size, so it is a finite-size effect. Global turns require undamped information propagation and motility weak
enough that the perturbed individual does not leave before the turn completes; otherwise the group fragments.

## Contribution

Clarifies the conditions (efficient propagation, limited motility) for coherent collective
response to local threats, connecting to the inertial spin model of [[attanasi-2014-information]].

## Key results

- Collective response time grows with group size (simulation).
- Coherent turns need undamped propagation and moderate motility; otherwise fragmentation or inefficient response.

## Methods and models

Vicsek-like and inertial spin models with local perturbations. arXiv 2302.07592.
