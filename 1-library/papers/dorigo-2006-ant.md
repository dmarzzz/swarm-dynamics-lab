## Summary

Tutorial-review introducing ACO as the most studied swarm-intelligence technique inspired by ants: ants deposit
pheromone to mark favourable paths, and ACO exploits a similar mechanism for optimisation. Surveys notable
applications since the early 1990s and notes a growing body of theoretical results that guide practitioners.
