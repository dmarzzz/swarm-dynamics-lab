## Summary

Qualitative analysis of public videos and documents on the Love Parade disaster in Duisburg on 24 July 2010. It argues that people stumbled and piled up through a domino effect caused by crowd turbulence ("crowd quakes"), the result of amplifying feedbacks and cascading effects typical of systemic instabilities, rather than mass panic or deliberate pushing. It proposes a new scale for the criticality of crowd conditions and discusses citizen science for investigations.

## Contribution

Reframes crowd disasters as systemic failures and distinguishes crowd quakes from panic stampedes and crowd crushes. The same footage was later re-analysed quantitatively by [[gu-2025-emergence]].

## Key results

- Qualitative (abstract): crowd turbulence as the immediate mechanism; misunderstandings about "panic" corrected; criticality scale proposed.
