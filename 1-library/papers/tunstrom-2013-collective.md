## Summary

Golden shiners in groups of 30 to 300 are mapped onto order parameters (polarization and rotation), revealing
at least three dynamically stable collective states: swarm (slow, dense, disordered), milling and polarized.
Increasing swim speed drives transitions from swarm to the locally ordered states; milling and polarized
states coexist in a bistable regime with transitions driven largely by perturbations (boundary and internal).
Stability of states depends on group size. Read at abstract level via Europe PMC.

## Contribution

Empirical confirmation of the multistability and state set predicted by [[couzin-2002-collective]] in a real
vertebrate collective, and the standard use of the polarization/rotation order-parameter plane.

## Key results

- Measured: three states (swarm, mill, polarized); speed-dependent transitions; mill-polarized bistability;
  group-size dependence.

## Methods and models

Laboratory schools of golden shiners (30-300 fish), automated tracking, order parameters O_p and O_r, state
transition statistics.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read; shallow tank (quasi-2D) with boundaries.

## Relevance to us
