## Summary

The study relates reward aggregation curvature to gains from heterogeneous effort allocation, then tests related reward structures in MARL. Richer observations can remove empirical heterogeneity gains by enabling identical policies to behave differently.

## Limitations and open questions

This concerns allocation and learned behavior, not vendor identity. Include homogeneous policies with different observations or roles.
