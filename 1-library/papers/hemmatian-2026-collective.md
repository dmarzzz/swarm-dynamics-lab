## Summary

This synthesis organizes hybrid human-machine cognition around networks linking heterogeneous participants. It discusses attention, memory and reasoning as collective processes and considers how roles and interaction constraints shape the resulting group behavior.

## Contribution

Network-science treatment of hybrid cognition; author version of a forthcoming handbook chapter.
