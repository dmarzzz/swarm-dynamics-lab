## Summary

A review of social simulation with LLM-empowered agents, organised as a progression in scale: individual simulation (mimicking specific people or demographic groups), scenario simulation (multiple agents collaborating towards goals in a specific context) and society simulation (agent societies reflecting real-world complexity). For each type it covers architecture or key components, objectives or scenarios, and evaluation, then summarises datasets, benchmarks and tools, and discusses risks and challenges. A companion repository lists sources (https://github.com/FudanDISC/SocialAgent).
