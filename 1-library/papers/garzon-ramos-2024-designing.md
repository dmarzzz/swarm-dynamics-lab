## Summary

A short essay that borrows the puzzle/problem/mess distinction from organisational theory to characterise how
hard it is to design collective behaviours for robot swarms. A puzzle has a known solution form, a problem has a
clear formulation but open solutions, and a mess involves interacting, ill-defined issues. The authors argue
that research goals have often treated swarm design as a puzzle or problem while real-world deployment is a
mess, and point to promising directions.
