## Summary

A short (8-page) architecture-and-review paper proposing LLM-Centric Agentic AI for UAV Swarms (LAUS): closed-loop cognitive architectures that integrate perception, memory, reasoning/planning and action for adaptive drone swarms in search and rescue and environmental monitoring. It reviews enabling technologies (onboard and edge computing, 5G/6G connectivity, multimodal intelligence, cybersecurity), analyses threats such as Priority Manipulation Attacks that distort decisions and degrade network performance, and lists open problems: hallucination-resistant reasoning, onboard LLM deployment under size, weight and power constraints, and security benchmarks for perception-reasoning attacks.

## Contribution
