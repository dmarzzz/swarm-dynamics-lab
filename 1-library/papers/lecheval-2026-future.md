## Summary

A perspective on where collective animal behaviour research is heading. The field has combined lab experiments with models from mathematics and physics; VR systems, drone recordings, automated tracking and AI are reshaping methods and require broader interdisciplinarity. The authors describe research questions and approaches they expect to be central, how these will engage more disciplines, the challenges of integrating diverse practices, and practical steps.
