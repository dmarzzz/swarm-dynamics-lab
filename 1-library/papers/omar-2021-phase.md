## Summary

Omar, Klymko, GrandPre and Geissler show that for 3D active Brownian particles MIPS coexistence is only metastable over
a broad range of conditions: it decays at long times by active crystallization. Just above the MIPS critical point the
liquid–gas binodal is superseded by crystal–fluid coexistence, with a solid–liquid–gas triple point; nucleating the
crystal needs a rare near-close-packed fluctuation, feasible only at high activity. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Revises the 3D ABP phase diagram established by [[stenhammar-2013-continuum]]-era simulations, analogous to the 2D
melting–MIPS picture of [[digregorio-2018-full]].

## Key results

- MIPS metastable to active crystallization in 3D; triple point (abstract).
