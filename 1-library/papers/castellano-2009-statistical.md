## Summary

A review of statistical-physics models of collective social phenomena: opinion dynamics (voter, majority-rule,
bounded-confidence and related models), cultural and language dynamics, crowd behaviour, hierarchy formation,
human dynamics and social spreading. It highlights connections to traditional statistical physics (ordering
kinetics, phase transitions) and emphasises comparing model results with empirical social data.

## Contribution

The standard physics review of opinion and consensus formation, complementary to the control-theoretic
tutorials [[proskurnikov-2017-tutorial]] and [[bernardo-2024-bounded]].
