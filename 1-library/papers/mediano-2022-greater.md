## Summary

An accessible review and extension of the information-decomposition theory of causal emergence:
emergence is information about a system's temporal evolution that cannot be obtained from its parts
separately. It discusses interpretation issues, misunderstandings and the advantages of the formalism.

## Contribution

Best entry point to the PID/PhiID emergence framework of [[rosas-2020-reconciling]], written to be
applied.

## Key results

- Summarises and extends the formal theory; clarifies interpretation (review, no new data reported in abstract).

## Methods and models

Review of PID, PhiID, practical criteria Psi/Delta/Gamma. arXiv 2111.06518.

Abstract-level read.
