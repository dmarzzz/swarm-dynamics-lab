## Summary

A model of a flock of cooperative units in which "intelligence" (following a few informed lookout
birds) emerges at criticality from the interplay of frequent organisational collapses, which free birds from
each other, and flock-wide spatial correlation, which transmits information from lookouts with a delay set by
the time between collapses.

## Contribution

Early modelling claim linking criticality, intermittent collapses and information transmission
from informed minorities in flocks.

## Key results

- At criticality, organisational collapses plus flock-size correlation let the group follow lookout birds (model result).
- Transmission delay equals the interval between consecutive collapses (model result).

## Methods and models

Decision-making / Vicsek-like model of cooperative units on a lattice-like flock with lookout birds;
simulation.

## Limitations and open questions

Stylised model; the link to real flocks is qualitative. Abstract-level read.
