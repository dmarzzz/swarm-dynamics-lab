## Summary

The article adapts an epidemic model to LLM-agent worms, separating poisoned reservoirs from pre-execution interception. It derives an agent reproduction number and relates classes of defence to the model parameters.

## Contribution

Analysis of propagation, lifecycle security, containment or trust-boundary assessment.

## Key results

Analytic reproduction-number model described in the publisher abstract. No empirical prevalence or success-rate values were supplied there.

## Methods and models

IEEE publisher abstract read via reader; metadata cross-checked in Crossref. Poisoned-reservoir epidemic model, per primary abstract.

## Limitations and open questions

Full equations and calibration were not read; analytic thresholds need validated deployment parameters.
