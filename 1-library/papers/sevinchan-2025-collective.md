## Summary

Investigates a spin model of collective decision-making with heterogeneous preferences and tunable
non-linearity of social interactions on sparse random graphs, using two susceptibility measures for internal and
external perturbations, and asks how the susceptibility maximum depends on connectivity.

## Contribution

Extends criticality-based decision models from complete graphs and lattices to random networks.

## Key results

- Maximum susceptibility depends on network connectivity (simulation/theory).
- Suggests collectives could adjust network structure or interaction non-linearity to stay at maximal susceptibility.

## Methods and models

Spin model with heterogeneous biases on random graphs; susceptibility measures. arXiv 2411.19829.

Abstract-level read.
