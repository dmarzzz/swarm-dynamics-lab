## Summary

Pogobot is an open-source swarm robot (about 250 euros per unit) built for research at the interface of swarm robotics and active matter. It offers vibration- or wheel-based locomotion, fast directional infrared communication and several sensors in a compact package. More than 200 units are in daily use across several universities for self-organisation, programmable active matter, discrete reaction-diffusion-advection systems and social-learning studies. The paper documents hardware, software API, communication protocols and locomotion.

## Contribution

A Kilobot successor aimed at physicists and roboticists alike, with faster, directional communication.
