## Summary

Systematisation of knowledge on AI-driven agents that interact with blockchains, covering on-chain data analysis, transaction strategy optimisation and smart-contract vulnerability detection, with a focus on security and privacy. The authors claim it is the first SoK specific to AI agents for blockchain and organise applications, limitations and research directions; the abstract gives no quantitative results.

## Contribution

Review article mapping the AI-agent-for-blockchain space; included because the task asks for every review found.
