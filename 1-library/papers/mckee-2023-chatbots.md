## Summary

A feasibility study showing ChatGPT can imitate Linux, Mac and Windows terminals and interfaces for TeamViewer, nmap and ping, across ten tasks a honeypot would need to answer. The authors argue the value lies in extending attacker time-to-conquer and gathering TTPs.

## Contribution

Likely the first proposal of LLMs as honeypot back-ends (per [[bridges-2025-sok]]).

- Ten honeypot tasks demonstrated qualitatively with ChatGPT (abstract).
