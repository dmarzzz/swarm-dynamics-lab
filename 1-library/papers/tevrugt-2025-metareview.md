## Summary

A review of reviews: te Vrugt and Wittkowski survey the more than 600 existing review articles on active matter and
organise them by subfield, from statistical mechanics of active particles to medical microrobots, biological systems and
artificial swimmers, as an entry point to the literature. Read from the abstract on the page in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.
