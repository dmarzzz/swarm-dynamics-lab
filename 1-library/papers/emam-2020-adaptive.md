## Summary

Robots update specialization estimates from progress and reassign tasks when capabilities are unknown or change. Simulations and physical experiments demonstrate adaptive allocation under changing conditions without requiring an explicit environment model.

## Limitations and open questions

Adaptive specialization is established in robotics. Transfer to delayed, noisy language-agent feedback is a testable extension.
