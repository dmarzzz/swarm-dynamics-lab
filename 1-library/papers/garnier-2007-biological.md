## Summary

Review of the biological roots of swarm intelligence in self-organised behaviour of social insects. Covers the core
principles of decentralised organisation (self-organisation, stigmergy), groups collective behaviours into four
colony-level functions, and discusses how modulation of individual behaviour by environmental or internal factors
gives colonies flexibility; argues that understanding colony adaptation requires studying these modulations in
uncertain environments.

## Contribution
