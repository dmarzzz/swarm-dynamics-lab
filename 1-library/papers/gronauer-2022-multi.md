## Summary

A survey of recent multi-agent deep RL in three parts: the structure of training schemes used to train multiple agents (centralised, decentralised, CTDE), the emergent patterns of agent behaviour in cooperative, competitive and mixed scenarios, and the challenges that arise only in multi-agent settings (non-stationarity, credit assignment, scalability, partial observability) with the methods used to address them.

## Contribution

A widely used map of deep MARL with an explicit section on emergent behaviour patterns, more recent than [[hernandez-leal-2019-survey]].
