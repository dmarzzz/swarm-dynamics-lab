## Summary

A slow LLM placement agent and fast convex allocator coordinate shared compute, with a critic screening disruptive migrations. The reported experiments show improved deadline fulfillment across varying load conditions.

## Limitations and open questions

Multirate agent control and migration costs are established. Jev is not automatically preferable to the fast exact allocator.
