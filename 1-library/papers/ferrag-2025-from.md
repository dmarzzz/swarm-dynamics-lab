## Summary

This survey connects input-level threats to host-to-tool and agent-to-agent protocol risks in LLM-agent workflows. It organizes more than thirty attack techniques by attacker capabilities, objectives and affected layers, and discusses dynamic trust management, provenance and sandboxed interfaces. The authors report expert review and comparison with public incidents and vulnerability repositories as validation of the taxonomy.

## Contribution

Published evidence or modelling of propagation, containment or evaluation across agent trust boundaries.

## Key results

More than thirty threat techniques are categorized, according to the abstract. Taxonomy validation is not a quantitative containment test.
