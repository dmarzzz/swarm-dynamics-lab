## Summary

Full title on the publisher page: Security and Game Theory: Algorithms, Deployed Systems, Lessons Learned. Monograph on Stackelberg security games and the systems deployed from them. The publisher description says the method is to randomise security schedules to avoid predictability, weighting target importance and likely adversary reactions, and that the book studies long-term deployments with the Los Angeles airport police and the US Federal Air Marshals Service. I read only the Cambridge Core book page.
