## Summary

A position paper arguing that LLM MAS research relies on trial-and-error because (1) there is no structured taxonomy of design factors and (2) there is no metric separating genuine collaboration gain from extra compute. It proposes a collaboration gain metric Gamma that normalises for budget, a factor-attribution paradigm, and a MAS factor library that splits the design space into control-level presets and information-level dynamics.

## Contribution

Methodological agenda for controlled MAS experiments, from the ChatDev/MacNet group ([[qian-2023-chatdev]], [[qian-2025-scaling]]).
