## Summary

Tests graph agents by varying path multiplicity and evidential origins while keeping content fixed. The abstract reports model-dependent judgment effects and more repeated walks under redundant paths. Provenance-aware post-training improves synthetic accuracy but lowers accuracy on scientific claims despite reducing repetition.

## Contribution

Provenance-sensitive exploration can trade reduced repetition for lost source coverage.
