## Summary

Survey of LLM-generated text detection covering watermarking, statistics-based detectors, neural detectors and human-assisted methods, plus datasets and their limits. Identifies open problems including out-of-distribution generalisation, attacks, real-world data issues and the lack of a sound evaluation framework. Resources at github.com/NLP2CT/LLM-generated-Text-Detection.

## Contribution

Review article mapping the per-item detection field.

- Review (abstract): taxonomy of detectors and datasets; open problems in OOD robustness, attacks and evaluation.
