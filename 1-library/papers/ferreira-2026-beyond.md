## Summary

The authors compare debate and generation-budget-matched voting while varying personas, temperature and model identity. Across the reported 23 models and five tasks, diversity does not establish debate superiority. The abstract highlights serving-context overflow as a confound capable of changing the comparison.

## Contribution

Makes budget matching and actual delivered context central controls for debate claims.
