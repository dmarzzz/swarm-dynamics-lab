## Summary

Human teams with unevenly distributed evidence were joined by a bot informed by learned correlations among judgments. The intervention supported underrepresented opinions; it affected opinion dynamics and individual accuracy while producing only small team-performance effects.

## Contribution

VAE-informed intervention versus random bot in a repeated hidden-profile prediction task.

## Key results

Teams of ten included one bot. Minority persistence and polarization increased; the abstract does not establish a large collective accuracy gain.
