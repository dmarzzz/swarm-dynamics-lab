## Summary

The first part of a control-theory tutorial on social dynamics. It covers the most classical models of opinion
formation in social groups and relates them to recent results on multi-agent systems, arguing that new models,
network theory and data tools are closing the gap between social network analysis and control. Part II (2018,
same journal) covers further models.

## Contribution

A standard control-community entry to opinion dynamics (classical models such as [[degroot-1974-reaching]]),
framed with consensus tools; one of the most cited forward citations of
[[cucker-2007-emergent]] (Part II).

## Key results

- Abstract-level: tutorial; classical models and their relation to multi-agent consensus results.
