## Summary

Review article in the first issue of the journal Swarm Intelligence. Gives the authors' snapshot of PSO a decade after
its introduction: how the algorithm has changed, the variants derived, applications, theoretical studies of the
parameters and their effects, current research and open problems.

## Contribution

The standard PSO review cited by most later work (including [[pinnau-2017-consensus]] and
[[grassi-2021-particle]]); its reference list is the canonical map of early PSO variants and theory (topologies,
FIPS, bare-bones, dynamic environments, stagnation analyses).

## Key results

- Review; no new results. Abstract states it covers variants, research directions, applications and open problems.
- Backward references (OpenCitations) include stagnation-distribution analyses, Kadirkamanathan stability, small-world
  topology, Reynolds boids and Watts-Strogatz small worlds.

Literature review (abstract and reference list read).

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level reading. Dated (2007); theory has moved to order-2 stability and mean-field results
([[bonyadi-2017-particle]], [[huang-2023-global]]).
