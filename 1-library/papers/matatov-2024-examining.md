## Summary

Examines art subreddits with and without explicit AI bans, using posts where authors disclose AI use and comments that accuse others of using AI. Through the end of 2023 AI posts and accusations were under 0.5% of image posts; disclosed AI posts dwindled while accusations persisted and grew more negative in tone, especially where no explicit rule existed. AI content is used more by newcomers.

## Contribution

Uses self-disclosure and accusations as proxies for prevalence, showing suspicion outlasts disclosed use.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract): AI posts and accusations under 0.5% of image-based posts through 2023.
- Measured (abstract): accusations persist and turn more negative as disclosed AI posts decline.

## Methods and models

Reddit post and comment analysis of disclosure and accusation language. Abstract read only.

## Limitations and open questions

Disclosure-based counts miss undisclosed use by construction. Abstract depth.

## Relevance to us

Background on community-level detection by accusation, a noisy crowd detector; relevant to the false-accusation cost of any swarm detector.
