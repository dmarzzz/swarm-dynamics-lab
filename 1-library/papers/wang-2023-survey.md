## Summary

A systematic survey of LLM-based autonomous agents: a unified construction framework (profile, memory, planning, action modules), applications in social science, natural science and engineering, and evaluation strategies, with challenges and future directions. A companion repository tracks new work.

## Contribution

The most-cited single-agent survey; defines the vocabulary (profile, memory, planning, action) used to describe the individual agents inside swarms.

## Key results

- Review; no new data.

## Methods and models

Literature review. Repository: https://github.com/Paitesanshi/LLM-Agent-Survey

## Limitations and open questions

Focus is the individual agent; multi-agent dynamics are a minor theme.

## Relevance to us

Background for agent design; cite for definitions, not for collective results.
