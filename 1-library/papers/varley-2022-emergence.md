## Summary

Proposes that emergence is the conversion of information from one type to another under
coarse-graining. Using partial information decomposition of mutual information in Boolean networks and a new
"synergy bias" measure, it shows that synergy can increase at macroscales even when total mutual information is
unchanged.

## Contribution

Unifies effective-information causal emergence and PID-based emergence under an information
conversion view.

## Key results

- Coarse-graining can convert redundant/unique information into synergy without changing total mutual information (Boolean network examples).

## Methods and models

PID on Boolean networks; synergy bias measure. arXiv 2104.13368.

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level read; toy systems.
