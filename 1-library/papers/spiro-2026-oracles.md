## Summary

GPT-4o, Claude and Gemini forecast 568 resolved binary prediction questions with mean pairwise error correlation r = 0.77 (0.78 excluding likely-leaked questions), despite separate developers. A second study tracks community forecasts across the ChatGPT launch and finds a shift in the LLM-predicted direction (r = 0.20) that the abstract says is fully explained by other factors.
