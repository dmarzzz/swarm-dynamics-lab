## Summary

Reviews synchronisation of oscillators constrained to interact on complex network topologies, covering how structure shapes the emergence of sync, the interplay between structure and
function, numerical and analytical approaches, and applications in neuroscience, engineering and computer science, and
social science.

## Contribution

The standard physics review of network synchronisation; with [[rodrigues-2016-kuramoto]] (Kuramoto-specific)
and [[dorfler-2014-synchronization]] (control-theoretic) it maps the static-network literature that the
time-varying ([[ghosh-2022-synchronized]]) and mobile-agent literatures extend.
