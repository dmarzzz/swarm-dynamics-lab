## Summary

Contrasts manual metaheuristic design (designer intuition, often metaphor-driven) with automatic design using
algorithm configuration tools that assemble algorithms from components. Reviews the criticism of "novel" metaphor-based
metaheuristics and proposes automatic design as an alternative that could make that trend obsolete, then offers
suggestions for improving the field.

## Contribution

States the constructive programme of the critics: component-based frameworks plus automatic configuration (e.g.
their PSO-X framework, cited by [[vermetten-2024-large]]) instead of new metaphors.
