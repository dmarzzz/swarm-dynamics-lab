## Summary

Review article on authorship attribution in the LLM era. It organises the field into four problems: human-written text attribution, LLM-generated text detection, LLM-generated text attribution (which model), and human-LLM co-authored text attribution, and discusses generalisation across domains and explainability as the main open challenges. A maintained paper list accompanies it.

## Contribution

A taxonomy that separates detection (is it AI?) from attribution (which AI?), useful for scoping swarm forensics.

## Key results

- Survey; no new measurements. Identifies generalisation and explainability as key open problems (abstract).

## Methods and models

Literature review with benchmark and detector catalogue.

## Limitations and open questions

Text-only; agent behaviour, timing and network side channels are out of scope. Abstract-only reading.
