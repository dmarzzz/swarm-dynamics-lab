## Summary

Builds two taxonomies of nature- and bio-inspired optimisers, one by source of inspiration and one by algorithmic
behaviour (how the population is updated), and classifies 518 algorithms (in the latest arXiv version, covering
2020-2024). For each, identifies the most similar classical algorithm. Finds a poor relationship between an
algorithm's inspiration and its behaviour, and that more than one-fourth of the reviewed solvers are versions of
classical algorithms.

## Contribution

The largest systematic classification of SI/bio-inspired algorithms; supplies the quantitative backbone for the
claim that most "new" swarm algorithms are variants of PSO, DE or GA.

## Key results

- Measured (abstract, arXiv v5): 518 algorithms classified; more than 25% are versions of classical algorithms;
  inspiration is a poor predictor of behaviour.

## Methods and models

Literature classification with two taxonomies and analogy to classical algorithms (abstract only; 89-page arXiv
version not read in full).

## Limitations and open questions

Abstract-level reading. The arXiv version has been updated several times; the journal version (2020) covers fewer
algorithms than v5.

## Relevance to us

Use as the lookup table when someone proposes a "new" swarm optimiser in the hackathon: find its classical analogue
first.
