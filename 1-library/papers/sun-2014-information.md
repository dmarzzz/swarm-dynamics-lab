## Summary

Uses transfer entropy to analyse simulated swarms containing covert leaders, agents that follow the
same interaction rules as others but also act on external information. Covert leaders can be distinguished from
followers because they receive less transfer entropy than followers.

## Contribution

Shows incoming transfer entropy as a signature for detecting covert leaders in swarms.

## Key results

- Covert leaders receive less transfer entropy than followers (simulation).

## Methods and models

Swarm simulations with leadership models; transfer entropy between agents. arXiv 1407.0007
(journal-ref listed as ci-2014/141).

## Limitations and open questions

Simulation; abstract-level read.

## Relevance to us

Simple leader-detection diagnostic for agent swarms; interpret with the caveats in
[[sattari-2022-modes]]. Related: [[wang-2012-quantifying]], [[butail-2016-model]].
