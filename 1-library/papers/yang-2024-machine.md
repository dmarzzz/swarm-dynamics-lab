## Summary

A Review of how machine learning helps micro- and nanorobots, where actuation and locomotion defy conventional modelling, control suffers strong environmental disturbance, and in vivo tracking is noisy. It covers ML for design, actuation, locomotion, planning, tracking and navigation, with applications in micromanipulation, targeted delivery and therapy, bio-sensing and diagnosis, and closes with open issues and prospects.

## Contribution

The standard recent entry point to learning-based control at the micro/nano scale, including (judging by its scope) learned control of microrobot swarms. Most cited of the microrobot reviews found in this audit (99 Crossref citations).
