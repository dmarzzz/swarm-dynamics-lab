## Summary

Alternating magnetic fields program hematite colloidal particles into four swarm modes, liquid, chain, vortex
and ribbon, with fast, reversible transformations between them. The chain passes narrow channels, the
herring-school-like ribbon performs large-area synchronised manipulation, and the colony-like vortex aggregates
densely to handle heavy loads. Discrete particle simulations explain how each mode forms, the "tank-treading"
motion of chains and vortex merging; swarms can be steered in any direction and the vortex's chirality switched.

## Contribution

The most cited demonstration of multimode collective behaviour in field-driven microrobot swarms.

## Key results

- Measured: four reversible collective modes with distinct functions (from abstract).

## Methods and models

Hematite colloids under programmed alternating magnetic fields; discrete particle simulations. Abstract read.

## Limitations and open questions
