## Summary

A USC demonstration in which ChatGPT-driven bots with distinct personalities and political views discussed a fictional ballot proposition with human participants on a private Mastodon server. The bots passed as human, adapted their arguments to replies and spread disinformation; college-student participants in initial runs failed to identify them.

## Contribution

A qualitative demonstration of 'sleeper' LLM bots that blend in before being used; preliminary evidence only.

## Key results

- College students in initial experiments failed to identify the bots (abstract; no rates given).

## Methods and models

Private Mastodon server, ChatGPT personas, human participants. Abstract-level read.

## Limitations and open questions

Preliminary, no quantitative detection rates; the title's claim that such bots 'are already a political threat' is not measured in the paper as summarised.
