## Summary

Argues voting, electoral rules and LLM judges all aggregate forward (evidence to label) estimates that inherit correlated errors, and adds a reverse posterior from Bayesian backward reasoning with an explicit likelihood; agents are ranked by Jensen-Shannon cross-path consistency. On DDXPlus across five backbones, log-linear fusion performs best, with the largest gains where agents disagree.

## Contribution

A differently factorised signal to decorrelate collective decisions.
