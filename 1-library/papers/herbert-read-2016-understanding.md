## Summary

Review of how trajectory data from computer-vision and GPS tracking, analysed with statistical-physics tools,
reveal the interaction rules animals use in moving groups. Rules differ between species and between
individuals in the same group, which affects collective decisions and the evolution of collective motion.
Covers between-species similarities and differences in spatial and directional organisation, whether groups
of one species adapt their rules, and the ecological consequences of individual differences. Read at abstract
level via Europe PMC (open access, PMC5091654).
