## Summary

Kumar, Soni, Ramaswamy and Sood make millimetre-sized tapered rods motile on a vibrated surface among a medium of
spherical beads. Above a threshold bead area fraction the rods spontaneously align their velocities: a flocking
transition mediated by the medium (a moving rod drags beads; neighbours reorient in the bead flow "like a weathercock").
Theory and experiment agree on the phase diagram in rod and bead concentration and on power-law correlations near the
boundary. Read from the abstract in the Semantic Scholar API record in `url`; details beyond the abstract are not checked.

## Contribution

Flocking of non-living agents through a mediating medium rather than direct alignment, extending vibrated-granular
active matter ([[narayan-2007-long]], [[deseigne-2010-collective]]).
