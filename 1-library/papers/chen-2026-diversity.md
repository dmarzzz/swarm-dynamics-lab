## Summary

Studies diversity in multi-agent ideation at three levels. Stronger, more aligned models give diminishing marginal diversity; authority-driven dynamics suppress semantic diversity relative to junior-dominated groups; group-size scaling gives diminishing returns and dense topologies accelerate premature convergence. The authors call this diversity collapse from structural coupling.

## Contribution

Diminishing returns in N and premature convergence under dense topology, on an open-ended task.
