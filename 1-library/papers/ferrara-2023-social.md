## Summary

Ferrara reviews the state of social bot detection and argues that LLM chatbots break text-based detection, then proposes four directions: generative agents for synthetic training and evaluation data, multimodal cross-platform detection based on network and behavioural signatures of coordination, extension to non-English and low-resource languages, and federated detection models shared across platforms without pooling user data.

## Contribution

The most-cited early statement of what LLMs change for bot detection; frames coordination signatures as the durable signal.

## Key results

- Four research directions listed (abstract): synthetic data from generative agents; multimodal, cross-platform coordination detection; non-English settings; federated detection.
- Position piece; no new measurements.

## Limitations and open questions

No empirical test of the claims. Written in mid-2023, before most in-the-wild LLM-bot evidence.
