## Summary

This review examines combined prompt-manipulation and traditional web-security risks in agentic workflows, including AI worms and multi-agent infections. It argues that conventional application security checks do not by themselves constrain language-mediated authority transfer, and discusses prompt isolation, runtime security and privilege separation. Claims about discovery priority and vendor mitigations remain the authors' claims.
