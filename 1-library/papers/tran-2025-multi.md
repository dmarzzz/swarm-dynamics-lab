## Summary

Surveys the collaborative aspect of LLM multi-agent systems with a framework along five dimensions: actors, collaboration types (cooperation, competition, coopetition), structures (peer-to-peer, centralised, distributed), strategies (role-based, model-based) and coordination protocols. Reviews applications (5G/6G, Industry 5.0, question answering, social and cultural settings) and lists open challenges toward "artificial collective intelligence".

## Contribution

A structure-centred taxonomy that is directly useful for classifying swarm-like (distributed, peer-to-peer) vs orchestrated systems.

## Limitations and open questions

Taxonomic; does not cover statistical-physics or collective-dynamics measurement work.
