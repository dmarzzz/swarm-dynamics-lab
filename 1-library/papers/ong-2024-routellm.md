## Summary

Preference-trained routers select between stronger and weaker models to reduce inference cost while preserving response quality in reported benchmarks. The authors also test transfer when the underlying models change.

## Limitations and open questions

Query routing is already studied; a swarm question needs state, feedback, interactions or resource contention beyond independent queries.
