## Summary

Survey that unifies LLM fingerprinting (non-intrusive, from intrinsic characteristics) and watermarking (intrusive, deliberately embedded) under the term implicit identity. It gives a lifecycle taxonomy across datasets, models and generated content, separates similarity-based attribution from keyed verification, and proposes evaluation around identifiability, robustness and deployability.

## Contribution

Clarifies terminology that the field uses inconsistently, which matters when combining fingerprint and canary evidence.
