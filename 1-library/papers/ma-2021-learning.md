## Summary

The paper replaces broad communication with learned request-reply selection in multi-agent path finding. Neighbors are selected according to whether their presence changes a local decision, aiming to reduce unnecessary communication while maintaining coordinated movement.

## Contribution

Decision Causal Communication with decentralized selection from local observations.

## Key results

The abstract reports high success with low communication overhead, without numerical effect sizes.
