## Goal

Follow the experiment worker and agent lifecycle runbooks to turn owner-selected HX-31 into Poietic Agents: an initially uniform swarm that discovers shared services, sheds redundant capabilities and hardens repeated work into cheaper execution. Produce a concrete prospective design and an honest launch-readiness record. Preserve the distinction between design publication, exploratory qualification and formal confirmation.

## Done when

- Specify workload, adaptation mechanism, strong baselines, units, quality and freshness constraints, total cost, shifts, stages, controls and kill criteria.
- Write the plan before implementation; include machine-readable design, agent/context contracts, pre-run assessment and visualization mapping.
- Publish the immutable design, prepare condition-specific TLDRs, and record public registration status without fabricating runs or approvals.
- Request independent design review and scope the outstanding prior-art work to the selected question.
- Validate repository structure, cross-file consistency and links; record unresolved launch gates and an actionable handoff.
