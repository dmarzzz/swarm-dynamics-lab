## Goal

Collect the best non-academic writing on swarm dynamics across all topics: explainers, lab blogs, engineering write-ups of agent systems, and essays that argue a position.

## Seeds

Seeds come from memory and are starting points, not citations. Open each one, confirm the details, and catalogue it only if it checks out.

- Craig Reynolds' Boids page (red3d.com/cwr/boids)
- Engineering write-ups on multi-agent LLM systems, including Anthropic's post on how its multi-agent research system was built (2025) and Cognition's 'Don't build multi-agents' (2025), which argue opposite sides
- Quanta Magazine coverage of flocking, active matter and swarmalators
- Lab websites of the groups behind the seminal papers (they often host explainers and videos)

- Follow blog links from the X thread scans and from paper project pages.
